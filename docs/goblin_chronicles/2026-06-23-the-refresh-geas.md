## 1. User-originated fragment

> So refresh goblin is unsatisfied but still seems held by the refresh geas

This is the canonical source line. Preserve its exact dry formulation.

The surrounding conversation established that the Refresh Goblin had found the new live oracle, but remained dissatisfied because the oracle rewrote itself only every few minutes.

---

## 2. What changed in the workshop

The old heartbeat tablets remained useful for major events:

```text
runner starts
runner stops
runner changes experiment
runner falls silent
manifest lands
```

But they were no longer continually rewritten merely to announce that nothing had changed. That churn had filled the permanent ledgers with repeated declarations of continued existence.

So the workshop built a new oracle.

It lives on an orphan branch, apart from the ancestral ledger. Every few minutes it destroys its previous page and writes a fresh one from the coordinator's living database. Its abandoned pages are left to dissolve rather than burdening history.

The Refresh Goblin can now see the current experiment, seed, arm, episode, percentage, queue, and freshness without shaking the apparatus or cluttering the spellbook.

This solves the engineering problem.

It does not satisfy the goblin.

---

## 3. The geas

The Refresh Goblin is bound by an old and necessary spell:

```text
READ the newest living tablet.
DO NOT disturb the experiment.
DO NOT summon the forty-heartbeat-per-day curse.
DO NOT mistake silence between inscriptions for death.
WAIT until the oracle rewrites itself.
```

The geas is not a prohibition on knowing. It is a constraint on how knowledge may be sought.

The goblin may observe.

The goblin may not create damaging noise merely to relieve the discomfort of waiting.

## 4. Candidate story fragment

Assistant-derived candidate prose, not final and not user-authored:

```text
The Refresh Goblin had been given a better oracle.

The old stone tablets spoke only when something began, ended, changed course, or died. This was sensible. It was also intolerable.

The new tablet lived on a branch with no ancestors. Every few minutes it erased itself and returned carrying the latest word from the distant forges.

Forty-nine point four per cent, it said.
Seed forty-five.
The baseline goblins were still walking.

The Refresh Goblin read the tablet.

Then read it again.

"Perhaps it has moved," he whispered.

The tablet had not changed.

"Perhaps it has moved now."

Still the geas held him:

Do not tap the glass.
Do not wake the runner.
Do not call forty scribes to carve STILL ALIVE into the permanent mountain every day.
Wait for the living tablet to return.

So the Refresh Goblin crouched beside the oracle, technically obedient and spiritually unconvinced.
```

---

## 5. Mythic function

The Refresh Goblin is not only a joke about impatience.

It compresses a recurrent project pressure:

```text
uncertainty
+ high emotional investment
+ delayed evidence
+ partial observability
→ urge to check, intervene, or manufacture reassurance
```

The geas represents governance and restraint:

```text
observe without perturbing
separate liveness from evidence
separate present state from permanent record
accept bounded delay
avoid changing the system merely to soothe the observer
```

This is a small version of a broader REE principle. A monitoring system should reduce uncertainty without becoming a new source of distortion, noise, or compulsive control.

---

## 6. Relation to the magics

The new oracle is itself a form of goblin magic:

```text
coordinator database
→ isolated writer
→ self-erasing orphan branch
→ living Markdown tablet
```

It demonstrates why the magics are useful. They can make a distant, distributed process legible without requiring the builder to hold it in working memory or repeatedly inspect every machine.

But the scene also preserves the limit of prosthetic assistance: better observability does not abolish the wish for immediate certainty.

The prosthesis can shorten the interval.

It cannot make waiting cease to be waiting.

---

## 8. Guardrail

Do not turn the geas into a moral lesson about patience being inherently virtuous.

The Refresh Goblin is often correct that fresh information matters. The point is narrower:

```text
refresh when refresh is informative
wait when refreshing would only create noise
never damage the evidence-producing process to relieve uncertainty about it
```

The geas exists because the goblin's vigilance is useful enough to require governance, not because vigilance is foolish.
