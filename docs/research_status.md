## What this work is, and is not

None of the REE materials, related manuscript drafts, or prototype results has
been accepted for peer-reviewed publication. A repository, public release, DOI,
or timestamped record is evidence that material was made public at a given time;
it is not evidence of peer review, journal acceptance, independent validation, or
clinical efficacy.

Some threads may be incomplete, superseded, unsuccessful, or retained primarily
as a record of investigation. The public site is intended to make that state
visible rather than smooth it away.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active research | A live line of inquiry or implementation work, subject to change. |
| Draft manuscript | A manuscript draft that has not been accepted for publication. |
| Submission attempt / not accepted | A manuscript or proposal that did not achieve publication acceptance. |
| Prototype | Exploratory software or a bounded demonstration, not a validated product. |
| Preliminary result | An early result whose interpretation remains constrained by its setup. |
| Negative or inconclusive result | A result that did not support the tested expectation or cannot resolve it. |
| Archived / superseded | Material retained as a versioned research record, not current guidance. |

## How to read the site

- Read source links and revision context alongside any summary.
- Treat architectural claims, hypotheses, experimental results, and commentary
  as different kinds of material.
- Use the claims and evidence records for the latest project-specific state.
- Do not treat the work as clinical advice, a validated safety system, or a
  substitute for independent replication and professional judgement.

## Updates and corrections

The REE Assembly site is generated from the repository. Its current research
front, claims records, and public data exports are maintained through that
pipeline. Narrative status labels and cross-project relationships are reviewed
explicitly so that public-facing claims remain legible when the underlying work
changes.
