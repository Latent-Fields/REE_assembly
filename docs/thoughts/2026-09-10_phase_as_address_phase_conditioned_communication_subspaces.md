## 1. Why this is a separate thought

Several lines of reasoning that previously looked adjacent now appear capable of being expressed as one organising problem:

1. brain dynamics are strongly oscillatory and phase-dependent;
2. communication between neuronal populations can depend on relative timing and coherence rather than anatomy alone;
3. travelling waves provide moving spatial phase gradients and are associated with selectively directed information flow during wakefulness;
4. task uncertainty can leave irrelevant information strongly represented while making normally separable task dimensions interfere with one another;
5. REE's current mutual-legibility work is already framed in terms of receiver-potent communication subspaces, frozen endpoints, causal use, and dynamic compatibility;
6. anaesthesia may preserve substantial neural activity and information transfer while disrupting the organisation that determines which information can effectively influence what next;
7. ephaptic coupling and other field/regulatory mechanisms provide possible routes by which population activity could feed back onto local excitability and timing.

The synthesis is not that REE should contain artificial brain waves. The stronger and more useful possibility is:

> **Phase may function as an addressing coordinate for causal access: a representation can remain present continuously while its ability to influence a particular receiver depends on when, and in which organism-level dynamical state, it is presented.**

This makes oscillatory timing, communication geometry, and dynamic information governance different views of the same control problem.

---

## 2. The central decomposition

A useful decomposition is now:

- **representation** — what information exists;
- **legibility / transformation** — whether another system could understand it;
- **communication subspace** — which dimensions of the sender are receiver-potent;
- **phase / temporal address** — when that receiver-potent channel is open;
- **routing geometry** — how these temporally gated channels are arranged across the organism;
- **causal privilege / jurisdiction** — which represented information can actually alter the next state of the organism.

This makes it possible for the same information to be simultaneously:

- present;
- decodable by an external observer;
- structurally connected to a downstream system;
- yet temporarily unable to affect that downstream system.

The missing variable is not necessarily content or connectivity. It may be **temporal accessibility**.

---

## 3. Phase as an addressing variable

The key conceptual move is to treat phase not merely as a property of an oscillation, but as a possible **addressing coordinate**.

A compact analogy is:

- geometry = where information lives;
- communication subspace = which portion of that geometry is visible to a receiver;
- phase = when that address is readable;
- travelling wave = where the read window moves next;
- regulation = what controls the read schedule;
- causal privilege = what information consequently gains jurisdiction over downstream state.

This differs from a simple global attention gate. A global scalar gain can say "more" or "less" processing. A phase-addressed system can support many simultaneously represented streams whose causal efficacy differs according to timing and receiver.

Thus the relevant object may be not merely:

`S_comm(A -> B)`

but:

`S_comm(A -> B | phi, c)`

where:

- `phi` is an endogenous phase or routing coordinate;
- `c` is the current control / organismal context.

A sender dimension could lie inside the receiver-potent subspace at one phase and outside it at another without the sender representation itself disappearing.

---

## 4. Geometric and temporal separability

The recent feature-interference work sharpens an earlier intuition: irrelevant information need not be deleted in order to avoid behavioural interference. It can remain represented provided it is sufficiently separated from the dimensions governing the current decision.

This gives at least two forms of separability.

### 4.1 Geometric separability

Two information streams occupy sufficiently distinct population dimensions:

`S_A approximately orthogonal to S_B`

The irrelevant stream remains available but does not strongly project into the currently receiver-potent computation.

### 4.2 Temporal separability

Two streams need not be perfectly geometrically orthogonal if they become effective at different temporal addresses:

`A effective around phi_A`

`B effective around phi_B`

The system can therefore achieve the functional equivalent of orthogonalisation through temporal multiplexing.

This matters because biological circuits are reused. Perfect anatomical or representational separation for every concurrent process would be expensive and inflexible. Phase-dependent access could permit overlapping circuitry to support multiple streams without indiscriminate interference.

A likely biological system uses both forms together rather than choosing one.

---

## 5. Travelling waves as moving read windows

A travelling wave is a spatially organised phase gradient. In the present synthesis, the important possibility is therefore not that a travelling wave is a packet carrying semantic content.

Instead:

> **A travelling wave may instantiate a moving pattern of receiver availability.**

One population becomes maximally receptive, then another, then another. If sender activity is appropriately timed, the resulting sequence could create a privileged causal trajectory through the network.

Abstractly:

`A --phi1--> B --phi2--> C --phi3--> D`

The propagation is therefore a changing pattern of effective connectivity rather than a fixed anatomical route.

This provides a possible dynamical implementation of the previous thought's phrase:

> a travelling frontier of causal privilege across the cognifold.

It also explains why wakefulness could be associated with more reliable directionally tuned information routing without requiring more total information transfer.

---

## 6. Uncertainty as a failure of segregation

The task-uncertainty result suggests a broader interpretation of cognitive interference.

Under uncertainty, failure need not be:

`irrelevant information became too strong`

alone.

It can instead be:

`irrelevant information remained strong + the system lost sufficient segregation between relevant and irrelevant causal channels`.

That loss of segregation could occur at several levels:

- representational subspaces become less orthogonal;
- irrelevant dimensions overlap more strongly with the communication subspace;
- temporal windows become less differentiated;
- phase relationships become less selective;
- routing motifs become less stable;
- top-down regulation fails to maintain the appropriate receiver-potent geometry.

These possibilities are not alternatives in principle. They may form one multiscale failure.

This suggests that **information governance is not equivalent to filtering-out**. It may instead consist in preserving a rich representation while continuously keeping non-current content outside the causal path relevant to the present computation.

---

## 7. Regulatory systems as geometry-and-phase controllers

The hypothesised routing layer requires control variables. These should not yet be collapsed into one mechanism.

Candidate biological contributors include:

- excitation/inhibition balance;
- recurrent inhibitory timing;
- neuromodulatory gain and precision-like control;
- thalamocortical coordination;
- laminar feedforward/feedback timing;
- intrinsic membrane resonance;
- astrocytic and metabolic regulation;
- extracellular field effects / ephaptic coupling;
- other mesoscopic population dynamics.

The relevant abstraction for REE is broader than any one of these:

> **local and global regulatory state alters the effective geometry and timing of communication without necessarily altering what is represented.**

This is compatible with REE's existing control-plane concepts such as precision, gain, mode, arousal, and veto, but the present thought adds a requirement that such variables may need to regulate *relations between modules in time*, not merely scalar properties of individual modules.

---

## 8. Ephaptic coupling as a candidate contributor, not the foundation

Ephaptic coupling is particularly interesting because it permits population activity to influence nearby neuronal excitability through extracellular electric fields, creating a possible feedback loop:

`local neural activity -> extracellular field -> altered excitability/timing -> altered local neural activity`

This is attractive for the present theory because the regulating state is partly generated by the activity it regulates. It provides a route to genuine circular causality and mesoscopic self-organisation.

However, the current thought should not depend on ephaptic coupling being necessary or dominant.

Three distinctions must remain explicit:

1. **dynamic information governance** is the computational hypothesis;
2. **phase-conditioned communication** is one candidate dynamical implementation principle;
3. **ephaptic coupling** is one candidate biological contributor to the phase/regulatory dynamics.

The computational principle survives if ephaptic effects turn out to be minor and regulation is predominantly synaptic, thalamic, inhibitory, neuromodulatory, or some mixture.

---

## 9. Anaesthesia prediction

This synthesis changes the predicted computational lesion under general anaesthesia.

The prediction is not:

`oscillation disappears`

nor:

`information transfer disappears`.

Instead:

> **The system loses the ability to generate a sufficiently diverse, selective, rapidly reconfigurable set of phase-conditioned communication subspaces and routing trajectories.**

An anaesthetised system may therefore show:

- strong oscillatory activity;
- large-scale travelling waves;
- substantial or even increased total information transfer;
- intact local representations;
- intact local subsystem dynamics;

while nevertheless losing:

- directional informational tuning;
- flexible phase relationships;
- selective receiver access;
- differentiated routing motifs;
- the ability to sequence causal privilege appropriately across systems.

This predicts that the relevant difference between conscious and anaesthetised-like states is not raw activity but the **combinatorial richness and selectivity of `(representation, subspace, phase, receiver)` configurations** available to the organism.

---

## 10. Sleep, replay, and temporal interface maintenance

This also extends the hippocampal replay hypothesis.

Replay may maintain more than a static correspondence between sender representation and receiver representation. It may maintain the timing relationship through which that correspondence becomes usable.

The maintained object could therefore be:

`sender content <-> receiver-compatible representation <-> appropriate temporal/routing state`

or, more abstractly:

`content <-> access correspondence`.

Correct replay could preserve which representation should become receiver-potent under which context and temporal state. Shuffled replay could expose the same states and the same amount of activity while corrupting that access relationship.

This provides a new interpretation of **causal access correspondence** introduced in the dynamic-routing thought: correspondence may include a temporal coordinate.

## 11. Immediate REE assay: phase-conditioned communication subspace

The first experiment should be diagnostic and external to the organism.

Do not add oscillators to `ree_core` first.

### 11.1 Record candidate endogenous coordinates

For suitable information-preserving interfaces, record:

- sender state;
- actual receiver input and internal state;
- task-relevant and task-irrelevant features;
- actions / committed candidates;
- existing control-plane variables;
- recurrent / periodic state variables already present;
- sleep/wake or developmental mode;
- time since salient events, commits, prediction errors, or control transitions.

If no literal oscillatory variable exists, derive candidate cyclical or phase-like coordinates from recurrent dynamics rather than inventing one.

### 11.2 Estimate conditional communication geometry

Estimate a baseline communication subspace `S_comm(A -> B)` using the existing Reduced Rank Regression (RRR) plan.

Then condition or stratify it on candidate phase/routing state:

`S_comm(A -> B | phi_bin)`

Measure:

- subspace principal angles across phase bins;
- held-out receiver prediction;
- task information retained in each conditioned subspace;
- task-irrelevant intrusion;
- downstream behavioural sensitivity;
- whether phase-conditioned models outperform a static subspace at matched complexity.

### 11.3 Key positive signature

A strong result would be:

> information remains continuously decodable from the sender, but its downstream causal effect varies systematically with an endogenous phase/routing coordinate, and the receiver-potent communication subspace changes with that coordinate.

That would distinguish routing from representation loss.

## 12. Interference experiment

A particularly clean assay should reproduce the uncertainty structure in REE.

Construct two simultaneously represented feature families, `A` and `B`, with context determining which is behaviourally relevant.

Across high-certainty and low-certainty conditions measure:

- `decode(A)` and `decode(B)` from the source;
- principal angle between `S_A` and `S_B`;
- overlap of each with `S_comm`;
- phase-conditioned overlap with `S_comm(phi)`;
- intrusion of the irrelevant feature into action;
- behavioural performance.

Potential outcomes:

### Geometry-only

Uncertainty changes `angle(S_A, S_B)` but phase-conditioned communication adds no explanatory power.

**Interpretation:** static representational segregation may be sufficient.

### Timing-only

Representation geometry stays similar but causal impact alternates with phase/routing state.

**Interpretation:** temporal multiplexing is sufficient for segregation.

### Joint

Geometry and phase-conditioned access both reorganise and jointly predict behavioural intrusion.

**Interpretation:** stronger support for multiscale information governance.

### Null

Neither geometry nor temporal routing explains interference better than a static consumer model.

**Interpretation:** do not add this layer.

## 13. Necessary falsifiers

The idea should be discarded or sharply weakened if any simpler explanation survives matched tests.

Strong falsifiers include:

1. a static communication subspace explains behaviour and receiver activity as well as any phase-conditioned model;
2. global scalar gain explains the effect without receiver-specific temporal structure;
3. random temporal labels perform as well as the endogenous phase coordinate;
4. phase predicts correlation but not causal susceptibility;
5. the effect disappears after controlling for ordinary task context, action identity, or arousal;
6. apparent phase dependence is generated by trivial periodicity in environment sampling;
7. conditioned subspaces improve only by adding model capacity and fail complexity-matched controls;
8. downstream behaviour is unchanged when the hypothesised read window is selectively disrupted.

A successful theory must explain more than periodic covariance.

---

## 14. Causal lesion ladder

If observational evidence survives, use matched perturbations:

1. **phase shuffle** — preserve all values but scramble temporal alignment;
2. **phase flattening** — remove the temporal modulation while preserving mean gain;
3. **receiver-specific phase swap** — give receiver B the timing assigned to receiver C;
4. **direction reversal** — reverse a learned propagation sequence where meaningful;
5. **slow-mode lock** — collapse a diverse routing repertoire into one dominant periodic mode;
6. **static matched-gain control** — use the same integrated gain without phase structure;
7. **content-preserving perturbation** — verify that source decodability remains intact.

The key anaesthesia-like result would be:

`content preserved + total communication preserved or increased + directional/selective routing degraded + behaviour degraded`.

---

## 15. Relationship to existing mutual-legibility work

This thought does not replace the current bridge and communication-subspace programme.

It adds one dimension to it.

The existing sequence remains:

1. establish sender information;
2. establish actual receiver access;
3. estimate communication subspace;
4. test constrained bridges;
5. test causal and behavioural use;
6. test dynamic compatibility.

The present thought asks, after those are measurable:

7. is communication geometry state- or phase-conditioned?
8. does that conditioning explain when information becomes causally effective?
9. can temporally targeted perturbations dissociate content from access?
10. does replay maintain this temporal access relationship?

Thus the phase hypothesis belongs naturally as a **conditional extension of the missing interface instrument**, not as a parallel architecture programme.

---

## 16. Architectural restraint

No permanent phase mechanism should be introduced merely because biological cognition is oscillatory.

The implementation order should remain:

`observe -> model -> condition -> perturb -> compare simpler rivals -> only then implement`.

If an explicit artificial oscillator is eventually required, it should earn its place by improving selective communication, interference resistance, causal routing, developmental robustness, or sleep/replay maintenance beyond matched non-oscillatory controls.

The goal is not biological decoration.

The goal is to discover whether **time itself is part of the address by which REE's representations become mutually usable**.

---

## 17. Broader theoretical consequence

If supported, this synthesis would imply that a cognitive state's effective architecture cannot be described by a static wiring diagram or by static latent geometry alone.

At each moment, the organism would instantiate a temporary effective graph:

`G_eff(t) = f(anatomy, representation geometry, control state, phase, history)`

Different information streams can therefore share the same physical or computational substrate yet belong to different effective networks at different times.

The organism's functional architecture becomes something continuously constructed rather than simply possessed.

This would provide a common language for:

- attention and uncertainty;
- communication subspaces;
- travelling waves;
- oscillatory coherence;
- anaesthesia;
- replay and interface maintenance;
- precision/gain regulation;
- possible ephaptic/field effects;
- and REE's broader requirement for dynamic information governance.

The emerging proposition is:

> **Cognition may depend on maintaining a dynamically changing, phase-indexed geometry of causal access in which rich information remains broadly represented but only context-appropriate information is granted effective downstream jurisdiction at the right time.**

Or, in the shortest form:

> **Phase may be part of the address.**
