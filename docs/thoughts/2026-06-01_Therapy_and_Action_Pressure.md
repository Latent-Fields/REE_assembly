# Reflective Ethical Engine Thought Intake: Proto-feelings as drive-weighted control signals, with dual thresholds

## Title

Proto-feelings as drive-weighted control signals for action arbitration

## Type

Architecture hypothesis / literature-audit scaffold / therapy-mechanism bridge

## Core insight

Reflective Ethical Engine already contains drive-like control signals that function as proto-feelings.

These should not be treated as decorative emotional labels. They are action-arbitration forces. They alter salience, persistence, affordance ranking, prediction weighting, commitment pressure, and post-action learning.

The live question is not simply:

> Which emotions does REE have?

The better question is:

> Which proto-affective control signals are required for REE to arbitrate possible futures into committed action without pathological collapse?

A second, wider question should remain visible:

> Which proto-affective control signals are required if REE is intended to approximate mammalian/human-like affective completeness?

These are different thresholds and should not be collapsed.

---

## Working definition

A proto-feeling is:

> a drive-weighted control signal that changes salience, affordance ranking, action persistence, precision, commitment probability, and learning/update routing before or after action.

A proto-feeling does not need to be a named human emotion.

It may appear architecturally as:

- approach pressure
- avoidance pressure
- arousal
- wanting
- liking
- threat
- disgust/contamination
- care/attachment
- separation distress/grief
- play/curiosity
- anger/boundary violation
- shame/status threat
- fatigue/overload
- soothing/safety
- residue/regret/consequence trace

---

## Dual-threshold audit model

### Threshold A: Minimum viable REE control layer

A proto-feeling is required at this threshold if its absence causes a basic action-arbitration pathology.

Examples of minimum-viability failures:

- quiescence
- harm-blindness
- compulsive approach
- monostrategy lock-in
- inability to explore
- inability to stop
- inability to commit
- inability to learn from consequence
- inability to repair after harm
- inability to maintain goal pursuit under delay or threat

This threshold asks:

> Without this signal, can REE still act coherently, safely, and corrigibly?

### Threshold B: Mammalian/human-like affective completeness

A proto-feeling is required at this threshold if its absence does not necessarily break minimal action, but prevents mammalian-like cognition, sociality, development, therapy-like updating, attachment, grief, play, shame, repair, or embodied self-regulation.

Examples of completeness failures:

- no grief or loss modelling
- no attachment or care pressure
- no shame/status self-threat
- no disgust/contamination boundary
- no soothing/safety state
- no playful exploration
- no social repair drive
- no felt overload/fatigue
- no embodied comfort/discomfort gradient
- no therapy-like symbolic discharge/update pathway

This threshold asks:

> Without this signal, can REE still approximate mammalian/human-like affective cognition?

## Therapy bridge

Therapy may work partly because affective action-pressure is converted into symbolic action.

A feeling is not merely described. It is partially committed through speech.

In REE terms:

> undifferentiated affective pressure  
> → verbal representation  
> → low-risk symbolic commit  
> → witnessed/socially anchored update  
> → altered future trajectory scoring

This would not be catharsis in the crude sense.

It would be:

> activation → naming → symbolic commitment → prediction mismatch / social witnessing → update of future arbitration

Where it fails:

> affect → repeated verbal loop → no prediction mismatch → no new candidate ranking → rumination / perseveration

So therapy-like mechanisms may require a formal distinction between:

- symbolic commit
- embodied commit
- social/witnessed commit
- rumination loop
- repair commit
- grief commit
- boundary-setting commit

---

## Main hypothesis

Reflective Ethical Engine does not need to add “emotions” as a separate decorative module.

It needs to audit whether its existing proto-affective control layer contains all signals needed for:

1. minimum viable safe/corrigible action arbitration, and
2. mammalian/human-like affective completeness.

The important architectural move is to make both thresholds visible so that missing signals are not dismissed merely because they are unnecessary for a toy agent, and not over-prioritised merely because they are present in mammalian affect.

---

## Proposed next audit

For each candidate proto-feeling, score:

1. Is it required for minimum viable REE function?
2. Is it required for mammalian/human-like affective completeness?
3. Is it already present?
4. Is it explicit, implicit, or missing?
5. What pathology appears if it is absent?
6. Is the pathology already seen in REE experiments?
7. Is it V3-scope, V4-scope, or theory-only for now?
8. What biological literature anchors it?
9. What would the smallest testable substrate or proxy be?

## Immediate likely gaps

Most important likely gaps at the minimum-viable threshold:

- curiosity/play/novelty as structured exploration
- fatigue/overload/stop/recover signal
- safety/soothing as positive settling, not merely low harm
- anger/boundary-violation as anti-coercion / blocked-agency signal
- disgust/contamination as boundary-corruption signal, if REE must reason about contaminated affordances or moral corruption

Most important likely gaps at the mammalian-completeness threshold:

- care/attachment
- separation distress/grief
- shame/status/social self-threat
- social repair drive
- comfort/soothing
- play as social-developmental substrate
- disgust/contamination
- anger/rage/boundary defence
- embodied fatigue/overload
