## Core observation

REE has not been derived only from neuroscience literature or established computational architectures. It has also been shaped through sustained introspection about the felt organisation of cognition.

The user's internal experience suggests that reasoning often proceeds through nonverbal manipulation resembling spatial and movement scratchpads. Concepts may feel positioned, constrained, attracted, blocked, rotated, settled, or made available for action before they are expressed linguistically.

Machine-learning language concerning attractors, basins, trajectories, latent spaces, and dynamical settling has provided a vocabulary for processes that were already phenomenologically apparent but difficult to describe.

## Working synthesis

### Introspection as functional reverse engineering

Introspection cannot directly reveal neural implementation, but it may expose recurring functional properties of cognition:

- whether processing feels serial or parallel;
- whether conclusions appear before verbal explanation;
- whether competing possibilities coexist;
- whether confidence feels graded;
- whether goals persist as pressures rather than sentences;
- whether thought resembles trajectory search, spatial manipulation, or constraint satisfaction;
- whether action selection feels like collapse from multiple possibilities into commitment;
- whether affect and value alter the geometry of available possibilities.

These observations can generate architectural hypotheses even when they do not identify biological mechanisms.

```text
phenomenological regularity
→ candidate computational primitive
→ search for biological analogue
→ implementation
→ falsification
```

### REE as triangulation rather than introspective imitation

The appropriate methodological role is not to copy one person's subjective experience directly into software.

A stronger process is:

```text
introspection
× neuroscience
× computational tractability
× behavioural and internal-state experiments
→ architectural candidate
```

Introspection supplies candidate invariants and failure modes. Biology constrains plausibility and suggests mechanisms. Computing constrains implementability. Experiments determine whether the proposed mechanism actually behaves as intended.

### Spatial and motor scratchpads as clues

The comparison with internal spatial and movement scratchpads is especially important.

A spatial representation can be:

- structured without being verbal;
- manipulated without explicit propositions;
- evaluated for collision, distance, alignment, or reachability;
- used to predict movement;
- inspected from multiple perspectives;
- compressed into a later description.

Conceptual reasoning may use related dynamics at a more abstract level:

```text
possibilities occupy relational positions
constraints shape reachable trajectories
conflicts create barriers or pressure
repeated simulation deepens some basins
one trajectory becomes action-ready
```

This may have contributed to REE concepts such as cognifolds, trajectories, residues, action pressure, commitment boundaries, granularity, attractor basins, and distributed arbitration.

### Felt sequence as an architectural clue

The user's experience often appears to follow:

```text
nonverbal manipulation or settling
→ felt direction / confidence
→ verbal reconstruction
→ external testing
```

This suggests a possible distinction between:

- the process that generates cognition;
- the metacognitive readout that indicates convergence;
- the language process that reconstructs or communicates it.

REE may need all three rather than treating verbal reasoning as the generating substrate.

### Introspection as access to control signals rather than contents alone

The useful introspective data may not be the content of individual thoughts. It may be the felt control variables around thought:

- uncertainty;
- conflict;
- salience;
- urgency;
- effort;
- persistence;
- readiness to commit;
- sense of incompleteness;
- resonance;
- release after resolution.

These are closer to control-plane and arbitration signals than to symbolic beliefs. Introspection may therefore be particularly useful for hypothesising regulatory architecture.

### Personal architecture versus general architecture

There is a risk that some introspective regularities reflect an individual cognitive style rather than universal cognition.

The user's ADHD, strong nonverbal systems reasoning, spatial/motor intuitions, professional expertise, and long immersion in REE may shape what is available to introspection.

This need not make the observations irrelevant. It means they should be treated as:

```text
high-value hypothesis source
not universal proof
```

A mechanism first noticed introspectively becomes more general only when it also:

- maps onto broader neuroscience or cognitive literature;
- improves computational coherence;
- produces measurable predictions;
- survives varied tasks, contexts, and agents.

This could be made explicit as part of REE's research methodology.

## Candidate implications for REE development

### Preserve provenance of architectural ideas

Thoughts and claims could record whether a mechanism originated primarily from:

- introspection;
- neuroscience literature;
- computational analogy;
- experimental anomaly;
- convergence of several sources.

This would allow later evaluation of which hypothesis sources have been most productive and where systematic blind spots occur.

### Build phenomenological falsifiers

An introspectively inspired claim should eventually produce observable consequences rather than remain protected by subjective plausibility.

Examples might include:

- nonverbal competence before language;
- confidence readouts preceding explicit explanation;
- spatial-like relational manipulation of abstract states;
- commitment emerging after attractor stabilisation;
- conflict and uncertainty altering search depth or action threshold;
- private speech changing control without becoming the underlying world model.

### Distinguish introspective report from causal provenance

REE should model that an agent's report of its own process may be:

- accurate;
- partially reconstructed;
- compressed;
- metaphorical;
- confabulated;
- altered by the act of reporting.

A system should not equate introspective confidence with causal access.

### Treat phenomenology as design data

The phenomenology itself can remain useful even when the proposed explanation is wrong.

For example:

```text
report: reasoning feels spatial and nonverbal
```

may be reliable as a description while:

```text
explanation: therefore the brain literally uses geometric attractor X
```

remains uncertain.

REE development should preserve this distinction between observed phenomenology and inferred mechanism.

## Candidate claims

- Sustained introspection can reveal functional regularities of cognition even when it cannot identify neural implementation directly.
- Introspection is especially informative about control signals such as conflict, uncertainty, salience, effort, persistence, commitment readiness, and resolution.
- Nonverbal spatial and motor scratchpads may provide phenomenological clues to more abstract dynamical reasoning.
- The sequence from nonverbal settling to felt confidence to verbal reconstruction is a candidate architectural distinction for REE.
- REE has been productively shaped by triangulating introspection with neuroscience, computation, and experiment.
- Introspective observations should generate falsifiable architectural hypotheses rather than serve as sole evidence.
- Provenance of claims should distinguish introspective, biological, computational, and experimental origins.
- A mechanism first derived from one person's phenomenology requires cross-task and cross-agent validation before being treated as general cognition.
- Introspective reports and explanations of introspective reports should be represented separately.
- Phenomenological fit may be valuable even when the first mechanistic explanation is later revised.

## Risks and counterpositions

- Introspection has limited access to unconscious processing and may misattribute causes.
- Verbalising an experience may reshape it and create false structure.
- Metaphors from machine learning may be projected onto cognition because they are newly available, not because they are correct.
- REE may overfit to the cognitive style of its designer.
- Subjective coherence may protect an architecture from revision if not paired with explicit falsifiers.
- Some apparently nonverbal reasoning may depend on previously learned linguistic structure that is no longer consciously accessible.
- Spatial metaphors may obscure symbolic, probabilistic, or social mechanisms operating beneath the experience.
