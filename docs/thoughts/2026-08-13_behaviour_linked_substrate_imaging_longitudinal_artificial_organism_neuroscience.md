# Raw thought intake — Behaviour-linked substrate imaging and longitudinal artificial-organism neuroscience

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** Raw thought for later refinement, literature review, feasibility assessment, and experimental decomposition.

## Originating thought

> “I wonder about applying some brain scan like behavioural analysis. We could look at what activity in r e substrate is associated with what behaviour. Extending temporally and associative”

This thought treats REE increasingly at the whole-organism level rather than solely as a collection of individually tested mechanisms. The central proposal is to observe internal REE substrate activity simultaneously with behaviour, then investigate which distributed internal patterns are associated with particular behaviours, behavioural transitions, environmental events, internal states, and developmental changes.
