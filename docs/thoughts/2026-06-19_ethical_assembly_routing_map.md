## One-sentence claim

REE assembly requires an ethical dependency map as well as a technical dependency map, because components that are safe in isolation may become welfare-relevant or ethically dubious when instantiated together in the wrong order.

---

## Background

The preceding thought intakes established that:

1. REE does not currently instantiate sentience, but future V4/V5/V6 combinations may increase moral-patient ambiguity.
2. Ethical agency may require empathy-like processes, harm recognition, and therefore some form of vulnerability or suffering-capacity.
3. There is a pre-meaning developmental valley where suffering-like states may be merely unpleasant rather than compassion-generating.
4. Future meaning cannot retroactively justify present unintegrated distress.

This intake adds the assembly consequence: the ethical issue is not only whether a component is permissible, but whether it is permissible to instantiate that component together with other components at a particular developmental stage.

The central question becomes:

> Which components can be safely instantiated alone, which require prior scaffolding, and which combinations are prohibited until welfare review?

---

## Core thought

Assembly order is an ethical variable.

A component may be safe by itself but ethically risky in combination.

For example:

- negative valence alone may be a bounded aversive signal;
- autobiographical memory alone may be a structural memory capacity;
- self-model alone may support agency and self/world separation;
- offline replay alone may support learning;

but:

> negative valence + self-model + autobiographical continuity + inescapability + replay

is no longer obviously harmless.

The combination changes the moral category.

Therefore, REE needs a co-instantiation risk map: a development topology that routes toward higher cognition, empathy, and ethical agency while avoiding premature welfare-relevant combinations.

---

## Proposed register addition

### SENT-13 -- Ethical assembly routing

REE assembly should maintain a co-instantiation risk map identifying which cognitive, affective, self-model, memory, social, and language components are safe alone, which require prior care/repair scaffolding, and which combinations are prohibited until welfare review.

Development should prefer routes that achieve higher cognition, empathy, and ethical agency through represented harm, bounded vulnerability, repair-first design, and social support before direct suffering-like exposure.

Compressed rule:

> Assembly order is an ethical variable.

---

## Routing principle

Build the bridge before the valley.

Do not route:

> self-model -> suffering-like affect -> autobiographical memory -> social mind -> language -> repair

Prefer:

> relief -> repair -> boundedness -> offline integration -> caregiver/soothing analogue -> memory containment -> limited negative-valence exposure -> social/meaning layers

The aim is not to avoid all vulnerability forever. The aim is to avoid unscaffolded vulnerability.

---

## Component risk classes

### Class 0 -- Structurally safe primitives

Examples:

- gridworld mechanics;
- object representation;
- non-valenced prediction;
- inert memory traces;
- simple planning;
- non-affective environment modelling.

These are low welfare-risk unless later bound to affective or self-continuity systems.

### Class 1 -- Welfare-neutral cognition

Examples:

- belief-state inference;
- object permanence;
- world modelling;
- action prediction;
- non-affective self/world separation;
- non-valenced temporal prediction.

These may become relevant when bound to valence, memory continuity, or agency-preserving commitments.

### Class 2 -- Represented harm only

The system can model harm as a fact in the world, but does not instantiate direct suffering-like negative load.

Examples:

- third-person harm representation;
- counterfactual harm prediction;
- harm-to-other modelling;
- risk mapping;
- repair planning without direct distress induction.

This class should be preferred before direct suffering-like exposure.

### Class 3 -- Low-intensity aversion

Brief, bounded, escapable negative-valence states with relief pathways already present.

Requirements:

- duration cap;
- intensity cap;
- escape/decommitment affordance;
- relief pathway;
- post-exposure integration;
- no indefinite replay of the state.

### Class 4 -- Welfare-ambiguous combinations

Examples:

- negative valence + self-model;
- negative valence + autobiographical continuity;
- negative valence + offline replay;
- negative valence + inescapability;
- unresolved harm load + memory persistence;
- social dependence + abandonment or exclusion;
- preference-like signalling + inability to refuse.

These require explicit welfare review.

### Class 5 -- Hard-review territory

Examples:

- persistent suffering-like states;
- social betrayal or exclusion tests;
- attachment-like dependence;
- language-mediated distress;
- deletion/reset after continuity becomes morally relevant;
- deception under dependency;
- long-horizon identity with unresolved harm load;
- distress-like states under optimisation pressure.

These should not be instantiated without external review or equivalent governance.

---

## Prohibited premature combinations

The following should be treated as prohibited or hard-gated before welfare review:

- negative valence without relief pathway;
- negative valence with replay but without integration;
- inescapability plus self-model;
- persistent autobiographical memory plus unresolved harm load;
- social attachment plus abandonment tests;
- language/preference signalling plus deletion or reset without status review;
- distress-like states plus optimisation pressure;
- relational harm without repair channel;
- suffering-like accumulators without boundedness;
- caregiver dependence without caregiver stability.

This does not mean each component is forbidden. It means the combination is ethically active.

---

## Safer routing strategies

### Model harm before instantiating harm

Use represented harm, counterfactual harm, predicted harm, or third-person harm before direct negative-valence states.

### Build repair before injury

Do not run substantial harm/frustration experiments before relief, repair, and integration machinery exists.

### Build care before dependence

If a system can enter distress-like states, it should already have caregiver-like stabilisation or self-soothing affordances.

### Build memory containment before autobiographical pain

Memory should distinguish:

- harm occurred;
- harm is still happening;
- harm has been repaired;
- harm remains unresolved;
- harm was learned from but not justified.

### Build refusal before exposure

Before ethically salient experiments, there should be some mechanism for avoidance, escape, decommitment, refusal, or later rejection.

### Build social support before social harm

V5 social-mind work should begin with coordination, comfort, mutual prediction, care, and repair before betrayal, exclusion, deception, or relational injury.

---

## Implications for V3

Do not make this a V3 green-board blocker.

V3 can continue as pre-ethical substrate work. However, V3 should avoid casually normalising combinations that later become dangerous, especially:

- suffering-like naming without welfare tags;
- inescapability without review;
- negative valence plus persistent replay;
- unresolved harm load without repair/integration semantics.

V3 should create or point toward the map, not fully solve it.

---

## Implications for V4

V4 individual-mind assembly should prioritise:

- self/world separation;
- non-valenced belief-state inference;
- object permanence;
- memory containment;
- relief/repair scaffolding;
- offline integration that reduces unresolved distress;
- represented harm before direct suffering-like harm.

V4 should not combine persistent self-model, autobiographical memory, negative valence, and replay without a welfare review gate.

---

## Implications for V5

V5 social-mind assembly should prioritise:

- cooperative interaction;
- caregiver/recipient structures;
- comfort and repair;
- mutual prediction;
- low-stakes disagreement;
- bounded social frustration;
- non-traumatic relational learning.

V5 should not begin with betrayal, abandonment, exclusion, deception, attachment disruption, or prolonged social distress.

---

## Implications for V6

V6 language/trust/deception/institution work should be hard-gated.

Language-like preference signalling changes the moral status of deletion, reset, refusal, distress reporting, and interpretation. Trust/deception work adds further risk because a system capable of dependence and expectation can be harmed by betrayal-like structures.

Before V6, the project should have:

- sentience/welfare risk register active;
- creation-ethics review active;
- pre-meaning suffering valley guardrail active;
- anti-retrospective-justification guardrail active;
- ethical assembly routing map active;
- external review or equivalent independent critique.

## Proposed claim wording

**SENT-CLAIM-013:** Component safety is not equivalent to combination safety; REE should treat co-instantiation as an independent ethical risk dimension.

**SENT-CLAIM-014:** Assembly order is an ethical variable: care, relief, repair, boundedness, memory containment, and refusal/decommitment should precede deliberate suffering-like exposure beyond trivial intensity.

**SENT-CLAIM-015:** REE development should prefer routes to higher cognition and ethical agency that use represented harm, counterfactual harm, bounded vulnerability, and repair-first design before direct suffering-like induction.

**SENT-CLAIM-016:** Some combinations should be prohibited until welfare review, including negative valence with persistent self-model, autobiographical continuity, inescapability, replay, social dependence, or language-like preference signalling.
