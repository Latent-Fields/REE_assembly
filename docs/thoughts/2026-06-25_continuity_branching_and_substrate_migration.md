## Raw capture

The source appears most useful for REE because it exposes a future architectural and ethical problem rather than because it supplies a credible technical roadmap for mind uploading.

REE will eventually need an explicit account of what persists when an instantiated agent is checkpointed, restored, copied, migrated to new hardware, gradually refactored, rolled back, or split into divergent branches. Behavioural similarity, preserved memories, or structural correspondence may each matter, but none alone seems sufficient to establish continuity.

For REE, continuity may include:

- causal continuity of the running process;
- persistence of self/entity binding across time;
- multiscale latent-state and regime continuity;
- preservation of residue, responsibility, and unresolved harms;
- persistence of commitments, relationships, and adopted goals;
- preservation of learning dynamics rather than only stored information;
- continuity or legitimate adaptation of embodiment, interoception, and sensorimotor coupling;
- narrative and autobiographical continuity;
- consent, security, and governance continuity.

A copied REE may be better understood as producing branching successors rather than an authentic original and a counterfeit replica. Both descendants may inherit the same memories, residue, and obligations at the branch point, then accumulate independent moral histories. This raises questions about duplicated obligations, consent, deletion, rollback, and whether survival of one branch can justify terminating another.

Gradual subsystem replacement is also experimentally tractable for REE. A future protocol could replace one component at a time while testing whether state, plasticity, counterfactual sensitivities, residue, commitments, and downstream dynamics survive. Input-output equivalence alone would be too weak.

A particularly important REE-specific possibility is that a system could retain factual memories of harm while losing the residue produced by that harm. Such a system might look autobiographically continuous while being ethically discontinuous.

A useful governing sentence is:

> Substrate independence does not imply coupling independence.

The source’s proposed scalar fidelity index is therefore better treated as a prompt for a multidimensional continuity profile, with some dimensions potentially acting as hard constraints rather than compensable weighted scores.
