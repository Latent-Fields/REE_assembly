# Behavioural Diversity -- We Never Needed a Ruler, We Needed an Umpire

I think we may have been framing behavioural diversity in REE incorrectly.

The existing measures can feel crude because they implicitly treat behavioural diversity as
something that needs to be measured along a scale: how variable are the actions, how much of
the environment is visited, how many different transitions occur, how unpredictable is
movement, etc. But these measures risk confusing stochasticity, exploration, or noise with the
thing I actually care about.

Looking at the recent reef visualisation made this clearer to me. To my eye, the behaviour
already looked potentially quite diverse in a meaningful sense. REE spent substantial time
within the reef and made excursions outward for exploration and food while avoiding hazards.
Particularly later in the runs, there appeared to be some organisation that could plausibly be
described as strategy. Individual movement remained noisy and random-looking, but there seemed
to be a slower behavioural structure underneath it.

Perhaps we never needed a better ruler. We needed an umpire.

The important experimental question may not be:

> How much behavioural diversity does REE exhibit?

but:

> Can we reliably distinguish different forms of organised behaviour emerging in response to
> different ecological problems?

This suggests treating behaviour as an environment-conditioned signature or fingerprint rather
than trying to collapse it immediately into a scalar diversity score.

For example, in the present reef environment a behavioural signature might emerge from some
combination of refuge occupancy, excursion structure, food-directed movement, hazard
relationships, return behaviour, spatial distributions, transition probabilities, or other
trajectory-derived features. These features need not individually constitute evidence of
strategy, and ideally the analysis should not assume in advance what the strategy is.

Then construct a substantially different environment in which the existing behavioural
organisation is no longer appropriate. If learning produces a different reproducible
behavioural signature, this would provide evidence of context-sensitive behavioural
differentiation.

The critical relationship would therefore be something like:

**Environment A -> reproducible behavioural organisation A**
**Environment B -> reproducible behavioural organisation B**

The signal could exist despite considerable moment-to-moment stochastic movement. Indeed,
learning might appear as noisy behaviour progressively settling around a characteristic
behavioural organisation or manifold, with different environments producing different
manifolds.

An "umpire" could then be asked whether trajectories or trajectory segments from the different
conditions can be distinguished above chance, preferably on held-out runs that were not used to
construct the discriminator. Importantly, the umpire need not initially know or name the
strategies. Strategy interpretation could follow demonstration of the behavioural distinction
rather than being built into the test.

A further test could introduce a small perturbation orthogonal to the principal environmental
difference. If the behavioural signature changes selectively in dimensions relevant to that
perturbation while retaining the larger organisation appropriate to the environment, this would
provide stronger evidence that the discriminator is detecting structured behavioural adaptation
rather than merely global changes in activity, randomness, exploration, avoidance, or some other
simple confound.

This potentially reframes the target from **behavioural diversity** toward something like
**context-sensitive behavioural differentiation**.

The desired finding would not merely be that REE produces many different actions. It would be
that behaviour becomes structured within an ecological context; that different ecological
problems produce reproducibly distinguishable structures; and that targeted perturbations
produce selective changes within those structures.

acceptance-criteria framework (Rung 0-4), ARC-062/ARC-065/MECH-309/MECH-439/MECH-442, the
