## Proposed decision gates

### Gate A — representation preservation

Before adding a social layer, establish whether the current latent/world interface retains information necessary for action. Passing generic reconstruction while failing direction, controllability, or self/world probes should be treated as a failure for REE purposes.

### Gate B — episode adequacy

Before claiming sleep supports social learning, demonstrate that stored episodes include enough structure to distinguish an event from its causes and alternatives. A plain state-transition tuple is insufficient in deliberately confounded environments.

### Gate C — offline benefit

Only adopt a durable sleep-compression mechanism if it improves held-out causal attribution, intervention choice, transfer, or calibration—not merely training loss or replay reconstruction.

### Gate D — developmental necessity

Only introduce agent-intention inference once agents possess independently varying trajectories that cannot be represented adequately as moving obstacles or resources. This prevents an abstract “theory of mind” component being built ahead of the phenomena it must explain.

## Candidate measurements

- **Grounded retention:** probe accuracy for resource direction, available actions, controllability, self-caused change, and relevant other-caused change.
- **Compression:** memory footprint, predictive accuracy, transfer to a changed layout, and access to queryable abstract regularities.
- **Causal calibration:** probability assigned to the true cause, sensitivity to causal reversals, and discrimination of coincidence from intervention.
- **Ethically relevant behaviour:** avoids preventable harm to self/other, chooses repair after confirmed causal contribution, and abstains or seeks information when attribution uncertainty is high.

## Do not infer yet

- that sleep is required for all abstraction;
- that conscious access is a necessary property of good artificial control;
- that social-media causal-attribution results transfer directly to embodied agents;
- that an authority/gating mechanism is needed beyond existing precision, policy, or control-plane machinery.

These are useful hypotheses and evaluation prompts, not settled REE architecture.
