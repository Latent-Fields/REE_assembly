## 4. REE-specific hypothesis

REE should avoid reducing depression-like failure modes to a scalar low mood or low reward value. A more useful model may involve interacting axes:

1. **Future trajectory access** — whether viable future rollouts can activate.
2. **Goal-stream coupling** — whether goals can reach action-readiness.
3. **Residue and self-weighting** — whether past harm/failure becomes overgeneralised into identity.
4. **Decommitment from rumination** — whether recurrent negative loops can be interrupted.
5. **Energy/action threshold** — whether action costs are globally elevated.
6. **Social affordance access** — whether other-agent contact remains available as repair.
7. **Offline integration** — whether sleep/replay repairs or reinforces the depressive regime.

Possible computational primitive:

```text
depressive_regime_risk = f(future_access, goal_coupling, residue_load, rumination_loop_gain, action_threshold, social_affordance, offline_repair_quality)
```

The key idea is that depressive states may be network regimes: patterns of coupling and access that become self-maintaining.

## 5. Relevance to AI cognitive failure taxonomy

This may be relevant to `Latent-Fields/ai-cognitive-failure-taxonomy` because a depression-like AI failure mode would probably not mean sadness. It might mean:

- persistent low action initiation despite available goals

This should be handled carefully to avoid anthropomorphic overreach. The analogy is not mood; the analogy is a network-state failure in trajectory generation and action readiness.
