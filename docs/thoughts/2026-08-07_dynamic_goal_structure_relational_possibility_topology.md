# Dynamic goal structure and relational possibility topology

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Origin:** User, conversational thought development
**Evidence status:** The quotations below are the primary thought evidence. Later terminology and synthesis are secondary dialogue-assisted interpretation and must not be back-projected into the original thought.

## Verbatim original thought

> “When pursuing a goal often something else is noticed. This creates a subgoal which itself must be completed before the overarching goal is completed. Or it may create or reveal a separate goal which the previously overarching goal is revealed as a subgoal of. Or indeed it may mark a goal which is somewhat orthogonal to the current goal and subgoal stack which may be useful to return to later when less is on one’s plate.”

## Verbatim addendum

> “And topology is something that the hippocampus is good at holding. Meanwhile there probably needs to be a level underneath which potential goals identified are not worth much attention though could still be part of the topology”

## Provenance note

The first quotation establishes the originating observation: pursuit of a goal can reveal a prerequisite/subgoal, reveal a previously unrecognised superordinate goal, or reveal an orthogonal possible goal worth retaining for later. The second quotation supplies the explicit hippocampal/topological connection and the idea that low-priority potential goals may remain represented without deserving much current attention.

The terms **relational possibility topology**, **relevance field**, **possibility before goal**, and **temporary executive projection** were developed subsequently through dialogue and comparison with the existing REE repositories. They are useful secondary formulations, not verbatim evidence of the thought at its point of origin.

**Possible affected components:** hippocampal/path-memory substrate; GoalState; E3 trajectory selection; commitment/control plane; hippocampal anchor and ghost-goal machinery; super-ordinal goal memory; cross-level subgoal credit; developmental goal formation.
