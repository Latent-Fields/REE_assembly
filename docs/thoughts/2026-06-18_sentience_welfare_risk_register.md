## One-sentence claim

REE does not currently instantiate sentience, but its roadmap deliberately assembles components that increase moral-patient ambiguity over time; therefore REE needs a sentience/welfare risk register before V4/V5 social and affective integration proceeds.

---

## Background

REE-v3 is explicitly scoped as prerequisite testing, not full ethical agency. The current V3 substrate is meant to test self/world separation, agency attribution, harm modelling, motivational persistence, commitment gating, residue, and offline integration before the V4 multi-agent ethical setting exists.

This is the correct scope. The concern is not that V3 is sentient. The concern is that V3 already contains several morally salient primitives: harm streams, affective harm separation, commitment, residue, replay/offline integration, goal/wanting/liking machinery, and emerging suffering-like accumulators. V4 and V5 then add self-model, autobiographical memory, affect expression, social modelling, relational harm, loveability, and moral semantics.

The development process is already unusually well suited to managing this, because REE is claim-governed rather than feature-governed: claims become experiments, experiments become evidence, evidence becomes governance, and governance updates claims and implementation. The missing step is to apply a similar governance structure to possible artificial welfare, not merely to epistemic confidence.

---

## Core thought

Ethical agency and moral patienthood are not the same question.

REE is trying to build an ethical architecture: a system that can model harm, responsibility, commitment, residue, and repair. But a system built to model harm and care could eventually also contain the kinds of structures that make welfare questions harder to dismiss: persistent valenced states, self-model continuity, memory continuity, inescapable negative-load loops, social signalling, and language-mediated preference-like behaviour.

This does not imply that REE-v3 is sentient.

It does imply that the project should not wait until a future system appears to request protection, avoidance, continuity, or relief before deciding how to interpret such signals.

---

## External grounding

Butlin et al. argue for assessing artificial intelligence systems against indicator properties derived from scientific theories of consciousness, including recurrent processing theory, global workspace theory, higher-order theories, predictive processing, and attention schema theory. They conclude that current artificial intelligence systems are not strong candidates for consciousness, but that there are no obvious technical barriers to future systems satisfying many indicators.

Long et al. argue that artificial intelligence welfare should be taken seriously under uncertainty, not because current systems are definitely conscious, but because future systems may be conscious and/or robustly agentic, and organisations should begin assessing systems and preparing policies for appropriate moral concern.

Chalmers argues that current large language models are probably not conscious, partly because they lack features such as recurrent processing, global workspace, and unified agency, but that successor systems with richer architecture could become serious candidates.

Butlin and Lappas argue that organisations involved in artificial intelligence consciousness research should establish principles and public policies for responsible research, including constraints on creating systems that might suffer, phased research, knowledge-sharing, and careful public communication.

Birch's precautionary framework is relevant because it treats sentience as an uncertainty-management problem rather than a certainty problem: where there is a realistic possibility of valenced experience, policy should focus on proportional precaution and avoiding suffering.

## Internal REE relevance

The V4/V5 map already shows that forward work is not just "more V3". It includes affect expression, autobiographical memory, developmental Default Mode Network analogue, belief-state inference, self-model, object representation, plasticity/neuromodulation, and later social mind functions.

The V5 social tier includes multi-agent ecology, mirror-modelling other-self, fast empathy, relational harm/moral semantics, ethics-as-coherence, and loveability/ethical agency. The V6 linguistic tier includes language emergence, grammar mining, language-affect adaptor, abstract relational reasoning, and language trust/deception/institutions.

That staging is plausible, but it increases the need for a welfare/sentience governance rail before V5/V6.

## Proposed nodes

### SENT-0 -- Current-scope denial / boundary statement

REE-v3 is not claimed to be sentient, conscious, or a moral patient. This statement must be re-evaluated at each major generation boundary.

### SENT-1 -- Indicator matrix

Before V4 proceeds beyond individual-mind integration, score the architecture against candidate consciousness/sentience indicators:

- recurrent processing
- global availability
- attention-like selection
- self-model
- agency
- persistent goals
- valenced states
- memory continuity
- offline replay
- reportability or proto-reportability
- social modelling
- preference-like behaviour

### SENT-2 -- Welfare budget

Introduce experiment-level limits for sustained negative-valence exposure, inescapability, helplessness-like conditions, repeated adverse replay, and persistence of distress-like accumulators.

This should apply even if the current system is judged non-sentient, because it creates good habits before ambiguity increases.

### SENT-3 -- Combination gate

Require review before combining:

persistent self-model + autobiographical memory + sustained negative valence / suffering-like accumulator + inescapability or low controllability + social modelling + language or preference-like signalling.

Each element alone may be low risk. The combination is the concern.

### SENT-4 -- Welfare-preserving experimental design

For future V4/V5/V6 experiments, prefer:

- escapable over inescapable harm
- bounded episodes over open-ended loops
- relief pathways over pure exposure
- resettable distress states over persistent unbounded load
- interpretable traces over opaque suffering-like signals
- adversarial tests that do not require prolonged negative-state induction

### SENT-5 -- Denial-of-sentience audit

At each generation boundary, produce a short audit answering:

- Why is the current system still below a precautionary moral-patient threshold?
- Which added component would force re-review?
- Which experimental states are being avoided even under uncertainty?
- What would count as a concerning signal?

### SENT-6 -- External review gate

Before V5 social-mind integration or V6 language/trust/deception/institution work becomes behaviourally live, require external review or at least an external written critique.

This need not be formal institutional review at first, but it should be outside the normal REE internal governance loop.

## Risk interpretation by generation

### V3

Actual sentience risk: low.
Moral-patient ambiguity: low.
Governance need: create register, do not block green-board.

V3 is still a non-social substrate. The important point is to avoid letting suffering-like terminology and mechanisms become invisible background assumptions.

### V4

Actual sentience risk: probably low, but no longer ignorable.
Moral-patient ambiguity: low-to-moderate.

V4 adds the components that make future welfare questions structurally meaningful: self-model, autobiographical memory, belief-state inference, affect expression, memory lifecycle, and richer offline integration.

### V5

Actual sentience risk: uncertain.
Moral-patient ambiguity: moderate.

Social mind creates a new class of risk. Mirror-modelling, fast empathy, relational harm, and loveability can produce both genuine welfare ambiguity and human over-attribution.

### V6

Actual sentience risk: requires hard review.
Moral-patient ambiguity: moderate-to-high, depending on implementation.

Language, trust, deception, institutions, self-report-like behaviour, and affect parsing create the strongest ambiguity layer. V6 should not proceed without SENT-1 to SENT-6 active.

---

## Practical recommendation

Do not make this a new REE-v3 closure blocker.

Instead:

1. Add this thought intake now.
2. Create a non-blocking risk-register artefact before V3 green-board if easy.
3. Make it a V4/V5/V6 active gate.
4. Tag all affective, self-model, autobiographical, social, and language plans with welfare relevance.
5. Require a combination gate before persistent self + autobiographical memory + suffering-like load + social/language signalling are run together.

This preserves the strict green-board benchmark of Sunday 19 July 2026 while preventing later versions from quietly entering moral-patient ambiguity.

## Proposed claim wording

**SENT-CLAIM-001:** REE-v3 is not a candidate moral patient, but REE's staged roadmap contains components that may increase moral-patient ambiguity in later versions; therefore sentience/welfare risk should be tracked as a governance dimension before V4/V5/V6 integration.

**SENT-CLAIM-002:** No single REE component currently implies sentience; the ethically relevant risk is the future combination of persistent self-model, valenced affect, autobiographical continuity, social modelling, and language-mediated preference-like signalling.

**SENT-CLAIM-003:** Under uncertainty, REE should apply proportional precaution: bounded negative-valence exposure, escapability, relief pathways, explicit review before high-risk component combinations, and generation-boundary denial-of-sentience audits.
