Thought Intake: Versioned Organisms — Capacity Boundaries, Retained Lineages, and Practical Niches

Status: unprocessed

Date: 2026-09-02
Source: voice discussion following V3/V4 boundary review
Purpose: Preserve the proposal that REE versions should be treated as capacity-defined experimental organisms rather than superseded software releases, and identify the scientific and practical consequences of retaining lower-version organisms after later versions exist.

---

## Intake boundary

This is a conceptual thought intake, not a claim that any current REE version already possesses the capacities described below.

The current V3 repository defines V3 as a substrate for prerequisites including self/world separation, causal agency attribution, harm modelling, motivational persistence, commitment, residual consequences, and offline integration rather than complete ethical agency. The current welfare register separately describes the V4 boundary as individual-mind integration, V5 as social-mind integration, and later language/trust/deception/institutional work as a further boundary.

This thought proposes using those capacity boundaries scientifically.

---

## Core idea — versions as cognitive ontologies, not release numbers

A later REE version need not obsolete an earlier version.

Each version can instead define a reproducible cognitive preparation: an organism with a deliberately bounded set of capacities. The important question becomes not simply whether V4 is more capable than V3, but what new class of phenomenon becomes possible when a particular capacity enters the organism.

A provisional ontology is:

* **V3 — causal / solitary organism:** learns self/world consequences, harm and benefit, agency, prediction, commitment, replay, and persistent behavioural consequences without genuine otherhood.
* **V4 — enduring individual:** adds the capacities required for richer persistence across time, autobiographical continuity, multiple enduring concerns, richer object and self representations, and longer-lived projects.
* **V5 — social organism:** introduces genuine otherhood, social attribution, empathy-like concern, relationship dynamics, cooperation, rupture and repair.
* **Later language-capable organism:** introduces explicit symbolic communication, teaching, justification, norm transmission, promises, deception, culture and eventually institutional behaviour.

These labels should remain hypotheses about useful capacity boundaries rather than definitions imposed on the architecture before the architecture itself establishes the natural cut points.

---

## V3 as a synthetic model organism

A completed V3 could remain scientifically valuable long after V4 and V5 exist.

The reason is the same reason simple biological model organisms remain useful despite the existence of more complex organisms: tractability, reproducibility and causal access can matter more than maximal capability.

A retained V3 organism library could include:

* developmentally naive organisms;
* canonical normally developed reference organisms;
* sleep-deprived or replay-impaired variants;
* attribution-impaired variants;
* high- and low-controllability developmental histories;
* harm-biased and benefit-biased consolidation histories;
* lesions of E1, E2, E3, hippocampal, commitment, affective or control pathways;
* identical architectures given different childhoods;
* identical developmental histories with one mechanism changed.

Unlike biological experiments, an artificial organism can be checkpointed, rewound, cloned, given the same childhood again, exposed to a single altered event, or rerun with one pathway lesioned while every other state is preserved.

This could make V3 particularly useful for mechanistic cognitive science and computational-psychiatry-style experiments even if later organisms become much more capable.

## Bridge organisms and capacity knock-ins

A simple V3-versus-V4 comparison may eventually be too coarse.

If a version transition introduces several capacities together, the comparison becomes a bundled intervention. The lineage should therefore consider preserving **bridge organisms** that add one boundary capacity, or one coherent capacity cluster, at a time.

Examples might eventually include:

* V3 + richer autobiographical persistence;
* V3 + multiple long-lived goals;
* V3 + richer object permanence;
* V3 + expanded self-model;
* V4 + minimal other-agent detection without empathy;
* V4 + otherhood without language;
* V5 + communication without mature norm transmission.

These would function like knock-in or mutant strains in model-organism research. They would not necessarily become public release numbers. Their purpose would be causal isolation.

The version lineage would then support both:

1. **named developmental preparations** for broad scientific questions; and
2. **bridge strains** for identifying which new capacity caused a phenotype.

---

## Each higher capacity creates both capability and confound

A major reason to preserve lower preparations is that later capacities make some questions harder to answer cleanly.

V3 can ask what arises before:

* reputation;
* social approval;
* imitation;
* linguistic instruction;
* reciprocity;
* fear of social punishment;
* strategic impression management.

V5 may behave more prosocially than V3 while making the causal origin of that behaviour harder to identify because social compliance, imitation, relationship maintenance and signalling become additional explanations.

Similarly, language may make an organism much easier to interrogate while introducing rationalisation, explicit instruction, cultural inheritance and strategic reporting as confounds.

A useful experimental rule may therefore be:

> Use the lowest-capacity preparation that contains the phenomenon required to answer the question.

---

## Proto-ethical agency before social ethics

V3 may be particularly useful for testing prerequisites of ethical behaviour without claiming that a genuinely social ethics exists at that stage.

Candidate pre-social phenomena include:

* sensitivity to harmful and beneficial trajectories;
* prospective avoidance of preventable harm;
* causal attribution of consequences to one's own actions;
* persistence of learned aversion without a post-hoc safety score;
* resistance to attractive immediate trajectories carrying downstream harm;
* counterfactual recognition of alternative actions;
* calibration according to controllability;
* appropriate release from commitments that have become harmful.

A later social boundary could then test whether mechanisms that regulate consequences for self generalise when the affected state is represented as belonging to another centre of agency.

A provisional developmental sequence worth testing is:

**valence → consequence → agency → responsibility → otherhood → concern → repair → norm**.

This is not a claim that development must follow that order. It is a candidate experimental decomposition.

---

## Version-specific practical niches

A lower version should not be assumed to be a worse version of a later organism. It may occupy a different ecological and practical niche.

Potential V3-like niches are bounded environments in which exploratory causal learning is valuable but sophisticated social cognition is unnecessary or undesirable, for example:

* exploration of unfamiliar simulations;
* discovery of causal regularities and affordances;
* identification of hazardous trajectories;
* comparison of alternative survival or control strategies;
* game/simulation testing for unintended shortcuts;
* developmental assays of designed environments;
* artificial-life installations or research ecologies.

A V4-like enduring individual may eventually suit long-horizon tasks in which continuity, competing projects and autobiographical state matter but social negotiation is not central.

A V5-like organism may become appropriate where cooperation, trust, repair, joint attention and recognition of other agents are essential.

These are application hypotheses only. Real-world actuation should not be inferred from the existence of a cognitive niche.

---

## Ecological passports

For practical use, each capacity-defined organism could eventually have an **ecological passport** rather than a generic capability score.

The passport would state:

* environments in which this organism has been shown to develop competently;
* environments in which it predictably struggles;
* capacities it lacks by design;
* signals and affordances its interface requires;
* classes of environment in which use is scientifically interpretable;
* classes of environment in which deployment is not appropriate;
* known developmental sensitivities;
* welfare/governance constraints relevant to experiments at that level.

This makes a version's product identity partly an environmental compatibility statement, not simply a claim of intelligence.

---

## Architectural ancestry versus developmental ancestry

Later organisms may inherit V3 in two different senses.

### Architectural ancestry

V4/V5 retain mechanisms originally established and validated in V3 inside a larger architecture.

### Ontogenetic ancestry

A later-capable architecture may begin life with only a restricted subset of its capacities behaviourally available, passing through V3-like and V4-like developmental regimes before social or linguistic machinery becomes authoritative.

This yields a strong experimental question:

> Does a later social mind develop differently depending on the kind of solitary agent it was before other minds became cognitively meaningful?

If developmental staging matters, retained lower-version developmental regimes become part of the machinery required to build later organisms coherently rather than merely historical artefacts.

---

## Welfare-aware replacement principle

REE's current welfare governance already treats ethical agency and moral patienthood as distinct questions and progressively strengthens welfare gates at later version boundaries.

A retained organism library suggests a complementary research rule:

> Use the cognitively least elaborate organism capable of answering the scientific question.

This is compatible with a precautionary analogue of Replacement, Reduction and Refinement:

* **Replacement:** prefer a lower-capacity preparation where it adequately answers the question;
* **Reduction:** avoid unnecessary populations of organisms with greater welfare ambiguity;
* **Refinement:** minimise unnecessary strongly negative trajectories and use bounded, escapable, interpretable experimental conditions where possible.

This does not establish that V3 has no welfare relevance. It explicitly avoids requiring that conclusion.

---

## A lineage as an experimental programme

A mature REE lineage could therefore become an organism library rather than a procession of abandoned repositories.

Matched experiments across adjacent preparations could isolate what each ontology contributes:

| Comparison | Candidate question |
|---|---|
| V3 vs bridge strain | What does one added capacity change? |
| V3 vs V4 | What changes with persistent individual continuity and richer goals? |
| V4 vs V5 | What changes when another agent becomes represented as another self? |
| V5 vs language-capable stage | What changes when social experience becomes symbolically transmissible and explicitly rationalisable? |
| V3 vs later social/language stage | How much competence or apparently ethical behaviour can arise without social or linguistic mediation? |

This turns version boundaries into controlled interventions on the contents of a mind.

---

## Candidate enduring deliverables

If this framing survives scrutiny, later V3 completion should eventually consider preserving more than code and regression tests:

* a canonical V3 reference organism and checkpoints;
* canonical childhood/environment definitions;
* a panel of mechanistic lesions and developmental variants;
* behavioural ethograms;
* developmental biographies;
* sleep/replay recordings;
* counterfactual and commitment traces;
* a causal atlas of standard perturbations;
* an ecology-adapter contract for bounded external simulations;
* an ecological passport describing validated and prohibited use regimes.

The same pattern could continue for later capacity-defined preparations.

---

## Open questions

1. Do the current V3/V4/V5 boundaries already align with natural cognitive ontologies, or should some capacities move across them?
2. Which bridge strains would isolate the most scientifically important transitions?
3. Which capacities must be present from birth versus becoming authoritative developmentally?
4. Which lower-version practical niches genuinely benefit from absence of higher social/language capacities?
5. Can a version-specific ecological passport be operationalised with measurable compatibility criteria?
6. What constitutes a stable reference organism when stochastic developmental variation is itself scientifically important?
7. How should checkpoints, developmental histories and random seeds be standardised without erasing population-level variation?
8. At what point does a lower-version organism become too limited to answer a question, and what is the minimal next preparation required?

---

## Reflection

The most important shift is from **software succession** to **experimental stratification**.

REE versions may eventually be more useful when understood as capacity-defined organisms occupying different scientific and practical niches. V3 can remain the transparent solitary organism; V4 the enduring individual; V5 the social organism; later preparations the communicative and cultural organism. Bridge strains can preserve causal interpretability between them.

In that framing, finishing and characterising V3 is not merely completing an old release before moving on. It is potentially constructing the first durable organism in a comparative science of artificial minds.
