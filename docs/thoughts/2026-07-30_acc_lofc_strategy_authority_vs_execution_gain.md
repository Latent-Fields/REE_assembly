## Summary
A recent Nature Communications paper demonstrates a causal dissociation between ACC-dependent maintenance of goal-directed behavioural control and LOFC→central striatal control of habitual execution. This aligns strongly with the existing SD-032 cingulate integration substrate, particularly SD-032b and MECH-260, while suggesting a further decomposition between strategy authority and execution gain.

## Existing REE correspondence
- Strengthens SD-032 / SD-032b.
- Supports MECH-258 and MECH-260 as components of a broader action–outcome history integration substrate.
- Reinforces the architectural separation between dACC integration and downstream selection.

## Architectural extension
Separate:
1. Strategy authority (does accumulated action–outcome evidence retain control?)
2. Policy persistence (does the behavioural mapping continue?)
3. Execution gain (how strongly is the policy expressed?)

The paper provides causal evidence that strategy authority and execution gain are dissociable.

## Implication
Reframe MECH-260 as one manifestation of a broader substrate maintaining the authority of temporally extended action–outcome information over behaviour, rather than defining the substrate solely by anti-recency or anti-monostrategy effects.

## Validation questions
- Does weakening SD-032b reduce outcome sensitivity while preserving execution?
- Should REE explicitly model execution gain independently of policy authority?
- Can independent lesions of authority and execution produce the behavioural dissociations observed experimentally?
