# Dynamic coordination, accessible repertoire, and monostrategy

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** raw synthesis thought / assay-generative note; claim-ingested 2026-09-25 (see top marker)  
**Scope:** V3 monostrategy, V3/V4 phase-substrate boundary, dynamic routing, authority-field hypothesis, anaesthesia convergence, later social/ethical access  
**Related internal material:** `docs/thoughts/2026-09-04_authority_field_analog_cognition_ephaptic_coupling.md`, `docs/thoughts/2026-09-10_dynamic_routing_as_experimental_information_governance.md`, `docs/thoughts/2026-09-10_phase_as_address_phase_conditioned_communication_subspaces.md`, `docs/thoughts/2026-09-19_dynamic_consensus_post_translation_integration.md`, `docs/architecture/dynamic_consensus_integration.md`, `docs/architecture/monostrategy_developmental_analysis.md`, `docs/architecture/v3_v4_phase_substrate_boundary.md`

## Core proposition

REE may require more than multiple representations, goals, memories, and policies. It may require an **endogenous mechanism that continually reconstitutes its own effective causal topology**: which representations can influence which consumers, at what moment, with what strength, and for how long.

The biological analogy should be held at the right level.

The claim is **not**:

> ephaptic coupling is the brain's master coordinator and REE must reproduce it.

The more defensible claim is:

> awake cognition appears to depend on a dynamically reconfigurable coordination regime spanning travelling waves, phase/cross-frequency structure, thalamocortical loops, neuromodulatory gain, metastable state transitions, and possibly extracellular-field effects. Anaesthesia repeatedly disrupts this regime without simply erasing all local content.

If REE shows a corresponding failure in which alternative strategies or representations remain present but cannot acquire causal authority, then a dynamic-coordination substrate may become functionally necessary.

Ephaptic coupling would then be one candidate biological implementation principle among several, not the premise of the computational claim.

## Why anaesthesia matters to this question

General anaesthetics act through substantially different molecular mechanisms yet converge on profound loss of conscious responsiveness. Recent work increasingly points to convergence at the level of **population dynamics and coordination** rather than complete suppression of neural activity.

### Convergent dynamical destabilisation across anaesthetic classes

Eisen et al. (2026) compared propofol, ketamine, and dexmedetomidine using intracortical recordings and a population-dynamics stability analysis. Despite their different pharmacology, all three anaesthetics similarly reduced cortical dynamical stability. This was accompanied by slower recovery from sensory perturbations and longer stimulus-induced autocorrelation, and the destabilisation tracked moment-to-moment changes in consciousness.

- Eisen AJ et al. *Similar destabilization of neural dynamics under different general anesthetics*. **Cell Reports** 45(3):117048 (2026).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117048  
  PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41850281/

This is especially relevant to REE because it suggests a common failure may be **how neural states evolve and recover**, rather than a single lost content channel.

### Loss of metastability and altered integration/segregation

Jang et al. (2024) found that propofol-induced unresponsiveness shifted whole-brain functional organisation toward segregation and reduced metastability, with related changes also observed in natural sleep. Metastability here refers to the ability of the system to move among transient non-equilibrium configurations.

- Jang H et al. *Measuring the dynamic balance of integration and segregation underlying consciousness, anesthesia, and sleep in humans*. **Nature Communications** 15:9164 (2024).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53299-x

The REE translation is not that consciousness equals metastability. The narrower inference is that a functioning cognitive system may need access to a **rich and revisitable state repertoire**, and that collapse of this repertoire can coexist with preserved network structure.

### Structure increasingly dominates under anaesthesia

Jarraya et al. (2024) reported that distributed activity under propofol, sevoflurane, and ketamine became increasingly constrained by structural connectivity, alongside collapse of multiple dimensions of hierarchical cortical organisation. Central thalamic stimulation restored responsiveness and altered the distributed functional pattern.

- Jarraya B et al. *Local orchestration of distributed functional patterns supporting loss and restoration of consciousness in the primate brain*. **Nature Communications** 15:2171 (2024).  
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46382-w

A useful abstraction for REE is:

> the physical/architectural graph can remain largely intact while the set of dynamically accessible functional configurations narrows.

That is close to the distinction REE already makes between **encoded**, **decodable**, **natively accessible**, and **causally used** information.

## Travelling waves and dynamic communication

Bhattacharya et al. (2022) found that propofol loss of consciousness reorganised cortical travelling waves: slow-delta waves became stronger and more organised, while higher-frequency travelling waves associated with cognition weakened and lost structure.

- Bhattacharya S et al. *Propofol Anesthesia Alters Cortical Traveling Waves*. **Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience** 34(7):1274-1286 (2022).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1162/jocn_a_01856

The relevant lesson is not that slow waves are intrinsically "unconscious". It is that anaesthesia can reorganise the **spatiotemporal pattern of propagation** through which cortical regions influence one another.

More directly computational work supports the idea that wave-like dynamics can implement flexible communication rather than merely correlate with it. A distributed-and-dynamical communication model shows that interacting wave patterns can create, dissolve, and reconfigure communication subspaces between cortical areas, including attention-like switching.

- *Distributed and dynamical communication: a mechanism for flexible cortico-cortical interactions and its functional roles in visual attention*. **Communications Biology** (2024).  
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06228-z

A separate connectome-based model shows structural connectivity gradients can direct cortical travelling waves and frequency gradients.

- *Human connectome topology directs cortical traveling waves and shapes frequency gradients*. **Nature Communications** (2024).  
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-47860-x

These results strengthen the already-registered REE possibility that time-varying coordination can alter **who can talk to whom** without requiring synaptic rewiring.

## Thalamocortical coordination as a distributed access system

Toker et al. (2024) identified a reproducible cross-frequency pattern in which low-frequency activity in cortex or thalamus was encoded in high-frequency activity in the other structure. This bidirectional information-transfer pattern was diminished under propofol unconsciousness and seizures, while conventional nonspectral transfer measures did not show the same state dependence.

- Toker D et al. *Criticality supports cross-frequency cortical-thalamic information transfer during conscious states*. **eLife** 13:e86547 (2024).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.86547

Huang et al. (2024) further showed that propofol preferentially disrupted matrix-rich thalamocortical functional architecture and shifted the system toward a transmodal-deficient geometry. Matrix thalamic cells have diffuse projections and have been proposed to support temporal coordination, binding, and integration.

- Huang Z, Mashour GA, Hudetz AG. *Propofol disrupts the functional core-matrix architecture of the thalamus in humans*. **Nature Communications** 15:7496 (2024).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51837-1

For REE, the architectural lesson is that some biological coordination systems may not carry the primary content themselves. They may regulate **which distributed content becomes jointly effective**.

## Ephaptic coupling: plausible contributor, not settled requirement

There is credible evidence that endogenous electric fields can influence neural activity.

Fröhlich & McCormick (2010) showed that weak electric fields at amplitudes within the range of endogenous cortical fields can entrain and enhance neocortical network activity.

- Fröhlich F, McCormick DA. *Endogenous Electric Fields May Guide Neocortical Network Activity*. **Neuron** 67(1):129-143 (2010).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2010.06.005

Pinotsis & Miller (2026) modelled prefrontal recordings from a spatial delayed-response task and reported that inferred field-to-neuron influences were stronger than neuron-to-field influences and tracked trial-by-trial oscillatory-power variability.

- Pinotsis DA, Miller EK. *Ephaptic coupling can explain variability in neural activity*. **Cerebral Cortex** 36(6):bhag098 (2026).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhag098

This supports the plausibility of reciprocal neuron-field causation, but it does **not** establish ephaptic coupling as the principal mechanism of global cognitive coordination.

REE should therefore preserve a three-level distinction:

1. **Computational requirement:** dynamic reconfiguration of causal access may be needed.
2. **Dynamical implementation principle:** phase, waves, metastability, recurrent routing, or other temporal structure may implement it.
3. **Biological contributor:** thalamocortical loops, neuromodulation, extracellular/ephaptic fields, astrocytic regulation, and other substrates may jointly instantiate the principle.

The computational claim should survive even if ephaptic coupling proves minor.


## The missing bridge: mutual legibility -> dynamic coordination -> consensus -> strategy

A newly relevant result makes the connection between REE's mutual-legibility programme and the present monostrategy/coordination question much tighter.

Javadzadeh, Schimel and colleagues simultaneously recorded mouse primary visual cortex (V1) and lateromedial visual cortex (LM), combined focal optogenetic perturbation with biologically constrained latent circuit models, and found that reciprocal excitatory coupling generated an approximate line attractor across the joint system. Congruent cross-area states occupied slower modes; incongruent states decayed faster. Fine-grained agreement depended on selective like-to-like long-range connectivity.

- Javadzadeh M, Schimel M, Hofer SB, Ahmadian Y, Hennequin G. *Reciprocal connections dynamically build consensus between neocortical areas*. **Nature Neuroscience** (2026).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02437-3

REE had already separately ingested this result into the mutual-legibility programme as `docs/thoughts/2026-09-19_dynamic_consensus_post_translation_integration.md` and `docs/architecture/dynamic_consensus_integration.md`, including the candidate signature:

```
tau_agree > tau_disagree
```

with the timescale separation expected to degrade under reciprocal-coupling ablation or correspondence scramble.

The present synthesis reveals that this is not a separate curiosity. It provides a plausible **bridge** between four REE problems that had been treated partly independently:

1. **Mutual legibility:** can specialised subsystem A expose dimensions that specialised subsystem B can use?
2. **Dynamic coordination / routing:** when is A permitted to influence B, through which dimensions, and with what gain?
3. **Consensus dynamics:** once influence is reciprocal, which combinations of A and B persist, decay, or recruit wider systems?
4. **Monostrategy / escape:** does the organism retain the ability to leave a currently dominant joint state when context or evidence changes?

The resulting chain is:

```
local representation
    ->
mutual legibility / communication subspace
    ->
state-dependent routing / causal access
    ->
selective reciprocal coupling
    ->
agreement-dependent persistence or disagreement-driven revision
    ->
temporary cross-system coalition
    ->
evaluation / commitment / action
```

This changes the role of the coordination layer.

It may not merely determine **who can speak**. It may also determine **which mutually accessible interpretations can remain jointly active long enough to acquire behavioural authority**.

That is a direct route from mutual legibility to strategy expression.

### Translation is necessary but not sufficient

An alternative strategy may be perfectly represented in hippocampal, E1, E2, goal, or proposer state and even be translatable into the receiving subsystem's representational dialect. Yet it can still lose behaviourally if the recurrent coupling among the organism's systems repeatedly stabilises another coalition.

Therefore:

> **decodable -> legible -> routed -> reciprocally supportable -> causally authoritative**

should be treated as distinct stages.

A successful bridge can fail at the next stage if its output enters a coupled system whose attractor geometry continuously pulls activity back toward the old interpretation.

### Coordination has at least three jobs

The combined literature suggests a useful functional decomposition:

**Routing**  
Determine which systems/dimensions can currently influence one another.

**Reconciliation**  
Allow mutually compatible partial representations to stabilise one another without requiring representational identity.

**Escape**  
Prevent successful reconciliation from becoming irreversible false consensus when contradictory evidence, changed goals, or altered ecology require another coalition.

A system with routing but no reconciliation may remain fragmented.

A system with reconciliation but inadequate escape may become coherent but rigid.

A system with escape but poor routing may generate noise rather than flexible cognition.

The target is therefore not maximum consensus. It is **defeasible, context-sensitive consensus**.

### Dynamic consensus can solve or cause monostrategy

Healthy reciprocal coupling could help an organism assemble a coherent action from distributed partial information.

But the same mechanism can create a new failure mode:

```
E1 expects trajectory X
    ->
hippocampus retrieves X-consistent episodes
    ->
E2 preferentially elaborates X-consistent futures
    ->
goal / affect / precision signals reinforce X
    ->
E3 commits X
    ->
experience further strengthens X-consistent coupling
```

Nothing in that loop requires the alternative trajectory to be absent or unreadable.

The pathology is that the coupled system continually recreates the same internally self-supporting state.

This is **consensus collapse**: coherence has become attractor capture.

The anti-monostrategy question is therefore not merely "how many strategies are represented?" but also:

> **How many distinct, evidence-responsive cross-system coalitions can become stable enough to control behaviour, and can the organism destabilise one when its evidential support disappears?**


## Reframing monostrategy

REE's current monostrategy failures already have concrete known causes, including:

- collapsed or low-diversity candidate generation;
- near-uniform E3 scoring on cold substrate;
- developmental overcommitment;
- replay bias;
- flat or impoverished residue/goal geography;
- insufficiently mature goal-directed control;
- possible expected-return/mode-collapse pressures.

These should be fixed before invoking a new coordination substrate.

However, there is a **second possible form of monostrategy** that current analyses should explicitly distinguish:

### Type A — repertoire absence

Alternative strategies were never learned, represented, or proposed.

Predictions:

- low proposer diversity;
- poor decodability of alternatives;
- no latent competent alternative to recover;
- dynamic routing cannot rescue behaviour because there is nothing useful to route.

### Type B — repertoire access collapse

Alternative strategies or trajectory-relevant information exist internally but repeatedly fail to acquire behavioural authority.

Predictions:

- alternative strategies are encoded/decodable;
- appropriate alternatives may appear in hippocampal/proposer state;
- downstream consumers repeatedly select the same effective causal route;
- changing contextual access/routing can reveal behaviour that was already latent;
- static policy retraining is not required for the switch.

Type B is one REE analogue worth testing against the dynamic-coordination hypothesis.


### Type C — consensus collapse / over-stabilisation

Alternative strategies are represented and can reach the relevant systems, but reciprocal dynamics repeatedly stabilise the same joint interpretation or trajectory.

Predictions:

- alternatives are encoded and natively/bridgeably accessible;
- perturbations can transiently move individual subsystems toward an alternative;
- the coupled system nevertheless relaxes back toward one dominant cross-system coalition;
- congruent states around that coalition have excessively long dwell times;
- disagreement is extinguished even when it carries higher-quality evidence;
- scrambling or weakening selective reciprocal coupling changes the lock-in without erasing local competence;
- a healthy intervention must restore context-sensitive switching, not merely flatten every attractor.

This is distinct from Type B.

Type B asks whether an alternative can **enter** the relevant causal pathway.

Type C asks what happens **after it enters**: does the distributed system permit a new mutually supportable coalition to form, or does existing consensus topology pull everything back to the old one?

A strong Type-C assay therefore needs both the mutual-legibility ladder and a dynamical measurement such as agreement/disagreement relaxation, dwell-time geometry, or perturbation recovery.

The key failure is:

> **the organism can hear the alternative, but cannot reorganise itself around it.**


The key idea is therefore broader:

> A monostrategy organism may lack strategies, lack access to strategies, **or possess alternatives that cannot become a stable distributed consensus.**

## A missing architectural layer

REE is often described as:

```
representation
    ->
prediction
    ->
evaluation
    ->
commitment
    ->
action
```

The present synthesis suggests an intervening layer:

```
representation / memory / goals / counterfactuals
    ->
mutual legibility / communication subspaces
    ->
dynamic coordination / causal accessibility
    ->
selective reciprocal reconciliation
    ->
defeasible cross-system coalition
    ->
evaluation
    ->
commitment
    ->
action
```

The coordination layer is not another source of semantic content. Its first role is to determine which already-existing content can currently alter another subsystem. Once systems are mutually engaged, reciprocal dynamics may provide a second operation: stabilising compatible interpretations relative to incompatible ones. A third operation—escape—must preserve the ability to dissolve that consensus when new evidence requires reorganisation.

This directly links several REE problems:

- representation present but downstream reader weak;
- hippocampal content not behaviourally used;
- goal representations losing authority;
- monostrategy;
- attention and distraction recovery;
- switching after contingency reversal;
- imagination/perception separation;
- self/other information becoming action-relevant;
- later ethical concern: another's state must not merely be represented, but able to enter the organism's causally effective coalition.

## Relationship to the authority field

This thought strengthens, but does not prove, the existing authority-field hypothesis.

The authority field can be interpreted as the moment-to-moment state of **effective causal topology** over REE's representational systems.

It may control:

- effective distance between representations;
- temporary permeability of routes;
- direction of influence;
- gain/precision;
- persistence;
- competition;
- recruitment into a wider coalition;
- decay and release.

The current literature suggests such dynamic topology is biologically plausible and that several components of it are disrupted under anaesthesia.

What remains unproven is whether REE needs it.

That is now experimentally approachable.

## A decisive V3/V4 boundary experiment

The existing V3/V4 architectural commitment says that phase/ephaptic machinery should be promoted only when V3 exhibits a failure that the synaptic approximation cannot represent.

The following assay could operationalise that criterion.

### Prerequisite

Run only after the known Type-A monostrategy causes have been repaired enough that:

- candidate/proposer diversity is non-degenerate;
- at least two behaviourally useful strategies can be demonstrated or decoded;
- E3 score range is non-flat;
- reversal ecology creates a genuine need to switch strategy;
- baseline competence is stable enough that a routing intervention can be interpreted.

Without these prerequisites, a coordination test is uninterpretable.

### Experimental arms

Use the same content representations, learned weights, candidate set, and aggregate channel-access budget.

**A. Static routing baseline**  
Current/standard fixed access topology.

**B. Matched stochastic routing**  
Same average access and switching frequency as the dynamic arm, but route changes are random and state-independent.

**C. Open-loop periodic/phase routing**  
A deterministic oscillatory schedule independent of organism state.

**D. Endogenous state-dependent dynamic routing**  
Routing state depends on conflict, uncertainty, goal competition, memory relevance, novelty, prediction error, or other existing REE signals.

**E. Closed-loop field-like routing (only if D is insufficient)**  
Activity influences the coordination state and the coordination state feeds back onto access, creating a reciprocal activity -> field -> activity loop.

The experiment should stop at D if D explains the effect. Literal wave/ephaptic machinery should not be added for biological resemblance alone.

### Primary outcomes

Measure:

- number of viable strategies expressed;
- transition rate among viable strategies;
- reversal-adaptation latency;
- probability of selecting the newly appropriate strategy;
- preservation of baseline competence;
- action/proposal entropy;
- hippocampal alternative-trajectory access;
- fraction of decodable alternatives that become causally used;
- dwell-time distribution in strategy/authority states;
- recovery after distractor or perturbation;
- relapse into the previous attractor;
- ecological reward/viability only as a secondary outcome.

### Causal-access outcome

The strongest diagnostic is not "more diverse behaviour".

It is:

> the same represented candidate becomes behaviourally effective or ineffective depending on the endogenous coordination state, with no change in the candidate's semantic content.

That would be direct evidence for dynamic causal privilege.

### Negative controls

Include:

- phase/routing shuffle;
- matched total gate-open time;
- matched noise amplitude;
- matched parameter count;
- mismatched state-dependent routing;
- routing driven by future/privileged information prohibited;
- content ablation to confirm routing cannot create competence ex nihilo;
- simple entropy/anti-recency control to test whether ordinary diversity pressure is sufficient.

### Discriminating result

A result that would support promotion of dynamic coordination toward V3 would look like:

1. useful alternative strategies are demonstrably present;
2. static routing fails to express them appropriately;
3. stochastic or periodic routing does not rescue adaptive switching;
4. endogenous state-dependent routing does;
5. the effect is eliminated by routing-state shuffle or targeted causal intervention;
6. competence is preserved rather than replaced by undirected exploration;
7. the result replicates across seeds and at least one altered ecology.

This would show that the missing substrate is not merely policy diversity, noise, or exploration pressure.

It would show that **dynamic access to existing competence is load-bearing**.


### Add a consensus-dynamics discriminator

The routing arms above test whether an alternative can gain causal access. They do not by themselves test whether mutually legible systems can form or escape a distributed consensus.

Once a Type-B access rescue is demonstrated, add a second-stage discriminator using two or more coupled systems whose relevant dimensions are already mutually legible.

Measure:

- `tau_agree`: relaxation/persistence timescale for matched perturbations that place systems in mutually compatible states;
- `tau_disagree`: relaxation timescale for equally strong incompatible states;
- direction of convergence after conflicting evidence;
- whether the higher-quality evidence wins rather than the historically dominant subsystem;
- dwell-time distribution around the dominant coalition;
- time to escape after contingency reversal;
- whether reciprocal-coupling ablation, one-way coupling, or correspondence scramble abolishes the timescale separation;
- whether a shared but false signal can create pathological consensus.

A healthy result is **not** simply large `tau_agree - tau_disagree`.

The desired signature is conditional:

> behaviourally grounded agreement persists longer than unsupported disagreement, but strong counterevidence can still destabilise the current coalition and permit a different mutually supportable state to form.

This distinguishes adaptive consensus from self-confirming lock-in.

### Revised V3/V4 trigger

Dynamic coordination would have a stronger case for V3 promotion if either of two load-bearing deficits survives simpler fixes:

**Access deficit:** useful latent alternatives exist, but only endogenous state-dependent routing lets them affect behaviour.

**Consensus/escape deficit:** alternatives are legible and routed, but the coupled system repeatedly returns to one maladaptive coalition; a selective reciprocal-coupling/escape mechanism restores evidence-sensitive switching under matched controls.

Neither result by itself implies that literal oscillations, travelling waves, or ephaptic coupling are required. The minimal computational mechanism that rescues the phenotype should be tested first.


### Result that would keep the V4 deferral intact

Do not promote the phase/ephaptic cluster if:

- proposer diversity fixes solve monostrategy;
- ordinary anti-recency/entropy mechanisms solve it;
- a static learned bridge is sufficient;
- random switching performs as well as state-dependent routing;
- no useful latent alternatives exist;
- routing effects disappear under matched controls;
- the apparent benefit comes from introducing new information rather than changing access.

In those cases, the V3 synaptic approximation remains sufficient.

## Relation to anaesthesia: a falsifiable artificial analogue

If the dynamic-routing account becomes load-bearing in REE, an artificial "anaesthesia-like" perturbation becomes possible without claiming that REE is conscious.

The intervention would selectively reduce:

- metastability of routing states;
- cross-system information transfer;
- dynamic communication-subspace reconfiguration;
- recovery from perturbation;
- access by weaker alternative strategies;

while leaving much local representation and learned content intact.

Predicted phenotype:

- narrower behavioural repertoire;
- longer dwell in dominant attractors;
- impaired reversal/switching;
- more structurally stereotyped trajectories;
- preserved local prediction or representation better than integrated adaptive action.

Recovery after restoring coordination should re-expose previously learned competence without wholesale retraining.

That would be a much stronger analogue of anaesthetic disruption than simply reducing activation.

## Why this matters beyond monostrategy

The largest implication is later social/ethical REE.

A5-A7 require more than recognising another.

The other's predicted state must sometimes gain sufficient causal authority to alter:

- candidate evaluation;
- harm/benefit comparison;
- commitment;
- residue;
- repair;
- self-interest trade-offs.

A static "other representation" is therefore not enough.

The same coordination machinery that prevents monostrategy may eventually be what allows **different sources of relevance to become temporarily authoritative**, including the state of another agent.

This creates a possible bridge:

> cognitive flexibility and ethical flexibility may share a substrate-level requirement: multiple internally represented possibilities must remain dynamically able to acquire causal authority.

That is a much stronger and more testable proposition than "ephaptic coupling produces ethics".


## Conjectural downstream implication: a functional niche for conscious access

The architecture above suggests a further possibility that should **not** be folded into the core coordination claim.

A separate conjecture note asks whether a regime combining:

```
access -> reconciliation -> settlement -> escape
```

could provide a functional niche for conscious access: specialised systems become mutually available, recursively constrain one another, reach a temporary organism-level settlement, and retain the ability to replace that settlement when evidence changes.

This is kept deliberately separate in:

`docs/thoughts/2026-09-24_consciousness_as_defeasible_coordination_regime.md`

The conjecture is about a possible **functional/access-consciousness role** only. It does not attribute consciousness to REE and does not explain phenomenal experience. No phase/ephaptic/coordination substrate should be promoted on the basis of this interpretation.


## Frontier status

The literature is rich enough to support the biological plausibility of dynamic coordination, but sparse on the exact artificial-organism problem REE poses:

> how should a persistent artificial agent learn and regulate its own time-varying internal causal topology so that dormant but appropriate competence can regain authority without degenerating into noise?

This is therefore a **Class S / possible Class F** problem under the frontier-evidence doctrine:

- literature strongly constrains the phenomenon;
- several candidate biological and computational mechanisms exist;
- the exact REE learning/control rule may still have to be experimentally discovered.

The Frontier Mechanism Discovery Loop is therefore appropriate if the simpler V3 mechanisms fail.

## Central hypothesis

The most useful formulation is:

> **REE may need an endogenous dynamic coordination layer that links mutual legibility to behaviour by controlling both causal access and the stability of reciprocal cross-system coalitions. Monostrategy can therefore arise from repertoire absence, repertoire-access collapse, or consensus collapse. Dynamic coordination is justified only when useful alternatives already exist and simpler diversity/translation fixes cannot make them acquire and retain context-appropriate distributed authority.**

The biological literature makes this hypothesis plausible.

REE experiments must decide whether it is necessary.
