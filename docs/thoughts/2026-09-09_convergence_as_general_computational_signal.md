# Convergence as a General Computational Signal

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Status:** unprocessed working thought; not a claim registration, architectural commitment, or experiment request  
**Origin:** discussion of information-hunger, degeneracy, basal-ganglia-like arbitration, and the subjective character of insight  
**Related REE work:** `2026-08-12` bounded information-hunger / closure thought; `2026-08-31_play_as_developmental_information_hunger.md`; basal-ganglia selector constitution (ARC-107 / MECH-447–450 family); TCRT; claim rotation / predictive equivalence; mutual legibility.

## 1. Starting observation

REE increasingly contains **partly independent ways of evaluating what matters**.

A candidate action can be approached from several directions:

- predicted benefit or goal progress;
- harm and viability;
- current drive state;
- uncertainty / information value;
- memory and prior outcomes;
- social consequences;
- rules, commitments and prohibitions;
- current self-state and vulnerability;
- model-based and habitual predictions;
- current control / precision state.

The same plurality also appears at a different grain during research. A question becomes unusually attractive when several otherwise distinct lines of work independently point toward the same unresolved structure. A representational failure, a biological analogy, a behavioural observation and an architectural difficulty may all begin to indicate the same hidden problem.

And subjectively, a strong insight can feel similar: several ways of seeing a problem abruptly become recognisable as **different views of one structure**.

This suggests a possible general computational motif:

> **Agreement among sufficiently independent, differently generated evaluations may itself be useful information.**

The proposal is not that agreement proves truth, nor that REE should collapse all evaluations into one scalar. The proposal is narrower:

> **Cross-view convergence can be represented as a meta-signal that helps allocate commitment, attention, compute, exploration or representational revision.**

## 2. Why degeneracy makes this interesting

Biological and cognitive systems are often degenerate: structurally different mechanisms can yield the same functional outcome. Degeneracy is normally discussed as a source of robustness, compensation and evolvability.

But degeneracy creates another possibility.

If several systems are capable of evaluating the same candidate from **different informational routes**, then their convergence can carry information not present in any one evaluation considered alone.

For example:

```text
route A: trajectory prediction says action X is viable
route B: episodic memory finds X safe in analogous contexts
route C: harm model finds no protected-boundary violation
route D: current drive state makes X useful
route E: social model finds X acceptable
```

The computationally important quantity may be neither:

```text
max(A, B, C, D, E)
```

nor simply:

```text
A + B + C + D + E
```

because the routes may differ in reliability, relevance and independence.

Instead the system may benefit from knowing:

```text
How many genuinely different evidential routes point in the same direction,
with what reliability,
and how much of that agreement is redundant?
```

That is a **convergence signal**.

## 3. Three initial forms

### 3.1 Action convergence

Several specialised evaluators favour the same candidate action or trajectory.

This does not automatically mean the action should be selected. Protected vetoes, catastrophic-tail risk, irreversibility or a single highly reliable contradiction can still dominate.

But convergence can legitimately alter:

- commitment readiness;
- how much additional deliberation is worth buying;
- whether residual uncertainty is decision-relevant;
- which candidate remains in the eligibility set;
- confidence that a decision is robust to the failure of any one evaluator.

This connects directly to the existing REE idea that certainty is impossible and closure must depend on whether further information is likely to change consequential action.

### 3.2 Epistemic / research convergence

Several independent investigations point toward the same unresolved unknown.

Examples in REE include an experimental bottleneck, a biological literature, a representation-contract problem and an architectural thought independently focusing attention on one interface.

Such convergence may be a good reason to allocate **information hunger** toward that unknown, because resolving it has unusually high expected cross-model value.

The important distinction is:

> curiosity asks where useful uncertainty exists; convergence asks where resolving one uncertainty may simultaneously update several partially independent models.

This could provide an endogenous mechanism for choosing what to think about next.

### 3.3 Insight convergence

A new representation or abstraction suddenly explains, compresses or aligns several previously separate models.

Subjectively this can feel like:

> “Ah — these were all the same thing from different angles.”

The candidate computational signature is not the feeling itself. It is a sudden event in which one representational change produces **joint improvement across multiple previously distinct views**.

Possible ingredients include:

- reduced description complexity;
- reduced predictive error;
- increased transfer across contexts;
- previously separate relations becoming expressible under one abstraction;
- agreement increasing without simply deleting contradictory evidence.

This deserves its own child thought and literature review rather than being assumed here.

## 4. Connection to the basal-ganglia-like selector constitution

REE's basal-ganglia work already resists a simple scalar-monarch architecture. Candidate actions are intended to be subject to eligibility, Go / No-Go pressures, conflict-sensitive hold, threshold modulation, recurrent competition and lawful permission to commit.

The present thought is compatible with that architecture but does **not** say that the basal ganglia literally compute a convergence score.

A more cautious relation is:

> a constitutional selector gives multiple drives, valuations and constraints lawful access to action; a convergence signal could be one meta-variable describing the geometry of their current agreement or disagreement.

This matters because different signals may deserve different voting power depending on state.

Hunger should matter differently when depleted versus satiated. Threat evidence should matter differently when vulnerability is high. Information hunger should matter differently when an uncertainty is reducible and consequential. A social obligation may become non-negotiable under one commitment state and merely advisory under another.

Thus votes are not fixed.

A useful conceptual form is:

```text
weighted pull = evaluator output × reliability × relevance × current-state gain
```

but **convergence is not identical to weighted pull**. Two strongly correlated evaluators should not count as two independent votes merely because both are loud.

## 5. Independence is load-bearing

Naive consensus is dangerous.

Five subsystems that all inherit the same upstream representation error are not five independent pieces of evidence. Ten agents trained on the same biased corpus may produce a highly confident consensus while sharing one error. Several REE mechanisms may appear independent architecturally while actually depending on the same collapsed `z_world` feature.

Therefore any convergence signal must estimate or at least respect **effective independence**.

Relevant factors include:

- shared upstream inputs;
- shared training signal;
- shared objective function;
- shared representational bottleneck;
- common causal ancestry;
- correlated residual errors;
- copied evidence or replayed information;
- one subsystem directly conditioning another.

The system should ideally distinguish:

```text
five routes agreeing independently
```

from:

```text
one error echoed through five routes.
```

This is closely analogous to scientific evidence: multiple methods are most informative when their failure modes are not identical.

## 6. Convergence should often allocate compute before it allocates authority

A safe initial architectural principle is:

> **Use convergence first as an attention / compute-allocation signal, not as an automatic truth or action signal.**

If several independent systems unexpectedly point toward the same unknown, that unknown can receive more investigation.

If several views suddenly become explainable by one abstraction, the candidate abstraction can receive more testing.

If several action evaluators agree, the system may reduce the expected value of further deliberation — but only after checking vetoes and relevant dissent.

This keeps convergence useful without creating a new scalar ruler.

## 7. Dissent is information too

The complement of convergence is not failure.

Persistent disagreement among competent, partially independent systems can indicate:

- hidden context dependence;
- an underspecified representation;
- a missing variable;
- incompatible reference frames;
- regime change;
- different time horizons;
- different protected constraints;
- model misspecification.

Therefore the architecture should preserve **structured dissent**.

A convergence signal should not merely reward agreement. It should make both of these events salient:

```text
unexpected convergence
unexpected divergence
```

The first may indicate a shared underlying structure.
The second may indicate that the apparent shared structure was incomplete.

This connects naturally to claim rotation and predictive-equivalence envelopes: agreement inside one experimental domain should not be mistaken for universal equivalence.

## 8. Diversity versus convergence

There is an immediate danger of premature convergence.

A system rewarded for agreement can learn to make its evaluators similar. That would destroy exactly the diversity that gives convergence evidential value.

Therefore:

> **Convergence is valuable only while meaningful diversity is preserved.**

REE may need pressure toward both:

- **diversity / degeneracy:** maintain genuinely different routes, models, hypotheses and strategies;
- **convergence detection:** notice when those different routes nevertheless support the same relation, action or abstraction.

This is a tension, not something to optimise with a single monotonic objective.

A child thought should develop this directly.

## 9. Relation to existing ideas rather than duplication

### Bounded information hunger / closure

The August closure thought already describes cases where several partly independent systems support an action while residual uncertainty remains, and argues that uncertainty should be reduced only when it can materially change consequential action.

The present thought adds:

> the *pattern of agreement across those systems* may itself help estimate whether another unit of deliberation is worth buying.

### Play / developmental information hunger

The developmental play thought treats exploration as seeking learnable uncertainty and learning progress.

The present extension asks whether **cross-model impact** should contribute to information value: an unknown may be especially attractive when resolving it improves several models at once.

### Basal-ganglia selector constitution

The selector work already provides multiple competing channels, state-sensitive gating and permission-to-commit.

The new proposal is not another selector. It is a possible meta-observable over the relation among currently active evaluators.

### Claim rotation / reality as semantic checksum

Claim rotation asks whether different representational views really express one claim and uses experiments to find where they diverge.

That is an epistemic analogue of this thought: convergence across views is interesting only when their independence and equivalence envelope are understood.

## 10. Candidate formal distinction

For evaluators `i = 1...n`, let each produce a task-relative vector `v_i` over candidate actions, hypotheses or representational changes.

A crude convergence quantity might depend on:

```text
C = alignment of weighted evaluator vectors
    corrected for covariance / shared ancestry
```

while the ordinary action pull is something like:

```text
P = weighted aggregate preference over candidates.
```

`C` and `P` are different.

High `P`, low `C`:
one or two powerful systems dominate despite disagreement.

Moderate `P`, high `C`:
many independent systems weakly favour the same candidate.

Low `P`, high `C`:
several systems agree that no candidate is worthwhile, or agree on avoidance / continued investigation.

High conflict:
large competent vectors point in different directions.

The formal notes document should explore covariance correction, effective number of independent evaluators, and whether convergence is better represented as vector alignment, reduction in posterior entropy, robustness to evaluator dropout, or some combination.

## 11. Falsifiers and failure modes

This thought should weaken substantially if:

1. a simple common-currency value signal explains behaviour as well as a convergence-aware system across the relevant regimes;
2. convergence adds no held-out predictive or control value once evaluator reliabilities are known;
3. apparent benefits disappear when correlated evaluators are collapsed into one effective source;
4. convergence pressure causes representational homogenisation and worsens robustness / exploration;
5. a single dissenting high-reliability channel must routinely override consensus, making aggregate convergence behaviourally irrelevant;
6. research-convergence scores merely track salience, novelty or uncertainty and add no ability to prioritise high-cross-model-value unknowns;
7. candidate insight metrics fail to distinguish genuine cross-model restructuring from ordinary confidence, fluency or reward.

## 12. Experimental programme implied by the thought

A useful progression is:

1. **Synthetic independent voters:** build several evaluators with controllable reliability and error correlation. Test whether an independence-aware convergence signal improves resource allocation over raw summation, max, majority vote and common-currency baselines.
2. **Correlated-error trap:** make several evaluators share one hidden failure mode. A valid convergence mechanism should discount the apparent consensus.
3. **Minority-correct regime:** one highly reliable specialist contradicts many weak evaluators. Test whether reliability and veto structure protect against groupthink.
4. **Information-hunger allocation:** offer several unknowns with equal uncertainty but different expected numbers of models updated by resolving them. Test whether convergence-aware information hunger preferentially investigates the higher cross-model-impact unknown.
5. **Representational convergence / insight:** create tasks where a latent re-description simultaneously simplifies several previously separate prediction problems. Test whether a measurable convergence event precedes or accompanies improved transfer.
6. **REE-native assay:** only after the synthetic quantities have range and discriminability, expose the signal diagnostically over existing REE channels before allowing it any causal authority.

## 13. Strongest current formulation

The thought is not:

> Majority vote is intelligent.

Nor:

> Agreement means truth.

It is:

> **A cognitive system containing genuinely different evaluative and representational routes can treat unexpected agreement among them as information. The value of that agreement depends on reliability, relevance and effective independence. Used cautiously, convergence may help decide what to commit to, what to investigate next, and when a newly discovered abstraction deserves the special status of an insight candidate. Its complement — structured disagreement — must remain preserved because diversity is what makes convergence informative.**

The next work should therefore be evidence-first: archaeology, literature review, formalisation, and discriminating assays. No claim should be minted from this thought until those passes are complete.
