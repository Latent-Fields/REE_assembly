## 4. REE-specific hypothesis

REE may need to distinguish humour/laughter from simple positive reward.

A possible model:

1. **Constraint conflict appears** — multiple interpretations or action expectations clash.
2. **Threat remains low or is reclassified as low** — the conflict does not require defensive commitment.
3. **Reframe occurs** — the system finds a non-threatening interpretation.
4. **Laughter signal releases load** — control pressure drops; social field may align.
5. **Exploratory/play mode opens** — the system can continue without rigid commitment.

Possible computational primitive:

```text
laughter_event = f(conflict_detected, threat_reclassified, social_safety, reframe_success, load_release, shared_alignment)
```

This may explain why laughter is closely linked to social repair. It marks a transition: the system was under interpretive or social pressure, but the pressure can now be released safely.

---

## 5. Relation to REE failure modes

Failure to represent laughter/play/release may contribute to:

- **rigid overcommitment:** inability to soften interpretation after benign reframe
- **social threat overclassification:** ambiguous social cues remain threatening
- **feedback entrapment:** conflict loops cannot discharge
- **belief fixation:** interpretations cannot enter play/simulation mode
- **repair failure:** social error cannot be converted into shared low-threat context
- **excessive literalism:** absurdity or playful contradiction treated as factual conflict

This could later be relevant to `Latent-Fields/ai-cognitive-failure-taxonomy`, especially around social repair, overcommitment, and mode-switching failures.
