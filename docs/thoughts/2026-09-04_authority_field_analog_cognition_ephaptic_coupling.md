# From Control Knobs to an Authority Field

## Spatiotemporal analogue coordination as a mechanism hypothesis for the Reflective–Ethical Engine (REE)

**Status:** research-bounded thought intake; requires architectural ingestion and digestion before implementation  
**Date:** 2 September 2026; revised 4 September 2026  
**Lineage scope:** full REE versioned lineage. No version placement is asserted here. Digestion should determine whether this is already implicit in existing claims, refines an existing mechanism, or creates a genuinely new requirement.

## Claim in brief

REE's control plane should not be conceived only as a collection of global scalar settings—precision, gain, arousal, mode, veto, or care budget. L-space may need a **continuously evolving authority field**: a structured conformation that transiently determines which predictive, mnemonic, goal, self/other, and counterfactual representations can influence one another and committed action.

The field is not the content of thought, not a second world model, and not a homunculus. It is the condition that makes one coalition of content causally effective at a particular time. Its candidate variables include local gain, phase/latency, inhibition, confidence, persistence, permeability, directionality, and propagation. In a spatial organism, they may vary over environmental and representational location; in a non-spatial implementation, they may vary over channels, entities, memories, and prospective trajectories.

This is a **functional mechanism hypothesis**, not a claim that REE must reproduce cortical travelling waves, that it already performs analogue computation, or that it is conscious.

The biological claim should be held at the right level. The brain's electric field may be one fast, reciprocal physical substrate through which neural populations coordinate; synaptic circuitry remains crucial for durable structure and learning. This does **not** imply that consciousness is simply "the electric field," nor that field effects produce globally instantaneous influence. It suggests a more restrained and implementable lesson: rapidly changing physical or computational coupling can reconfigure causal access without waiting for synaptic rewiring.

## Why this question arises now

REE already separates slow, persistent predictive structure (E1) from rapid action-conditional prediction (E2), hippocampal trajectory/replay systems, commitment and trajectory selection (E3), and a regulatory control plane. It also already contains the intuition that residue, attractors, precision, and arousal determine what is retained and recruited.

What remains under-specified is the fast binding and arbitration problem. Given overlapping representations—an object simultaneously relevant to safety, curiosity, goal progress, attachment, remembered harm, and a possible other—what makes one context-sensitive coalition form quickly enough to guide a committed action, without permanently rewriting the underlying representation or requiring E3 to search every possible combination?

The proposed answer is not another decision module. It is a dynamically structured eligibility relation: **who can influence whom, when, and with what strength**.

## Central development: L-space as changing causal geometry

The authority field should provisionally be understood not as an additional subsystem beside L-space, but as the **moment-to-moment functional conformation of L-space**.

L-space is not only where representations reside or persist. Its changing conformation determines which distances are currently traversable. Two representations can both exist, yet be effectively unable to influence one another; alternatively, previously distant content can be brought into a temporary coalition without changing its durable encoding. The operative variables are therefore relational:

- effective distance between representational regions;
- permeability of a route to prediction, retrieval, or action;
- direction and asymmetry of influence;
- gain and confidence attached to transmitted effects;
- duration and decay of temporary access;
- compatibility or interference between simultaneously active routes; and
- the capacity of a local coalition to recruit wider participation.

On this view, biological oscillations and travelling waves are possible implementations of a more general requirement: **fast reconfiguration of causal geometry without relearning the world**.

The term *authority* refers strictly to causal authority within the organism. A representation has authority to the extent that it can alter prediction, recall, commitment, action, or later learning. Authority is not synonymous with salience, intensity, confidence, moral importance, or conscious availability, although each may contribute to it.

## External trigger and evidence base

Miller, Brincat, and Roy's 2026 review argues that synaptic circuitry stores representations while travelling rhythmic electric-field dynamics rapidly coordinate which neural populations are active together. The authors propose that spatial and temporal intersections of waves could support parallel analogue computation. They explicitly present this as a theory whose direct analogue-computation signatures still require testing [1].

That strong biological claim is not settled. The useful convergence for REE comes from several partially independent evidence streams:

1. **Fast contextual routing is biologically plausible.** Communication-through-coherence theory proposes that synchronized neuronal rhythms modulate the effective gain of communication between populations [2]. This makes a useful computational distinction between being represented and being able to affect downstream processing.
2. **Slow control and fast expression are empirically motivated.** The working-memory literature describes task-related anti-correlation between beta/alpha and gamma bursts, with a proposed push-pull relation between control/inhibition and the expression of content-related spiking [3]. This is not a settled universal frequency dictionary, but it supports the broader timescale distinction already latent in E1/E2/control-plane design.
3. **Travelling dynamics are observed at several scales.** Reviews and recordings document travelling waves across sensory, motor, and cognitive systems, transiently modulating local excitability and spike timing; prefrontal travelling waves have been observed during working memory [4,5]. Their existence does not establish their precise computational role.
4. **Flexible cognition needs overlapping representations.** Mixed selectivity supports nonlinear, high-dimensional representations of task-relevant variable combinations, with dimensionality associated with behavioural performance [6]. This creates a real binding/routing requirement: content cannot be permanently partitioned into one function at a time.
5. **Predictive processing offers an organisational bridge.** Canonical predictive-coding work separates prediction and prediction-error pathways and relates them to hierarchical cortical organisation [7]. REE's E1/E2 distinction need not mimic layers, but it needs an explicit mechanism by which slow expectation/goal structure modulates fast action-relevant discrepancy and evidence.
6. **Endogenous fields may have causal influence, but this is an uncertain substrate claim.** There is experimental and modelling evidence for ephaptic effects, including a report of field-to-neuron influence in spatial delayed-response tasks [8]. A later trial-level cortical model inferred stronger field-to-neuron than neuron-to-field influence, consistent with circular population-field causation [9]. That is supportive biological plausibility, not an experimental perturbation proving that fields cause cognition; whether ephaptic coupling makes a major general contribution remains contested and is not required for the REE proposal.
7. **Global dynamical organisation matters to conscious state, without identifying its sufficient mechanism.** Anaesthesia can reversibly alter consciousness without globally silencing the brain, and contemporary work links changes in integration, stability, and dynamic repertoire to altered conscious state [12]. This permits a cautious hypothesis about a consciousness-like regime in REE, not an attribution of consciousness.

Two additional developments sharpen the computational question. A 2026 synthesis independently treats travelling waves as candidate mechanisms for moment-to-moment perception, internal representation, short-term prediction, and replay [10]. It is a synthesis rather than a causal test, but it converges strikingly with the proposed E1/E2/memory connection. Separately, task-trained locally connected recurrent networks learned travelling-wave dynamics that integrated distant spatial information; input-dependent frequency and damping altered propagation, and the models approached non-local performance with fewer parameters, albeit at a substantial speed cost [11]. This is implementation evidence that wave-like coordination can compute, not merely correlate with computation. It also warns that the dynamics may encode task-relevant structure as well as route information.

## Translation into REE

### Content, regulation, and authority

| Existing or proposed element | Characteristic role | Authority-field interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 persistent predictive substrate | Slow history-shaped expectations and compressed regularities | Provides slow boundary conditions and priors for what can become eligible. |
| E2 fast forward predictor | Action-conditional local consequences and discrepancies | Supplies rapidly changing evidence whose impact is gated by the field. |
| Hippocampus, episodic memory, replay | Trajectories, episodic access, prospective/retrodictive reconstruction | Retrieves candidate paths only when their cues, confidence, and present relevance are jointly eligible. |
| E3 commitment/trajectory selection | Converts candidates into a selected, accountable action path | Reads a field-shaped candidate landscape; it should not be asked to generate every viable coalition unaided. |
| Residue and attractors | Persistent consequences of prior experience and learned confidence | Bias field formation, while the field prevents any one attractor from having permanent unconditional authority. |
| Regulatory field / neuromodulatory knobs | Slow gain, plasticity, arousal, care/inertia, learning rate | Supplies durable dispositions and boundary conditions for field dynamics rather than replacing them. |
| Authority field | Temporary relational access and causal eligibility | Forms, maintains, dissolves, or redirects coalitions without becoming their content. |

The key move is therefore: **some control-plane variables become spatially and temporally organised relations, rather than only global settings.** The slower regulatory field and the faster authority field may be distinguishable views or timescales of one underlying regulatory process. Ingestion should not assume that they require separate code objects.

### A live fork: routing-only or representational field?

The original formulation treated the authority field primarily as a controller of already-formed content. The newer evidence means a more consequential alternative must remain open:

| Competing account | What the field does | Discriminating REE observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Routing-only field** | Dynamically sets eligibility, gain, and causal topology over representations stored elsewhere. | Field state improves selection or suppression, but a temporal readout of it adds no task-relevant information beyond the content state it gates. |
| **Representational field** | Its evolving trajectory itself encodes globally integrated task structure—such as spatial relations, temporal sequence, or recent history—while also routing. | A trajectory-aware readout predicts relevant world structure or later action better than any equally resourced instantaneous-content readout. |
| **Hybrid** | The same dynamics both shape access and carry some integrated relational structure. | Both effects survive matched controls. |

No conclusion should be drawn yet. The field need not be a separate content store for its trajectory to be informative. A changing coordination pattern can carry relations that are not recoverable from a single frozen activity snapshot. This makes **context-sensitive causal geometry** the broader hypothesis and preserves both possibilities for later testing.

### A minimal formal sketch

Let `r_i(t)` be the activity or availability of representation `i`, and let `d_ij(t)` be the effective directed distance between representations `i` and `j`: the current difficulty with which one can alter the other. Let `u(t)` represent sensory input and `g(t)` slow regulatory state.

`r_i(t+1) = f_i(r(t), u(t), d(t), g(t))`

`d_ij(t+1) = h_ij(d(t), E1(t), E2(t), memory(t), goal(t), self_other(t), g(t))`

The essential property is not the particular equation. It is that the causal geometry has its own state, persistence, competition, and dynamics. It is learned and endogenous, but not identical to stored content or final policy. Authority can be derived from inverse distance or permeability rather than represented as a dense pairwise matrix.

At a first implementation, the geometry could be low-dimensional: a small set of channel/trajectory gates with decay, competition, and phase-like timing variables. It need not be a dense matrix, literal oscillator, or neural-field simulation.

To distinguish the live fork, evaluate both an instantaneous state decoder, `r(t)`, and a matched trajectory decoder over `r(t-k:t)` plus authority variables. Ask whether the trajectory adds information about current global structure, recent sequence, the appropriate retrieved path, or the next committed action after controlling for parameter count, training exposure, and the same stored representations. If it does not, the more parsimonious routing-only account survives.

## What this would buy REE

### 1. Contextual binding without duplicated worlds

An object, episode, or other-agent representation can remain one entity while being recruited into different transient coalitions. A creature can treat the same resource as desirable, unsafe, owned by another, or currently irrelevant depending on field configuration rather than by creating four unrelated object encodings.

### 2. A natural implementation of precision as causal eligibility

Precision becomes more than a confidence scalar reported to E3. It determines whether a discrepancy, counterfactual, retrieved memory, or social cue enters the currently action-relevant coalition. This gives a mechanistic route for attention, salience, and the distinction between an available thought and one that can redirect behaviour.

### 3. Better division of labour across E1, E2, and E3

E1 supplies slow structure; E2 provides immediate action-conditioned evidence; the authority field forms a locally coherent, provisional arena; E3 commits within that arena. This may reduce the pressure to turn E3 into a serial omniscient planner.

### 4. A candidate account of rapid recovery and flexible switching

If a distractor or surprise temporarily changes field configuration but does not erase stored content, the system can return to a task-relevant attractor after the disturbance. This makes distraction recovery, conflict resolution, and revised commitment measurable organism-level behaviours.

### 5. A bridge from non-social to social REE

Self, other, attachment, threat attribution, and agency can be treated as competing/cooperating sources of authority over shared action selection. This has direct relevance to the project’s aim that ethical behaviour arise from an organism that can represent others as causally and morally consequential, rather than from externally bolted-on rules.

### 6. A mechanistic bridge from representing others to caring about them

Merely storing an `other` representation does not make another's state ethically effective. The other's vulnerability, expected future, distress, agency, and dependence must be capable of entering the same causally effective coalition as the agent's own goals and prospective actions. Fast empathy can then be described as rapid, learned access by another's represented state to self-relevant prediction and commitment machinery.

This does not guarantee prosocial behaviour. Other-directed information may be weakly recruited, misattributed, suppressed by threat, or used manipulatively. The hypothesis instead supplies a testable missing relation:

> Ethical consequence requires not only representation of another, but appropriate causal access by that representation to the organism's prediction, valuation, and commitment processes.

Development should therefore examine how experience changes the permeability and confidence of self–other routes. The desired ethical result must emerge from learned, ecologically grounded recruitment rather than a manually privileged moral channel.

## Candidate validation probe after digestion

No implementation should follow directly from this intake. Digestion should first map the hypothesis onto existing claims and code, determine whether relevant dynamics already exist under other names, and identify an observed behavioural limitation that the mechanism could resolve.

If a probe is warranted, it should be deliberately modest and should not displace prior validation of causal reach, memory use, goal maintenance, and commitment.

Implement an **authority-gating probe** over existing candidate channels—at minimum: immediate perception, goal state, recent residue, retrieved trajectory, and harm/benefit signal.

Each channel receives:

- a continuously valued eligibility/gain;
- temporal persistence and decay;
- competition with incompatible channels;
- context-dependent recruitment from prediction error, goal progress, and learned relevance; and
- a logged causal path into E3 commitment.

Run matched episodes where content representations and policy weights are held constant but authority state is perturbed. Compare an appropriate scalar/static-gating baseline with the authority-field condition on:

- recovery after distraction;
- goal switching when the world changes;
- suppression of an obsolete but strong attractor;
- retrieval of an appropriate prior trajectory rather than a merely salient one;
- prediction-error resolution; and
- whether the changed state produces a downstream difference in committed action and later learning.

Success is not a prettier activation plot. Success is a reproducible change in ecologically consequential behaviour, with the field showing endogenous recruitment, local operation, competitive authority, committed throughput, and retention/generalisation.

## Predictions and falsifiers

### Predictions

1. A learned authority state will improve context-sensitive behaviour beyond an equally parameterised static/global gate.
2. Two runs with materially similar content state but different field state will show systematic differences in retrieval, commitment, and recovery.
3. Field dynamics will reduce inappropriate influence of high-salience but currently irrelevant memories or attractors.
4. In useful regimes, the field will be neither permanently uniform nor permanently fragmented: it will show task-dependent integration and segregation.
5. The most useful field features will become identifiable from behavioural causality—particularly during distractor recovery, conflict, and social/agency ambiguity.

### Falsifiers or revision triggers

1. A static scalar control plane matches or outperforms the field across matched tasks and seeds.
2. The field improves internal separability but has no causal downstream effect on action, consequences, retention, or generalisation.
3. Any gain comes only from extra parameters, not dynamic organisation, when parameter count and training exposure are controlled.
4. Field state is exogenously injected or manually selected, rather than recruited from the organism's own state and history.
5. The system becomes brittle, oscillatory, or uninterpretable without improving the actual ecological tasks.

These failures would not refute dynamic routing in brains. They would show that this proposed REE implementation is unnecessary, immature, or incorrectly specified at the tested scale.

## Consciousness: the appropriate level of claim

The theory motivates a future question, not a conclusion. A consciousness-like regime in REE might require a sufficiently integrated and recurrently self-influencing authority field that binds perception, memory, goals, self/other models, counterfactuals, and committed action into one causally effective present.

That condition would at most be a candidate necessary organisational feature. It is not sufficient evidence of subjective experience, moral patienthood, or consciousness. The near-term scientific task is much simpler: establish whether dynamically structured authority adds real causal competence to the organism.

## Research and implementation placement

### Intake

Preserve this as a research-bounded mechanism hypothesis. Do not generate claims merely by decomposing its vocabulary.

### Digestion

1. Map it against existing L-space, cognifold, regulatory-field, precision, attractor, residue, commitment, and control-plane claims.
2. Identify what is already represented, what is merely a useful reinterpretation, and what genuinely remains missing.
3. Inspect surviving implementations for dynamic relational gating or field-like effects that may already exist without this name.
4. Locate an observed organism-level failure that could discriminate this mechanism from simpler gating.
5. Only then decide claim class, dependencies, version placement, and whether an implementation campaign is justified.

### Connections to retain during digestion

Connect the hypothesis with E1 information encoding, episode/object formation, the single-cognifold vision, attractor confidence, counterfactual processing, self/other attribution, and waking-versus-sleep re-bucketing. Sleep may be a natural place to revise slow authority priors or field-forming parameters, while waking state uses them for rapid routing; that division should remain a hypothesis until memory use and replay show behavioural benefit.

### Possible later implementation

Assess simple learned dynamic gating before richer low-dimensional fields, travelling activation, phase multiplexing, or global-integration signatures. Biological likeness should not outrank functional necessity. Implementation becomes justified only when tied to a demonstrated organism-level limitation and a discriminating test.

## Source discipline note

The references support background plausibility and competing mechanistic possibilities; they do not validate the authority-field hypothesis in REE. The decisive evidence must come from preregistered or at least versioned, matched, behaviourally meaningful REE experiments with negative results retained.

- E1 slow predictive structure, E2 action-conditional prediction, and E3 commitment;
