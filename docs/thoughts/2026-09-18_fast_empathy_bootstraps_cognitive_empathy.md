# Fast empathy bootstraps cognitive empathy (and what "object" means in self -> object -> other)

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Origin:** surfaced in conversation during the `ree-lit-pull-am-b` scheduled literature pull
for ARC-089 + ARC-094 (REE_assembly f2af0b691f2). Not a mobile capture -- a long-held view
stated in response to the pull's finding.

## The thought, verbatim

> "so it seems that the empathy systems may require restating. This was not unexpected given
> that we were building a huge base of v3 before even considering how empathy may work and
> there is highly likely to be other ways of instantiating it"

> "I had always thought the fast empathy system might bootstrap higher cognitive empathy.
> Literature search for object stage would be reasonable and may draw from developmental
> psychology and other fields"

And, asked which sense of "object" ARC-094's `self -> object -> other` intends -- the
cognitive/developmental object (a physical thing, the third term of triadic attention) or the
object-relations object (an internalised other) -- the answer was:

> **"Both, deliberately."**

## Why it matters

The morning pull had reported ARC-094's constructive mechanism as CHALLENGED by Krishnan et al.
2016 (somatic and vicarious pain patterns cross-predict at chance). That reading assumed
ARC-094 was a RUNTIME claim -- that a mature empathic response is computed by routing through
the self's own affective code. Under the bootstrapping reading the same finding is the
PREDICTED endpoint of differentiation from a shared origin, not a refutation. The claim's
defect is wording, not substance.

## Possible affected components

- E3 / commitment gate (fast empathy must become commitment-relevant, not merely predictive)
- control plane (relevance routing across self/object/other)
- affect primitive register (`docs/architecture/affect_primitives.md`)
- the ARC-059 maturational spine (self -> objects -> others)
- V5 social tier; nothing in V3
