## Core conclusion

The proposed synthesis is coherent as one thought, but only if "sleep" is not treated as one undifferentiated learning operation.

REE may need a protected offline mode in which four different kinds of work can occur:

1. **retention and interference repair** — preserve competencies that waking learning has made harder to retrieve;
2. **representational reorganisation** — alter schemas, equivalence classes, attractor boundaries, and episodic relations using evidence distributed across episodes;
3. **recalibration and attribution repair** — integrate conflicting outcomes, adjust confidence or precision, and correct noisy single-episode causal assignments; and
4. **behavioural-access repair** — restore the route by which retained predictive or procedural knowledge can again influence proposal, selection, and action.

These operations share a reason to be offline: they can require replaying non-current content, comparing non-contiguous experiences, changing representations that waking control is actively using, or training a readout without immediate sensorimotor demands. They do **not** share the same write target, provenance rule, or success measure.

The most important refinement is therefore:

> **Sleep may repair access to behavioural authority, but sleep must not confer authority directly.**

Offline processing may reorganise representations, rehearse readouts, or produce candidate changes. Whether those changes matter must still be demonstrated after waking through endogenous recruitment, competitive authority, fresh commitment, execution, ecological consequence, and retention/generalisation. This preserves ARC-020's protected consolidation boundary and ARC-130/ARC-131's causal-reach doctrine.

The result is not a generic claim that sleep improves REE. It is a typed account of what changed, where it changed, what the change is allowed to write, and how it later earns behavioural influence.

## Why this is not simply another rebucketing thought

`2026-08-31_replay_rebucketing_decision_relevance.md` already develops the case that replay can revisit non-contiguous episodes, test whether current object or episodic equivalence classes remain decision-useful, and safely split, merge, reweight, or re-index those classes during a decoupled phase.

The present thought does not repeat that proposal. It adds three missing distinctions around it:

- **reorganisation is only one offline function**; retention, recalibration, and access repair can dissociate from it;
- **memory availability is not behavioural availability**; a representation can survive while its route into action fails; and
- **offline change is not behavioural authority**; repaired information must still win a waking competition and produce a fresh commitment.

Rebucketing is therefore one member of a wider family of protected offline transformations.

## Existing REE commitments already imply a typed sleep boundary

REE's current sleep architecture already rejects the idea of unrestricted offline learning.

ARC-011 treats sleep as necessary for long-term integration, including world-model improvement, residue contextualisation, and precision recalibration. ARC-020 then limits the write scope: offline phases may update representational parameters, residue/viability integration structures, precision priors, and replay priorities, but may not mint commitment tokens, act externally, or directly mutate privileged authority stores from untyped proposals.

The current architecture also divides waking and sleep-capable processing:

- MECH-092 permits quiescent replay while awake;
- SD-017 proposes a minimal V3 phase switch in which SWS-analog processing installs or differentiates context structure and REM-analog processing fills causal-attribution slots;
- MECH-272 changes replay routing by operating state;
- MECH-273 aggregates episode-local self-attribution into a more durable self-model;
- MECH-275 generalises Bayesian aggregation across attribution domains;
- MECH-285 prioritises replay using schema staleness rather than simple recency; and
- ARC-063 already proposes a waking-light/sleep-heavy asymmetry for candidate-rule refinement.

The repo's evidence history is correspondingly mixed and informative. V3-EXQ-702 later passed three of three seeds for the armed sleep-aggregation cluster, with non-degenerate OFF baselines and load-bearing signatures for MECH-272, MECH-273, and MECH-285. That result shows that state-dependent routing, staleness-biased sampling, and replay-derived self-model updates can be made operative. It does not establish the broader transformational claim in this document.

The current record still exposes more basic limits:

- ContextMemory write-address selection is implemented but remains under validation; occupancy has not yet established useful content discrimination.
- Earlier organism-level sleep observations were reset-confounded and did not log a clean post-sleep behavioural outcome.
- V3-EXQ-909 measured internal replay diversity and goal-latent retention, not behavioural reorganisation.
- In that lineage, replay diversity was effectively collapsed, and the environment reset invalidated spatial content at the same boundary at which it would need to be expressed.
- Several design-era documents are stale relative to later implementations and governance events, so an intake must reconcile current status before changing any claim or build route.

These are not side notes. They show why a sleep theory must instrument the whole causal chain. A null behavioural effect could originate in waking content poverty, storage, replay selection, transformation, retention, readout, commitment, or the ecology in which the change must be expressed.

## Four offline operations that should not be conflated

| Offline operation | Main question | Candidate REE write target | Positive evidence | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retention / interference repair | Did previously acquired competence survive later learning? | replay-supported parameters, cortical/episodic traces, stable procedural structure | old-task probe and behaviour remain or recover | improved loss alone means useful behaviour survived |
| Representational reorganisation | Did the system discover or revise shared structure? | E1/ContextMemory schemas, equivalence classes, attractor boundaries, compression grain, hippocampal indices | transfer across changed surface features; improved state construction; index continuity | better recall is necessarily abstraction |
| Recalibration / attribution repair | Did conflicting or noisy evidence yield a better-calibrated posterior? | attribution maps, self-model residuals, precision/confidence priors, contextualised residue | reduced misattribution, calibrated uncertainty, changed decisions on relevant cases | confidence increase is accuracy or truth |
| Behavioural-access repair | Can retained knowledge again influence proposal, selection, commitment, and execution? | readout, eligibility, routing, proposal or policy-access mapping under typed gates | restored behaviour with retained upstream model; fresh commitments and ecological effects | imagined success became experienced evidence or direct authority |

All four may use replay. Replay is a transport and sampling operation, not a sufficient description of the transformation.

### 1. Retention and interference repair

The conservative sleep function is protection from interference. Multiple waking tasks can modify shared parameters so that an earlier competence becomes inaccessible or degraded. Replay can interleave older traces with newer learning and reduce this loss.

Bazhenov, Delanois and Krishnan's 2026 preprint, *Not Just After One*, is useful because it varies a common scheduling assumption. Sleep-like unsupervised replay did not have to occur after every individual task. Several tasks could be learned sequentially before one offline phase partially restored performance across prior tasks. The authors also report that task-specific information decayed gradually rather than disappearing immediately.

For REE, this suggests a **deferred consolidation window** rather than a compulsory sleep pass after every episode. The important variable is the amount and kind of intervening change that can accumulate before recovery becomes impossible or incomplete.

This does not establish that delay is free. "Several tasks before sleep" is an existence result in one artificial network, not a universal scheduler. The partial rather than complete recovery is equally important: the deferred window likely has a capacity or interference boundary.

### 2. Representational reorganisation

Retention preserves an old mapping. Reorganisation changes the mapping itself.

Solomon and colleagues' 2026 preprint, *Memory Reactivation during Sleep Promotes Structure Abstraction*, provides unusually direct evidence for this distinction. Participants learned novel categories whose surface features differed but whose relations followed graph structures. When learning phases occurred in immediate succession, the authors found no transfer benefit. In a second experiment, participants either remained awake across a three-hour interval or napped while one learned category was cued using targeted memory reactivation. Transfer appeared when the reactivated and later categories shared structure despite having no overlapping features, and the benefit increased with the number of cues presented during slow-wave sleep.

The result matters for REE because its dependent variable is not simple item retention. It is **surface-independent structural transfer**. That is close to what a rebucketing or schema-change claim should predict: relational organisation learned in one content domain changes learning in a new domain whose sensory features do not overlap.

The study remains a preprint and uses targeted cueing, a human nap, and a particular category-graph task. It does not prove that ordinary sleep spontaneously performs the same abstraction, nor that slow-wave sleep is the exclusive locus. It does supply a much stronger experimental shape than "post-sleep performance improved."

Oyarzún and colleagues' 2017 study adds another relevant property. Cueing one member of an overlapping memory network during sleep strengthened some associated memories and weakened others as a function of associative strength. Offline reactivation was therefore selective and relational, not a uniform multiplier on every trace.

This supports the possibility that REE sleep should be able to:

- strengthen a shared relation while pruning weak or misleading associations;
- revise which contexts count as equivalent;
- compress regular structure without erasing exception traces;
- preserve the original episodic provenance after changing its retrieval neighbourhood; and
- change a later learning rate or transfer pattern, not merely immediate recall.

The risk is equally clear. Selective reorganisation can overgeneralise, erase minority cases, or let salience dominate structure. A transformed representation needs reversible lineage and tests against held-out consequences.

### 3. Recalibration and attribution repair

Some offline work changes neither raw retention nor category grain. It changes how evidence is weighted.

Chen and colleagues' 2026 preprint, *Reactivating Conflicting Evaluative Memories during Sleep Reduces Decision Ambivalence*, trained opposing cue-outcome valences on successive days and then re-presented half the cues during NREM sleep. Cueing promoted integration of the conflicting memories and reduced next-day decision ambivalence, particularly for negative-to-positive reversals. The ambivalence change appeared for items that had integrated after sleep; cue-elicited delta power predicted ambivalence reduction and spindle probability related to integration.

This is not direct evidence for REE confidence dynamics, and the valence asymmetry limits generalisation. It does, however, motivate a more precise offline question:

> Can replay of conflicting evidence alter the geometry and confidence of a later decision without simply deleting one side of the conflict?

That is close to MECH-273/275's intended role. Waking produces episode-local causal signatures under noise, delay, and incomplete counterfactual information. Offline aggregation can compare those signatures across episodes, correct systematic bias, and write a posterior or precision change rather than a new autobiographical event.

For REE, useful recalibration would include:

- delayed-outcome correction in self-attribution;
- confidence reduction when apparently stable mappings conflict across contexts;
- confidence increase only when independent experienced evidence converges;
- residue contextualisation that preserves harm facts while reducing misattribution;
- reopening option space when an earlier path-closing inference was too broad; and
- separating reduced ambivalence caused by evidence integration from reduced ambivalence caused by attractor collapse or gate lockout.

The last distinction is crucial. A single dominant attractor can look "confident" while being less informed. Confidence should therefore be tested against calibration, counterfactual sensitivity, reversibility, and outcome—not against low score entropy alone.

### 4. Behavioural-access repair

Nijjer's 2026 preprint, *The World Model Remembers, the Actor Forgets*, supplies the sharpest evidence for a fourth operation. In a small Dreamer-family continual-learning agent, an unbounded replay buffer preserved earlier experience and component probes indicated that the world model retained old-task reward discrimination, values, and termination structure, while the actor's behaviour collapsed.

Under a frozen world model and identical imagined trajectories, reinforcement learning in imagination recovered the lost skill in zero of three seeds. Supervised self-imitation from graded dreams recovered it in three of three. Interleaved rehearsal retained the reported four-task and eight-task chains in all three seeds.

The narrow result is a component dissociation: forgetting can be a failed channel from retained model content to behaviour. It is not evidence that every actor forgets before every world model, and the reported system is small, preprint-stage, and limited to MiniGrid.

For REE, the finding maps naturally onto the existing causal-reach chain:

`representation -> endogenous recruitment -> local operation -> competitive authority -> fresh commitment -> execution -> ecological consequence -> retention/generalisation`

A system may retain the correct prediction yet fail at any downstream edge. Offline processing could in principle repair:

- a cue-to-retrieval mapping;
- a hippocampal proposal distribution;
- a model-to-policy readout;
- eligibility of a previously learned rule or trajectory;
- calibration of an E3 comparison input;
- competition against a dominant alternative; or
- the mapping from selected option to sustained committed execution.

Calling all of these "actor repair" would be too coarse for REE. The experiment must localise the broken edge.

## Authority is a waking result, not an offline write target

The phrase **authority repair** is useful only with a strict qualification.

ARC-130 and ARC-131 distinguish local competence from competitive authority and committed throughput. ARC-020 forbids offline mutation of privileged authority stores from untyped proposal channels. MECH-094 prevents imagined content from writing into experienced residue as though it had occurred.

Accordingly, sleep should not be permitted to write "this option now has authority." It may instead repair a condition that lets the option compete on waking:

- make the relevant representation retrievable;
- restore a readout that maps retained knowledge to an action proposal;
- recalibrate eligibility or confidence using allowed evidence;
- rehearse a procedural mapping under simulation provenance; or
- propose an authority-affecting change for later verified routing.

The waking system must then recruit that content endogenously, compare it against current alternatives, issue a fresh commitment, and produce behaviour in a relevant ecology.

This yields a safer formulation:

> **Offline processing may repair access to competition; waking operation determines authority.**

That wording prevents a useful continual-learning idea from silently violating REE's responsibility boundary.

## Simulation provenance: what a dream may and may not teach

The external machine-learning results create a productive conflict with MECH-094.

Nijjer's successful repair uses model-generated trajectories as supervised targets. Dream2Learn goes further: a classifier guides a frozen diffusion model to generate novel synthetic classes that are then used to reorganise latent space and improve forward transfer. Both demonstrate that generated experience can be a useful training signal.

Neither demonstrates that generated content should acquire the epistemic or autobiographical status of experienced events.

REE needs at least four provenance classes to remain separable during offline work:

| Content class | What it may support | What it must not become automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Previously experienced episode | replay, consolidation, re-indexing, experienced-outcome credit | a decontextualised universal rule |
| Reconstructed replay of an experienced episode | compression, comparison, retrieval rehearsal | a second independent experience |
| Counterfactual continuation from an experienced anchor | hypothesis testing, proposal rehearsal, uncertainty estimation | evidence that the imagined outcome occurred |
| Fully model-generated dream | competence probe, representation stress test, perhaps access rehearsal under a typed gate | autobiographical fact, residue write, realized reward, or direct authority |

A **realized-first** rule is a plausible safe starting hypothesis: offline rehearsal may preferentially use trajectories whose important action-outcome relation was previously realized, while generated variations remain hypothesis-tagged. This resembles Nijjer's practical grading discipline without importing self-imitation as REE's solution.

Even realized-first rehearsal is not automatically safe. A model can distort the continuation, overrepresent easy successes, or amplify a stale policy. The update target must be explicit:

- rehearsing retrieval is not writing a new episodic fact;
- rehearsing a ranking is not receiving new reward;
- adjusting an eligibility mapping is not minting a commitment;
- testing a counterfactual is not updating residue from harm that never occurred; and
- preserving a skill is not proof that the skill remains appropriate in the current ecology.

## Waking versus sleep updating should remain empirical

The literature does not support a clean equation of replay with sleep. Oudiette and colleagues showed that cue-driven reactivation during both wakefulness and sleep can influence which memories endure. REE already permits waking quiescent replay through MECH-092.

The stronger architectural proposal is not that deep change is impossible while awake. It is that different operations may have different **completion conditions**:

- some local updates can occur entirely online;
- some updates can begin online as provisional hypotheses and complete after cross-episode offline comparison;
- some updates may be possible in both modes but safer, less interfering, or more effective offline;
- some disruptive transformations may require a decoupled phase because the changed representation is also the coordinate system of current action; and
- some access failures may be repaired during waking practice if the old competence can still be recruited, but may require offline rehearsal if the competition itself prevents practice.

This partition should be measured rather than assigned by analogy.

SD-017's SWS-analog/REM-analog split remains a useful implementation hypothesis: install or stabilise slot structure before filling causal estimates. Yoshida and Toyoizumi's 2023 review similarly distinguishes better-developed models of selective NREM consolidation from more speculative proposals that REM reorganises representations. That evidential asymmetry should be preserved. A phase name is not proof of a distinct computation.

## Deferred sleep should be treated as a capacity curve

Bazhenov and colleagues make it possible to ask a better scheduling question than "sleep after every episode or not?"

Let `D` be the amount of intervening waking change since a relevant trace was encoded. `D` need not be elapsed time alone. It can include:

- number of new tasks or regimes;
- cumulative parameter change;
- schema conflict;
- model-error load;
- number of competing traces;
- representational drift;
- loss of readout accuracy; and
- developmental stage or replay fidelity.

The relevant object is a recovery curve:

`recoverable competence = f(D, replay coverage, trace quality, transform type, developmental competence)`

The curve may differ across the four offline operations. Retention may tolerate a long delay while attribution correction does not. Rebucketing may need several episodes before there is enough comparative evidence. Access repair may become harder after the proposal distribution stops sampling the old behaviour entirely.

This suggests that sleep scheduling should eventually be load- and competence-sensitive, not a single fixed episode count. It does not justify adding a scheduler before the underlying write, replay, and behavioural readouts are valid.

## Developmental learning: frequent sleep is not merely more adult sleep

The developmental evidence adds two constraints that can appear contradictory unless competence is explicit.

Seehagen and colleagues found that a nap of at least thirty minutes within four hours of learning helped six- and twelve-month-old infants retain newly learned actions across four- and twenty-four-hour delays. This supports frequent early consolidation windows.

At the same time, hippocampal replay machinery itself matures. Buhl and Buzsáki found that fast ripple oscillations emerge only late in the second postnatal week in rats. Noguchi and colleagues later showed that membrane-potential dynamics around sharp-wave ripples continue maturing toward adult-like temporal precision. Pochinok and colleagues' 2024 work links the developmental increase in inhibition to ripple emergence and stronger hippocampal-prefrontal communication.

The REE implication is not simply "infant agents should sleep more." It is:

> **Offline scheduling and offline competence develop together.**

Early agents may need frequent protected windows because traces are fragile and representations change rapidly, while their replay selection, sequence fidelity, cross-episode aggregation, and re-indexing competence are still immature.

Developmental tests should therefore separate:

- sleep-cycle frequency;
- amount and diversity of waking content;
- replay coverage and sequence fidelity;
- ContextMemory content discrimination;
- transformation competence;
- behavioural access after waking; and
- stage-appropriate ecology in which the result can be expressed.

A cold-start agent that fires many empty or degenerate sleep cycles has not demonstrated developmental consolidation. Longer observation under `no_grad()` is also not development if no relevant learning update occurs.

## The key measurement doctrine: transformation, not liveness

The current REE record already shows how easy it is to mistake mechanism activity for functional change.

The following are necessary diagnostics but insufficient outcomes:

- a sleep cycle fired;
- replay occurred;
- multiple slots were occupied;
- a parameter tensor changed;
- a posterior update was called;
- goal state remained bit-identical;
- replay diversity exceeded a low floor; or
- post-sleep movement looked smoother.

Each operation requires its own distal readout.

| Proposed operation | Required distal readout |
|---|---|
| Retention | preserved or recovered competence after interference, on old and transfer cases |
| Reorganisation | changed relational transfer, equivalence-class behaviour, generalisation, or state construction with trace/index continuity |
| Recalibration | improved calibration or causal attribution under held-out outcomes, not merely narrower uncertainty |
| Access repair | restored endogenous recruitment, fresh commitment, executed behaviour, and ecological consequence while upstream content is held fixed |

The diagnostic chain should be logged explicitly:

`waking experience -> valid write -> replay availability -> replay selection -> offline transformation -> retained change -> waking recruitment -> competition -> fresh commitment -> execution -> ecological consequence`

No final null should be interpreted before the earlier links are checked.

## Discriminating experiment family

No single experiment can test all four operations. A shared harness can hold provenance, waking history, sleep schedule, and causal-reach telemetry constant while changing the dependent variable.

### Experiment A: deferred retention window

Train sequential regimes with matched total waking updates and compare:

- sleep/replay after every regime;
- one sleep phase after several regimes;
- equal-duration waking rest with replay permitted;
- equal compute with replay disabled; and
- no offline phase.

Vary the number and interference of regimes before the deferred phase. Probe upstream model retention and downstream behaviour separately.

Support requires a non-trivial range in which deferred sleep restores or preserves prior competence better than matched controls. The useful result is the recovery curve and its failure boundary, not a binary sleep benefit.

### Experiment B: structural transformation without surface overlap

Adapt Solomon and colleagues' design to REE. Provide two task families with different objects or sensory features but a shared relational transition graph. Compare immediate successive learning, waking delay, ordinary sleep, and targeted replay of the first structure.

Measure:

- transfer to the second task family;
- whether shared relational structure becomes more retrievable;
- whether irrelevant surface details lose influence;
- preservation of exemplar-specific and exception information;
- ContextMemory equivalence-class or attractor change;
- hippocampal index continuity after reorganisation; and
- downstream state construction and commitment.

A recall gain without surface-independent transfer is retention, not structural abstraction.

### Experiment C: conflicting attribution and confidence

Create repeated contexts in which early action-outcome associations are later contradicted, with controlled delay and observation noise. Compare waking-only incremental updating with offline multi-episode aggregation.

Measure:

- self-versus-world attribution accuracy;
- posterior calibration on held-out episodes;
- E3 score margins and decision latency;
- counterfactual sensitivity;
- option reopening after an over-broad harmful inference;
- residue preservation for genuine harm; and
- whether reduced ambivalence reflects correct integration rather than one attractor suppressing all competitors.

The critical dissociation is confidence versus calibration. A sharper but wrong posterior is a failure.

### Experiment D: retained model, broken access

First establish a regime in which E1/E2 and hippocampal probes retain task-relevant information while behaviour has degraded. Do not infer this dissociation from reward alone.

Then intervene at successive edges:

- retrieval cueing;
- proposal generation;
- readout/ranking;
- eligibility or competitive gain;
- fresh commitment; and
- committed action persistence.

Compare offline rehearsal from:

- previously realized successful trajectories;
- reconstructed realized trajectories;
- counterfactual continuations with explicit hypothesis tags;
- fully generated dreams; and
- no rehearsal.

Track every write target and assert MECH-094 invariants. A successful repair must restore waking behaviour without treating generated outcomes as experienced reward, harm, or autobiography.

### Experiment E: developmental frequency by competence

At several developmental substrate states, cross sleep frequency with replay competence:

- frequent versus sparse offline phases;
- mature versus degraded replay sequencing;
- diverse versus impoverished waking experience; and
- valid versus degenerate ContextMemory addressing.

Measure stage-specific retention, structural transfer, attribution calibration, and behavioural access. This tests whether frequent early sleep helps only after prerequisite representation and replay capacities exist.

## Support conditions

The synthesis would gain support if:

- deferred offline replay preserves several sequentially acquired competencies over a measurable but bounded accumulation window;
- sleep-linked reactivation produces transfer of relational structure across non-overlapping surface features, beyond recall improvement;
- overlapping memories are selectively strengthened, weakened, or recontextualised without loss of source provenance;
- conflicting causal evidence yields better-calibrated attribution and decisions rather than simple confidence inflation;
- retained E1/E2 or hippocampal competence can coexist with behavioural failure in REE;
- a typed offline intervention repairs the specific downstream edge while upstream knowledge is held constant;
- repaired content regains influence only through waking competition and fresh commitment;
- simulated and experienced writes remain measurably distinct; and
- developmental benefits depend jointly on sleep timing, waking content, and replay competence.

## Falsifiers and boundary findings

The synthesis should be revised if:

- matched waking reactivation produces the same transformations with no extra benefit or safety from a decoupled phase;
- deferred sleep never recovers more than an equal-compute replay control, or delay has no interpretable capacity boundary;
- apparent structure transfer is explained by shared surface features, cue leakage, or better exemplar recall;
- rebucketing breaks autobiographical index continuity or erases exception traces;
- reduced decision ambivalence reflects attractor collapse, gate lockout, or uncalibrated certainty;
- upstream predictive retention and downstream behavioural failure cannot be dissociated in a relevant REE substrate;
- access repair works only when imagined outcomes receive the same write authority as real outcomes;
- repaired internal competence does not reach fresh commitment and ecological behaviour;
- frequent developmental sleep adds cycles but no transformation because replay content or sequencing is immature; or
- sleep effects disappear when environment resets no longer confound expression and the post-sleep ecology actually tests retained content.

Several nulls would be useful boundaries rather than refutations. Reorganisation may need a minimum number of overlapping episodes; retention repair may tolerate delay only below an interference threshold; access repair may be relevant only after the old behaviour stops being sampled; and waking replay may suffice for local changes while sleep matters only for disruptive schema or index revision.

## What is genuinely new for REE

Most ingredients are already present:

- ARC-011/020 define offline integration and its protected write boundary.
- SD-017 and MECH-272/273/275/285 define phase, routing, sampling, and aggregation.
- MECH-094 protects simulation/experience provenance.
- ARC-063 already distinguishes light waking from heavy offline rule-field refinement.
- the rebucketing thought already frames sleep as safe maintenance of representational coordinates.
- ARC-130/131 already separate competence, authority, throughput, and installability.
- the current developmental programme already asks for transformational competence rather than liveness.

The narrow additive synthesis is:

1. **A typed four-operation taxonomy.** Retention, reorganisation, recalibration, and behavioural-access repair are distinct offline outcomes with different targets and falsifiers.
2. **A strict authority boundary.** Sleep can repair access to competition but cannot directly mint behavioural authority.
3. **A provenance-safe access-repair question.** Generated trajectories may rehearse a readout without becoming experienced events; whether this is possible in REE is an open empirical question.
4. **A deferred-window hypothesis.** Sleep timing should be studied as a capacity curve over accumulated interference and structural evidence, not as a fixed after-every-episode ritual.
5. **A developmental co-maturation hypothesis.** Offline frequency and offline competence develop together; more sleep cycles do not substitute for valid content, replay fidelity, or waking expression.
6. **A unified causal-chain instrument.** Every sleep claim should localise change from waking write through post-sleep ecological consequence.

This does not presently justify a new sleep module. It more likely justifies an intake that refines experiment design, reconciles existing sleep claims, and asks whether behavioural-access repair is already owned by the ARC-130/131 causal-reach framework plus MECH-094.

## Version and governance routing

This thought concerns the full lineage and should not force a version label.

- Minimal offline integration already belongs to the solitary-organism stack where it is functionally required.
- Rich rebucketing, multi-scale abstraction, mature autobiographical re-indexing, and flexible access repair may require later individual-mind substrate.
- Social attribution adds a further constraint: offline aggregation must distinguish "unchanged by my action" from "caused by another agent" and must not compress away other-agent autonomy.
- Developmental scheduling should follow the earliest stage at which the corresponding representation, replay, transformation, and behavioural readout exist.
- Version routing must follow earliest functional necessity after the current claims and closure map are reconciled, not historical labels in stale design documents.

No claim should be registered, promoted, demoted, or moved from this raw thought.

## Candidate harvests for later intake

### Candidate architectural refinement

> Offline integration is a protected family of typed transformations—retention, representational reorganisation, recalibration/attribution repair, and behavioural-access repair—not a unitary replay update. Each transformation requires a declared write target, provenance class, and distal waking readout.

This may refine ARC-011/020 and MECH-030 rather than create a new mechanism.

### Candidate authority invariant

> Offline processing may restore a representation's access to waking competition, but competitive authority and commitment must be re-earned during waking operation; sleep cannot mint commitment or privileged authority directly.

This may already be derivable from ARC-020, ARC-130, and ARC-131. A duplication audit is required.

### Candidate provenance question

> Can hypothesis-tagged simulated trajectories update retrieval, readout, or eligibility mappings strongly enough to preserve competence while remaining categorically barred from experienced residue, autobiographical fact, realized reward, and direct authority?

This should remain an open question until reconciled experimentally with MECH-094.

### Candidate scheduling hypothesis

> The useful interval between waking acquisition and offline integration is a transform-specific capacity curve over interference, schema conflict, replay coverage, and developmental competence, not a universal fixed episode count.

This may refine INV-050 or a sleep scheduler only after producer and consumer telemetry are valid.

### Candidate experimental doctrine

> A sleep experiment must identify which offline operation is under test and log the complete chain from valid waking content through replay and transformation to waking recruitment, fresh commitment, ecological consequence, and retention/generalisation.

This likely belongs under ARC-130/131 and existing experiment governance.

## Provisional formulation

> **Sleep is not where REE escapes reality; it is where the organism can temporarily stop acting in order to reorganise what action has taught it. The protected phase may preserve old competence, revise relational structure, recalibrate attribution and confidence, or repair a broken route from retained knowledge to behaviour. But imagined events must remain imagined, harm facts must remain intact, and no offline result becomes authoritative merely because it was consolidated. The change matters only when the waking organism can recruit it, let it compete, commit afresh, and live differently because of it.**
