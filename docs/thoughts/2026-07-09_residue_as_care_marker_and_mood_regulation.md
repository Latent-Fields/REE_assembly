## Two insights (see intake for full development)

1. **Residue is the behavioural marker distinguishing care from mere simulation.** The `z_beta` leak (MECH-164) gives simulation of another's affect, which is equally the torturer's tool; the discriminator is that the torturer feels no residue. Residue that *drives the search for another way* is the observable signature of care — and hence a falsifier for INV-001's "ethics from prediction alone." (Answers the WS-13 audit's §2.5 crux.)

2. **Residue's persistent valence necessitates mood regulation (psychiatric mood ≠ affect).** Residue carries a *persistent* emotional valence (unlike phasic affect); because that valence persists and would otherwise accumulate and dominate, it must be kept in check — and **that necessity is what mood regulation is** (residue forces, mood-regulation responds; not mood regulating residue). Mood must be free to dip into different valences (excursion is functional) but **must return to baseline**; the system breaks on failure-to-return — stuck valence (melancholic rumination / agency-erosion) or suppressed excursion (numbing → callousness / dehumanisation, EXT-009). Mood-regulation is therefore an ethical precondition, not merely an affective state.
