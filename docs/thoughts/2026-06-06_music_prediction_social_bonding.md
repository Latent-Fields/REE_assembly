## 1. Why this belongs in REE_assembly

REE's social architecture will eventually need more than explicit other-agent modelling. It may also need mechanisms for low-bandwidth cooperative coupling: shared attention, shared temporal prediction, synchronised affect, and embodied co-regulation.

Music may be useful as a compact model of social cognition because it can bind:

- predictive expectation
- temporal entrainment
- affective anticipation and release
- somatosensory/body-state coupling
- shared attention
- group synchrony
- cooperative timing
- reward without explicit instrumental goal

This may become relevant to V4/V5 social architecture, especially if REE needs to distinguish coercive coupling, soothing coupling, affiliative synchrony, and cooperative shared-world attention.

## 4. REE-specific hypothesis

REE may eventually need a distinction between at least three social-coupling modes:

1. **Instrumental cooperation** — agents coordinate because goals align.
2. **Representational alignment** — agents attend to and predict the same external structure.
3. **Affective entrainment** — agents' body-state and valence trajectories become partially synchronised.

Music may expose mode 2 and mode 3 especially clearly. The system does not need a verbal proposition to bond; it may bond through shared predictive rhythm, shared expectation, and shared resolution.

Speculative computational primitive:

```text
shared_prediction_coupling = f(common_attention_target, temporal_alignment, prediction_error_coherence, affective_resolution, bodily_entrainment)
```

This should not collapse into simple reward. The point is structured co-prediction: two agents become more socially coupled because they are navigating the same unfolding pattern.
