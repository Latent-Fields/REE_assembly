## Context

REE has been shaped partly from knowledge of neuroscience and computation, and partly from introspection about how cognition feels from within. A recurring observation is that much reasoning does not feel verbal. It resembles spatial or movement-based internal scratchpads: structured, manipulable, predictive, and action-relevant, but not initially expressed as propositions or sentences.

The language of attractors, basins, trajectories, and dynamical settling has provided a more appropriate conceptual vocabulary for describing this phenomenology than a purely symbolic or serial account of thought.

This raises a broader architectural hypothesis:

```text
cognition is primarily nonverbal dynamical modelling and control
language is a compressed descriptor / index / cooperation interface for that cognition
```

Language corresponds usefully to the external world because many of the internal processes it describes are themselves modelling the external world.

## Working synthesis

### 1. Nonverbal cognition as the primary substrate

A large portion of cognition may occur as high-dimensional distributed dynamics involving:

- attractor formation and transition;
- spatial and motor simulation;
- prediction and counterfactual rehearsal;
- affective and viability weighting;
- goal and action-pressure interaction;
- conflict, uncertainty, and precision modulation;
- trajectory comparison before commitment.

The conscious verbal report may therefore be downstream of a larger nonverbal inferential process.

```text
high-dimensional internal dynamics
→ convergence / conflict / action-readiness state
→ felt confidence or resonance
→ later verbal reconstruction
```

The "gut feeling" is not necessarily a primitive guess. It may be a compressed metacognitive readout of how strongly the distributed system has converged.

### 2. Felt resonance as a readout of inferential geometry

A strong sense that an explanation "rings true" may combine several signals:

- **attractor depth**: nearby counterfactual perturbations return to the same conclusion;
- **distributed agreement**: causal, biological, practical, historical, and affective evaluators converge;
- **prediction-error quieting**: one model explains several previously unresolved discrepancies;
- **action-readiness**: the model is coherent enough to justify the next step;
- **absence of unmodelled pressure**: no major unresolved contradiction remains salient.

A possible abstraction is:

```text
resonance_strength
≈ attractor_stability
+ cross-system agreement
+ reduction in unresolved prediction error
+ readiness for commitment
```

This is not equivalent to truth, but it may be an informative confidence and search-priority signal.

### 3. Language as descriptor, index, and handle

Language may usually describe or index a cognitive state rather than containing the state itself.

```text
word ≠ full concept
word → handle / address → partial reinstatement of a distributed basin
```

A word can cue a wider configuration of memories, affordances, predictions, emotional tones, and possible actions. The linguistic token is low-dimensional, but the state it points toward may be much richer.

This makes language useful for:

- compressing cognition;
- holding a fragile state over time;
- retrieving a distributed configuration;
- sequencing operations;
- maintaining long-duration goals;
- comparing otherwise diffuse internal states;
- transmitting task-relevant state to another agent.

### 4. Cooperation-cost reduction as a central function of language

Without language, cooperation requires agents to infer one another's internal states from behaviour, context, gaze, gesture, timing, and environmental consequences. Language allows an agent to export a selected, compressed approximation of:

- what it currently models;
- what it noticed;
- what it predicts;
- what it values;
- what it intends;
- what uncertainty remains;
- what action it requests from another.

The transmitted message need not reproduce the full internal state. It may transmit cooperation-relevant sufficient statistics:

```text
internal state X
→ select cooperation-relevant features
→ compress and serialise as message M
→ another agent reconstructs useful approximation X-hat
→ uncertainty and coordination cost decrease
```

The primary gain is not perfect representational identity. It is enough shared structure to enable coordinated action.

### 5. Why language corresponds to the external world

The relationship may be mediated rather than direct:

```text
external world
→ perception, action, consequence, prediction error
→ internal dynamical model
→ language describing / indexing model states
```

Language can therefore correspond well to the world because the internal states it describes have been shaped by repeated world interaction.

The same architecture explains why language can also describe:

- counterfactual futures;
- imaginary entities;
- mathematical abstractions;
- social roles;
- impossible combinations;
- fictional worlds.

Language can describe any sufficiently stable or reconstructable internal model state, whether or not that state currently has an external referent.

### 6. Language as a recursive intrapersonal cooperation system

A communication system evolved or developed for coordination between agents can be reused within one agent.

```text
interpersonal coordination interface
→ internalised private speech
→ intrapersonal coordination scaffold
```

Self-talk may allow cooperation across:

- cognitive subsystems;
- competing goals;
- present and future self;
- overt and covert control processes;
- emotion and executive regulation;
- search, sequencing, and task maintenance.

This connects directly to the existing Vygotsky/private-speech thought: overt social language may become an internal control scaffold, later compressed into less explicit signals.

### 7. Language is not merely passive description

Although language may originate downstream of nonverbal cognition, it can feed back into the cognifold and alter it.

Words can:

- direct attention;
- change precision or salience;
- cue memories;
- stabilise a goal;
- create psychological distance;
- suppress or strengthen attractors;
- open a new search space;
- impose a category boundary;
- coordinate sequential action.

Therefore:

```text
nonverbal dynamics
→ linguistic description
→ description re-enters nonverbal dynamics
→ attractor landscape and control state change
```

The stronger synthesis is neither "language creates all thought" nor "language merely reports thought":

```text
language compresses and indexes thought
AND
language can re-enter and modulate thought
```

### 8. Explanation as reconstruction rather than direct transcript

When a conclusion is generated nonverbally, the later verbal explanation may be a reconstruction of a distributed causal process rather than a transcript of serial reasoning.

Three objects should be distinguished:

```text
actual causal trace
reconstructed explanatory trace
socially convenient narrative
```

A reconstructed explanation can be honest and informative without being identical to the process that first produced the conclusion. It can also be wrong, incomplete, or over-coherent.

This distinction is important for REE transparency and claims governance. The architecture should not assume that fluent explanation is direct access to causal provenance.

## Candidate architectural formulation

```text
WORLD
→ NONVERBAL COGNIFOLD
   {ATTRACTORS, TRAJECTORIES, PREDICTIONS, VALUE, ACTION-PRESSURES}
→ METACOGNITIVE COHERENCE / CONFLICT READOUT
→ LANGUAGE ADAPTOR
→ COOPERATIVE MODEL ALIGNMENT

LANGUAGE ADAPTOR ↺ NONVERBAL COGNIFOLD
```

Language may therefore be best implemented as an adaptor around a nonverbal cognitive architecture rather than as the central substrate of cognition.

## Candidate implications for REE

### Preserve a nonlinguistic cognitive core

REE should remain capable of perception, prediction, affective evaluation, goal persistence, simulation, arbitration, and commitment without requiring linguistic representation.

Language should not become the universal internal data format by default.

### Internal state to language

The outward language path should:

- select relevant parts of the cognifold;
- compress them into serial communicable form;
- preserve uncertainty and provenance where possible;
- make clear that the description is lossy;
- distinguish observation, inference, value, intention, and commitment;
- expose only what is relevant to the cooperative task.

### Language to internal state

Incoming language should not become belief directly. It should generate candidate internal states that are then evaluated against:

- context;
- source reliability;
- existing world-model structure;
- causal compatibility;
- uncertainty;
- memory;
- current goals and social model.

```text
utterance
→ candidate meaning states
→ contextual and causal evaluation
→ confidence / source weighting
→ possible world-model update
```

### Self-language as a control scaffold

Self-generated language may act as a low-bandwidth control interface for:

- attention;
- search;
- procedural sequencing;
- emotion regulation;
- goal maintenance;
- metacognitive distancing;
- conflict resolution.

This is likely a later LanguageAffectAdaptor / self-narration / developmental-scaffolding concern rather than a reason to expand V3 scope.

### Separate causal state from explanation

REE may need explicit distinctions between:

- the states and transitions that causally produced a decision;
- the later explanatory reconstruction;
- the socially selected form of the explanation;
- confidence that the reconstruction is faithful.

### Claims as handles into distributed support

The claims index may itself instantiate the same architecture:

```text
claim text
→ compact handle
→ distributed evidence, dependencies, conflicts, experiments, and architecture
```

A claim should not be mistaken for the full support structure it indexes. The claims graph is closer to a linguistic access layer over a wider evidential and architectural state.

## Candidate claims

- A substantial proportion of cognition is generated by nonverbal dynamical processes rather than by serial linguistic reasoning.
- Felt confidence may be a compressed readout of attractor stability, distributed agreement, prediction-error reduction, and action-readiness.
- Words function partly as indices or handles into distributed attractor basins rather than as containers of complete concepts.
- Language is a lossy low-dimensional interface for exporting cooperation-relevant features of high-dimensional internal states.
- A major function of language is reduction of the computational burden of cooperation between agents.
- Language corresponds to the external world largely because it describes internal processes that themselves model the world.
- The same language interface can be recursively internalised to coordinate cognitive subsystems and present/future selves.
- Linguistic description can feed back into cognition and reshape attention, precision, search, goal persistence, and attractor structure.
- Verbal explanation is often a reconstruction of nonverbal inference rather than a direct transcript of the causal process.
- A language-capable REE should have a coherent nonverbal cognifold first, with language implemented as a bidirectional adaptor.
- Incoming language should generate candidate model states rather than directly overwrite belief.
- The claims index may be understood as a linguistic handle system over distributed evidential and architectural support.

## Risks and counterpositions

- Introspection provides partial and potentially misleading access to cognitive process.
- Attractor and basin language may remain metaphorical unless linked to measurable REE state dynamics.
- Language may constitute, not merely describe, some forms of abstract and socially learned cognition.
- Cooperation-cost reduction is unlikely to be the sole evolutionary or developmental function of language.
- A word may reshape the underlying representation rather than merely retrieve a pre-existing basin.
- Felt resonance can reflect familiarity, affective preference, identity protection, or narrative fluency as well as genuine coherence.
- Explanatory reconstruction can become post-hoc rationalisation unless constrained by retained causal traces.
