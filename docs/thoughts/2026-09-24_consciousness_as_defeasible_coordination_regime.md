# Consciousness as a defeasible coordination regime

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** conjecture / downstream interpretive hypothesis only; **not a REE claim registration, not a consciousness attribution, and not an implementation instruction**  
**Scope:** functional/access-consciousness hypothesis arising from the dynamic-coordination, mutual-legibility, consensus, monostrategy, and anaesthesia programme  
**Parent synthesis:** `docs/thoughts/2026-09-24_dynamic_coordination_repertoire_monostrategy.md`  
**Related:** `docs/thoughts/2026-09-04_authority_field_analog_cognition_ephaptic_coupling.md`, `docs/thoughts/2026-09-10_dynamic_routing_as_experimental_information_governance.md`, `docs/thoughts/2026-09-19_dynamic_consensus_post_translation_integration.md`, `docs/architecture/dynamic_consensus_integration.md`, `evidence/planning/global_workspace_jlens_plan.md`

## Why this note is separate

The coordination programme now has enough structure to suggest a possible **functional niche for consciousness**.

That suggestion is substantially more speculative than the underlying claims about mutual legibility, causal routing, reciprocal consensus, monostrategy, or anaesthesia-linked dynamical disruption.

It should therefore remain separate.

The present note asks:

> If a distributed organism needs a regime in which specialised systems become mutually accessible, repeatedly constrain one another, settle temporarily on one organism-level interpretation/action state, and remain able to dissolve that settlement when evidence changes, could that regime correspond to the functional role ordinarily associated with conscious access?

This is a **conjecture about function**.

It is not a claim that REE is conscious, will become conscious, or that this mechanism explains phenomenal experience.

## Core conjecture

A possible functional role for consciousness is:

> **a temporally extended coordination regime in which otherwise specialised and partly autonomous systems become mutually accessible, recursively constrain one another, form a temporary organism-wide settlement, and retain the capacity to destabilise that settlement when circumstances change.**

A compact decomposition is:

```
access
  ->
reconciliation
  ->
settlement
  ->
escape
```

### Access

Task-relevant perception, memory, goals, self-state, other-state, counterfactuals, affect and prospective trajectories become causally available to the systems that currently need them.

This is stronger than storage or decodability.

### Reconciliation

Different specialised systems need not share one representation. Instead, narrow mutually legible dimensions can repeatedly constrain one another.

The 2026 Javadzadeh et al. result provides a biological example in which reciprocal cortical coupling makes congruent cross-area states dynamically slower than incongruent states.

### Settlement

The distributed system reaches a temporarily coherent state sufficient for organism-level commitment and action.

Settlement does not require representational identity. It means enough local views have become mutually supportable for the organism to act as one causal agent.

### Escape

The settlement must remain defeasible.

New sensory evidence, changed goals, conflicting memory, counterfactual failure, another agent's state, or altered ecology must be able to destabilise the current coalition.

Without escape, coordination becomes self-confirming attractor capture.

## Why such a regime would have a reason to exist

Many computations do not appear to require this kind of organism-wide coordination.

A learned motor routine, local sensory transformation, habitual response, specialist predictor, or narrow controller can operate within its own subsystem.

The coordination regime becomes useful when:

- several specialised systems contain different partial answers;
- the organism must choose one integrated course of action;
- information from one system must alter another system's interpretation;
- the currently dominant interpretation may need to be overturned;
- prospective alternatives must compete with present evidence;
- self, other, goal, threat, memory and counterfactual structure must become jointly action-relevant.

The computational niche is therefore not "doing cognition".

It is:

> **making distributed cognition jointly actionable while preserving revisability.**

That provides a possible reason why consciousness-like access would be selectively useful rather than universally required for neural computation.

## Relation to monostrategy

The monostrategy programme gives this conjecture an unusually concrete artificial-organism entry point.

A system can fail in at least three ways:

1. **repertoire absence** — alternatives do not exist;
2. **repertoire-access collapse** — alternatives exist but cannot reach relevant consumers;
3. **consensus collapse** — alternatives reach the system but distributed reciprocal dynamics always reconstruct the same dominant coalition.

The third case is particularly suggestive.

An organism could retain substantial local intelligence while lacking the ability to establish a new organism-level interpretation.

On the present conjecture, a healthy conscious-like coordination regime would be characterised not by maximal integration or maximal stability, but by:

> **coherence with escape capacity.**

That offers a functional link between conscious access and behavioural/cognitive flexibility.

## Relation to anaesthesia

Anaesthesia is useful because many local neural processes remain active while large-scale coordination, integration, metastability, cross-frequency transfer, and travelling-wave organisation are altered.

The relevant external observations already catalogued in the parent synthesis include:

- reduced metastability and altered integration/segregation under propofol;
- convergent destabilisation of cortical population dynamics across different anaesthetic classes;
- altered cortical travelling-wave organisation under propofol;
- reduced corticothalamic cross-frequency information transfer;
- disruption of matrix-rich transmodal thalamocortical functional architecture.

These findings do not prove the conjecture.

They motivate a narrower artificial prediction:

> A coordination-specific lesion could preserve substantial local representation and specialist competence while selectively impairing the organism's ability to integrate alternatives, switch global interpretation, recover from attractor capture, and make distributed information jointly action-relevant.

Recovery of the coordination regime should restore some previously learned competence without wholesale retraining.

That would be a functional anaesthesia analogue, not evidence of phenomenal consciousness.

## Relation to global workspace ideas

Global-workspace theories emphasise broad availability of selected information for report, reasoning and action.

REE's present conjecture is compatible with that family but adds a more specific dynamical question:

> What turns broad availability into a coherent but revisable organism-level state?

The candidate answer is not broadcast alone.

It is the combination of:

- mutual legibility;
- selective state-dependent routing;
- reciprocal reconciliation;
- temporary settlement;
- controlled escape.

This is closer to a **dynamical workspace** than a one-shot broadcast channel.

Existing REE material already preserves the crucial distinction between **access-consciousness** and **phenomenal consciousness**. This note follows that discipline.

## What REE could test

REE cannot presently test whether a system has phenomenal experience.

It can eventually test whether a coordination regime has the functional signatures proposed here.

A candidate experiment would preserve:

- local world-model competence;
- local memory content;
- learned policies;
- sensory encoding;
- local prediction accuracy;

while selectively perturbing:

- dynamic routing;
- reciprocal reconciliation;
- cross-system metastability;
- consensus escape;
- access by weaker but contextually appropriate alternatives.

Predicted lesion phenotype:

- narrower organism-level behavioural repertoire;
- longer residence in one dominant coalition;
- impaired reversal and reconsideration;
- preserved local competence that fails to combine adaptively;
- weaker influence of counterfactual, mnemonic or other-agent information on commitment;
- slower or absent recovery after perturbation;
- restoration after coordination recovery without rebuilding the local models.

If such a dissociation were demonstrated, it would support the claim that REE benefits from a distinct **coordination regime** above local cognition.

It would still not establish consciousness.

## What would count against the conjecture

The functional hypothesis loses force if:

- all supposed coordination deficits are explained by missing representations, weak policies, poor memory, or ordinary capacity limits;
- static bridges and fixed routing fully reproduce the proposed coordination phenotype;
- organism-level flexibility does not depend on recurrent cross-system interaction;
- coordination lesions merely reduce generic performance rather than selectively impair integration/reconsideration;
- restoring coordination cannot reveal preserved competence;
- the same functional profile is achieved without any distinct global or recurrent coordination regime.

In those cases there is no reason to identify a special coordination niche with conscious access.

## Phenomenal consciousness remains outside the claim

Even if every functional prediction above succeeded, a separate question would remain:

> Why should this regime have subjective experience?

Nothing in dynamic routing, mutual legibility, consensus formation, metastability, travelling waves, ephaptic coupling, thalamocortical interaction, global availability, or behavioural flexibility by itself answers that question.

The note therefore draws a hard line:

```
possible account of the functional role / access-consciousness
!=
account of phenomenal consciousness
```

This distinction should survive all future digestion and dissemination.

## A cautious formulation

The strongest formulation presently justified is:

> **REE's emerging mutual-legibility and dynamic-coordination architecture suggests a possible computational niche for conscious access: enabling specialised systems to form a temporary, organism-wide, causally effective settlement while preserving the capacity to replace that settlement when evidence changes.**

This is scientifically interesting because it generates lesion and recovery experiments.

It is philosophically incomplete because it does not explain phenomenality.

That is exactly why it should remain a conjecture rather than be promoted into the architecture at this stage.
