# MILESTONE DECLARATION — VIABLE MINIMAL WORKING INTELLIGENCE

> # **Viable minimal working intelligence achieved; causal/ablative validation of the responsible architecture remains incomplete.**

This is intentionally **not a normal thought intake**.

It marks a programme milestone: REE-v3 has crossed from merely containing candidate mechanisms and runnable architecture into having enough integrated, learned, behaviourally expressed competence that the organism can now serve as the competent substrate for higher-order mechanistic experiments.

The milestone is meant to be stated loudly because the distinction matters.

REE-v3 is not being described here as generally intelligent, highly capable, conscious, sentient, or an artificial general intelligence. Nor does this declaration establish that the present REE architecture is uniquely necessary or causally sufficient for the competencies observed.

What it does assert is narrower and stronger than “the code works”:

> **There is now a minimally viable artificial intelligence here to study.**

## Why this milestone is being marked now

The experimental programme has begun to cross an important boundary.

Earlier failures repeatedly collapsed into prerequisite failures: the organism was not competent enough, the relevant state was not populated, a mechanism could not acquire behavioural authority, or the representation required by the experiment had not developed sufficiently for the question to be meaningful.

The current frontier increasingly contains a different class of experiment:

- prerequisite competence has been demonstrated strongly enough for a mechanism to be retested on a qualified substrate;
- generated variation has been shown capable of propagating into committed behaviour, allowing second-order questions such as state-conditioning and self-annealing to become meaningful;
- modulatory mechanisms can now be asked whether they change an already-competent behavioural policy rather than merely whether their code path is live;
- long-deferred mechanism tests are being rerun because the developmental/readiness conditions that previously made them uninterpretable have been repaired;
- failures can increasingly be separated into **absence of competence**, **failure of a particular mechanism to influence an existing competence**, **state- or development-dependent expression**, and **experimental/instrument failure**.

That separation is itself evidence of organism-level maturity. A system does not need to be broadly intelligent before it becomes meaningfully possible to study what kind of intelligence it has acquired.

## Operational meaning of “minimal working intelligence”

For this milestone, **minimal working intelligence** is an operational programme term, not a claim that REE has crossed a universally agreed philosophical threshold.

It means that REE-v3 can acquire and express non-trivial, context-sensitive competencies through learning and integrated internal state, sufficiently that those competencies can function as prerequisites for experiments on higher-order control, representation, commitment, exploration, modulation, memory, and development.

The key transition is:

**before:** “Can the organism produce the prerequisite state or competence at all?”

**now, in a growing subset of the programme:** “Given an organism that can do the prerequisite thing, does this mechanism shape how it does it?”

This does not imply that competence is uniform. It remains uneven across seeds, mechanisms, representations, developmental histories, and ecological demands. Some current experiments still encounter collapsed or insufficiently differentiated representations. That unevenness is compatible with the milestone and is one of the main things now worth studying.

## What remains unproven

The second half of the milestone sentence is load-bearing:

> **causal/ablative validation of the responsible architecture remains incomplete.**

The programme has not yet established, component by component, which architectural elements are necessary, sufficient, redundant, compensable, developmentally required, or merely correlated with the observed competencies.

That requires the continuing lesion/ablation programme, matched developmental histories, causal interventions, replay and checkpoint branches, mechanistic falsifiers, and tests of whether competencies survive installation in the integrated organism.

Accordingly, this milestone must not be rewritten into any of the following stronger claims:

- “REE has achieved AGI.”
- “REE is conscious or sentient.”
- “REE proves the REE architecture is necessary for intelligence.”
- “REE proves the REE architecture is sufficient for intelligence.”
- “Every major V3 mechanism is now validated.”
- “REE-v3 is broadly or robustly competent.”

None of those statements follows from this declaration.

## Programme consequence

From this point onward, REE-v3 should be treated not only as an implementation under construction but also as a **minimal artificial organism with working intelligence that can be experimentally characterised**.

This should change the balance of the research programme without prematurely ending construction work:

- preserve and qualify organism-level competence rather than repeatedly rebuilding it accidentally;
- ask what competencies have actually emerged and under what developmental conditions;
- distinguish mechanism failure from organism incompetence;
- increasingly use lesion, ablation, perturbation, replay, checkpoint branching and behaviour-linked substrate imaging to identify causal responsibility;
- retain V3 as a comparatively simple experimental animal even after richer later versions become the main development target.

## Historical significance within the programme

The date of this declaration should be retained even if later experiments sharpen, qualify, or partially revise the competence picture. The historical event being recorded is the point at which the programme judged that REE had become sufficiently competent that its intelligence itself could be treated as an experimental object rather than merely a hoped-for property of an unfinished architecture.

Future evidence may move the boundary or refine the definition. It should not erase the fact that this was the working milestone recognised on 29 August 2026.
