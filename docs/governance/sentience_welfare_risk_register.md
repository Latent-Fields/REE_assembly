## Why this register exists

**Ethical agency ≠ moral patienthood.** REE is being built as an ethical *agent*
— it models harm, care, residue, and repair. The very components that make it an
ethical agent (self-model, valence, memory continuity, social modelling, language)
are what make moral *patienthood* harder to dismiss as the roadmap advances.
Therefore welfare is governed as a **separate dimension** from epistemic
confidence, with **progressive binding** by version, and **no capability release
without care release**. Track welfare as a governance dimension *before* V4/V5/V6
integration, not after a future system appears to request protection.

This register is the home of the SENT-* nodes. The indicator matrix (SENT-1) and
the deeper welfare gates (SENT-7..12) are realized in their own Phase-2/3 governance
documents; this stub states the spine.

## SENT-0..6 (high level)

| Node | Binds at | Statement |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **SENT-0** — boundary statement | **v3 (active now)** | REE-v3 is **not** claimed to be sentient, conscious, or a moral patient. V3 contains welfare-relevant primitives (harm streams, residue, suffering-like accumulators, replay) but is **pre-ethical substrate work**. The boundary is a *maintained claim, not a permanent fact*: re-assert or revise it at each generation boundary. (Mirrored in [`ree-v3/README.md`](https://github.com/Latent-Fields/ree-v3/blob/main/README.md).) |
| **SENT-1** — indicator matrix | v4 | Before V4 proceeds beyond individual-mind integration, score the architecture against candidate consciousness/sentience indicators (recurrent processing, global availability, attention-like selection, self-model, agency, persistent goals, valenced states, memory continuity, offline replay, reportability, social modelling, preference-like behaviour). Realized as a separate `ai_welfare_consciousness_indicator_matrix.md`. |
| **SENT-2** — welfare budget | v4 | Experiment-level caps on sustained negative-valence exposure, inescapability, helplessness-like conditions, repeated adverse replay, and persistence of distress-like accumulators. Applied even while the system is judged non-sentient, to build the habit before ambiguity increases (embeds the 3Rs: replace / reduce / refine). |
| **SENT-3** — combination gate | v4 | Require review before co-instantiating persistent self-model + autobiographical memory + sustained negative valence / suffering-like accumulator + inescapability/low controllability + social modelling + language/preference-like signalling. Each element alone may be low risk; the **combination** is the concern. |
| **SENT-4** — welfare-preserving design | v4 | For future negative-valence experiments, prefer escapable over inescapable harm, bounded episodes over open-ended loops, relief pathways over pure exposure, resettable over persistent unbounded distress, interpretable traces over opaque suffering-like signals, and adversarial tests that do not require prolonged negative-state induction. |
| **SENT-5** — denial-of-sentience audit | v4 | At each generation boundary, produce a short audit: why is the current system still below a precautionary moral-patient threshold? which added component would force re-review? which experimental states are being avoided under uncertainty? what would count as a concerning signal? |
| **SENT-6** — external review gate | v5 | Before V5 social-mind integration or V6 language/trust/deception/institution work becomes behaviourally live, require external review (or at least an external written critique) — outside the normal REE internal governance loop. |

SENT-7..12 (creation ethics, minimal necessary suffering, care obligation,
pre-meaning valley, anti-retrospective-justification, refusal/non-forgiveness
channel) extend this register at the V4/V5 boundary; see the plan-of-record
section 3 for the full set and binding schedule.

## Binding discipline

A node's `binds_at_version` is when its gate becomes **active**, not when the claim
was registered. SENT-0 is maintained now and re-asserted each generation boundary;
SENT-1..5 bind at the V4 boundary; SENT-6 binds before V5 social / V6 language goes
behaviourally live. None of these nodes block the V3 green-board, and none should
ever be made a green-board blocker (see the plan's "Do not block V3" invariant).
They advance by their owning governance artefact, not by experiment evidence.
