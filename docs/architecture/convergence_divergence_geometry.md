# Convergence-Divergence Geometry

Stub for the 2026-09-09 convergence family, written and registered 2026-09-15 by the
thought-ingestion pass (session thought-pipeline-20260915).

REE contains partly independent ways of evaluating the same thing: harm and benefit prediction,
drive state, episodic precedent, uncertainty, social model, rules, self-state, model-based and
habitual prediction. The selector constitution (ARC-107 / MECH-449) already gives them lawful
access to action without a scalar monarch, and MECH-452 already records which segregated loop
voted for the committed action. What none of that represents is the STRUCTURE of the resulting
agreement: how many of those routes reached their verdict independently, and what would change
downstream if a disputed relation were resolved.

Four claims here, in dependency order. They share one posture, which the parent thoughts and the
2026-09-09 synthetic programme both insist on: exact joint inference is the normative ceiling and
is not contested; the object is a bounded-resource control observable; and no convergence quantity
acquires action authority before it has earned held-out predictive value in a shadow diagnostic.

## MECH-558

Vote count is not evidence count. When several partly independent evaluators favour the same
candidate, the epistemic weight of that agreement depends on how many effectively independent
evidential routes produced it, and the useful representation is a coarse dependency TOPOLOGY
rather than a scalar convergence score or a single effective-source count. A marginal-reliability
combiner counts one duplicated reason N times; a single global dependence scalar cannot separate
a correlated coalition plus independent specialists from uniform moderate dependence. Independence
correction is additive to reliability weighting and protected-veto structure, never a replacement,
and any convergence quantity must be validated by an independent consequence rather than by a
transformation that forces agreement.

## MECH-559

Evidence-dependency topology and downstream-leverage topology are separate, state-conditioned
variables. "How many independent reasons do I hold" and "how many distinct consequential consumers
would change if this were resolved" are different relations over the same subsystems, and using
either as a proxy for the other misallocates queries, rollouts and experiments. Structured
divergence becomes an information-hunger signal only after multiplication by reducibility and
consequence: raw disagreement is attracted to irreducible noise and to broken evaluators.
Competence is domain-indexed, uncertain provenance must be represented as uncertain rather than
hardened into ancestry labels, and both graphs change with context.

## INV-108

Convergence must be detected over a preserved diversity, never trained into the evaluators. An
architecture that treats agreement as evidence creates a gradient toward manufacturing it. No
agreement objective may be optimised into the evaluators; the estimator must not teach its own
evidence base; provenance and disagreement history are preserved; a competent dissenter keeps a
protected route to attention; and the target is competent non-redundant route preservation, not
maximum diversity and not maximum agreement. The diversity that matters is error and causal
diversity, not geometric dissimilarity.
