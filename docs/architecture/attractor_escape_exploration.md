# Stuckness-Triggered Attractor-Escape Exploration

`evidence/planning/thought_intake_2026-08-30_false_bottom_exploration_noise.md`.

## MECH-527 {#mech-527}

Exploration variation exists (at least in part) to let the organism escape a **false
bottom** — an attractor that is locally stable and appears low-energy but is persistently
inadequate. The trigger is **confidence without successful resolution** (natural substrate:
MECH-482's epistemic_deficit accumulator), not near-tie uncertainty (that is MECH-440's
weakened formulation, explicitly distinguished). The perturbation escalates ABOVE the
immediate action level — candidate trajectories, strategies, retrieved attractors, the
precision/authority of the dominant attractor — and decays after successful resolution
(triggered annealing, not a tonic floor). A valid falsifier requires a genuine false-bottom
ecology (delayed-return trap, blocked-path detour, post-rule-change persistence, sparse
multi-step reward, or a globally suboptimal loop basin) and organism-level DVs (escape
probability, discovery latency, basin quality, post-resolution exploration decay,
no-unnecessary-exploration control).
