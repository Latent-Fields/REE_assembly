# Play as Developmental Information Hunger

Source thought: `docs/thoughts/2026-08-31_play_as_developmental_information_hunger.md`; intake:
`evidence/planning/thought_intake_2026-08-31_play_as_developmental_information_hunger.md`.

## MECH-528 {#mech-528}

Self-relative affordance uncertainty: the model under calibration is
`P(outcome | self-state, world-state, action)`, not `P(outcome | world-state, action)`. A
change in the self (growth, capability change) makes previously learned environmental
relations uncertain again; exploration should selectively RETURN to the affected affordance
regions. Distinct from SD-025/ARC-057 (place/density curiosity over the hippocampal map)
and from MECH-314b (generic uncertainty) — the conditioning variable is the changing self.

## ARC-136 {#arc-136}

Play-like behaviour emerges without a play module when: basic viability is secure, no urgent
commitment dominates, spare behavioural capacity exists, reachable states contain learnable
(reducible) uncertainty, and exploration is predicted to produce learning progress. The play
frontier MIGRATES (bodily → object → causal → social → symbolic → abstract affordance
spaces) as learning progress saturates, yielding an endogenous developmental curriculum.
Explicitly TENSIONED with ARC-019 (externally imposed curriculum gates — rival hypothesis:
imposed stages risk hidden oracles) and with the INV-058/MECH-194/ARC-049 explicit
play_mode-gate cluster (rival architectural reading: no PLAY primitive needed). Deliberately
NOT the strong equation `play = information gain` (play also serves bonding, practice,
regulation, signalling).
