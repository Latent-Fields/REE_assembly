## GOV-BRIDGE-1 {#gov-bridge-1}

When a version-boundary substrate expansion (V3->V4, V4->V5, V5->V6) bundles more than one
boundary capacity together, checkpoint and retain the intermediate **bridge organism** states —
each adding one boundary capacity, or one coherent capacity cluster, on top of the prior
generation — as first-class comparison artefacts, not merely as engineering scaffolding
discarded once the full expansion lands.

Bridge organisms are not public release numbers. Their purpose is causal isolation: so that a
downstream behavioural or substrate phenotype observed after a multi-capacity transition can
still be attributed to the specific capacity that produced it, the way a knock-in or mutant
strain isolates one genetic change in model-organism research.

This composes with, and does not replace:

- **GOV-V3FREEZE-1** — freezes the single *terminal* artefact of a completed generation.
  GOV-BRIDGE-1 extends the same freeze-and-preserve precedent to *intermediate*, non-terminal
  states captured mid-transition.
- **GOV-V4CUT-1** — decides which V3 capabilities V4 assumes and prefers claim *splits* over
  wholesale relabelling in the claims registry. GOV-BRIDGE-1 is the build-artefact analogue: prefer
  preserving intermediate *organisms*, not just splitting the *documentation*.
- **GOV-SUBIMG-2** — already owns the causal method (checkpoint branch-and-replay against a
  matched unperturbed sibling branch) that bridge organisms would be compared with once retained.
- **ARC-124 / ARC-125 / INV-098 / SD-096 / SENT-16** — the built continuity/branching/provenance
  cluster supplies the checkpoint/branch/provenance *mechanics*. GOV-BRIDGE-1 reuses them for a
  different purpose: deliberate scientific retention across a lineage of distinct preparations,
  not identity-and-obligation ethics for a single continuing entity.

## Deliverables this rule implies (not separately registered as claims)

- A canonical V3 reference organism and checkpoints.
- A panel of mechanistic lesions and developmental variants (extending the existing
  consolidation-pipeline lesion harness — SD-068/SD-071/SD-072/SD-073 — beyond sleep phases to
  E1/E2/E3, hippocampal, commitment, affective and control pathways generally).
- An **ecological passport** per retained preparation: validated environments, struggle
  environments, capacities lacked by design, interface requirements, interpretable-use classes,
  prohibited-use classes, developmental sensitivities, welfare/governance constraints.
- Version-specific practical niches — application hypotheses only; real-world actuation should
  not be inferred from the existence of a cognitive niche.
