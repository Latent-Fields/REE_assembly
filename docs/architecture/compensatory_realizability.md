# Compensatory Realizability: Recovery Without Restoration

Registered 2026-09-08 from `docs/thoughts/2026-09-06_recovery_without_restoration_compensatory_realizability.md`
via `evidence/planning/thought_intake_2026-09-06_recovery_without_restoration.md`.

External starting point: Grover S, Wen W, Reinhart RMG, *Neuromodulation for restoring and
amplifying brain function*, Nature Neuroscience (2026-09-04), DOI 10.1038/s41593-026-02434-6,
which distinguishes **restorative normalization** (push dynamics back toward a healthy-like
state) from **compensatory amplification** (find a repurposed process already supporting
function and selectively strengthen it), and names four enabling factors: multiple
realizability, multiscale neuroplasticity, precision readiness, activity selectivity.

**Provenance caveat, carried from the source thought:** only the publicly accessible Nature
preview was available. The framework is established by it; the details of the authors' full
argument are not. The REE mapping below is a DERIVED hypothesis, not a claim about what that
paper demonstrates.

## ARC-140 -- Compensatory realizability

> the capacity to preserve or recover a useful function by reorganizing causal responsibility
> across internal processes rather than by returning the system to one canonical implementation.

An adaptive artificial organism should not be required to recover by reconstructing its former
internal state. If a functionally important route degrades, an organism with genuine internal
adaptability may recruit another representation, memory route, predictor, latent dimension or
regulatory relationship that partially restores useful behaviour -- and if that substitute can
then be selectively strengthened *while the original deficit remains*, the organism has shown
recovery without restoration.

This reframes what counts as an interesting failure. When a pathway weakens, the first question
is no longer only *how do we fix the failed component?* but:

> **What did the organism try to become in order to keep functioning?**

### The discrimination problem is the claim's centre of gravity

The central experimental danger is mistaking **covert restoration** for compensation. Similar
surface behaviour cannot establish multiple realizability. Evidence is only strong when:

- the original pathway remains observably impaired;
- a *different* internal process becomes necessary for the recovered behaviour;
- ablating that substitute selectively removes the recovered competence;
- later restoring the original pathway produces competition, redundancy or reorganisation --
  rather than revealing it had silently returned already.

### The four-arm design this requires

1. **No intervention** -- lesion remains, spontaneous compensation only.
2. **Restorative-normalization analogue** -- restore the damaged route toward baseline.
3. **Compensatory-amplification analogue** -- leave the lesion, selectively strengthen the
   substitute the organism actually recruited.
4. **Control amplification** -- strengthen a comparably sized but *non*-compensatory process.

Arm 4 is not optional: without it, "generic added capacity" is an unexcluded explanation of any
recovery, and the result says nothing about selectivity.

### Precision readiness -- a terminological warning

The source's "precision readiness" must **not** be silently equated with REE's own
precision/gain machinery. The overlap is suggestive and the technical meanings may differ. The
testable residue is a good REE question in its own right: not only *what new representation was
learned?* but *what previously available representation became newly consequential?*

## MECH-541 -- Maladaptive over-stabilized compensation

Compensation need not stay beneficial. A workaround that is initially adaptive may become
over-strengthened, over-generalized or too persistent, trapping the organism in a distorted
solution after the original problem has changed or resolved.

> Pathology may arise not only because the original mechanism is damaged, but because an
> adaptation to that damage becomes over-stabilized.

Predicted routes by which a compensatory pathway turns pathological: acquiring excessive
precision or gain; dominating ambiguous perception; becoming resistant to counterfactual
correction; persisting after the initiating lesion resolves; recruiting memory and sleep
processes that progressively stabilise the workaround; reducing flexibility by making one
emergency solution the default policy.

**Registered detection signature:** improved *local* performance accompanied by poorer
generalization, flexibility, calibration, or counterfactual revision. A compensation that
improves everything is not this failure mode.

The experiment is the mirror image of the recovery experiment: progressively amplify an
initially useful substitute and locate the point at which compensation stops being adaptive.
Hysteresis is the diagnostic -- the compensatory solution persisting even after the original
route is restored.

## Sequencing

This work requires a V3 competence stable enough to lesion cleanly, and the source thought is
explicit that it **must not distract from establishing that baseline**. Candidate targets once
maturity permits: the observation -> z_world -> predictor interface, memory-route substitution,
and state-dependent precision/gain control.

## Relation to mutual legibility (ARC-139)

Under the federation model, multiple realizability is not only a property of internal
representations -- it may also be a property of the **interfaces among them**. A compensatory
pathway might emerge by strengthening an existing communication subspace, rotating which
dimensions are exposed to a consumer, recruiting a new low-rank readout, changing temporal
coupling, or letting replay retune cross-system correspondence. Whether compensation is
representational or interfacial is itself a measurable question once MECH-537/538's instruments
exist.
