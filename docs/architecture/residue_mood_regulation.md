<a id="mech-595"></a>
## MECH-595 -- a tonic mood regulator with return-to-baseline dynamics

Post-commit residue cannot be erased (INV-004 / INV-006) and carries a persistent signed valence, so the
tonic affective state it feeds must be free to make functional excursions (grief, low mood, elevation) yet
return toward a baseline band. Failure to return (stuck valence, rumination, erosion of agency) and
suppressed excursion (blunting, callousness, EXT-009) are the two opposite failures of one regulator, and
both degrade ethical function. V4: V3 has no signed tonic mood variable (`ree_core/cingulate/pacc_analog.py`:
"REE has no valence-signed setpoint at V3"); the nearest substrate, SD-032e, integrates z_harm_a into an
unsigned drive scalar.

<a id="q-110"></a>
## Q-110 -- is the caregiver bond a distinct channel for metabolising residue?

Does the being-loved bond integrate residue's valence load without erasing it, beyond what repair-completion
release (MECH-412) and social soothing of the suffering state (MECH-355) already do? V5 (caregiver
substrate).
