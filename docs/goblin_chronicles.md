# The Goblin Chronicles

The **Goblin Chronicles** are the continuing story-layer record of the REE project: real project turns retold in the language of goblins, magics, forges, reefs, ledgers, rulers, and expeditions.

They are deliberately separate from the scientific evidence. A Chronicle may preserve what a failure *felt like* or why a turn mattered, but it does not replace the claims ledger, experiment record, failure autopsy, or implementation history. The technical record remains the place where the world answers.

The origin tale is told on [REE for My Parents](ree_for_my_parents.md). The dated Chronicles continue from there.

> **Publication note:** the canonical working copies are maintained in `Latent-Fields/ree-paper/fantasy/`. This page is the public reading index. Public mirrors are intended to track the canonical Chronicle files without rewriting them into a cleaner success story.
