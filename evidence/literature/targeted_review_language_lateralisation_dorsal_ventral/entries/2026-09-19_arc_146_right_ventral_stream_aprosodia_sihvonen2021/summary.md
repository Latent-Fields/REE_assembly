# Right ventral stream damage underlies both poststroke aprosodia and amusia (Sihvonen et al., 2022)

## What the study did

Thirty-nine stroke patients, right or left hemisphere, tested twice -- subacutely and at three months.
The prosody tasks asked them to hear a word spoken with different stress patterns that changed its
meaning, and to hear words spoken with six different emotions and say which emotion it was. The music
tasks asked them to judge pairs of short melodies as same or different in pitch or in rhythm.
Structural MRI at both timepoints fed machine-learning lesion-symptom mapping and deterministic
tractography.

## The finding, and why it is better than the intake asked for

The 2026-04-16 intake gated `affect.bilateral_right_biased_coupling` on right-hemisphere affective
prosody evidence. What it gets back is more specific than "right hemisphere". Aprosodia mapped to
right frontoinsular and striatal areas, and the strongest predictor -- in multiple regression, and
stable over time -- was reduced fractional anisotropy and lower tract volume of the right inferior
fronto-occipital fasciculus. The authors' conclusion names the route: damage and disconnection of the
right *ventral* auditory stream.

Set that beside Hickok and Poeppel. The dorsal stream, carrying acoustic-to-articulatory mapping, is
strongly left-dominant. The route whose damage abolishes affective prosody is right-biased and
ventral. Two routes, opposite lateralisation biases, different functions. That is the strongest
available form of ARC-146's assertion that structured symbolic routing and affective coupling are
architecturally distinct with a ventral interface between affect and symbol -- and it is why the
intake was right to insist the affect MECH wait for it rather than be registered on the generic
picture.

## Three things I would not let past into the claim text

The authors do not describe an affective system. They describe a route that integrates
*rhythmic-melodic acoustic information* in both prosody and music, and that is why aprosodia and
amusia co-occur so tightly. Emotion may be a downstream reading of suprasegmental acoustic structure
rather than the route's content. If REE registers this as an "affective coupling" route it is naming
the function more optimistically than the evidence does; the safer reading is a prosodic-acoustic
route that affect depends on.

The locus raises a question that belongs in front of governance rather than being settled here.
Right frontoinsular and striatal damage is salience-network territory -- SD-032a's own anatomy. It is
genuinely open whether the affect route is part of the language architecture at all, or is the
salience system read through an auditory channel. ARC-146 as currently worded assumes the former.

And every assay is perceptual: which meaning, which emotion, same or different. Deficits in
*recognising* affective prosody do not establish that the same route *produces* it. ARC-146's binding
interface is evidenced on the input side only, which is worth stating rather than letting the claim
read as bidirectional.

## Confidence

0.78. Human patients, two timepoints, converging lesion-symptom mapping and tractography on exactly
the function at issue -- so transfer risk is genuinely low, which is rare in this corpus. Capped by a
single cohort of 39 and by the acoustic-versus-affective interpretive gap.
