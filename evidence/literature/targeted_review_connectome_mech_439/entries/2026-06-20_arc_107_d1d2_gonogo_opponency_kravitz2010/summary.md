# Kravitz et al. 2010 — D1/D2 direct/indirect-pathway opponency as the causal substrate of Go/No-Go selection

**Claim grounding:** ARC-107 (BG selector constitution), MECH-449 (Go/No-Go eligibility constitution), MECH-448 (rank-preserving F→eligibility demotion) · ARC-106 component 1 (direct/indirect Go/No-Go opponency)
**Source:** Kravitz AV, Freeze BS, Parker PRL, Kay K, Thwin MT, Deisseroth K, Kreitzer AC. *Nature* 466:622–626 (2010). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature09159) · PMID 20613723.
*According to PubMed.*

## What the paper did

For two decades the "classical model" of the basal ganglia — direct pathway (D1 MSNs) facilitates movement, indirect pathway (D2 MSNs) inhibits it — was an inference from anatomy and lesion data, never tested by direct, selective activation of each pathway in a behaving animal. Kravitz and colleagues closed that gap with cell-type-specific optogenetics: Cre-dependent channelrhodopsin-2 in the dorsal striatum of D1-Cre and D2-Cre BAC-transgenic mice, so that blue light selectively drove either the direct-pathway or the indirect-pathway medium spiny neurons. Bilateral excitation of **indirect-pathway** MSNs produced a parkinsonian state — increased freezing, bradykinesia, and decreased locomotor initiation. Excitation of **direct-pathway** MSNs did the opposite — reduced freezing and increased locomotion — and, in a 6-OHDA mouse model of Parkinson's disease, direct-pathway activation *rescued* the freezing, bradykinesia and initiation deficits. This is the canonical causal demonstration that the two pathways are genuinely opponent control channels.

## Why it matters for the ARC-107 constitution

This is the biological grounding for the single most load-bearing structural commitment in ARC-107: that committed-action selection should be arbitrated by **bounded, oppositely-signed Go and No-Go pressures**, not by one scalar score owning the gate. The whole motivation for the constitution (design note §1) is that REE's selector behaves like `candidates → F-score dominance → committed argmin`, where F carries ~88–89% of committed-selection variance (V3-EXQ-571). Kravitz shows that the brain's selection substrate does not have a single monarch: a *promotion* channel and a *suppression* channel are held in balance, and tilting that balance is what changes behaviour. MECH-449's "bounded Go (promote when evidence/value/drive/rule/safety make a channel eligible) AND bounded No-Go (suppress unsafe/stale/perseverative/irrelevant channels)" is a direct functional import of this opponency.

It also anchors the **MECH-448 divergence**, which is the subtle and important one. Canonical value divisive normalisation (Carandini & Heeger 2012; Louie/Khaw/Glimcher 2013 — both already in this review) is *order-preserving* and *pooled-symmetric*: every option is divided by a shared field, so the ranking survives. MECH-448 demotes **only F** and removes it from the committed argmin — a *rank-altering*, *single-target* operation that exceeds canonical DN and therefore (per ARC-106 §4.4) needs its own justification. Kravitz supplies the biological precedent for rank alteration: the brain alters which action wins not by symmetric renormalisation but by **opponent population competition** — a separately-controllable suppression channel that can override the would-be winner. So MECH-448's stronger-than-DN operation is not an arbitrary engineering liberty; it mirrors how a real Go/No-Go substrate achieves what symmetric normalisation cannot.

## Limitations and the divergence ledger

Three caveats keep this honest. First, the readout is **motor vigour** — freeze versus locomote — which is one level coarser than "which committed *action class* wins". A paper this coarse grounds the *function* (opponent gating beats single-scalar monarchy) but cannot, by itself, prove that a Go/No-Go constitution recovers committed-action *diversity* rather than merely toggling action on and off. That discriminant is exactly what the REE falsifier must carry (design note §4, the lawful-conversion bar), not the literature. Second, biology implements opponency with **two distinct neuronal populations**; REE has neither separate populations nor a vigour axis. The grounding is of the function, not the implementation — and reading it as a mandate for two literal channels would violate ARC-106 guardrail G2 (reuse-before-duplicate), since MECH-260's dACC suppression already hosts a No-Go-like function that the constitution should *generalise*, not duplicate. Third, the manipulation is bilateral, sustained, non-physiological pathway-wide excitation; it shows the pathways *can* bidirectionally control behaviour, not that the brain's normal graded balance maps cleanly onto a per-candidate eligibility envelope.

## Confidence

I put this at **0.72 (supports)**. Source quality is near-ceiling (Nature, causal optogenetic gold standard, foundational, heavily replicated), but mapping fidelity is only moderate-high — the function transfers, the implementation and the readout level do not — and transfer risk is genuinely elevated by the mouse-motor → REE-abstract-selection jump. As with every entry in this review, the paper grounds the *reference architecture* that ARC-107 imports; it does not and cannot certify that REE's specific levers (MECH-448 demotion, MECH-449 constitution) will work. That remains the falsifier's burden.
