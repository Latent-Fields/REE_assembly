# Frank (2006) — Hold your horses: a dynamic computational role for the STN in decision making

**Source:** Frank, M.J. (2006). *Neural Networks* 19(8):1120-1136. [DOI 10.1016/j.neunet.2006.03.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2006.03.006) · PMID 16945502. (According to PubMed.)

**Claim under test:** MECH-439 (F-dominance bounds committed-action diversity; the proposed fix V3-EXQ-689a `_gap_scaled_commit_pick` + conflict-graded top-k, `k=f(top-F gap)`, is a basal-ganglia hyperdirect-pathway analog).

## What the paper did

Frank extended an existing neural-network model of cortico-basal-ganglia action selection — which already reproduced response-selection deficits in dopamine-depleted (Parkinsonian) circuits — by adding the subthalamic nucleus (STN) and its hyperdirect (cortico-subthalamic) and subthalamic-pallidal projections. The model's central result is that the STN does not select an action directly; it modulates *when* the system is allowed to commit. Increased cortical response conflict — multiple frontal motor channels active at similar strength — drives the STN to transiently raise the global threshold that any channel must cross before a response is gated through. The effect is to suppress premature commitment, and the model shows this has substantial downstream consequences for *which* response is ultimately selected, "particularly when there are multiple competing responses." The paper also reproduces emergent pathological oscillations under simulated dopamine depletion and the mixed effect of STN lesions (fewer oscillations, but impaired conflict-sensitive decision making).

## Why it grounds the proposed fix

This is the foundational computational statement of exactly the mechanism MECH-439's fix translates. The four-channel cluster autopsy localised the committed-action-diversity ceiling to a single structural property: F (the primary harm/goal score) monopolises ~88-89% of E3 committed-selection variance (V3-EXQ-571), so every modulatory stream drowns at the argmax. Frank's model is the canonical demonstration that real basal ganglia do *not* resolve multi-option competition by a pure argmax over a single dominant score — the hyperdirect pathway interposes a conflict-graded hold that keeps competing options in play at near-ties. REE's `k=f(top-F gap)` is the discrete analog: when the gap between the top F-scores is small (the high-conflict / near-tie regime), widen the committed-selection consideration set so a non-dominant stream can convert. That the brain implements precisely a conflict-graded threshold-raising hold over BG action selection is what makes the 689a lever a biological translation rather than an engineering patch — which is the whole point of grounding it before adjudication.

## The load-bearing divergence (treated as load-bearing, not a caveat)

Per the `biology_before_formal_definitions` discipline, the gap between the implementation and the biology is the part that earns its keep. Frank's STN raises a **continuous, temporal** threshold on a **within-trial evidence accumulator**: the hold *buys time*, and the benefit is entirely parasitic on the fact that more evidence accrues during the delay and can overturn the early leader. REE's `k=f(F-gap)`, by contrast, widens a **discrete consideration set (top-k) at a single commit step** with no within-trial temporal accumulation — it substitutes set-width for time. The two are equivalent only when widening the set is paired with a competing signal that can outscore F *within* the shortlist. The biology itself predicts the failure mode: Frank's hold is useless without an independent accumulation process able to reverse the leader given time; REE's analog is correspondingly inert wherever F also dominates the within-shortlist scoring. This is not a hypothetical — it is exactly what the cluster observed (654g seed-44 ARM_ON committed-class distribution byte-identical to ARM_OFF despite live CRF; 625e monostrategy with the shortlist active). So Frank 2006 supports MECH-439's *framing* strongly while sharpening its risk: a near-ties set-widening lever inherits the biology's contingency, and a 689a PASS should be read as a confirmed translation **only if the committed-entropy lift correlates with the per-tick F-gap** (the experiment's pre-registered falsifier). If it does not, the divergence says the real target is the missing within-trial competition — i.e. direct F-variance rebalancing, the autopsy's Reading-2.

## Confidence

I assign **supports, confidence 0.74**. Source quality is high (canonical, foundational, with novel predictions later confirmed empirically). Mapping fidelity on the qualitative mechanism is high — this genuinely is the computational ancestor of the fix — but discounted for the temporal-threshold-vs-discrete-set structural difference. Transfer risk is moderate: model-to-model transfer is reasonable, but REE lacks the within-trial accumulator that carries the biology's benefit, so the analog can fall silent in exactly the F-dominated regime MECH-439 names. The honest reading is that this paper makes the *direction* of the fix biologically respectable and simultaneously tells us where it can fail.
