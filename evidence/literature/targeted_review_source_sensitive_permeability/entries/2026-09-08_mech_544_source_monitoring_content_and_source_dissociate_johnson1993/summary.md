# Johnson, Hashtroudi & Lindsay 1993 -- content survives, source does not

Psychological Bulletin 114(1):3-28, DOI 10.1037/0033-2909.114.1.3, PMID 8346328. MECH-544's notes say source-tag decay is "the part with the clearest external grounding", and that is correct. This is the founding statement of the source-monitoring framework, and it establishes exactly the dissociation the claim needs: what you remember and where it came from are separable, and the second fails while the first survives, systematically and lawfully.

The framework's account is that memories do not arrive labelled. Source is attributed at retrieval, by judgement processes weighing the qualitative characteristics of a remembered experience -- how much perceptual detail it has, what contextual and semantic information accompanies it, what cognitive operations are recorded -- against criteria that shift with task, motivation and care. Misattributions follow predictably: they increase as sources resemble each other, and they can occur while the content is remembered accurately. For MECH-542, this is also the mechanism by which a protected variable could fail selectively while permitted variables update correctly, which is what makes the two claims a coherent pair rather than a claim and its caveat.

There is a real architectural divergence to record, and it is more interesting than a transfer caveat. MECH-544 speaks of source-tag decay, which presumes a tag that is stored and then weakens. The source-monitoring framework's central commitment is that there is no such tag: source is reconstructed each time, from features. Those are different systems and they come apart empirically. A stored-tag system's errors should track storage interval and interference; an attribution system's errors should track the similarity between candidate sources and the decision criterion in force, and should be manipulable at retrieval without touching storage at all. If REE implements a decaying tag and reproduces the human phenomenon, it will have reproduced it by a route the biology does not use, and the two will diverge under retrieval manipulations.

That is worth deciding deliberately rather than inheriting, and it is the kind of choice the claim is still free to make. Supports at 0.72.
