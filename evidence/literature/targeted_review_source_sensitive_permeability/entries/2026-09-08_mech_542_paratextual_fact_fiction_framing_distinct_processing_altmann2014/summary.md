# Altmann et al. 2014 -- the positive fact/fiction finding that did not consistently reproduce

Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience 9(1):22-29, DOI 10.1093/scan/nss098, PMID 22956671, PMC3871725. Readers were given narratives that were identical in content but framed by paratextual information as factual or as fiction. Reading in the factual mode engaged what the authors describe as an action-based reconstruction pattern, with faster behavioural responses; the fictional mode engaged what they read as a constructive simulation of what might have happened, resembling the imagination of hypothetical scenarios.

I have banked this deliberately alongside Hartung et al. 2017, because the pair is the finding rather than either one alone. MECH-542's notes state that fact-versus-fiction framing produced distinct processing signatures in some experiments but that later work has not consistently reproduced broad differences. That is accurate, and this entry is the "some experiments" half. It is a small imaging study with interpretive activation mapping; the large behavioural replication found nothing.

The two do not settle each other, and it would be a mistake to treat the larger study as simply overturning the smaller one, because they measure different things. Reading-time behaviour and blood-flow patterns during comprehension are not the same dependent variable, and it is entirely coherent for a source label to change internal processing without changing immersion ratings or recall. But that coherence has a consequence REE should take seriously: if the effect lives in internal processing and not in behaviour, then MECH-542's eventual assay has to measure internal update directly. An assay that looked only at downstream behaviour would reproduce the null and read it as a refutation.

What this study does not do is distinguish selective permeability from mode-switching. It shows a difference by source frame; it says nothing about WHICH variables were authorised to update, which is the whole content of MECH-542. Mixed at 0.48, scored low on purpose so it cannot carry the claim by itself.
