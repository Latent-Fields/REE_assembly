# Disinhibition Through Diffusion of Responsibility and Dehumanization (Bandura, Underwood & Fromson, 1975)

MECH-012's boldest sentence is the first one: in collective systems, language becomes the *primary*
medium through which ethical residue is managed or displaced. Primary is a ranking word, and a
ranking claim needs a comparison. Most of the relevant literature does not supply one -- it
manipulates responsibility structure, or it manipulates framing, but rarely both against each other.
This 1975 study is the exception I was able to find, and it is the reason the entry sits here despite
its age.

Students were given the opportunity to punish another group with electric shock, ostensibly to help
train them. Two things varied. Responsibility was either personalized or diffused across the group.
And the target group was characterised, in a remark the participants merely overheard, as
"perceptive, understanding", or neutrally, or as "animalistic, rotten". No authority pressure was
applied and no anonymity was induced; the design deliberately manipulated nothing but the actors'
perception of the people they were about to hurt. Both factors disinhibited aggression, and the
linguistic one was the stronger of the two. The overheard adjective outweighed the accountability
structure.

The translation into REE is unusually clean, and it changes what one would build. If the symbolic act
worked by arguing the agent out of an already-registered residue, it would have to fight that residue
case by case, and one would expect structure -- clear personal accountability -- to dominate, because
structure is what determines whether the argument ever has to be made. What the result suggests
instead is that the label operates on the target's standing to generate residue at all. That is a
much cheaper operation and a much more portable one: it is applied once, to a category, and it
carries to every subsequent encounter with any member of that category. This also predicts something
uncomfortable about mitigation. A REE safeguard that protects the agent's own residue channel --
insisting the agent registers that they acted, that the choice was theirs -- addresses the
responsibility limb and leaves this one entirely untouched, because the operation was never performed
on the agent.

Two further details deserve to survive into the claim. The humanized characterisation produced
*more* restraint than neutral, which means the channel runs both ways: whatever mechanism lets an
institution deny residue is the same mechanism by which it can absorb residue on a member's behalf.
MECH-012 names absorption and denial as separate institutional options, and this is the one design I
found that exhibits both as signed movements along a single axis. And punitiveness escalated across
trials in the disinhibited conditions rather than settling at a fixed offset -- a symbolic act sets a
trajectory, not a level. Any REE monitor that samples residue once after a reframing will read the
first point of a ramp and record it as the effect size.

Now the honesty. This is a fifty-year-old paper from a research culture with no preregistration, with
the sample sizes and analytic conventions of its time, in a shock-escalation paradigm no ethics
committee would approve today. I could not obtain the full text -- both the publisher and the
open-access mirror refused -- so I am relying on the secondary literature's account of the design and
I have quoted no statistics, because I could not verify any. I found no direct replication, though
the finding is heavily cited and the broader moral-disengagement programme it founded has been
meta-analysed (Ogunfowora et al. 2022, this directory). There is also a real gap between what was
measured and what MECH-012 is about: the dependent variable is harm *delivered*, not residue
*managed*. Greater willingness to inflict harm is consistent with attenuated residue and is not a
measurement of it; no affective or physiological index was taken. I have set source_quality at 0.55
and mapping_fidelity at 0.88, and let the aggregate sit at 0.68 -- which is my way of saying that
this is the right experiment, run too long ago, and that if REE ever acquires the substrate to test
MECH-012 directly, this is the design to modernise first.
