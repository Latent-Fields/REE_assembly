# Why the avoidance-cost readout is a real dependent variable and not a restatement

Wong and Pittig ran a fear-acquisition procedure followed by an avoidance test in which avoiding
carried an explicit cost. Fear acquired to the conditioned stimulus generalised to novel stimuli that
belonged to the same *conceptual category* as the threat -- not to items from a fear-irrelevant
category -- and participants then avoided those generalised stimuli despite paying for it. A further
finding matters for interpretation: reductions in costly avoidance tracked reductions in
unconditioned-stimulus expectancy, which the authors read as evidence that behavioural approach can
itself drive extinction learning.

I have filed this entry with `evidence_direction: unknown` on purpose, and the reason should be
explicit so that a governance reader does not mis-weight it. This paper does not tell us whether
MECH-028 is true. It says nothing about cognitive modes, nothing about transitions between them, and
nothing about whether learning is preserved across a state boundary. It is here because MECH-028's
CONFIRMING criterion commits to two readouts, and the second of them -- the avoidance cost, defined
as approach-suppression of non-harm resources in the post-transition mode -- needs a warrant that it
is a construct at all.

That warrant is what this paper supplies. Over-generalised avoidance is separately measurable; it
imposes real, quantifiable cost that subjects willingly pay; and its magnitude dissociates from the
fear response. The dissociation is the load-bearing part. MECH-028 predicts that the (B) mode-locked
arm will show *either* harm recurrence (it cannot consolidate what it learned) *or* a rise in
avoidance cost (a hypervigilance lock), and that disjunction is only meaningful if the two can come
apart. Wong and Pittig show that in humans they do. Without something like this, one could reasonably
object that avoidance cost is just harm learning measured with a different sign, and the (B) arm's
two predicted deficits would collapse into one.

There is a condition attached, and it is a design condition rather than a caveat about the paper.
The generalisation here runs along conceptual category membership. REE's avoidance-cost readout
presumes that the substrate's non-harm resources sit on some gradient along which avoidance can
over-spread. If V3's resources carry no such structure -- if they are perceptually and categorically
unrelated to the hazards -- then the readout has nothing to generalise along and will read flat for
a reason entirely unconnected to mode transitions. That is a degeneracy the claim's precondition
section does not currently name, and it is worth adding before the run. Confidence 0.58, reflecting
a solid methodological warrant and an honest mid-range mapping to a dependent variable rather than
to the claim.
