# Sleep and the generalization of fear learning (null result)

## What the paper did

Davidson, Carlsson, Jonsson and Johansson asked the question MECH-018's second readout asks, in the
cleanest form available to a human paradigm. Participants watched a random sequence of a small and a
large circle, one of them paired with an aversive sound. After a delay containing either a nap or
quiet wakefulness, they saw the two original circles again together with eight novel circles graded
in size between them. The generalisation gradient behaved as it should: fear responses scaled with
similarity to the conditioned stimulus. But the gradient was no steeper after sleep than after wake.
There was no difference in the degree of generalisation between the two groups.

## Why this entry is here

I have written three supporting entries in this directory, and it would be easy to leave this one
out. It is a null, it is small, and it is a nap study. I think omitting it would be the wrong call,
because of where it falls.

MECH-018 has four readouts, but they are not equally decisive. Preservation is close to definitional
for a field whose invariant is that it cannot be erased. Compression and traversability can both be
satisfied by uniform decay, which is why the claim carries a naive-decay comparator at all. The
readout that actually distinguishes integration from every cheaper alternative is (ii), the
specificity ratio -- and readout (ii) is the one the literature disagrees about. Zhang et al. (2024)
find fear over-generalising when sleep is withheld. Lerner et al. (2021) find REM improving
discrimination while degrading recall. Davidson et al. find nothing at all. Three competent groups,
three answers, on the readout that decides the claim.

The specific value of this entry is that it makes MECH-018's third falsifying branch concrete. The
claim pre-registers that the operation might be "geometrically inert -- no change on any of the four
readouts while the cycle is live". That branch usually reads like a formality, the null nobody
expects. This paper is a human observation of precisely that null, in the paradigm closest to the
readout's geometry. The inert outcome is not a formality; it has happened once already.

## How much weight it should carry

Not a great deal, and I want to be honest about the reasons. A single null with a modest sample is
weak evidence of absence -- it says the effect is not robust across designs, not that it is absent.
The delay was a nap, and every mechanism the supporting entries in this directory invoke is
REM-mediated; a nap is REM-poor, so "a nap does not sharpen fear specificity" is a much weaker
statement than "sleep does not", and this design cannot separate them.

There is a subtler point too. The paradigm varies one perceptual dimension -- circle size -- with no
context manipulation anywhere in it. MECH-018 does not claim sleep narrows a kernel along a
perceptual axis. It claims residue becomes tied to CONTEXT, so that harm learned in one setting stops
pricing structurally similar but contextually distinct settings. Davidson et al. may be testing the
easier and less interesting half of the readout and correctly finding nothing there. If so, the
lesson for the REE experiment is a design lesson: the specificity ratio should be computed against
context-matched non-harm locations, not merely against z_world-distance-matched ones, or the REE run
risks reproducing this null for the same reason.

## Confidence

0.52, direction weakens. Modest weight, deliberately. Its function in the governance record is not
to argue MECH-018 is false but to stop the three supporting entries from reading as a consensus that
does not exist, and to put the inert-outcome branch of the claim's own falsification criteria on the
record as an observed possibility rather than a hypothetical one.
