# Dissecting components of reward: 'liking', 'wanting', and learning

Berridge, Robinson & Aldridge (2009), *Current Opinion in Pharmacology* 9(1):65-73.
DOI [10.1016/j.coph.2008.12.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.coph.2008.12.014) - PMID 19162544 - PMC2756052.
Retrieved via PubMed.

## What the paper does

This is the canonical short statement of a decomposition that has shaped affective neuroscience for
two decades: reward is not one thing. It separates into at least three components that can be pulled
apart experimentally -- `liking` (hedonic impact, the felt pleasantness of a reward, measured in
rodents by stereotyped orofacial affective reactions), `wanting` (incentive salience, the
motivational pull a reward or its cue exerts), and learning (the predictive associations and
expectancies, including prediction-error-driven association formation, that link cues to outcomes).
Each has a partly distinct substrate: hedonic impact is generated by anatomically tiny opioid and
endocannabinoid hotspots in the nucleus accumbens shell and ventral pallidum; incentive salience is
mediated by the mesolimbic dopamine system and is distributed across it; associative learning is
carried by prediction-error machinery that is, again, not the same as either.

The important methodological point for our purposes is *how* the dissociations were established.
Not by correlating the three and observing that the correlation is imperfect -- by intervening.
Microinject an opioid agonist into the hedonic hotspot and `liking` rises without a matched rise in
`wanting`. Sensitise the mesolimbic dopamine system and `wanting` rises while `liking` stays flat or
blunts. That is the shape of evidence MECH-055's CONFIRMING clause asks for, and it is worth
noticing that the field arrived at the same methodological requirement independently.

## How it bears on MECH-055

MECH-055 asserts that hedonic tone, valence, and signed prediction error stay distinct rather than
collapsing into one affect scalar, and its NON-DEGENERACY PRECONDITION (b) demands that the VALENCE
vector's components be independently computable *and independently perturbable*. This paper is the
biological precedent that such a demand is satisfiable and non-trivial: `liking` and `wanting` map
almost directly onto REE's `VALENCE_LIKING` (index 1) and `VALENCE_WANTING` (index 0) in
`ree_core/residue/field.py`, and the learning component maps onto the prediction-error side that
MECH-055 calls axis 3. Two of the claim's three pairwise separations therefore have a strong
biological warrant.

The third does not, and the gap is worth being explicit about rather than papering over. REE's
axis 1 is mu/kappa -- a softmax-temperature and commitment-threshold regulator, a control-plane
inertia term. Berridge's `liking` is not that; it is REE's axis 2. The claim's own NAMING NOTE
concedes this, and it means this entry supplies evidence for the axis-2/axis-3 separation and for
axis 2's internal structure, and no evidence at all about either separation involving axis 1. Two
of three is the honest accounting.

## Where it cuts against the claim

Berridge et al. are clear that the three components *normally operate together* and dissociate only
under targeted intervention. That is a real problem for a test design that looks for non-degenerate
variance during ordinary REE operation: an architecture as separable as the mammalian reward system
could still show tightly coupled channels in a free-running rollout, and a null result from such a
test would be uninformative rather than falsifying.

Two further asymmetries deserve recording. The biological `liking` system is fragile and tiny --
cubic-millimetre hotspots whose disruption abolishes hedonic enhancement while leaving motivation
intact -- whereas `wanting` is system-wide. REE gives the two adjacent, equal-footing slots in a
single six-dimensional vector, which is simply not the architecture this literature describes. And
the `learning` component here is associative prediction error in general; it is never decomposed
into harm and benefit sides. So this paper gives no support whatever to the harm/benefit duality
MECH-055 requires inside axis 3 -- which is exactly the half MECH-054's 2026-08-08 finding records
as architecturally absent from REE.
