# Tang, Mei, Harvey et al. 2026 -- Goal-directed hippocampal theta sweeps during memory-guided navigation

Tang and colleagues, using large-scale hippocampal and prefrontal-cortex recordings in rats
navigating toward memorised goals in an open field, distinguished two kinds of within-theta-cycle
place-cell sequences: a stereotypic left-right alternating sweep (present regardless of learning)
and a second, learning-dependent kind that predicts the animal's upcoming trajectory toward a
remembered goal. The paper proposes a circuit mechanism for the second kind: a subpopulation of
CA1 cells that encode goal direction egocentrically (relative to the animal, not in absolute
coordinates), combined with a reduction in feedback inhibition, as the basis for generating these
goal-directed sweeps. The finding I found most load-bearing for REE's purposes is that these
goal-directed theta sweeps are coordinated with prefrontal cortex activity and preferentially
replayed during sharp-wave ripples (SWR) -- i.e. the same content that sweeps forward during
active theta-cycle navigation is the content preferentially selected for offline SWR replay.

This is, of the four papers in this lit-pull, the one that speaks most directly to MECH-289's
specific architectural claim: that the hippocampal trajectory generator is a single substrate
operating in two modes REE already names -- "online (waking, theta-analog)" and "quiescent
(offline, SWR-analog)." MECH-289's own registration note already draws that theta/SWR distinction
from other literature (Gupta et al. 2010); Tang et al. is new evidence that these two modes are
not just analogous but mechanistically coupled -- the theta-sweep content and the SWR-replay
content are the same learned, goal-directed material, generated by (or at least gated by) the
same CA1 subpopulation. It also independently corroborates MECH-236's goal-conditioning premise
via the egocentric-goal-direction cell finding, complementing Yu et al.'s dissociation-design
evidence for the same underlying premise from a different angle.

One nuance worth stating rather than flattening: this paper is explicit that goal-directed sweeps
are a *learned*, distinct subpopulation, not a property of hippocampal sequence generation in
general. MECH-289's own anti-recency claim (grounded in Gupta 2010) is about *which* trajectories
get proposed once the generative apparatus exists; this paper adds that whether a sweep is
goal-directed AT ALL depends on learning history. Neither claim contradicts the other, but they
should not be conflated into a single undifferentiated "hippocampal sweeps are goal-directed"
statement.

As with the other entries in this cluster, this paper says nothing about SD-098's actual
claim (relational vs. stored node-type status for goal/subgoal), and nothing about whether the
mechanism generalises beyond physical spatial navigation to REE's abstract action-object or
possibility-topology space -- both remain open architectural questions, not resolved by this
evidence.
