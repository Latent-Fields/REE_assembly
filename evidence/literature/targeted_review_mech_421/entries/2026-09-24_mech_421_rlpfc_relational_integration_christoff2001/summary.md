Christoff and colleagues scanned adults solving problems adapted from Raven's Progressive Matrices that required tracking zero, one or two relations at once. Prefrontal activation appeared only in the contrast between two-relation and one-relation problems, not between one and zero. Bilateral rostrolateral PFC (lateral area 10) and right DLPFC carried the effect, and left RLPFC stayed selective for two-relation problems even after trials were matched for response time and accuracy.

For MECH-421 this is grounding for the operation underneath structure-mapping: holding several relations at once and aligning them is processed as its own load, distinct from retrieving or applying a single relation. The claim's "map relational structure regardless of surface features" needs exactly that step, sitting above single-relation inference (MECH-419/MECH-420).

It does not reach far. The task is within-domain matrix completion, not cross-domain analogy, and the authors themselves suggest RLPFC may be about manipulating self-generated information in general. So it does not tell REE whether analogy needs a dedicated operator (Q-074); a traversal account could use the same integration resource.

Confidence 0.52: a solid, replicated anchor for relational integration as a component process, with limited reach into the specific cross-domain mapping claim.
