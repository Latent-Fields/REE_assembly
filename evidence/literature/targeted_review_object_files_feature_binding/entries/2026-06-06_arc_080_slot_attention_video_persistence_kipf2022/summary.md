# SAVi (Kipf et al. 2022) — slots that persist, predicted forward by a transition model

**Paper.** Thomas Kipf et al., *Conditional Object-Centric Learning from Video* (SAVi), ICLR 2022 (arXiv:2111.12594). This is the temporal extension of the Slot Attention idea. Where the original (Locatello 2020) discovers objects in a single image, SAVi maintains a set of slots *across frames*: each step, a learned transition module predicts the slots forward, and a corrector re-binds them to the new frame. Optionally the slots are conditioned on a first-frame cue (a coordinate or hint at where objects are), and the whole thing is weakly supervised by optical flow rather than labels. The upshot is that each slot tracks one object *as the same entity* over time — discovery plus persistence, not just discovery.

**Why this is the most REE-shaped paper in the set.** The object-binding design fork ends with an observation worth making concrete here: the architecture SAVi describes is *slots + a forward model that predicts them* — and REE already has the forward model. E2 is exactly a transition/predictor module. So "build a token-keyed object-file layer and let E2 predict the slots forward" is not a new paradigm REE would have to invent; it is a SAVi-shaped construction using a component REE already runs. That matters for the decision procedure's step 1 (build the slot layer): it tells us the slot predictor is plausibly E2, not a bespoke module, which lowers the cost and the architectural risk of the whole object-file step.

**What it grounds for ARC-080 and MECH-045.** Two things the single-frame slot literature could not. First, **persistence**: SAVi slots survive frame to frame as the same object-token — the object-file-buffer property MECH-045 asserts and the beginning of ARC-080 Pillar 1 (object permanence). Second, it shows persistence is *learnable end-to-end* with a transition model rather than hand-built tracking. Together with the Locatello entry (single-frame binding) and the Lisman-Jensen entry (capacity), the directory now spans the three jobs the design fork separates: addressing, binding, and persistence/capacity.

**The seams, stated plainly.** SAVi does not get persistence for free. It leans on a weak-supervision scaffold — optical flow as the prediction target and a first-frame conditioning cue to seed the slots. Strip that away and unsupervised temporal binding degrades. REE has no optical flow and no pixels; its `z_object` is value-coupled (SD-049/SD-057 drive and goal) and reconstruction-free. So importing SAVi means finding a REE-native analog of the conditioning/seeding signal — plausibly drive/goal salience seeding a slot — and a REE-native substitute for the flow/reconstruction objective that makes slots specialise. And the persistence SAVi demonstrates is across *visible* tracking; full *through-occlusion* permanence (the hard core of Pillar 1) is only partially addressed. I have therefore tagged this to ARC-080 and MECH-045 at the level of *buildability and REE-shape* and held confidence to 0.71: a strong, reproduced existence proof (high source quality), with transfer risk raised by the weak-supervision scaffold and the same reconstruction-vs-value mismatch the Locatello entry flagged.
