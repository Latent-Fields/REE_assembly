# Beshara & Murphy (2016) -- serotonin is instructive, not merely permissive, but in the opposite direction

According to PubMed ([DOI 10.1523/ENEURO.0126-15.2015](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0126-15.2015), PMID 26730408), Beshara, Beston, Pinto and Murphy asked what fluoxetine actually does to the synaptic machinery when it reopens ocular-dominance plasticity in adult rat visual cortex. The intuitive story -- that raising serotonin rejuvenates the cortex back into a juvenile critical-period state -- turned out to be wrong. Fluoxetine did not shift the balance of glutamatergic and GABAergic synaptic proteins toward juvenile forms. It pushed them toward the MORE mature subunits (GluN2A, GABAA-alpha1) and, when paired with monocular deprivation, normalised the glutamatergic gain in a neuroprotective way. The plastic state fluoxetine creates is a novel one, not a replayed one.

## Why this speaks to the MECH-204 conflict

MECH-204's non-trivial move is the word "actively". It claims 5-HT withdrawal during REM does not merely PERMIT precision recalibration -- it CALIBRATES it, by removing the benefit gradient and thereby defining a neutral reference. The permissive alternative -- withdrawal just lifts a brake and recalibration proceeds on its own terms -- is the competing reading, and that instructive-vs-permissive distinction is the heart of the live conflict on this claim.

This paper is the clearest evidence I found that serotonergic state is instructive rather than permissive in cortical plasticity. Serotonin manipulation here does not toggle a gate; it determines the character of the resulting synaptic regime. That is precisely the half of the MECH-204 argument that is hardest to defend on first principles, and this result supports it.

## Why it is only a mixed, low-confidence entry

The honesty problem is direction. In Beshara & Murphy it is ELEVATED serotonin that instructs and enables plasticity. MECH-204 says it is serotonin WITHDRAWAL that instructs. Taken at face value across systems, that is a directional tension, not an endorsement. And the systems really are different: chronic, structural, developmental ocular-dominance plasticity in a primary sensory cortex is not obviously the same beast as phasic, online, precision-prior recalibration during REM. So the instructive-not-permissive principle transfers; the directional sign does not, and might even cut against the claim.

I have scored this mixed at 0.48, with mapping fidelity deliberately low (0.40) and transfer risk high (0.60). The point of including it is not to move MECH-204's confidence much -- it is to put the actual conflict on the record with a paper that engages it, so that the experimental probe the backlog is asking for (does 5-HT withdrawal instruct, and in which direction?) is designed against the strongest contrary framing rather than around it.
