# Fornal, Auerbach & Jacobs (1985) -- serotonergic raphe neurons fall near-silent in REM

According to PubMed ([DOI 10.1016/0014-4886(85)90074-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-4886(85)90074-3), PMID 3996511), Fornal, Auerbach and Jacobs recorded pharmacologically-identified serotonergic neurons in nucleus raphe magnus of freely-moving cats and measured their discharge across the sleep-wake cycle. The numbers are the useful part: 4.49 spikes/s in active waking, 2.14 in slow-wave sleep, and 0.20 in REM -- a roughly 95% collapse of serotonergic output at REM entry.

## Why it is here

MECH-204 asserts that tonic 5-HT withdrawal during REM does real computational work -- it sets the zero-point against which precision recalibration happens. That claim only gets off the ground if the withdrawal is deep and reliable, not a modest dip. This paper supplies an independent quantitative floor for that premise. It matters that it comes from the Jacobs laboratory rather than the Hobson/Friston lineage that supplies most of the existing entries in this review directory: the near-total REM-off pattern is not an artifact of one recording tradition, it is a pan-raphe rule that shows up in a caudal nucleus recorded by a different group with a different agenda.

## What it does not do

Two honest limits. First, nucleus raphe magnus is a bulbospinal, antinociceptive serotonergic nucleus -- it is not the dorsal raphe that actually projects to the forebrain circuits carrying precision priors. So this is corroboration that "serotonergic raphe cells go quiet in REM" is general, not direct evidence about the specific tone that MECH-204's zero-point reads. The dorsal-raphe-specific work (Sakai & Crochet 2001) already sits in this directory and carries that load.

Second, and more important for the live conflict on this claim: establishing THAT 5-HT withdraws in REM says nothing about WHETHER that withdrawal actively calibrates a neutral reference (the MECH-204 reading) or merely lifts a brake that permits recalibration to proceed (the permissive alternative). This paper is a substrate fact. It moves the premise, not the disputed mechanism. I have scored it a clean support at 0.70 -- strong on source quality, moderate on mapping fidelity precisely because it grounds the premise rather than adjudicating the question the experimental backlog on MECH-204 is actually trying to settle.
