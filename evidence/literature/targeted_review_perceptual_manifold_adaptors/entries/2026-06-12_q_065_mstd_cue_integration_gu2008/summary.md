# Gu, Angelaki & DeAngelis (2008) — Neural correlates of multisensory cue integration in macaque MSTd

*According to PubMed.* Gu, Y., Angelaki, D. E., & DeAngelis, G. C. (2008). *Nature Neuroscience*, 11(10), 1201–1210. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2191)

**Claim tested:** Q-065 (per-sense adaptor metric origin + cross-modal negotiability) — **L3 layer** of PA-7 (multisensory convergence currency). Complements, rather than duplicates, the STS entries already held under `targeted_review_mech_103_multisensory` (Nath & Beauchamp 2011, Venezia 2017, Zhang 2025): this is the dorsal-stream optic-flow / vestibular case, and it speaks to Q-065's *negotiability* question directly.

## What the paper did

Gu and colleagues trained macaques to discriminate their direction of self-motion (heading) using two cues: a visual optic-flow field and a vestibular (inertial-motion) signal. Behaviourally, the animals combined the two cues *near-optimally* — combined-cue thresholds approached the statistical ideal predicted by reliability-weighted (maximum-likelihood) integration, where each cue is weighted by its precision. They then recorded single neurons in area MSTd while the animals performed the task. Three findings define the paper. First, MSTd neurons combined visual and vestibular inputs by a roughly *linear* rule with *subadditive* weights. Second, the population split by tuning: "congruent" cells (same heading preference for visual and vestibular stimuli) showed combined-cue sensitivity *improvements* that paralleled the behavioural gain, whereas "opposite" cells (conflicting preferences) showed *degraded* combined sensitivity. Third, the responses of congruent cells were more tightly correlated with the animals' trial-by-trial perceptual choices than those of opposite cells — suggesting the brain preferentially reads out the congruent population during integration. Together this is among the cleanest demonstrations that statistically optimal multisensory integration has an identifiable neural substrate.

## Why it matters for the claim — and why the direction is "mixed"

Q-065 part (2) is the deepest open problem in the perceptual-adaptor cluster: how are heterogeneous sense-specific geometries — smell's gradient, sight's (non-Riemannian) colour and object manifolds, touch's contact/pressure geometry, proprioception's body-state geometry — made *mutually negotiable* inside one shared world model? The roadmap names this the unification problem that MECH-103's precision-weighted fusion *presupposes* but no claim specifies. Gu et al. supply the biological form of the **currency** half of an answer: *reliability / precision weighting is a real, neurally implemented mechanism* for trading off sensory channels, and congruent MSTd cells are a concrete candidate substrate for the precision-routing / negotiation layer the roadmap parks on ARC-005. That is genuine support, and it is why this paper belongs in the L3 layer.

But it also exposes — sharply — the part of Q-065 that optimal-integration neuroscience does *not* solve, which is why I have set `evidence_direction: mixed` rather than `supports`. The two cues here are **redundant estimates of the same physical variable** (heading), already living in a shared one-dimensional estimation space. Reliability weighting can only operate once the cues are commensurable enough *to be weighted at all*. Q-065's hard content is precisely the step *before* that: mapping a gradient manifold and a non-Riemannian colour manifold into a common space in the first place. The paper solves the *weighting* problem and presupposes away the *unification* problem. So it simultaneously supports "a negotiation currency exists and is precision-shaped" and cautions "that result is demonstrated only for shared-estimand cues, not for structurally dissimilar manifolds" — which is exactly the boundary a V4 designer must not paper over.

## How it translates to REE

The usable import for the roadmap is twofold. (1) It biologically licenses precision/reliability as *the* currency MECH-103 fusion and ARC-005 routing should be built around — REE already commits to precision-weighting, and this is the canonical primate evidence that the brain does the same near-optimally. (2) It re-frames PA-5 (the cross-modal negotiation pillar) correctly: the open work is not "implement reliability weighting" (that part is well-grounded) but "specify the map from each heterogeneous sense-manifold into a commensurable space *so that* reliability weighting can apply." The congruent/opposite-cell dissociation is also a useful design hint — a negotiation layer may need explicitly congruent units, and conflicting-tuned units may actively *hurt* integration, which is a non-obvious constraint to carry into PA-5.

## Confidence reasoning

`mixed`, confidence **0.60**. Source quality is near-ceiling (0.90): *Nature Neuroscience*, single-unit recording tied to psychophysics with an explicit optimal-integration model and a reliability manipulation — about as rigorous as multisensory neurophysiology gets. The aggregate is pulled down by mapping fidelity (0.58): the shared-estimand setup is a genuinely narrower problem than Q-065's heterogeneous-manifold negotiation, and for a claim whose whole point is the *unification* step, that gap is load-bearing, not cosmetic. Transfer risk is moderate (0.40, macaque heading task → REE cross-modal currency). The "mixed" direction is the honest record: it advances the *currency-exists* half of Q-065 while leaving — and helping to sharpen — the *currency-for-dissimilar-manifolds* half that remains the open V4 problem.
