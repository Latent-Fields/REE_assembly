# Proops, McComb & Reby 2009 -- the same horse, seen then heard

A herd member walks past and disappears; a moment later a whinny plays from a hidden speaker. If it is that individual's call, the subject horse carries on. If it is a different familiar horse's call, it turns faster, looks longer and looks more often. The representation of "that individual" is therefore not modality-local: what was built from sight is checked against sound.

This is about as literal an instance of the IDENTITY candidate as comparative cognition offers. The relation that must survive a boundary crossing (visual subsystem to auditory subsystem) does survive it, in an ungulate. Taken with the magpie mirror result and the cleaner-wrasse mark test, the relation recurs across three lineages with different brains and bodies, which is the pattern GOV-CONTRACT-1 asks domain 4 to look for.

Two limits. The boundary here is sensory, and sensory cross-modality is a milder test than translation between engines with different representational geometries. And every positive species is social; individual recognition is an ecological problem those species share, so the embodied-ecological alternative has not been ruled out. The classification note says architectural-leaning, not architectural.
