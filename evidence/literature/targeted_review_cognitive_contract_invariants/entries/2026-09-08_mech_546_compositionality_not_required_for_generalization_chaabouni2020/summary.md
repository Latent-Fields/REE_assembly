# Chaabouni et al. 2020 -- structure is not forced by need

The intuition behind MECH-546 is that if two systems must transmit a relation, the channel will come to mark it. Chaabouni, Kharitonov, Bouchacourt, Dupoux and Baroni tested the nearest thing to that intuition that can be tested in a laboratory: do emergent languages that generalise to unseen combinations of attributes have to be compositional? They do not. Generalisation happens without compositionality, the degree of compositionality does not predict it, and where compositional structure does appear it is because it is easier for a new learner to pick up, so it survives transmission.

For MECH-546 this is a weakening result and I have logged it as such. The claim's own two-directional test says that a grammaticalised distinction lacking developmental, comparative or computational support should classify as cultural. Here the computational domain says that marked structure in a channel is explained by the iterated-learning bottleneck, Kirby, Cornish and Smith's mechanism, rather than by what the content requires. So the default reading of any marked distinction, in a natural or an emergent language, is cultural until another domain says otherwise. That is GOV-CONTRACT-1's rule restated from the artificial side.

For ARC-142 the bearing is mixed, and the mixed part is the useful part. A channel can carry the needed information without the relation being separately encoded. That is the translatable-around possibility the governance rule insists we preserve: an invariant may be recoverable at the receiver without ever having been marked in transit. MECH-545's within-boundary control exists to tell those two cases apart, and this paper is a reason it has to.

What the paper does not do is test any of the five candidates. Its objects are attribute-value tuples, not identities, times, provenances, agents or confidences. It is evidence about the logic of the inference, not about the invariants.

Confidence 0.66, weakens MECH-546, mixed for ARC-142. No claim status changes.
