# The case against economic values in the orbitofrontal cortex (Hayden & Niv, 2021)

**Claim tested:** MECH-035 -- VALENCE is vector-valued and ranked without scalar collapse.
**Direction:** mixed | **Confidence:** 0.60

## What the paper does

Hayden and Niv take aim at what they regard as neuroeconomics' founding assumption: that the brain
computes economic value as a distinct, represented quantity, and that orbitofrontal cortex is where
it lives. Their method is deflationary reanalysis rather than new data. They work through the classes
of evidence usually offered -- offer-value neurons, chosen-value neurons, value correlates in human
vmPFC fMRI -- and argue in each case that the observed correlation is explicable without value being
the encoded variable. Attention, salience, motor preparation, and above all the agent's *policy* all
covary with value in the standard tasks, and a neuron tracking any of them looks like a value neuron
from the outside. Their positive proposal is that animals learn action policies directly, and that
the value-shaped correlates are shadows of the policy rather than inputs to it.

## What it says about MECH-035

MECH-035 has two halves, and this paper lands hard on one and awkwardly on the other. That is why I
have filed it mixed rather than as support, despite it being the paper a partisan reading would want
most.

The half it supports: MECH-035's denial of scalar collapse. The strongest objection to REE's
architecture here would be "the brain solved this by computing a common currency, so an architecture
that refuses to is departing from biology for no reason". Hayden and Niv make that objection expensive
to state. If the common-currency signal is not established even in its home region -- and they argue
it is not -- then declining to compute one is declining to import a contested claim, not departing
from a settled one.

The half it complicates: MECH-035's positive posit. The claim is not merely that no scalar exists; it
is that VALENCE *is* a vector of predicted deltas across named streams, and that E3 (ARC-003) ranks
candidates by dominance or lexicographic comparison over that vector. Hayden and Niv's alternative
does not deliver a vector where the scalar was. It removes the ranking stage. Under direct policy
learning there is no valence readout of any arity for a comparison rule to operate over. So the paper
agrees with REE about what is absent and disagrees about what is present.

## Limitations and what could go wrong

The deflationary argument is not selective, and REE should expect it to be turned on REE. Their core
move -- a quantity correlating with value need not be value -- applies directly to MECH-035's
non-degeneracy precondition, which requires the named component streams to be "independently
computable per candidate trajectory". Showing that an internal REE quantity correlates with, say,
HARM across candidates does not establish that HARM is the represented variable rather than something
that covaries with it in the scenario. That is a design burden on MECH-035's first experiment, and it
comes from this paper's own logic.

It also needs saying that this is one side of a live controversy, not the field's verdict. Padoa-
Schioppa, Glimcher and Rustichini have well-resourced positions on the other side; the Levy and
Glimcher entry in this same directory is that position, and it is not the weaker paper. Quoting
Hayden and Niv as settled neuroscience would be the mis-citation to guard against here.

## Confidence reasoning

Source quality 0.82: two senior investigators, peer-reviewed in Behavioral Neuroscience, widely
cited, and honest about its own status as an argued position. Mapping fidelity 0.55 is what holds the
aggregate down to 0.60 -- the paper's positive proposal is orthogonal to, and arguably incompatible
with, the mechanism MECH-035 asserts, so half its content pushes the claim and half undercuts its
subject. Transfer risk 0.45 covers two separate exposures: the brain-to-artificial-substrate analogy,
and the risk of a future REE document citing a contested position as though it were consensus.
