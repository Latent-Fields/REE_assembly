# Amat et al. 2005 -- mPFC control detector gates the DRN default (ARC-079)

**What they did.** Amat and colleagues used the classic yoked escapable/inescapable tailshock design in rats and inactivated the ventral medial prefrontal cortex (infralimbic and prelimbic) with muscimol during the stressor. They then looked at dorsal raphe serotonergic activation and at the familiar downstream consequences of uncontrollable stress.

**What they found.** With mPFCv silenced, a *controllable* stressor behaved like an uncontrollable one: DRN activation and the behavioural sequelae returned. Control protects only if the prefrontal detector registers it. The authors draw the conceptual conclusion explicitly. Stress-induced brainstem activity is not *caused* by a lack of control. It is the default, and the presence of detected control *inhibits* it.

**Why this matters for ARC-079.** ARC-079 leans on Maier & Seligman 2016 for its inversion: disengagement is the default and persistence is gated. That review is secondary. This paper is the primary causal result it rests on, so it belongs under ARC-079 directly. The topology matches closely. There is a separable detector (MECH-340's PersistenceAppraisal), a default that runs whenever the detector is silent, and an inhibitory relationship rather than a failure tally driving the default. Is it enough that the *shape* matches? For an architecture hypothesis, shape is most of what literature can give.

**Where the mapping strains.** The "default" here is not a quiet, adaptive letting-go. It is a stress sequela: exaggerated fear and worse later escape. ARC-079 treats disengagement as the *safe* default and places pathology at the two failure modes of the gate. Amat 2005 cannot tell us whether the ungated state is benign. It only tells us that it is ungated. The paradigm is also aversive and immediate, whereas ARC-079 concerns the slow fate of a goal trace in a hippocampal bank.

**Confidence.** 0.62. Strong source, the right causal architecture, and a moderate but real translation gap from shock sequelae to goal-trace re-probing.
