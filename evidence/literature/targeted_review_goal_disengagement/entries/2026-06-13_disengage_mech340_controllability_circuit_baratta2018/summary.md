# The control gate is a separable circuit, present-but-silent (Baratta et al. 2018)

**Claim under test:** MECH-340 — the persistence/efficacy gate. A control/efficacy-detection signal maintains an unresolved-goal bank entry as an active re-probe target (MECH-293); its *absence* is the default (the entry stops being re-probed and decays in MECH-292 rank, with the SD-039 trace preserved, not erased). The gate is held ON by a controllability/efficacy appraisal — explicitly *not* by accumulated failure, repeated suppression, bare effort-cost, or staleness. The claim's evidence note (citing Koster 2011, Gillan 2016/2021) flags that the gate must be **explicit and separable** from the default it overrides. This paper is about that separability.

## What the paper did

Baratta and colleagues exploit the classic stressor-controllability design: two animals receive *identical* tailshock, but one can terminate it (escapable control) while the other is yoked to it (inescapable, no control). In male rats the well-charted result is that behavioural control abolishes the stressor's sequelae — no escape deficit, no exaggerated fear, no dorsal-raphe serotonergic activation — and it does so through a prelimbic (medial prefrontal) circuit that projects to and inhibits the dorsal raphe nucleus. The new finding is a **dissociation in females**: female rats successfully acquire the controlling response, and the prelimbic→DRN circuitry is anatomically present, yet behavioural control fails to *engage* the prelimbic cells that project to the dorsal raphe, and the stress-buffering effect of control is absent. Critically, **pharmacological activation of the prelimbic cortex restores** the buffering. So the protective gate is present, can fail to be recruited, and can be switched back on causally.

## Why this is the right kind of evidence for MECH-340

Maier & Seligman (2016), already in this review folder, established the *polarity*: disengagement is the unlearned default and control-detection is the gated override. What that canonical paper does not, on its own, settle is whether the override is a **distinct, separable mechanism** or just an emergent property of how much control an animal happens to have. MECH-340 commits to the former — an explicit per-entry persistence gate that reads a control/efficacy appraisal and can be ON or OFF independently of the goal's history. Baratta et al. is, to my reading, the cleanest causal demonstration of exactly that separability. The fact of control is held constant (females acquire the response, the circuit exists), and yet the protective gate either engages or does not, with the difference attributable to recruitment of one specific cortical projection — and reversible by directly activating it. That is "the gate is a separate thing that can be present-but-silent," which is precisely the property the claim's design needs and which a history-keyed abandon-trigger would not have.

It also sharpens MECH-340's *hard negatives*. Because identical control with successful acquisition produces opposite outcomes depending on circuit engagement, the gate cannot be keyed on attained-control-as-fact; it must read detected-control-as-signal. That rules out, on the same evidence, gating on accumulated success counts or objective task controllability — and by extension the failure/effort/staleness signals MECH-340 already excludes by API shape (the gate reads only the appraisal struct).

## Caveats and the boundary it draws

This is rat aversive-stressor neuroscience — escape deficits, social exploration, dorsal-raphe 5-HT — not an agent re-probing a stored goal. What transfers is the *circuit logic* (a separable control-detection gate over a default), not the behaviour, and the import is therefore a structural analogy with genuine transfer risk. The female-specific finding is itself a boundary as much as a confirmation: it shows gate-presence does not entail gate-engagement, and the REE analogue of *what determines engagement* is unspecified. I am using the sex dissociation only as evidence that presence ≠ firing, not as any claim about sex per se.
