# Empowerment, consolidated (Salge, Glackin & Polani 2014)

## What the paper did

This is the long review chapter that gathers a decade of empowerment work into one place. Empowerment is restated as the channel capacity between an agent's actions and its subsequent sensor states -- the maximal mutual information an agent can push from "what I do now" into "what I will be able to perceive later." The chapter's contribution is breadth rather than novelty: it walks through how the *same* information-theoretic quantity, applied across different sensorimotor configurations and embodiments, reliably produces behavior that seeks controllable, option-rich regions of state space and avoids dead ends. It also raises, fairly explicitly, the reading of empowerment as a safety-flavored "keep your options open" heuristic.

## Why it is relevant to MECH-454

For MECH-454 this review does the same load-bearing job as the Klyubin origin paper but with more reassurance attached. MECH-454 wants E3 to carry a cost for foreclosing the agent's *own* reachable-option space -- the self-directed, non-harm analog of INV-026's foreclosure-of-others. The fact that empowerment recovers option-preserving behavior across many embodiments matters here: it suggests that "preserve your reachable futures" is not a quirk of one toy model but a substrate-general signal one could plausibly fold into E3 as a term. So the review strengthens the *representation/magnitude* half of the claim.

## The honest limit of the mapping

It strengthens exactly and only that half. Like the Klyubin paper, this review never conditions option-preservation on forecast uncertainty. Empowerment is weighed the same whether the agent is confidently right or blindly guessing. MECH-454's whole point -- generalizing DR-12's prediction-error-conditioned E3 confidence -- is that the foreclosure cost should *appear under uncertainty and dissolve under confidence*. None of that is here. Worse, the review's "keep options open" safety framing is about broad environmental controllability, which is the reading most likely to collapse into resisting *any* irreversible change -- the over-preservation / chronic-indecision pole MECH-454 explicitly flags as the failure of an un-gated cost.

## Confidence reasoning

I set confidence at 0.60, just below the Klyubin origin entry, and the reason is deliberate: these two empowerment sources anchor the *same* half of MECH-454, so I do not want two entries silently double-counting one component into apparent strength. This review earns its place by adding breadth and robustness -- the option-preservation signal is general, not a single-model artifact -- not by adding the missing uncertainty-gate. As with the rest of this pull, the components of MECH-454 are well-supported in the literature; the synthesis is REE-novel.
