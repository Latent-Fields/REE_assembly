# Empathy: Its ultimate and proximate bases (Preston & de Waal 2002)

## What the paper did

This is the target article -- published in *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* with extensive peer
commentary -- that gave the field its unifying account of empathy: the Perception-Action Model
(PAM). The proximate claim is mechanistically specific. Perceiving another individual's state
automatically activates, in the observer, the observer's *own* representations of that state, and
those representations in turn drive somatic, autonomic and motivational responses. Empathy is not a
special faculty bolted onto cognition; it is what falls out of the fact that perceiving a state and
being in that state share a representational substrate. Preston and de Waal argue this single
mechanism, modulated by similarity, familiarity, prior experience and salience and regulated by
prefrontal cortex, scales from emotional contagion and mother-infant responsiveness up through
sympathetic concern and targeted helping -- and that it explains the graded distribution of empathy
across species, developmental stages and individuals, as well as a range of empathy disorders.

## Why it matters for MECH-164

If Singer et al. (2004) are the *imaging* evidence that another's harm is represented on the self's
valuation substrate, Preston and de Waal are the *theory* of why that is so -- and the theory is, in
effect, MECH-164's proximate mechanism stated in the empathy literature's own vocabulary. MECH-164
says care emerges when another agent's inferred terrain is weighted with self-like motivational
force in ordinary E3 selection, with no separate ethics circuit. PAM says: the other's state is
represented on the self's substrate (shared representation), and motivated responding -- helping,
care -- follows from that shared activation. The two statements are the same claim at different
levels of description. This is strong conceptual grounding for the architectural commitment that
care does not require a dedicated module (INV-001): in the best-supported account of biological
empathy, it doesn't.

What makes this entry especially valuable for the *plan* rather than just the claim is that PAM
supplies exactly the variable MECH-164 deliberately leaves open and RHM-5 is registered to close.
MECH-164 fixes the *structure* (self-like symmetry) but not the *weight or schedule*. PAM's
modulators -- similarity, familiarity, experience, salience -- are precisely the terms that set how
strongly the other's state couples into the observer's representation. In other words, the empathy
literature's own answer to "how much does the other's terrain couple?" is "it depends on these
modulators, under prefrontal regulation" -- which is the RHM-5 calibration question, already partly
answered in biology. That is a genuine forward signal for the V5 substrate, not just a sanity check
on MECH-164.

## Limitations and caveats

PAM is a domain-general *state-matching* account -- emotions, distress, arousal. It grounds the
shared-representation and self-like-weighting backbone of MECH-164 and its precursor INV-005, but it
is not specific to the *goal*-gradient inference that MECH-164 adds on top of harm-mirroring; the
goal half of the terrain still rests on the inverse-planning / teleological-stance entries in this
pull. It is also a theoretical and comparative synthesis, so the evidence is the marshalled
cross-species literature rather than a single decisive experiment -- I have rated source quality 0.82
and mapping fidelity 0.72 accordingly. And there is a failure mode the paper itself implies and that
REE must respect: automatic, ungated perception-action matching predicts emotional contagion and
*personal distress* -- the observer overwhelmed by the other's state -- which is the empathic-collapse
pole RHM-5 must design against. PAM only avoids that with prefrontal regulation layered on top of the
matching. A REE substrate that implements self-like weighting without an analogous regulatory /
calibration gate would inherit the contagion failure mode. That caveat is, again, useful design
information, not a weakness in the support: PAM is the right backbone, and it comes with its own
warning label.
