# Bor, Schwartzman, Barrett & Seth (2017) -- Theta-burst TMS to prefrontal or parietal cortex does not impair metacognitive visual awareness

**What they did.** This is a direct attempt to replicate Rounis et al. (2010), with the design tightened. They added an active control stimulation site and ran a between-subjects experiment over both DLPFC and posterior parietal cortex. A second, rigorous within-subjects experiment targeted DLPFC alone.

**What they found.** Neither experiment found a metacognitive impairment. The instructive part is what did produce one: letting back in the data that are unsuitable for signal detection analysis. With those trials included, a "significant" impairment appears, and the authors argue it is invalid.

**What it means for ARC-116.** It takes away the most-cited causal evidence that a metacognitive readout can be knocked out while first-order performance stays intact. That does not argue *for* collapse. A null at one site with one protocol says this lever does not separate the stages, not that the stages are one. But it changes where ARC-116's support comes from. The support now rests mostly on existence arguments. Monkeys have confidence without language (Kiani and Shadlen). People have narratives without the generating choice behind them (choice blindness). Clean causal dissociations are thinner on the ground. That is a good reason for the claim to be tested by building the dissociation, as its own falsifier proposes, rather than by leaning on the neuroscience.

**A transferable warning.** The most useful thing here for REE is methodological. A readout/generation dissociation can be manufactured by analysis choices, specifically by including conditions where the readout measure is ill-posed. Any REE experiment on ARC-116 that uses a meta-d'-like readout needs the same discipline: exclude floor and ceiling cells where the measure is undefined, before claiming preservation or degradation. This is the claim's non-degeneracy precondition seen from the other side.

**Confidence.** 0.4 as a weakening. It is solid as a replication, but only indirect as evidence against the claim itself.
