# Weber et al. 2023 -- Mixed-selective subspace partitioning lets multiple codes coexist without collapse

**Claim touched:** ARC-063 element (i), the representational geometry of a multi-rule field. Cross-ref MECH-309 (monomodal collapse).

## What the paper did
Using human intracranial recordings from PFC during a context-dependent task, Weber and colleagues showed that coexisting representations of past and present task variables compete, incurring a behavioural switch cost, and that the brain resolves this by partitioning the codes into *distinct low-dimensional neural subspaces*. This partitioning strongly attenuates the switch cost -- the authors frame it as a central building block of flexible cognitive control.

## Why it matters for ARC-063
This addresses the gap the pre-design audit flagged as most underspecified: *how* a distributed CandidateRule field could hold many candidate rules at once without them collapsing into a single smooth representation -- which is precisely the MECH-309 monomodal-collapse failure that sank the ARC-062 weak-reading falsifiers. Weber gives the population-geometry answer: mixed-selective populations keep competing representations separable by placing them in distinct low-dimensional subspaces. ARC-063's field can borrow this as its representational strategy -- candidate rules as partitioned subspaces rather than as competing scalar heads (which is exactly where the gated-policy heads collapsed).

## The honest caveat
The codes that are kept separable here are past vs present task *states*, not labelled candidate *rules*. ARC-063 extrapolates the subspace-partitioning geometry from states to rules; the geometric mechanism is general, but the rule-as-subspace mapping is an REE extension, not a demonstrated rule-field. The paper also shows interference is only *attenuated*, not abolished -- so a real CandidateRule field should predict residual switch/interference costs, which is a useful falsifiable consequence to keep.

## Confidence
0.70 -- supports. Strong human intracranial evidence for the coexistence-without-collapse geometry ARC-063 needs; discounted for the state-vs-rule target gap.
