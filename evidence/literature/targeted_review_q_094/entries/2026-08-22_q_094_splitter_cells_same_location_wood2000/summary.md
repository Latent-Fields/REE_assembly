# Splitter cells: different episodes in the same place -- Q-094

Wood, Dudchenko, Robitsek and Eichenbaum recorded hippocampal complex-spike neurons while rats ran continuous spatial alternation on a modified T maze. The design does the one thing Q-094 needs: it holds the scene and the overt behaviour constant. On the central stem the animal is in the same place, moving the same way, and the only thing that differs is whether this is a left-turn or a right-turn episode. Two-thirds of the recorded cells fired differentially by trial type on that common stem, with variation in running speed, heading and position on the stem explicitly accounted for.

Q-094 asks whether REE's active context representation should include internal variables -- goal, bodily state, confidence, inferred social situation -- rather than being primarily sensory scene identity, and it does so against MECH-150's recorded decision to query E1 ContextMemory with `z_world` alone. Wood et al. is the biological instance of precisely the comparison Q-094 poses. Matched `z_world`, differing task/goal variable, and the representation differentiates anyway. Whatever else is true, the hippocampus does not behave like a system whose context key is scene identity. That is real support for the inclusion pole, and it is the reason this entry sits at the top of the Q-094 pull.

But the caveat here is not a formality and I want it stated sharply, because it is the specific thing that separates "the biology includes internal state in context" from "MECH-150 chose wrongly." Wood et al. show that the internal variable is present in the **represented state** of the hippocampal population. MECH-150 is a decision about what goes into the **retrieval query**. In REE those come apart cleanly: a substrate could perfectly well carry goal information in the represented state while still keying context lookup on `z_world` alone, and nothing in this paper rules that architecture out. If anything, splitter coding is at least as consistent with "the internal variable modulates the representation retrieved" as with "the internal variable is part of the lookup key." Q-094 should be read as still open after this entry, with the inclusion pole better supported and the exact locus of inclusion unresolved.

Three further bounds. The design cannot separate prospective from retrospective coding -- the differential firing is equally consistent with encoding where the animal is going (Q-094's goal reading) and where it came from. A substantial minority of cells did not differentiate, and others did so only in combination with spatial and behavioural factors, so the signal is a majority property rather than a clean partition and a context system built on it inherits genuine noise. And the manipulation exercises exactly one of Q-094's four candidate internal variables: journey/goal. It is silent on bodily state, confidence and social situation, and therefore silent on the continuously-varying case where Q-094's stated destabilisation worry actually bites. That gap is what the Kennedy and Shapiro entry in this same directory is doing.

One last honest note: Q-094 carries a non-degeneracy precondition that no literature can discharge. MECH-153 records ContextMemory cosine similarity near 1.0 without a supervised context objective, and two query compositions over an undifferentiated bank are indistinguishable by construction. This entry raises the prior that inclusion is worth testing; it does not make the substrate comparison meaningful. That still has to be earned in the substrate.

Confidence 0.76.
