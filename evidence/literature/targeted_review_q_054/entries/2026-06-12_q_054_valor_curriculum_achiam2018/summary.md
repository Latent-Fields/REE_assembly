# VALOR (Achiam et al. 2018) — discriminability is capacity-limited, so diversity must be co-grown

Achiam J, Edwards H, Amodei D, Abbeel P. "Variational Option Discovery Algorithms." ([arXiv:1807.10299](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.10299)).

If DIAYN establishes *that* diversity and discriminability are coupled, VALOR is the paper that shows the coupling has critical structure — which is the part of Q-054 that hypothesises a phase transition ("below some floor the discriminator cannot learn; above it accuracy rises monotonically"). VALOR (Variational Autoencoding Learning of Options by Reinforcement) frames option discovery as a trajectory autoencoder: the policy encodes a sampled context into a trajectory, and a decoder tries to reconstruct the context from the completed trajectory. That decoder is the direct analogue of ARC-062 — recover the context from the behaviour.

The result that matters for Q-054 is the curriculum. Achiam et al. found training unstable when the number of contexts is fixed, and stabilised it with a curriculum that *increases the number of contexts only when decoder performance is sufficiently strong*. Their abstract states this "allows a single agent to learn many more modes of behavior than it could with a fixed context distribution." In other words: push diversity faster than the decoder can track and the discriminative cut collapses; grow them together and you get far more distinguishable modes. That is exactly the critical, threshold-like relationship between behavioural diversity and discriminability that Q-054 expects to see as a phase transition in ARC-062 accuracy versus upstream `first_action_entropy`.

The honest caveat — and it is a real one — is that VALOR characterises a *ceiling*: how many modes a decoder of given capacity can distinguish, and how fast you may add them. Q-054 asks the *dual* question, a *floor*: the minimum upstream entropy below which no reliable cut exists at all. These are two faces of the same diversity↔discriminability surface, but they are not the same number, and reading VALOR's ceiling as Q-054's floor would be an inversion. If ARC-062's failure in the V3 substrate turns out to be decoder-capacity-limited rather than diversity-limited, VALOR is the more relevant diagnosis and the "raise the entropy floor" intervention would be aimed at the wrong term.

What VALOR contributes most usefully, then, is an argument that the 0.497-nat question may be mis-framed as static: the better design may be a *scheduled* diversity target co-grown with discriminator competence (a curriculum), rather than a single fixed floor — which dovetails with the joint-training caution from the DIAYN entry. Confidence 0.60: directly on the critical-structure point, but it is the ceiling-dual of Q-054's floor, and the transfer from VALOR's growth curriculum to a fixed-stream sweep is moderate.
