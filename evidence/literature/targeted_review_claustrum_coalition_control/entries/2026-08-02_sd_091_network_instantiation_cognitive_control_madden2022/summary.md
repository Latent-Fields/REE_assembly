# A role for the claustrum in cognitive control (Madden, Stewart, White, Krimmel, Qadir, Barrett, Seminowicz & Mathur, 2022, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*)

This is the paper the source thought is built on top of, so it is worth reading it on its own terms rather than through the lens of what REE wants it to say. The authors are trying to solve a genuine problem in claustrum research: decades of hypotheses -- multisensory binding, salience detection, attention gating, consciousness "orchestration" -- that do not obviously fit together, largely because each was generated from a different slice of the anatomical and physiological data. Their proposed resolution, network instantiation in cognitive control (NICC), is that the claustrum's job is not any one of these computations but rather the assembly of whichever cortical network a given cognitive-control demand requires, under direction from frontal cortex. Salience detection, multisensory binding, and attention-gating findings are then read as downstream consequences of whichever network got instantiated, not as the claustrum's primary function.

For REE this is the closest thing to a direct biological precedent for the parameter/topology distinction SD-091 asserts. NICC is, structurally, a claim that the brain has a control-plane output that is graph-valued (which cortical areas get coupled) rather than purely scalar (how much gain or precision a given area gets) -- and that this graph-valued output is driven by a separate, frontally-sourced control signal, not by the content of the network being instantiated itself. That is a fair paraphrase of what SD-091 calls G_t and MECH-481 calls "coalition instantiation." It is worth being honest, though, about how much weight that precedent can bear. NICC is presented explicitly as a synthesis with testable predictions, not a settled finding -- the paper spends real effort reconciling prior, mutually awkward claustrum hypotheses, which tells you the field has not converged on this account yet. And NICC as stated is a claim about a single generic instantiation function, not about a taxonomy of typed control demands producing qualitatively distinct coalitions. MECH-481's specific claim -- that PROVENANCE_CHECK and COUNTERFACTUAL_EXPANSION recruit different subsystem sets -- is an extrapolation from NICC, not something this paper tests or asserts.

The mapping caveat worth stating plainly: this is anatomy and functional imaging providing computational inspiration, not computational architecture providing anatomical proof. REE's own guardrails (carried verbatim from the source thought into both claims' notes) already say not to over-claim here -- do not read the claustrum as "the seat of" coalition control, and do not treat this paper as validating the specific ten-item control-demand taxonomy, which is REE's own invention. What it does firmly establish is that the parameter-vs-topology distinction is not an ad hoc REE invention either -- it tracks a live, actively-argued distinction in the claustrum literature itself.

Confidence is set at a moderate 0.6: strong source quality (a well-cited TICS review from the group most active in this specific research question) and reasonably direct mapping fidelity for the architectural-level claim (SD-091), but meaningfully lower fidelity for the mechanistic, typed-recruitment claim (MECH-481), and non-trivial transfer risk in going from "claustrum instantiates networks" to "REE's control plane should emit a graph-valued output with its own persistence and dissolution conditions." This entry should be read as motivating evidence for building the architecture, not as evidence that the specific 4-arm falsifier MECH-481 proposes would come out any particular way.
