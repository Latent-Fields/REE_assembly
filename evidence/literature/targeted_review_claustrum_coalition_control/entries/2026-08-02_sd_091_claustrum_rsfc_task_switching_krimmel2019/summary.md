# Resting state functional connectivity and cognitive task-related activation of the human claustrum (Krimmel, White, Panicker, Barrett, Mathur & Seminowicz, 2019, *NeuroImage*)

This is the earlier, foundational paper behind both the 2022 review and the 2025 Huang et al. paper in this cluster -- it is where the group first worked out how to isolate a clean claustrum BOLD signal from the immediately adjacent insula, putamen, and caudate (a genuine methodological problem, given the claustrum's thin, sheet-like anatomy), and used that method for two things: resting-state functional connectivity, and activation during a conflict task.

The connectivity result is the more load-bearing one for SD-091: claustrum shows extensive functional connectivity with exactly the cortex you would expect a cognitive-control-relevant hub to be wired to -- anterior cingulate, prefrontal, and parietal regions -- replicated across a 7T and a separate 3T dataset. That is a reasonable anatomical precondition for the topology-control story: if the claustrum is going to selectively couple frontal control demands to distributed cortex, it needs to actually be connected to that cortex, and this paper is good evidence that it is.

The task result is smaller but more interesting for MECH-481 specifically, and worth flagging rather than glossing over. During the high-conflict condition of a multi-source interference task, claustrum activated significantly at the ONSET of the difficult trials, but not during the remainder of the difficult condition. That is not what you would predict if coalition instantiation, once triggered, simply persists tonically for as long as the demand is present -- it looks more like a phasic switch-detection signal than a sustained-coalition-maintenance signal. That is directly relevant to Gamma_t, the persistence/completion/dissolution component of REE's proposed control output, and it is worth registering as a real empirical constraint rather than letting the architecture assume coalitions are held open by continuous top-down drive. It may be that dissolution and re-engagement happen fast enough that a slow BOLD signal simply cannot resolve sustained activity, so this should not be over-read as ruling out sustained coalitions -- but it is at minimum a caveat, not a confirmation, of the "coalition persists until resolved" framing in the source thought's proposed sequence.

Neither result tests whether DIFFERENT kinds of cognitive-control demand recruit DIFFERENT cortical configurations via the claustrum -- this paper uses one interference task, so it cannot speak to typed-demand selectivity, which remains MECH-481's open empirical gap.

Confidence: 0.6. Careful methodology and a genuine replication (7T + 3T) support source_quality; mapping_fidelity is moderate because the connectivity finding is directly useful for SD-091's "pathways exist" premise while the task finding raises as many questions about timing as it answers.
