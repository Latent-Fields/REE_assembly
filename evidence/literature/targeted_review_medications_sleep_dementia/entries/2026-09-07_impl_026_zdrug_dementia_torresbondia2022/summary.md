# Z-drugs and dementia: the row IMPL-026 had no evidence for, now partly defended and partly undercut

Until this entry, IMPL-026's Z-drug row -- zolpidem, zopiclone, zaleplon, classified WORSENS on the strength of predicted Phase 1 (SWS depth) reduction plus mild Phase 4 (REM) reduction -- rested on nothing but inference from the benzodiazepine row. The existing directory covers classical benzodiazepines through Billioti de Gage (2014) but has never covered the Z-drugs separately. That is a real gap, because the whole point of giving Z-drugs their own row is the assertion that they differ.

Torres-Bondia and colleagues followed more than 83 000 benzodiazepine-receptor-agonist users against more than 84 000 non-users aged over 45 in the Catalan Health Service registry from 2002 to 2015. The crude hazard ratio for incident dementia among users was 1.22 (95% CI 1.15 to 1.31). Then it stopped being significant once confounders were adjusted for. What survived adjustment was a dose relation: risk was elevated at the highest doses, and for shorter-acting benzodiazepines and Z-drugs, particularly among women.

For IMPL-026 this cuts both ways, and I think that is the honest reading rather than a fence-sitting one. It supports the table's *comparative placement*: Z-drugs pattern with the GABAergic family rather than with the architecture-preserving agents, which is exactly what a phase-suppression account predicts and is not a trivial result. It does not support the table's *unqualified* WORSENS. A risk that appears only at the highest dose stratum is a different claim from "harmful over years" at therapeutic dose, and IMPL-026's categorical schema -- WORSENS, NEUTRAL, IMPROVES, CONDITIONAL -- has no way to say "worsens above a threshold". Arguably the Z-drug row should be CONDITIONAL, with dose as the condition. That is a concrete, cheap revision the table could absorb.

The deeper limitation is that this study contains no sleep data whatsoever. It observes prescriptions and it observes dementia diagnoses. Everything in between -- the SWS suppression, the spindle preservation, the Phase 1/Phase 4 story that is the entire content of IMPL-026's mechanistic column -- is unobserved. So the study can tell us the endpoint is in the predicted direction under some exposures, and it cannot tell us the pipeline is the reason. A fall-risk pathway, an anticholinergic co-prescription pathway, or plain reverse causation from prodromal insomnia would each produce this same table of numbers. The female-predominant signal is worth flagging precisely because IMPL-026's mechanism is stated without sex terms and therefore predicts nothing about it.

Confidence 0.70, direction mixed. The size and duration of the cohort are genuinely strong; what holds this back is that the strongest thing it establishes about IMPL-026 is that one of the table's rows is stated with more certainty than the data licenses.
