# Rosenberg et al. 2019 -- SUNRISE 1's primary report, and what it is not evidence for

JAMA Network Open 2(12):e1918254, DOI 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2019.18254, PMID 31880796, PMC6991236. This is the primary publication of the trial whose architecture analysis carries IMPL-026's head-to-head assertion, and I have banked it separately so the corpus records which paper shows what.

What it shows is efficacy. In 1,006 adults aged 55 and over, lemborexant beat placebo on latency to persistent sleep, sleep efficiency and wake after sleep onset, and beat zolpidem extended release 6.25 mg on wake after sleep onset in the second half of the night, by 6.7 minutes at 5 mg and 8.0 at 10 mg. Tolerability was good; of six serious adverse events, four were on zolpidem and none was judged treatment-related.

What it is not is evidence about sleep architecture. Latency, efficiency and wake after sleep onset say how much and how continuously a person slept, not what the sleep was doing. IMPL-026 places dual orexin receptor antagonists at the head of its ordering on pipeline grounds, and less waking is compatible with better, worse or unchanged consolidation. The architectural question is addressed only by the companion analysis, banked alongside this entry, and there the answer is partial.

The boundaries also matter for how far IMPL-026 can generalise. Thirty days, adults 55 and over with sleep-maintenance insomnia, 86% women, one fixed low geriatric dose of one Z-drug, sponsor-run with sponsor-affiliated authorship. IMPL-026's disease-progression-modifying claim is about years of exposure in mild cognitive impairment, and neither this trial nor anything else in this directory tests that horizon. Mixed at 0.55.
