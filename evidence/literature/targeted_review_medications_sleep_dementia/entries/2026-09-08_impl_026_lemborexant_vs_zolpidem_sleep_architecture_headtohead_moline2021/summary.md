# Moline et al. 2021 -- the head-to-head exists, and it does not say what IMPL-026 says

The chip asked whether the head-to-head PSG evidence behind IMPL-026's most consequential comparative claim exists. It does. SUNRISE 1 (Study 304) randomised 1,006 adults aged 55 and over with insomnia to placebo, zolpidem tartrate extended release 6.25 mg, or lemborexant 5 or 10 mg, with laboratory polysomnography at baseline, nights 1 and 2, and nights 29 and 30. This paper is its sleep-architecture analysis: Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine 17(6):1167-1174, DOI 10.5664/jcsm.9150, PMID 33590823, open at PMC8314653.

On REM, IMPL-026 is right. Lemborexant increased REM sleep more than zolpidem at both timepoints -- by 8.9 minutes at 5 mg and 15.7 at 10 mg on nights 29 and 30 -- and zolpidem did not differ from placebo on REM at all. It also shortened REM latency by roughly half an hour relative to zolpidem. In INV-045 terms, Phase 4 is where the head-to-head favours the orexin antagonist, and it does so consistently.

On slow wave sleep it is not right, and in places it is backwards. On nights 1 and 2 zolpidem raised N3 above placebo (7.7 versus 2.4 minutes) while lemborexant 10 mg sat significantly below zolpidem. By nights 29 and 30 zolpidem had fallen below placebo and lemborexant 5 mg exceeded it, but only by 3.8 minutes, and the 10 mg dose showed no advantage. IMPL-026's sentence that orexin antagonists "preserve or improve SWS% and REM%" and are "superior to zolpidem on phase preservation" reads as a general architectural superiority. The trial supports it for REM and contradicts it acutely for slow wave sleep at the higher dose.

Two further things belong in the ledger and are currently absent from the claim. Lemborexant increased N1, the lightest stage, more than zolpidem at both timepoints. And the shortened REM latency is presented by the authors as correcting a REM deficit, by analogy with REM rebound after treating sleep apnoea, which is a plausible reading but an interpretive one; shortened REM latency is elsewhere a marker of pathology, and IMPL-026 should not silently bank it as preservation.

The authors are more modest than the claim that cites them: the architecture improvements "were small in general and may not be clinically meaningful or noticeable to the patient". These were exploratory endpoints, and no multiplicity adjustment is stated despite many comparisons across stages, doses and timepoints. The comparator was a single fixed low geriatric dose of zolpidem ER, so this is not a verdict on Z-drugs at usual doses.

Finally, the distinction the chip asked me to keep in view. Stage minutes are not consolidation-pipeline integrity. INV-045's Phase 3 is spindle coordination, and neither SUNRISE 1 paper reports spindle density, amplitude or slow-oscillation coupling. A drug could increase N2 minutes while degrading the spindle-coupled consolidation that N2 is supposed to be a proxy for, and this trial would not see it. Mixed at 0.6. The right revision to IMPL-026 is to narrow the assertion to REM and cite this trial, not to retire it.
