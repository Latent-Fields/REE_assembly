# SWS slow oscillations preferentially strengthen specific replayed sequences (Wei, Krishnan & Bazhenov 2016)

According to PubMed, this is a detailed thalamocortical network model with spike-timing-dependent plasticity ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3648-15.2016)) asking how the 0.2-1 Hz slow oscillations of stage 3/4 sleep actually convert a waking experience into a durable cortical trace. The headline mechanistic result: when hippocampal sharp-wave-ripple input is delivered to the cortical network during the slow oscillation, it produces *input-specific* synaptic changes that persist after the input is removed, and those changes then bias subsequent spontaneous replay toward the stimulated sequence. Slow-wave sleep, in this account, is not a passive rehearsal -- it is a directed strengthening engine whose target is whichever sequence the ripple input carries.

That is exactly the substrate Q-055 worries about. The question Q-055 asks is whether SD-017's SWS phase preserves the trajectory-class diversity that ARC-065 generates during waking, or whether Hebbian, winner-take-all dynamics during replay erode it. This paper supplies the "yes, erosion is mechanistically available" half of the answer: if the ripple content during sleep is dominated by the most-recently or most-heavily-weighted trajectory classes (the prediction MECH-120 makes), then the slow-oscillation/STDP loop will preferentially deepen those classes' cortical attractors, and the comparative standing of the under-represented classes will drift downward.

The honest limitation is that the model studies the strengthening of a *single* trained sequence against background noise, not the comparative fate of a *population* of co-existing learned behaviours. "Preferential replay" here means preferential relative to untrained activity -- it is not a measurement of diversity loss across a set of trajectory classes. So the paper establishes the strengthening mechanism and its phase-dependence, but it does not, by itself, show that strengthening one class *erodes* the others, nor does it engage the two counter-mechanisms Q-055 names: ARC-065 re-seeding diversity in waking inter-sleep windows, or replay being designed to sample rather than concentrate.

I have set evidence_direction to `supports` (the erosion arm) at confidence 0.62. It is high-quality, mechanistically explicit, and from a leading sleep-modelling group, but it is in-silico and frames the problem one-trace-at-a-time rather than as the diversity question REE actually needs answered. It tells us the gun exists and is loaded; it does not tell us it fires at diversity.
