# Sleep spindles preferentially rescue weakly encoded memories (Denis et al. 2021)

According to PubMed, this human nap study from the Stickgold group ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0818-20.2021)) is the cleanest counter-evidence I found to the Q-055 erosion hypothesis. Fifty-four participants learned word pairs to three distinct encoding strengths; half then had a two-hour nap, half stayed awake. Across the wake interval, weakly encoded memories decayed fastest -- the intuitive winner-take-all pattern. But in the nap group that effect was *attenuated until forgetting rates were equivalent across encoding strengths*, and the rescue of weak items was specifically predicted by fast spindle density during NREM, particularly spindles coupled to slow oscillations. Crucially, spindles did not correlate with memory for intermediate or strong items: sleep's protective action was *targeted at the weak traces*.

If that pattern generalises, it inverts the worry behind Q-055. The diversity-erosion account assumes sleep amplifies the strong at the expense of the weak. Here sleep does the opposite -- it preferentially protects the weak, equalising the distribution rather than concentrating it. Mapped onto REE, if SD-017 sleep preferentially defends the low-frequency, under-committed trajectory classes (the ones a Hebbian read of MECH-120 predicts would be lost), then sleep is plausibly a *net positive* for ARC-065 behavioural diversity. This is the strongest single reason to doubt that sleep is corrosive to diversity, and it is human data with a concrete physiological mediator, not just a model.

The mapping caveat is real and I have not soft-pedalled it: this is declarative, episodic word-pair memory, and "weak encoding strength" maps only loosely onto "low-frequency or under-committed trajectory class." The spindle-SO coupling mechanism that protects weak declarative traces may or may not be the mechanism operating on REE's procedural/behavioural replay. So this is support-by-analogy for the preservation arm, not a direct demonstration.

evidence_direction `weakens` (the erosion hypothesis), confidence 0.70 -- the highest in the Q-055 set, reflecting a robust human design with a physiological correlate, discounted for the declarative-to-behavioural transfer. Taken with the two Wei models, the literature picture is genuinely two-sided: the strengthening/competition machinery exists (erosion is possible), but sleep also runs a weak-trace-protection program that would preserve diversity. Which dominates in REE is exactly what the paired Q-055 experiment has to settle.
