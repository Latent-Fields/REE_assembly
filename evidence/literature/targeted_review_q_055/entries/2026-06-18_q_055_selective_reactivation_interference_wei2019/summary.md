# Selective replay augmentation interferes with competing -- especially weak -- memories (Wei et al. 2019)

According to PubMed, this companion thalamocortical-model study ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1427-19.2019)) extends the 2016 work to the multi-memory case, which is the case Q-055 actually lives in. The central finding: when several memories are trained in waking and then a cue associated with one of them is presented during slow-wave sleep, that cue selectively augments replay and consolidation of the cued memory *and interferes with consolidation of the others* -- with weak memories preferentially disadvantaged. The effect is sharply phase-gated: it is maximal when the stimulation lands just before the Down-to-Up transition of the slow oscillation and negligible during Up states.

This is the most direct piece of literature I found for the Q-055 erosion mechanism, because it demonstrates that sleep consolidation is *competitive*, not merely additive. If ARC-065's trajectory classes differ in strength or in how well their reactivation aligns with the optimal slow-oscillation phase, then the strong/well-aligned classes are consolidated at the active expense of the weak/poorly-aligned ones. Run that forward across many sleep cycles and you have a winner-take-all narrowing of behavioural diversity -- precisely the MECH-120 worst case. The fact that *weak* memories are the ones that lose is what makes this bite: diversity is carried disproportionately by the low-frequency, less-dominant classes, and those are the ones the model shows being suppressed.

The caveat that keeps me below 0.7 is that the interference is shown under *exogenous* targeted-memory-reactivation cueing, not spontaneous self-organised replay. In REE's sleep substrate there is no external experimenter delivering cues; the question of *what biases spontaneous ripple content* is left open -- and that is the very fork Q-055 is asking about. If spontaneous replay samples broadly (counter-mechanism b), the competition demonstrated here might be muted. And again, discrete cued associates are a coarse stand-in for a graded population of trajectory classes.

evidence_direction `supports` (erosion arm), confidence 0.66 -- a notch above the 2016 entry because it explicitly models the competitive, weak-loses dynamic that diversity erosion requires, while still being a simulation under artificial cueing.
