# Damage the moral faculty and the person goes; damage the memory and they stay

Strohminger and Nichols asked 248 caregivers — mostly spouses — to rate a family member with
frontotemporal dementia, Alzheimer's disease or amyotrophic lateral sclerosis on a battery of symptom
domains, and separately to say how far the patient still seemed to be the same person. Structural
equation modelling then asked which symptom domain carried the identity variance. The answer was the
moral faculty, and only the moral faculty. Amnesia contributed nothing measurable. The disease ordering
fell out accordingly: bvFTD worst for perceived identity, ALS least. Perceived identity change fully
mediated the deterioration of the patient–caregiver relationship, which is a sad finding and not the one
I am here for.

The reason this belongs in MECH-024's file is that MECH-024 is a claim about a 3×3 coupling matrix —
lesion selfhood, personality, ethics; read out selfhood, personality, ethics — and it stands or falls on
the off-diagonals. This study is one off-diagonal cell, run by nature rather than by us: damage the
ethics axis, and the selfhood readout moves.

But the load-bearing half of the result is not the positive finding. It is the null on amnesia. MECH-024's
falsifying branch is unusually well specified on this point: a matrix whose off-diagonals are all
explained by a single global-disruption factor — every readout degrading under every lesion in the same
proportion — is explicitly *not* confirmation. Collapse is not coupling. What Strohminger and Nichols
show is selectivity: two symptom domains, both severe, both distressing, and only one of them moves the
identity readout. That is the shape MECH-024 needs, and it is a shape that global-degradation accounts
cannot produce. If I were designing the REE coupling matrix and wanted one prior from the human
literature about what a *real* off-diagonal looks like, this would be it.

Now the caveats, and they are not small. The dependent variable is perceived identity, rated by the
caregiver, who is also the person rating the symptoms. That is a single-informant design with an obvious
route to shared-method variance, and more importantly it locates the construct in the wrong place for
REE. z_self coherence is a property of the agent's own recurrent state; perceived identity is a property
of somebody else's model of the agent. It is entirely coherent that moral conduct is what *observers*
track when deciding whether you are still you, while the patient's internal self-representation is
governed by something else. If that is the true story, this paper is evidence about folk psychology's
identity criteria rather than about the architecture of selfhood, and the mapping to MECH-024 largely
fails. I do not think that is the whole story — the bvFTD self-consciousness literature (see the
Arroyo-Anlló entry alongside this one) finds internal disturbance too — but the paper cannot rule it out.

The second caveat bears directly on MECH-024's non-degeneracy precondition. Disease is not a targeted
lesion. bvFTD degrades personality and ethics together, and MECH-024's whole design depends on lesions
being targeted enough that the diagonal stays the largest effect on its own row. Read strictly, this
study establishes that *some* frontally-mediated axis couples to identity; it cannot tell us whether the
coupling runs ethics→selfhood, personality→selfhood, or both. That distinction is precisely what the REE
experiment is for, and it is why this entry is graded 0.72 rather than higher. The human literature
supplies the existence proof and the structural-versus-global discipline; it cannot supply the row
assignment, because nature does not do targeted ablations.

Practically, this strengthens the case for running MECH-024's matrix after MECH-023 rather than before,
as the claim's own 2026-09-16 disposition recommends — but it also suggests an ordering within the matrix.
The ethics→selfhood cell is the one with the best prior support and therefore the cheapest informative
first cell. If that cell comes back inside the seed-noise band on built substrate while the human
analogue is this clean, that is a substantive finding about V3's residue field being inert with respect
to self-recurrence, and worth knowing before spending nine cells.
