# The personality row — and a second axis that turns out not to be one thing

Four of the five entries in this directory examine the ethics row of MECH-024's coupling matrix: lesion
ethics, watch selfhood. That is an accident of which literatures exist — focal lesions to valuation
structures are studied, deliberate manipulations of personality are not. But MECH-024's confirming branch
needs the coupling *graph* to be connected, every axis reaching every other, and a matrix argued entirely
from one row cannot support that. Glenn and colleagues supply the missing row, imperfectly, from
individual differences rather than from lesions.

The finding is that people scoring higher on psychopathic traits have a substantially weaker moral
identity — standardised coefficients around −.55 to −.58 across two studies of roughly 200 participants
each. Moral identity here is the self-importance of being a moral person: not what you judge, but what
you take to be constitutive of you.

Why that is the personality row: psychopathy is a trait construct, and on the cybernetic account REE is
borrowing for its personality axis (see the DeYoung entry alongside this one), a trait *is* a setting of
control-system parameters. Moral identity is a readout that is simultaneously selfhood and ethics —
which constraints are inviolable, held as a fact about who I am. A large coefficient running from the one
to the other is, in human correlational form, the personality row having non-zero off-diagonals.

Then the second finding, which is the one I would actually carry into the experiment design. Moral
identity and moral judgment *dissociated*. Psychopathy predicted more utilitarian responses on personal
dilemmas, and it predicted weaker moral identity, and the first did not explain the second. They are two
independent relationships, not one effect showing up twice.

Put that next to the Arroyo-Anlló entry in this same directory, which found the *selfhood* construct
fractionating — identity awareness spared while moral judgment and insight went. Two of MECH-024's three
axes, in two unrelated human literatures, turn out not to be single readouts. That is a pattern, and it
has a specific consequence for a 3×3 matrix design: if an axis is really two sub-readouts that respond
differently to a lesion, then whichever one you happen to instrument determines whether the cell comes
back significant. MECH-024 currently proposes to read ethics as the harm-veto and residue-cost rate over a
hazard probe set — a judgment-flavoured measure. This paper says the judgment-flavoured measure is the one
that dissociates *away* from the identity-flavoured aspect of ethics. If the identity-flavoured aspect is
the one that couples, the chosen readout will be the one that does not move, and two off-diagonals will
come back null for reasons that are about instrumentation, not architecture. Given how expensive the
matrix is — nine cells, three-plus seeds each, on a trained substrate per cell — that is the sort of thing
worth resolving on paper first.

Which runs into the honest limitation: REE has no moral-identity readout at all. It has a residue field
whose cost rate you can count and a self-recurrence state whose coherence you can measure. It has nothing
that measures which constraints the agent treats as constitutive of itself. So the finding most useful to
MECH-024 names a measurement the substrate does not currently support, and I would flag that as a
substrate gap rather than pretend the existing readouts cover it.

The methodological caveats are real and I will not dress them down. Self-report psychopathy, self-report
moral identity, online panels — a single self-presentation factor could produce that headline coefficient
without any coupling between the constructs, and the authors say as much. More fundamentally, this is
between-subjects covariation and MECH-024 is a lesion claim. People differing on three correlated traits
is entirely compatible with a common upstream cause acting on each independently, which is precisely the
common-cause reading MECH-024's falsifier declines to accept as confirmation. That gap — correlation
across agents versus intervention within one — is exactly what the REE experiment would close, and it is
the reason this entry is graded `mixed` at 0.55 rather than read as support.
