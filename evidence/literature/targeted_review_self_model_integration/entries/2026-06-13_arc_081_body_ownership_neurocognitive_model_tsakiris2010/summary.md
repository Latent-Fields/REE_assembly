# My body in the brain: a neurocognitive model of body-ownership (Tsakiris, 2010)

*According to PubMed (PMID 19819247).* Tsakiris, *Neuropsychologia* 48(3):703-712, 2010. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2009.09.034).

**Claims:** ARC-081 (self-as-object), SD-030 (E2 self-forward-model comparator), MECH-215 (self-model prerequisite for agentive prediction), INV-064 (maturational sequence, weak) -- the L1 body-ownership strand of self_model_v4:SELF-8, and the entry that harvests the insula + TPJ completion-set partners.

## What the paper did

This is the paper that turned the rubber-hand illusion from a striking demonstration into a mechanistic model. Tsakiris argues that body-ownership is not read off the senses directly but *computed* as an interaction between current multisensory input and internal models of the body, in three stages: (1) a pre-existing stored model of the body decides whether an external object is even a candidate for incorporation ("incorporeability"); (2) online anatomical and postural body representations modulate how the multisensory streams are integrated and recalibrate the visual and tactile coordinate frames; (3) the resulting referral of tactile sensation produces the subjective experience of ownership. He maps these onto a named network: right temporoparietal junction (the incorporeability test), secondary somatosensory cortex (online body representation), posterior parietal and ventral premotor cortices (coordinate recalibration), and right posterior insula (the felt sense of ownership).

## Why it grounds the claim

The architecture Tsakiris describes is, component for component, the shape of REE's self-model. A *stored model of the body* that constrains what counts as self is SELF-1's stateful z_self. *Multisensory input weighted against that stored model*, with ownership referred only when they cohere, is SD-030's self-stream comparator -- a residual between the predicted-self and the observed-self, gated by a tolerance. And the insistence that a stored self-model must exist *before* the referral step can run is exactly the MECH-215 logic: there is no coherent subject of agentive prediction until z_self is a stable, differentiated representation rather than an instantaneous snapshot. This is the single most architecturally faithful paper in the L1 strand.

It also pays the two completion-set debts the SELF-8 node was tasked to harvest. The **right TPJ** as the incorporeability test is the biological seat of the self/other boundary -- the construct REE's V5 social tier will need when it asks "is this body / this agency mine or another's?". The **right posterior insula** as the carrier of subjective ownership is the interoceptive partner that the MECH-214 strand (Craig 2009, Seth 2013) independently converges on: ownership is not just exteroceptive multisensory matching, it is anchored in an interoceptive sense of the body as mine. Both are surfaced here as *proposal-first* harvest, not registered (see the plan's decision log).

## Limitations and caveats

It is a synthesis model of a body-*part* ownership phenomenon, not a developmental study or an agentive-prediction experiment. The INV-064 tag is the weakest of the four and I have flagged it as such: Tsakiris's layered structure (stored model -> integration -> referral) *rhymes* with INV-064's "lower representational layers gate higher ones" logic, but it does not test the E1->E2->E3 maturational-necessity claim or the prefrontal-myelination prediction. The TPJ and insula localisations are harvested as candidate completion-set partners, not asserted as REE claims.
