# Abnormalities in the awareness of action (Blakemore, Wolpert & Frith, 2002)

*According to PubMed (PMID 12039604).* Blakemore, Wolpert & Frith, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 6(6):237-242, 2002. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1364-6613(02)01907-1).

**Claims:** SD-030 (E2 self-forward-model comparator), MECH-215 (self-model prerequisite for agentive prediction) -- the L2 sense-of-agency / efference-copy strand of self_model_v4:SELF-8.

## What the paper did

This is the compact statement of the comparator model of agency. Blakemore, Wolpert and Frith argue that optimal motor control depends on internal representations of three things: the *actual* state of the limbs and world, the *desired* state (the goal), and the *predicted* state (what a forward model says will happen given the issued motor command). Crucially, only some components of these representations reach awareness, and the sense that "I am the agent of this action" is built on a comparison between the predicted and the actual sensory consequences of one's own movements. They then read a range of neuropsychiatric symptoms -- the anarchic hand sign, phantom limbs, utilization behaviour, and delusions of control -- as selective failures of particular components of this internal-model machinery.

## Why it grounds the claim

SD-030 is, in REE terms, exactly this comparator placed on the self stream. The proposed residual `residual_self = z_self_observed - E2_self(z_self_{t-1}, a_actual)` is the difference between the *predicted* self-state (an E2 forward model driven by the action actually issued) and the *observed* self-state. This paper is the canonical source for the claim that such a predicted-vs-actual comparison is what the brain uses to attribute action to the self, and that when the prediction and the observation come apart, agency breaks in characteristic ways -- the passivity phenomena and delusions of control. That is the single cleanest biological warrant for SD-030's architecture, which is why I rate mapping fidelity higher here than anywhere else in this pull.

It also speaks directly to the second half of MECH-215. MECH-215 says coherent agency requires not just a stable z_self (prerequisite 1) but an E2 model of *how one's own actions transform that self-state* (prerequisite 2). Blakemore et al.'s point that awareness of action rests on efferent motor-command streams -- not merely on sensory feedback, as the phantom-limb movement illusions show -- is the biological form of exactly that prerequisite: without a forward model fed by the efference copy, there is nothing to compare the observation against, and agency cannot be represented at all.

## Limitations and caveats

This is a human motor-control and neuropsychiatry framework, not an experiment on REE. It grounds the *form* of the mechanism strongly but does not measure REE's z_self residual; the clinical dissociations are best read as predictions REE's SD-030 self-stream *should* reproduce, not as evidence that the current substrate does. And SD-030 is V4-deferred precisely because V3 has no clean z_self stream with its own forward model (E2 currently operates on combined z_gamma). So this entry raises literature_confidence only -- it promotes nothing and it does not relieve the substrate gate. I deliberately did not tag the body-ownership claims (ARC-081) here: this paper is about agency/efference, not multisensory ownership, and the two strands should not be conflated.
