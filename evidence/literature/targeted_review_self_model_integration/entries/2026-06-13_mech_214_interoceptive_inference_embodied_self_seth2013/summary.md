# Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self (Seth, 2013)

*According to PubMed (PMID 24126130).* Seth, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 17(11):565-573, 2013. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.007).

**Claims:** MECH-214 (goal referent must be E1-representable), MECH-215 (self-model prerequisite for agentive prediction), INV-064 (maturational sequence, weak) -- the L3 interoceptive-self strand of self_model_v4:SELF-8.

## What the paper did

Seth extends the predictive-coding / Bayesian-brain programme -- by then well established for vision, audition and action -- inward, to the body. His proposal is "interoceptive inference": subjective feeling states (emotions) are not raw read-outs of bodily signals but *inferences*, generated by a predictive model of the causes of interoceptive afferents. This generalises classical appraisal theories of emotion (which already held that feelings involve evaluation of physiological change) by giving the evaluation a concrete computational form, and it explicitly extends to the experience of body ownership and conscious selfhood, in health and in neuropsychiatric illness. Where Craig 2009 says the anterior insula *re-represents* interoceptive state, Seth says that re-representation is a *generative model* doing active inference.

## Why it grounds the claim

This is, of the six, the paper whose framework is closest to MECH-214's actual content. MECH-214 says a goal's satisfaction referent must be representable in E1's schema space for the goal to carry semantic information. Seth makes the satisfaction/affective state a *modelled* quantity -- the output of a generative model over interoceptive causes -- so "E1-representable" becomes, almost word for word, "inferable by the interoceptive generative model." And the failure mode falls straight out: if that generative model is impoverished, the satisfaction state cannot be inferred, and wanting decouples from its hedonic grounding. That is MECH-214's addiction/anhedonia prediction restated in predictive-coding terms -- pursuit of a proxy whose satisfaction referent the agent cannot actually model.

The second reason to value this entry is that it unifies the strands. SD-030/MECH-215 use a predictive-comparator architecture on the *motor* self (predicted vs observed self-state). Seth uses the *same* architecture on the *interoceptive* self (predicted vs observed bodily state). That is a strong hint that REE's self-model should not be two unrelated mechanisms but one comparator form running over two streams -- a motor-self stream (SD-030) and an interoceptive-self stream (MECH-214) -- which is a cleaner and more biologically defensible design than treating affect and agency as separate subsystems. Seth even notes the rubber-hand illusion has an interoceptive precision modulator, tying back to the L1 Tsakiris/Botvinick entries.

## Limitations and caveats

It is a theoretical framework, not a REE experiment -- it strengthens the *conceptual coherence* of the MECH-214 channel and the unification of the self-model's two halves, but it tests nothing in REE and relieves no substrate gate. The INV-064 tag is the weakest of the three claims and I have flagged it as such: Seth remarks that selfhood *develops* from interoceptive inference, which rhymes with INV-064's lower-layer-before-higher-layer prerequisite logic, but he does not test the E1->E2->E3 maturational-necessity claim. There is also a framework-adjacency caveat worth stating plainly: Seth's free-energy / active-inference vocabulary is *adjacent to* but not *identical with* REE's E1/E2/E3 decomposition, so the mapping is at the level of "same predictive-coding family," not "same model."

## Confidence

0.64. High source quality (an influential TICS framework, foundational for interoceptive predictive processing). Mapping fidelity is good for MECH-214 -- the satisfaction referent as an inferable/modelled quantity is nearly a restatement of E1-representability -- and decent for MECH-215, but weak for INV-064. Transfer risk is low-moderate because the predictive-coding self maps onto REE's generative E1/E2 only at the framework level. The honest reading: this paper makes the MECH-214 interoceptive-goal channel *theoretically coherent and well-motivated*, and suggests a unifying design for the whole self-model, without providing experimental evidence specific to REE.
