# Abnormalities in the awareness and control of action (Frith, Blakemore & Wolpert, 2000)

*According to PubMed (PMID 11205340, PMC1692910).* Frith, Blakemore & Wolpert, *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B* 355(1404):1771-1788, 2000. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2000.0734).

**Claims:** SD-030 (E2 self-forward-model comparator), MECH-215 (self-model prerequisite for agentive prediction) -- the L2 sense-of-agency / efference-copy strand of self_model_v4:SELF-8, companion to the 2002 TICS framing.

## What the paper did

This is the longer, lesion-grounded review behind the 2002 framework. Frith, Blakemore and Wolpert lay out that awareness of action draws on two sources -- sensory signals from skin and muscle, and the stream of motor commands that have been issued -- and that the brain maintains mental representations of both the actual/predicted state of the motor system and the intended actions. The contribution that earns it a separate entry is the *localisation*: they argue that representations of the current and predicted state of the motor system live in parietal cortex, whereas representations of intended movements are found in prefrontal and premotor cortex. They then walk a series of clinical breakdowns -- phantom limbs and anosognosia (movement experienced from motor-command streams with no sensory confirmation), utilization behaviour, and delusions of control (intention and felt consequence no longer linked) -- and assign each to the failure of a specific component.

## Why it grounds the claim

The 2002 entry establishes that agency is a predicted-vs-actual comparison; this 2000 paper adds the architectural separation REE actually cares about. By placing the *predicted-state* machinery (parietal) apart from the *intended-action* machinery (prefrontal/premotor), it gives a biological warrant for keeping SD-030's self-forward-model distinct from REE's goal/intention pathway (z_goal -> E3). In REE the predicted self-state is what E2_self emits and what SD-030's residual compares against the observation; the intended/goal state is a separate object that E3 scores. The fact that these doubly dissociate under lesions -- you can lose one and keep the other -- is precisely the kind of evidence that justifies REE treating them as separate representations rather than one fused signal. It also sharpens the MECH-215 prerequisite: agency requires an E2 self-transition model (the predicted self-state) as the subject of prediction, and the phantom-limb phenomena show what happens when the system represents agency from intention alone with no observed-self to compare against -- it hallucinates successful action.

## Limitations and caveats

This is a lesion-and-illusion synthesis, not a mechanistic recording, and the parietal/prefrontal localisation is at a much coarser grain than REE's z_self-vs-z_goal latent split -- so the mapping is a functional-architecture analogy rather than a tight correspondence. As with the 2002 entry, the clinical dissociations are predictions REE's SD-030 self-stream *should* reproduce once it exists, not evidence about the current V3 substrate (which has no clean z_self stream and runs E2 on combined z_gamma). The two L2 entries (this and Blakemore 2002) are genuinely complementary -- one states the comparator model, the other dissociates its components anatomically -- so I have kept both rather than collapsing them, but a reader should treat them as one converging line, not two independent confirmations.
