# Replay types dissociate by reward -- Ambrose, Pfeiffer & Foster 2016 (Neuron)

According to PubMed. Source: Ambrose RE, Pfeiffer BE, Foster DJ. "Reverse Replay of Hippocampal Place Cells Is Uniquely Modulated by Changing Reward." *Neuron* 91(5):1124-1136, 2016. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2016.07.047)

**What the paper did.** Awake replays come in two flavours -- forward (same order as experienced) and reverse (opposite order) -- and both occur at reward locations during sharp-wave ripples. Ambrose et al. asked whether changing the reward changes the two replay types differently. It does: only *reverse* replays scaled their rate with reward -- increasing when reward was increased, decreasing when reward was decreased -- while forward replays were unchanged. This established a clean functional dissociation between replay directions and a specific relationship between reverse replay and reward processing.

**Why it matters for MECH-319 -- two edges.** On the supporting edge: MECH-319 posits a *categorical* write-gate keyed to the simulation tag of the caller, which only makes sense if replay is not a homogeneous stream but carries discrete, distinguishable categories. Ambrose et al. demonstrate exactly that -- forward and reverse replays are functionally separable event-types -- evidence that the substrate carries discrete replay categories a tag-keyed gate could in principle read. On the tension edge, which I want to state plainly: reverse replay of *real* experience is recruited *for* reward learning (its rate tracks reward magnitude). That means a write-gate at the arbitration layer cannot be a blanket replay block. The selectivity MECH-319 needs is sharp: admit real-experience reward replay (which the brain clearly uses to learn), block the non-realized ghost/DMN passes. This paper is the clearest evidence that the gate must be conditioned on *realized-vs-simulated*, not on *replay-vs-online*.

**Where the mapping strains.** The categories distinguished here are forward vs reverse replay of real experience -- not realized vs simulated/ghost content, which is the cut MECH-319's tag actually makes. So while the paper proves replay is categorically structured, it does not prove the *specific* tag REE wants to gate on. And the binary admit/block character of MECH-319's gate is inferred from the functional dissociation, not directly measured; no one recorded a write-admit signal here.

**Confidence.** 0.68, and I have marked the direction *mixed* rather than supports. Source quality is high (Neuron, Foster lab, rigorous ensemble recording with a clean reward manipulation). Mapping fidelity is moderate: it supports the discriminable-replay-class premise but the measured cut differs from MECH-319's simulation tag, and the reward-scaling result sits in genuine tension with a write-suppression gate. The value of including it is precisely that tension -- it disciplines the claim toward a selective, realized-data-permitting gate rather than a crude replay block.
