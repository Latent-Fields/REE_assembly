# Vlaeyen & Linton 2000 -- the protective response becomes the disorder

Pain 85(3):317-332, DOI 10.1016/S0304-3959(99)00242-0, PMID 10781906. The fear-avoidance model is probably the single best-known clinical statement of MECH-541's proposition, in a domain the user knows well. Avoidance of activity after musculoskeletal injury is adaptive: it protects healing tissue. Where the pain is catastrophically interpreted, the avoidance and the associated hypervigilance outlast the acute phase, and then produce disuse, deconditioning, low mood and disability, which sustain the pain experience. The loop closes. What maintains the chronic disorder is not the original injury but the response that was appropriate to it.

That is MECH-541 almost word for word, in another substrate: a workaround that was initially adaptive becomes over-persistent and over-generalised, and traps the organism after the original problem has changed. It also supplies a mechanism the REE claim states only abstractly. The reason the loop does not self-correct is that the adaptation removes the evidence that would correct it -- if you never load the limb, you never learn that loading is safe. In REE terms that is a compensation which suppresses its own disconfirming input, which is a more specific and more testable route than "becomes resistant to counterfactual correction".

Two honest limits. First, this is a model of beliefs, fear and behaviour, not of internal representational reallocation; the shared structure is the temporal shape, not the mechanism at the level ARC-140 and MECH-541 care about. Second, the evidence base is uneven. Leeuw and colleagues' seven-year review of the model (Journal of Behavioral Medicine 30(1):77-94, PMID 17180640) finds support for individual links -- particularly fear to avoidance and avoidance to disability -- stronger than for the complete causal chain, and calls for prospective work. So the clinical analogue is real but is itself only partly evidenced, and a REE result that reproduced only the persistence would match only the best-evidenced fragment of it. Supports at 0.62.
