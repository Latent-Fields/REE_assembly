# Faity et al. 2026 -- learned nonuse, and the argument that persistence is not the same as pathology

Neurorehabilitation and Neural Repair 40(8):615-630, DOI 10.1177/15459683261445442, PMID 42080459, open via PMC13392161. This is the entry MECH-541 most needed, because the claim was registered on architectural argument alone and its clinical intuition -- a defence that outlives the threat it was built for -- has a precise motor analogue.

The definitions are the ones ARC-140 wants. True recovery is "resolution of impairment, allowing movements to be performed using premorbid neural substrates and motor patterns". Compensation is "alternative neural pathways and/or modified movement strategies for achieving functional goals despite persistent impairment". Recovery without restoration is not a novel framing in stroke medicine; it is the standard distinction, and ARC-140 can cite it as such.

For MECH-541 the learned-nonuse literature gives the over-stabilisation shape directly: early pain, effort or failure teach avoidance of the paretic limb, and "this avoidance pattern may persist even after functional recovery has progressed". The self-reinforcement is described too, as a defaulting to familiar solutions and a motor memory "that can impede new learning". A workaround formed under one set of conditions outlasting them, and entrenching itself, is exactly the mechanism the claim proposes.

The reason I have marked this mixed rather than supporting is the part of the paper that argues against the standard reading, and it is the most useful thing here. The authors contend that much nonuse is not maladaptive conditioning at all but optimal effort conservation given real neuromechanical constraints -- above a certain arm-weight-to-shoulder-force ratio, trunk compensation genuinely becomes the better organisation -- and they note that after constraint-induced therapy compensatory patterns partly re-emerge, "suggesting that their original compensatory strategy was closer to optimal". They also observe that CIMT itself may encourage a different compensation, increased trunk involvement.

That is a discipline MECH-541's assay has to absorb. Observing that a compensation persists, or that amplifying it does not reverse it, does not establish that it has become maladaptive; the organism may simply be right about its own constraints. The claim's amplification sweep will need an explicit cost model against which "stopped being adaptive" is defined, and it will need to identify which compensation is present rather than only whether function returned. Confidence 0.6.
