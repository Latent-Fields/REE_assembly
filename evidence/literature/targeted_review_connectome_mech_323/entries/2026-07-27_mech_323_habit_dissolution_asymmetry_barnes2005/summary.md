# Barnes, Kubota, Hu, Jin & Graybiel 2005 -- Activity of striatal neurons reflects dynamic encoding and recoding of procedural memories

Of the three parameters this pull was asked to ground, the dissolution threshold was the one with literally nothing behind it. MECH-323 forms chunks when outcome variance falls below F_low (default 0.15); MECH-324 dissolves them when variance rises above F_high (default 0.45); the asymmetry F_low < F_high is asserted in both claims' registered text and was, until now, uncited. Barnes et al. is the closest thing to a direct source, and it comes from the same laboratory and preparation as the Graybiel anchors already underwriting ARC-071.

The design is acquisition, then extinction, then reacquisition, with chronic multi-electrode recording in sensorimotor striatum throughout. The paper opens by stating the asymmetry as a known behavioural fact -- ingrained habits require extensive repetition to form, "learning not to perform such behaviours is notoriously difficult", and yet "regaining a habit can occur quickly, with even one or a few exposures". The neural finding is that task-related ensemble patterns "successively formed, reversed and then re-emerged" across the three phases, tracking behaviour closely, while task-irrelevant firing was suppressed, rebounded, and was suppressed again. That is an acquisition-versus-dissolution asymmetry with a substrate correlate, in the structure REE is modelling, which is about as good as the direction of F_low < F_high is going to get.

I want to be careful about what it does not do, because this is precisely the case where grounding by association would be tempting. The asymmetry demonstrated here is one of TIMESCALE and RETRIEVABILITY -- how much training each direction takes, and how fast the pattern comes back. MECH-324's registered "slower dissolution timescale than formation timescale" is directly supported by that. But F_high > F_low is a claim about a variance GATE, a different operationalisation of "harder", and the paper measures nothing resembling an outcome-variance threshold. So the inequality's direction is supported by analogy across two constructs, and the value 0.45 -- and the ratio 0.45/0.15 -- gains no support whatsoever. It remains an uncalibrated engineering default and the claims should say so.

The more interesting finding is one MECH-323/324 do not currently accommodate. The ensemble pattern REVERSES under extinction and then RE-EMERGES on reacquisition rather than being rebuilt from scratch. Dissolution in the biology is suppression with retention, not erasure. An REE implementation that removes a chunk from the library on crossing F_high implements the wrong operation: a dissolved chunk should persist in a dormant state with a lowered re-formation threshold. As registered, neither MECH-323 nor MECH-324 specifies this, and without it REE's dissolution will look cleaner than biology's and its re-formation dynamics will be wrong even if both thresholds happen to be right.

Marked `supports` at confidence 0.70. Source quality is high (0.92 -- Nature, chronic ensemble recording, tightly correlated with behavioural performance). Mapping fidelity is the constraint at 0.55, for the timescale-versus-threshold gap above, and the transfer risk is the usual one: rodent T-maze procedural learning with food reward, where REE's outcome dimension is an E3-score-receipt over policy primitives. The summary judgement is that the hysteresis RELATION is now reasonably grounded and the hysteresis VALUES are not grounded at all -- and the reason for writing this entry at length is to make sure the second half of that sentence stays visible next to the first.
