# Empathy fractionates by content, so there is nothing for a scalar to measure

Of the three papers in this pull, this is the one that bears most directly on ARC-094's prohibition -- the clause forbidding an `empathy_enabled` flag or an `empathy_score` primitive -- as opposed to its constructive mechanism.

Zhang and colleagues ran an activation likelihood estimation meta-analysis over thirty-five fMRI studies, splitting them by the kind of distress being observed. Empathy for another's *physical* pain produced significant left-lateralised activation in mirror-neuron-system regions. Empathy for another's *social* pain selectively engaged the mentalizing network. The differential analysis detected no shared activated areas between the two subtypes.

The architectural consequence is straightforward. If empathy for a broken arm and empathy for social rejection run on non-overlapping networks, then "empathy" is not one faculty with one magnitude. It is a family of responses assembled from whichever streams the content of the other's state makes relevant -- action and body machinery for bodily harm, mentalizing machinery for social harm. An implementation exposing a single `empathy_score` would be summarising across two systems that do not share an activated region, and the resulting number would correspond to no variable actually implemented in either. That is a stronger argument than the usual appeal to parsimony: it is not that a scalar is inelegant, it is that there is no quantity for it to denote.

This also converges with the other two entries from a different direction. Lamm et al. showed the recruited network shifts with the *route* of access -- picture versus abstract cue. Zhang et al. show it shifts with the *content* of the other's state. Krishnan et al. show the representations are dissociable even where the regions overlap. Three independent methods, three different partitions, and all three say the same thing: there is no stable, unitary empathy channel. ARC-094's prohibition survives all three. Its positive mechanism, as I noted in the Krishnan entry, needs restating.

The main caution is that the headline finding is a null. "No shared activated brain areas detected" is a conjunction failure in a thresholded ALE, and ALE conjunction sensitivity depends on how many studies contribute to each subtype and on cluster-forming thresholds. Complete non-overlap between two empathy conditions both of which are known to engage anterior insula in the wider literature is, frankly, likely to be an overstatement -- the true picture is probably partial dissociation rather than the clean separation the abstract implies. That does not undo the architectural point, which needs only substantial fractionation rather than total, but it does mean this should not be cited as establishing that the two systems are disjoint.

Two further limits. This is coordinate-based meta-analysis, so it inherits exactly the resolution problem Krishnan et al. exposed: it can say which regions engage, not whether representations are shared. And the physical-versus-social split is a distinction in the *target's* state, whereas ARC-094's self -> object -> other is a distinction in the referent of the affective stream. Those are different partitions. This paper tests the first and is silent on the second, which remains the least-evidenced element of the claim across this whole pull -- the object stage in particular has no correlate anywhere in this literature.

Confidence 0.68. Recent, adequately powered, and pointed at the right clause of the claim, discounted for resting its key result on a null and for using a partition that is not ARC-094's own.
