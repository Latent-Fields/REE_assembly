# Constructive episodic simulation as the biology of ARC-085's single event store

**Schacter & Addis (2007), *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B* 362(1481):773-786. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2007.2087)**
Claims grounded: **ARC-085** (primary), **MECH-365** (secondary).
*According to PubMed* (PMID 17395575).

## What the paper does

This is the canonical review-statement of the **constructive episodic simulation hypothesis**. Schacter and Addis assemble cognitive, neuropsychological and neuroimaging evidence for a single thesis: episodic memory is constructive rather than reproductive, and the same flexible system that recombines stored fragments to reconstruct the past is what lets us *simulate* the future. They take the system's well-documented error-proneness — memory distortions, false recognition, intrusions — not as a bug but as the signature of an adaptive design: a store that recombines elements rather than replaying fixed records is necessarily one that can mis-bind them. The headline empirical regularity is the large overlap, across behaviour, amnesic deficits, and fMRI/PET activation, between *remembering the past* and *imagining the future*.

## Why it grounds ARC-085

ARC-085 is the architectural commitment that retrospective replay (ARC-007) and prospective simulation (ARC-018) are not two parallel memory machineries but **one self-tagged event-token store** — replay reinstates tokens backward, simulation re-composes the same tokens forward. The constructive-episodic-simulation literature is the most direct biological warrant REE has for that commitment. "Flexibly extract and recombine elements of previous experiences" is exactly "forward re-composition of the same event tokens." Patients with episodic amnesia who also cannot richly imagine the future, and the shared default-network recruitment in both tasks, are the phenomena ARC-085 is trying to be the substrate-level explanation of.

## Where the mapping strains (honest boundary)

The evidence is for a **shared process / shared network**, and that is strictly weaker than ARC-085's **single store**. A two-store architecture whose stores share upstream constructive and scene-construction machinery predicts the same imaging overlap and the same correlated amnesic deficits. So this paper anchors ARC-085's *premise* (the two functions are deeply entangled) but does not by itself reach ARC-085's *conclusion* (they are literally one store). ARC-085's own falsifier — corrupt the shared store and watch past-recall and future-simulation fidelity degrade *together*, versus the independent degradation a two-store model predicts — is the discriminating test, and nothing here runs it. That falsifier is a V4 experiment, not a literature finding; this entry raises literature_confidence only and promotes nothing.

The second strain is the one MECH-365 exists to handle: the constructive system is *intrinsically* confabulation-prone. Schacter and Addis treat that as adaptive, but for REE it is precisely the hazard the one-way committed-vs-imagined gate must contain. So the paper supports MECH-365's *motivation* (a flexible recombinatorial store will leak imagined content into the record unless something stops it) without evidencing the gate mechanism itself.

## Confidence

0.78. A high-prestige, originator-authored, heavily-replicated review — source quality is not in question. The discount is mapping fidelity: shared-machinery evidence underdetermines the single-store architectural commitment, and the transfer from human episodic cognition to a synthetic event-token substrate is by analogy. Strong, directionally clear support for the premise; not a closed case for the architecture.
