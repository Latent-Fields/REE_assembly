# Reality monitoring (Johnson & Raye, 1981)

**Claim grounded:** MECH-366 (switchable episodic perspective tag) — the *imagined-vs-experienced* facet of the viewpoint label, as distinct from the field/observer spatial facet.

## What the paper did

Johnson and Raye proposed a working model for a problem we mostly solve without noticing: how does a person tell, after the fact, whether a memory is of something that actually happened to them or of something they only imagined, reasoned about, or dreamed? Their answer is that memories of externally derived (perceived) events and internally generated (imagined) events differ *qualitatively* — perceived memories tend to be richer in perceptual and contextual detail, internally generated ones carry more traces of the cognitive operations that produced them — and that a reconstructive decision process reads those attributes to attribute a source. Crucially, the same model predicts the characteristic *failures*: when an imagined event happens to be perceptually vivid, it can be misattributed as real.

## Key findings relevant to the claim

The load-bearing point for MECH-366 is that imagined-vs-experienced is not a stored ground-truth flag but a *represented, decidable property* recovered from the memory's attributes at retrieval. That is exactly the shape MECH-366 wants for a viewpoint label: a property *of the episode* that the system reads (and can re-read) rather than an immutable fact baked in at encoding. The task framed MECH-366 as carrying an "imagined-vs-experienced viewpoint label," and reality monitoring is the canonical mechanism for that axis.

## How it translates to REE

This entry grounds the *second* of MECH-366's two viewpoint dimensions. The field/observer spatial perspective is covered by St. Jacques (switchability), Rice & Rubin (separability), and Nigro & Neisser (existence). Reality monitoring covers the orthogonal imagined-vs-experienced axis — whether the episode is re-lived *as* perceived-and-committed or *as* imagined/simulated. Both are "viewpoint labels" on the same event token, along different dimensions, and both are distinct from SD-005's self-vs-world *content* split, which is the distinction the task asked me to secure.

The honest caveat, which I have logged rather than buried, is that this axis overlaps the provenance machinery REE already homes elsewhere: MECH-365's committed-vs-imagined gate and MECH-430's multi-dimensional source vector. Reality monitoring is, in effect, the biology behind those claims as much as behind MECH-366. So this entry should not be read as *independent* evidence — it grounds a facet MECH-366 shares with the provenance cluster, and I deliberately kept its confidence modest to avoid double-counting. It also carries the same weakener the MECH-365 Johnson-1993 entry already recorded: because the attribution is reconstructive and fallible (imagined content *can* be misremembered as experienced), biology grounds the *existence* of the label but leans against the *absoluteness* of a one-way committed-vs-imagined gate. REE's gate is the normatively safer-than-the-brain idealisation.
