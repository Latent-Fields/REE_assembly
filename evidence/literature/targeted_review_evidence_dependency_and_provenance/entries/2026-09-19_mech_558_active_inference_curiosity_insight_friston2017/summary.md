# Friston et al. 2017 -- a novelty bound, not evidence

This entry is unusual and I want the reason on the record rather than inferable, because a future session
reading `claim_ids_tested: ["MECH-558"]` could easily mistake it for evidence about MECH-558.

The chip routed this anchor as one that "bounds the DEFERRED aha claim". The history: the 2026-09-09
cross-model-restructuring thought drafted a mechanism claim
(`representation.cross_model_restructuring_insight_candidate`) and deliberately did not register it. The
intake's section 5 gives four reasons, the first of which is that "its general form is pre-empted by Friston
et al. 2017". The one clause nothing in REE owned -- self-confirmation as the representational-grain form of
evidential double-counting -- was instead held as a sentence in MECH-558's notes, "so it is not lost to
prose". MECH-558 is therefore the only registered claim this citation attaches to, and it attaches
bibliographically rather than evidentially.

The citation verifies cleanly. `neco_a_00999` is doi 10.1162/neco_a_00999: *Active Inference, Curiosity and
Insight*, Friston, Lin, Frith, Pezzulo, Hobson and Ondobaka, **Neural Computation** 29(10):2633-2683, 2017,
PMID 28777724. And it says what it was cited as saying. The paper gives a formal account of curiosity and
insight in active inference, shows that minimising expected variational free energy drives active sampling of
novel contingencies, and then moves from epistemic learning to structure learning, where "Bayesian model
reduction evinces mechanisms associated with sleep and has all the hallmarks of 'aha' moments". The deferral
reasoning was sound: a general claim that insight is cross-model restructuring which reduces the number of
independent rules required is, in its general form, this paper.

Why direction is `unknown` and confidence only 0.3. MECH-558's content is independence-aware convergence over
heterogeneous evaluators -- that the epistemic weight of agreement depends on how many effectively
independent evidential routes produced it, and that a coarse representation of dependency *topology* beats
both a marginal-reliability combiner and an effective-source scalar. Friston et al. address none of that.
There is no treatment of evidential double-counting, no dependency between evaluators, no independence
correction. Scoring this as `supports` would inflate MECH-558's literature record with a paper that is not
about it; scoring it as `weakens` would be equally wrong. `unknown` is the honest token, and mapping fidelity
is set to 0.2 so the aggregate is not quietly pulled up by the venue's prestige.

What the entry is actually for: it makes the deferral auditable. If a later session re-opens the insight
claim -- the intake recommends a child-of-MECH-423 framing, once ladder Stage 6 has run or MECH-529/531 land
-- the novelty bound is now verified rather than asserted, and that session can scope the claim to what REE
adds beyond Bayesian model reduction instead of rediscovering the pre-emption. That is a small service, and
0.3 is roughly what it is worth.
