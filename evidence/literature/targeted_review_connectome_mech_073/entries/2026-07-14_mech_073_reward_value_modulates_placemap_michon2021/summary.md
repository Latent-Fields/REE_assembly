# Reward value reshapes the hippocampal place map itself (Michon, Krul, Sun & Kloosterman 2021)

According to PubMed, Michon, Krul, Sun & Kloosterman (*Current Biology*, 2021; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.07.058)) recorded rat dorsal-CA1 place cells trial-by-trial during acquisition of place–reward associations with varying reward size. When a goal location carried a large reward, the place map reorganized and stabilized *faster*, driven by both firing-rate changes and the appearance/disappearance of place fields; replay activity followed the same dynamics and became selectively tuned toward the behaviorally relevant, high-value experiences. Value, in other words, is not a label hung on a finished map — it changes *which fields form and stabilize, and how the map replays*.

This is the strongest support I found for MECH-073's assertion that valence is intrinsic to hippocampal map geometry rather than applied downstream. If reward magnitude sculpts the very structure of the place representation — its rate code, its field composition, its replay content — then value is entangled with the map's geometry at formation time, not merely read off it by a downstream evaluator. That is the picture MECH-073 (via ARC-007 and MECH-035) is defending.

But I have to be careful, and the caveat is what keeps this at 0.62 rather than higher. "Value modulates the map" is a genuinely weaker statement than "valence is intrinsic to the map geometry." The modulation Michon et al. observe is entirely compatible with an *extrinsic* value signal — a dopaminergic or striatal reward input acting *on* the hippocampus to reshape it. Under that reading the map is still fundamentally a spatial/value-free structure that an outside signal edits — which is closer to the downstream account MECH-073 denies than to the intrinsic one it asserts. The study shows that value *affects* the map; it does not show that value is *represented within* the map's own geometric code. Read alongside Duvelle et al. 2019 (the value-free-map counter-evidence in this same directory), the honest position is that the electrophysiology is genuinely split — which is exactly why the backlog flags MECH-073 as active_conflict with a conflict ratio near 0.57.

Scored **supports** at **0.62**: high-quality single-unit electrophysiology directly on the hippocampal map, mapping fidelity capped because value-modulates-dynamics underdetermines valence-is-intrinsic-geometry.
