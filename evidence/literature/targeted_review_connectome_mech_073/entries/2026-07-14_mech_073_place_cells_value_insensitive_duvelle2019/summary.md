# Place cells encode a value-free map; value lives downstream (Duvelle et al. 2019)

According to PubMed, Duvelle, Grieves, Hok, Poucet, Arleo, Jeffery & Save (*Journal of Neuroscience*, 2019; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1578-18.2018)) built a task specifically to dissociate a spatial goal from reward consumption: rats navigated to one of two freely-chosen unmarked locations and waited to trigger reward delivered *elsewhere*, with the two goals differing in reward amount. Behaviorally the rats clearly processed value — they preferred the higher-value goal. But the neural result is the sharp one: dorsal CA1 *and* CA3 place cells were **not** modulated by goal value. There was goal-related out-of-field firing (replicating prior goal-encoding reports), but no value signal. The authors' conclusion is stated plainly — place cells "provide a spontaneously constructed value-free spatial representation" and form a map that "links to such aspects in other parts of the brain."

This is the most direct counter-evidence to MECH-073 in the file, and it is why the claim sits in active_conflict. MECH-073 asserts valence is intrinsic to hippocampal map geometry, *not* applied downstream. Duvelle et al. instantiate the opposite architecture almost verbatim: a value-free hippocampal spatial map, with value supplied by downstream structures (the paper points to the strongly-interconnected ventral striatum as the natural locus). Where Michon et al. 2021 (same directory) show value reshaping the map, Duvelle et al. — with a cleaner dissociation of goal from reward — find the map itself value-insensitive. The two are not trivially reconcilable, and the conflict ratio of ~0.57 is honestly earned.

The mapping caveat is the one place MECH-073 can still breathe, and I want to record it fairly rather than explain it away. This is *dorsal* hippocampus, and "value" here is external reward *magnitude* at a spatial goal. MECH-073, routed through ARC-007/MECH-035, may be scoped to a broader or more ventral/affective valence woven into map geometry — the ventral hippocampus is the pole classically associated with emotional/valence coding, and this task does not probe it. So the weakening is strong and direct *for the dorsal-spatial-external-reward case*, but it does not by itself refute an intrinsic-valence claim scoped to affective or ventral maps. The productive next step this suggests is a targeted probe (the backlog's `next_action` is exactly "run targeted experimental probe") that pins MECH-073's scope: is the intrinsic-valence claim about the dorsal spatial map (where this paper refutes it) or the ventral affective map (where it is untested)?

Scored **weakens** at **0.70**: well-controlled goal-vs-reward dissociation, high source quality, high-value direct counter-evidence; mapping fidelity trimmed slightly by the dorsal/external-reward scoping that leaves MECH-073 an affective-valence escape hatch.
