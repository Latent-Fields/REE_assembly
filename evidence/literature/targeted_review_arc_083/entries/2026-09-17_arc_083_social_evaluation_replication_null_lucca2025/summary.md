# Infants' Social Evaluation of Helpers and Hinderers: A Multi-Lab Replication (Lucca et al., 2025)

## Why a null result is in an evidence pull

I went looking for a falsifier. DEV-NEED-021 says otherness inference requires object permanence and
self-stability, and the cleanest way to break that is to find otherness inference arriving *first*.
The best-known candidate was Hamlin, Wynn & Bloom (2007, *Nature* 450, 557-559): six-month-olds
preferring a puppet that helped a third party over one that hindered it. Six months is before
manual-search object permanence and more than a year before mirror self-recognition. If infants that
young evaluate agents on their conduct toward other agents, DEV-NEED-021's ordering is simply wrong.

So I checked whether it holds. It does not. Thirty-seven laboratories on five continents,
preregistered, 1018 infants tested and 567 analysed: 49.34% preferred Helpers over Hinderers. Chance.
The inanimate control condition came out at 55.85%, also not distinguishable from chance. Hamlin is a
co-author on the replication of her own finding, which is worth saying plainly because it is the
strongest available signal that the null is not a failure of method or motivation.

## What this does and does not do for ARC-083

It removes the counterexample. That is all it does, and I have set the confidence at 0.45 and the
`mapping_fidelity` at 0.35 to make sure the entry cannot be read as doing more.

The distinction matters because this is the only `supports` entry among the four added in this pull,
and an aggregate reading of the directory will find a 3-to-1 weakening split with one supporting
counterweight. That framing would be wrong. The three weakening entries produce positive evidence
against the prerequisite — a matched-behaviour cross-cultural dissociation, a mediation null in a
controlled longitudinal design, a reversed ordering on the permanence half. This entry produces no
positive evidence for it. It restores the *absence* of contradiction from one direction, and absence
of contradiction is not support. ARC-083's prerequisite is exactly as unevidenced after this paper as
before it; it is merely no longer contradicted by Hamlin 2007.

The scope is narrow in another way too. This is a null on the hill paradigm. Infant goal attribution,
gaze following, and agent individuation are untouched, and any of those could still turn out to
precede object permanence. The early-otherness question is open; only this particular route into it
is closed.

## The calibration warning, which I think is the more useful contribution

A flagship *Nature* result, cited thousands of times, taught in every developmental course, did not
survive a properly powered test. That should change how the rest of this directory is read.

Look at what the other entries rest on. Bischof-Köhler's self-recognition-to-empathy link — the
canonical source of the dependency ARC-083 is carrying — comes from 126 children in a programme run
by one investigator, with no independent multi-lab replication of the kind that just dismantled
Hamlin 2007. Slaughter & Boh is seventeen completing infants on a question the field abandoned
unresolved. Kärtner et al. is 38 and 39. Sodian & Kristen-Antonow, the best of them, is 83.

None of these have been through what the hill paradigm has just been through. I do not know which of
them would survive it, and neither does anyone else. The honest position is that REE is currently
carrying an architectural dependency — one that gates a V4 pillar and shapes a whole V5 plan's
`readiness_gate` structure — on a developmental literature whose most famous result in this area just
evaporated under scrutiny. That is a statement about the *reliability of the evidence base*, not
about the truth of the claim, and it is the thing I would most want a future governance session to
know before it treats any of these four entries as settling anything.

## Confidence

0.45. Source quality 0.95 — nothing else in this directory is close. Mapping fidelity 0.35 — nothing
else in this directory is lower. That gap is the entry: methodologically the strongest paper here,
evidentially the most oblique. The aggregate weights mapping fidelity far above source quality, which
is what the interface contract's guidance asks for on an architectural claim, and is the same
weighting the three earlier hippocampal entries applied when they held near-ceiling *Science* papers
at 0.60.
