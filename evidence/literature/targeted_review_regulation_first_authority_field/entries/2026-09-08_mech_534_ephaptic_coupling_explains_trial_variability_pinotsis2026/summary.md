# Pinotsis & Miller 2026 -- the field pushes back harder than it is pushed

Cerebral Cortex 36(6):bhag098, DOI 10.1093/cercor/bhag098, PMID 42411828, 2 June 2026. The chip asked me to check whether MECH-499 or MECH-500's existing Pinotsis anchors already cover this, so it should not be duplicated. They do not. The nearest existing REE coverage is under MECH-270, which holds Anastassiou et al. 2011 on ephaptic coupling of cortical neurons and two 2010 papers on endogenous field effects; MECH-499 holds communication-through-coherence and gamma synchronisation work. This 2026 paper is new to the corpus.

It is also the most directly relevant item in the authority-field bibliography. Cortical oscillatory power waxes and wanes across trials, and that variability is usually put down to neuromodulation, uncertainty encoding or shifting excitability. Analysing prefrontal LFP during a spatial delayed-saccade task, the authors report that it is also driven by fluctuations in the ephaptic influence of mesoscale electric fields, and -- the load-bearing detail -- that field-to-neuron coupling was stronger than neuron-to-field, and tracked the trial-by-trial power changes. Their reading is circular causality: activity and extracellular field continuously shape one another.

For MECH-534 the directional asymmetry is the point. If the field were merely a summary of the spiking beneath it, the coupling would run the other way and the field would be a readout. Measured as stronger in the field-to-neuron direction, it behaves as a medium with its own state that acts on the units it connects, which is what an authority field would have to be.

Two restraints, one of which is uncomfortable for the claim. The analysis is a single dataset with model-based coupling estimation, and circular causality is inferred from coupling strengths rather than from intervening on the field. More importantly, the alternatives the authors set aside -- neuromodulation, uncertainty encoding, excitability -- are not excluded, and in REE terms those are precisely the existing precision machinery that MECH-534's notes decline to claim is insufficient. A REE implementation producing coupling variability from a precision signal alone would fit this data just as well. Supports at 0.62, and the claim's own brake stays on.
