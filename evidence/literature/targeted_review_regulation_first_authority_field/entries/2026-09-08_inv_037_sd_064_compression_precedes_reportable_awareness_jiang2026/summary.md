# Jiang et al. 2026 -- the structure is compressed before it is reportable

bioRxiv, DOI 10.64898/2026.08.22.746376, posted 26 August 2026. The citation in the intake is correct; the paper exists and says what it was said to say. It is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed, which matters for how it is used.

Participants learned category-based triplet rules while EEG and MEG were recorded, and the question was what changes in the neural format when implicit knowledge becomes reportable. The finding is that the compression is already there beforehand: implicit sequence knowledge "was already represented in an abstract, compressed format", with rule information generalising across triplet elements and regular-sequence elements becoming more similar while perceptual-category structure stayed separable. The authors define a compression rate for how efficiently local rule information enters that shared format, and its pre-awareness level predicts later awareness in the P3 window. Source imaging puts the pre-transition compression in left middle temporal cortex, with expansion into prefrontal cortex after awareness, and rule-specific theta transfer from temporal to prefrontal regions before the transition also predicts it.

The chip routes this at INV-037 and SD-064 as a lit-anchor candidate only, and that is the right ceiling. The attractive mapping is that competence precedes inspectability: the representation is structurally adequate and effective before the system can report on it, and what changes is routing and territory rather than content. That is INV-037's distinction between stored-and-retrievable and active-in-the-navigable-state, with a measured human instance.

Three cautions. Nothing here is causal; compression is measured, not manipulated. Nothing here demonstrates a capacity-limited bottleneck, which is what SD-064 actually asserts -- no competition between candidate contents, no capacity limit, no demonstration that the broadcast does work. And the predictor lives in the P3 window, which is this literature's own operationalisation of conscious access, so using it as independent evidence about a broadcast architecture risks arguing in a circle. Banked at 0.5 as a candidate anchor.
