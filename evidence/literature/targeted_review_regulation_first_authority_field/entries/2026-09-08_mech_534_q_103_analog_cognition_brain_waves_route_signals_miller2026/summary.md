# Miller, Brincat & Roy 2026 -- analog cognition, and why it frames Q-103 rather than answering it

Journal of Neuroscience 46(33):e0711262026, DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0711-26.2026, PMID 42618509, PMC13492466, 19 August 2026. The thought's citation, article number and all, is exact.

The thesis is the one MECH-534 builds on: cognition and consciousness may arise from bidirectional interactions between neuronal spiking and rhythmic electric field activity. Waves are not epiphenomenal readouts of spiking; they act back on it, supporting analog computation and flexibly routing signals so that the same neurons take different roles in different contexts. Goal-directed behaviour is cast as top-down control orchestrating populations through low-dimensional task dynamics, and the paper's closing move is that goal-directed thought, action and unified consciousness emerge from cortex-wide wave dynamics.

That is the clearest external statement of the authority-field premise -- coupling between representational regions with its own fast dynamics, rather than a static gate -- and MECH-534 may cite it as such. What it cannot do is discharge the claim's falsifier. MECH-534's primary test is against the static-gate alternative, and asserting that the field is dynamic is not testing whether a static gate would do as well. The claim's own notes are careful about this ("do not infer yet that an authority/gating mechanism is NEEDED beyond existing precision, policy or control-plane machinery"), and this paper does not change that.

On Q-103 I want to be precise, because it would be easy to over-read. Q-103 asks whether the coupling state itself carries task-relevant relational structure or only sets eligibility and gain. This paper asserts both branches. "Flexibly route neural signals" is routing-only language; "unified consciousness emerges from cortex-wide wave dynamics" is representational language. Quoting either half as settling the fork would be selective reading. What the paper does is establish that the fork is a live question among people who study the biology, which is worth having.
