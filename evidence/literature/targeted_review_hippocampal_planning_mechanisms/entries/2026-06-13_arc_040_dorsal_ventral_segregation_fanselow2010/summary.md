# Dorsal/ventral hippocampal functional segregation (Fanselow & Dong 2010) — ARC-040

**Claim:** ARC-040 — the REE hippocampal module requires a dorsal-predominant trajectory proposer (value-free spatial navigation) combined with a ventral-analog valence prior sourced from R(x,t) geometry; these are architecturally segregated, not merged.

## What the paper did

Fanselow and Dong took up a long-standing tension in the literature: one tradition treats the hippocampus as a purely cognitive memory structure, another as an emotion regulator whose dysfunction produces psychopathology. They argued that both are right — of different parts of the same structure. Reviewing behavioural, anatomical, and gene-expression evidence together, they proposed a functional segmentation along the hippocampal long axis into dorsal, intermediate, and ventral compartments. The **dorsal** hippocampus (posterior in primates) does primarily *cognitive* work — spatial memory and navigation. The **ventral** (anterior in primates) relates to *stress, emotion, and affect*. The connectivity backs this: dorsal gene expression correlates with cortical regions involved in information processing, while ventral gene expression correlates with the amygdala and hypothalamus — the affective/autonomic machinery.

## Why it matters for REE

This is the direct biological warrant for ARC-040's central architectural commitment. ARC-040 asserts that the hippocampal module must keep a *value-free spatial trajectory proposer* (dorsal-analog) architecturally **segregated** from a *valence prior* (ventral-analog), rather than collapsing them into one undifferentiated module. Fanselow and Dong supply exactly that segregation, and — crucially — ground it in *connectivity*: the dorsal stream wires to cortical processing regions, the ventral stream to amygdala/hypothalamus. That connectivity split is the reason the segregation is not cosmetic but functional, and it is the warrant for REE keeping navigation and valence as distinct streams (which is also what ARC-007's no-new-value constraint requires — the dorsal proposer must navigate existing terrain value-free, with valence entering as a segregated prior, not injected into the proposer).

## Caveats and confidence

Two boundaries. First, the segregation is a *gradient* (dorsal/intermediate/ventral) with overlap and a continuous transition, not a clean binary — REE's two-stream idealisation is a simplification of a graded axis. Second, and more specific: the paper establishes *that* the ventral pole carries affective function, but it does not commit that function to being an **accumulated valence prior sourced from a residue-field geometry**, which is ARC-040's particular computational claim. The biology supports the *segregation*; the *accumulated-prior-from-R(x,t)* mechanism is REE's design choice layered on top, and would need its own validation. With those caveats this is strong, low-transfer-risk support (the review includes explicit primate homology). Confidence 0.80 (supports).
