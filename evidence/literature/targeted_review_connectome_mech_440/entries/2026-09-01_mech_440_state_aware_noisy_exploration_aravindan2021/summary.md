# State-Aware Variational Thompson Sampling for Deep Q-Networks (Aravindan & Lee, 2021)

This entry earns its place less through its own results than through what its existence demonstrates. The authors derive a variational Thompson sampling approximation for deep Q-networks in which exploration comes from perturbing parameters with a learned noise distribution, and they interpret NoisyNets as an approximation to that variational method. They then propose State Aware Noisy Exploration -- SANE -- to improve on NoisyNets "by allowing a non-uniform perturbation, where the amount of parameter perturbation is conditioned on the state of the agent", implemented through an auxiliary perturbation module whose output is state dependent. Their stated motivation is that uniform, non-state-conditioned perturbation causes failures in states where exploration is risky.

The argument for MECH-440 is short and, I think, hard to escape. MECH-440's leg (ii) says the floor is "STATE-CONDITIONED by construction (weight noise x state-dependent activations perturbs different states differently, with no hand-set per-state schedule)". If that were true in the sense the claim needs, SANE would not be a contribution. A subsequent paper in the same line found it necessary to bolt on an explicitly state-dependent auxiliary module to obtain state-conditioned perturbation *magnitude*. The "by construction" argument is not wrong that activations differ by state -- it is wrong that this yields a controlled, usable dependence of perturbation magnitude on state, which is the property leg (ii) actually asserts and which V3-EXQ-959's C_STATE criterion actually tested.

It is worth being precise about how well that matches the experiment, because the correspondence is closer than it first looks. C_STATE asked whether noise-driven divergence concentrates in near-tie states, and found it did so on 2 of 6 seeds against a 4/6 bar, with the trend running in the predicted direction on 4 of 6 and reversed on 2. The autopsy's supporting observation is the more telling one: across all twelve relevant cells the difference in divergence rate between decisive and near-tie strata never exceeded 0.1031 against base rates of 0.68-0.91. Noise flips the committed argmin at close to the same rate whether the agent is confident or nearly tied. That is what "not usefully conditioned on state" looks like in measurement, and it is the same deficiency SANE was written to fix.

I have kept confidence at 0.78 and I would not want it read as higher than the evidence supports, for two reasons. First, this is an argument from a paper's framing of its own novelty, not from an experiment measuring whether NoisyNet's perturbation magnitude covaries with state; SANE's authors have an interest in distinguishing their contribution, and a reader could fairly object that they may understate what NoisyNets already achieves. Second, I could not verify a peer-reviewed venue for this preprint, and I did not read its experimental section closely enough to report benchmark results. Weight it as a corroborating architectural observation that supports the Fortunato re-read -- the two together say that the source algorithm establishes neither leg (ii) nor leg (iii) -- rather than as standalone empirical refutation.
