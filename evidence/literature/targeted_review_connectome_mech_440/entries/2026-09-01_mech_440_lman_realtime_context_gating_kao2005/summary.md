# Contributions of an avian basal ganglia-forebrain circuit to real-time modulation of song (Kao, Doupe & Brainard, 2005)

Songbirds are the cleanest natural experiment available on the question MECH-440 is really asking, which is where the variability in a selected action comes from and what sets its size. This paper brings three methods to bear on the same circuit: microstimulation in the anterior forebrain pathway produces specific changes in learned features of song; fluctuations in the circuit's activity correlate with song variability; and lesioning the pathway's output nucleus prevents the natural modulation of song variability while leaving song itself intact. That last result is the one that matters here, and it is a causal one -- remove the channel and you remove the modulation, not the behaviour.

This directory already carries an Olveczky 2011 entry making the dedicated-channel point, so I have written this one specifically for what it adds, which is timescale. The modulation demonstrated here is *real-time*: variability is gated moment to moment by the state of the circuit. That is the fast, state-conditioned regulation of variability. The same anatomical channel is also the one that, over a developmental timescale of weeks, withdraws as song crystallizes -- the slow annealing. So biology runs both regimes through one channel, but by way of different control signals acting *on* that channel, not as two emergent properties of a single learned parameter inside the motor pathway. That is the same architecture Dhawale et al. later characterise quantitatively as a fast outcome-driven process whose gain is set by a slower one, and it is worth noting the two results come from overlapping groups working on the same system.

Translated to REE, the argument is that noise magnitude should be an *input* to the selection head, arriving from a separable gating pathway, rather than a property the selection head's own parameters are expected to discover. MECH-440's substrate as built injects factorised-Gaussian weight noise directly into `_modulatory_accum` with sigma annealed by a local confidence EMA -- which is the right locus for propagation (leg i, and the V3-EXQ-687 fix) but is asking the injection site to also be the control site. On this evidence those are two different things, and the biology separates them.

Confidence is 0.75, held down by two honest limits. The first is overlap: part of what this entry says is already carried by the existing Olveczky record, and it should be read as adding the real-time-gating timescale rather than as independent corroboration of the dedicated-channel claim. The second is the usual songbird caveat, which is substantial. Song variability is continuous acoustic variability in a highly specialised, partly innate, developmentally scheduled learned motor sequence. MECH-440's observable is categorical entropy over committed actions in an artificial trajectory selector, with no developmental schedule and no crystallization. The claim that a lesionable gating channel is the right REE analogue is my architectural reading of the result, not something the paper asserts about artificial agents, and a reader who thought the analogy strained would not be being unreasonable.
