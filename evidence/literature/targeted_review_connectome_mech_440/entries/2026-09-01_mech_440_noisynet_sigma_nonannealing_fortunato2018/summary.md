# Noisy Networks for Exploration -- a re-read of MECH-440's own source (Fortunato et al., 2018)

This entry deliberately revisits a paper already in this directory. On 2026-06-29 it was entered as `2026-06-29_mech_440_noisynet_fortunato2018`, filed `supports` at 0.85, with the mapping stating that MECH-440's floor "self-anneals via a gradient-trained sigma" and is "state-conditioned by construction". Having gone back to the paper's own analysis section rather than its abstract, I do not think that reading survives, and rather than quietly amend the earlier record I have written a second one so the correction is visible and the audit trail intact.

The relevant passage is the paper's analysis of how the noise parameters actually evolve. Reporting the mean absolute noise parameter across three seeds on five Atari games, the authors write: "We observe that Sigma-bar of the last layer of the network decreases as the learning proceeds in all cases, whereas in the case of the penultimate layer this only happens for 2 games out of 5 (Pong and Beam rider) and in the remaining 3 games Sigma-bar in fact increases. This shows that in the case of NoisyNet-DQN the agent does not necessarily evolve towards a deterministic solution as one might have expected." They add that how sigma evolves "significantly differs from one game to another and in some cases from one seed to another seed", and characterise the result as NoisyNet producing a *problem-specific* exploration strategy.

Three things follow, and they map cleanly onto MECH-440's three legs. Leg (i), propagation, is genuinely what this paper demonstrates and is untouched: noise injected into the weights changes which action is argmax, which is the injection-site correction MECH-440 was right to import. Leg (iii), self-annealing, is not licensed -- the authors looked for it, found it in one layer and not another, in two games of five with the direction reversed in three, and drew the opposite conclusion to the one MECH-440 attributes to them. Leg (ii), state-conditioning, is not licensed either, though for a subtler reason: "problem-specific" is not "state-specific", and the paper reports no per-state analysis of sigma anywhere. The claim's "state-conditioned by construction" is an architectural assertion about weight noise interacting with state-dependent activations, and the source offers no measurement bearing on it.

The consequence is not that MECH-440 is refuted by this paper -- it is that leg (iii) never had the external support the claim recorded, and a load-bearing part of the registration rests on a misreading of the cited source. That reclassifies the leg from "inherited from the external analog" to "an independent REE hypothesis owing its own evidence". Which is precisely the status V3-EXQ-959 then tested, on six seeds with a proven instrument control, and did not support: a real ~21% reduction in sigma_scale_mean moved whole-arm yoked divergence by 9.1e-4, and the single passing seed was the least-annealed one. Read together, the source never established the property and the experiment looked for it and did not find it. That is a much cleaner evidential picture than either datum alone.

Two honest limits on how hard this should be pushed. The sigma analysis is a short observational section over five games and three seeds, not a controlled test, so it would be an over-read to conclude that learned sigma never anneals. And the reported quantity is a mean absolute noise parameter over a whole layer, which cannot exclude individual parameters annealing while the layer mean rises. The force of the entry is narrower than "self-annealing does not work" and sufficient for the governance question: MECH-440 asserted self-annealing on this paper's authority, and this paper measured it and reported it unreliable. There is also a seed-dependence point with direct methodological bite -- if the direction of sigma evolution varies by seed in the source algorithm, then a pre-registered directional prediction about annealing across six seeds needs a per-seed manipulation-engagement gate, whose absence the V3-EXQ-959 autopsy independently flagged as a real design gap.
