# Aston-Jones & Cohen (2005) — An integrative theory of locus coeruleus-norepinephrine function: adaptive gain and optimal performance

**Claim tested:** MECH-440 (state-conditioned self-annealing tonic exploration noise floor)
**Direction:** supports · **Confidence:** 0.76
**Source:** *Annual Review of Neuroscience* 28:403–450. According to PubMed (PMID 16022602), [DOI 10.1146/annurev.neuro.28.061604.135709](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.neuro.28.061604.135709). (A closely parallel exposition appears in *J Comp Neurol* 493(1):99–110, PMID 16254995, [DOI 10.1002/cne.20723](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.20723).)

## What the theory says

Aston-Jones & Cohen took the locus coeruleus out of its historical box ("arousal") and recast it as a controller of the exploration–exploitation trade-off. The key observation is that LC neurons operate in two modes. In the **phasic** mode, LC fires in tight, outcome-locked bursts following task-relevant decisions; this sharpens responding and supports *exploitation* of the current task. In the **tonic** mode, baseline LC firing is elevated and phasic responses blunted; behaviour becomes labile, the animal disengages from the current task and *searches for alternatives* — i.e., explores. Crucially, they propose that anterior cingulate and orbitofrontal cortex, which monitor task-related utility, *drive* the LC between these modes to optimise utility over both short and long timescales. They call this "adaptive gain": NE adjusts the responsivity (gain) of target circuits as a function of how well the current strategy is paying off.

## Why it supports MECH-440

MECH-440 posits a *tonic LC-NE exploration floor* that is state-conditioned and self-annealing. Aston-Jones & Cohen are the canonical theoretical statement of why such a floor should exist and why its level should track policy utility: tonic NE *is* the standing exploration drive, and it rises as the utility of the current task wanes. The framework also names the cortical monitors — ACC and OFC — that set the floor, and these are exactly the dACC/lPFC/OFC analogs that feed REE's selection. So at the level of *why a utility-contingent tonic exploration floor is the biologically right object*, this paper is the conceptual backbone. It was the second pillar (alongside Tervo et al. 2014) cited at the MECH-440 adjudication's biology-before-formal-definitions gate (supports 0.78).

## The caveat that keeps it at 0.76 — and why it actually sharpens MECH-440's design choice

There is a genuine tension worth being honest about. Adaptive gain is, in its own terms, a *global* responsivity modulation — NE turns the gain of target circuits up or down broadly. That is conceptually *closer to a temperature/gain knob than to learned per-parameter weight noise*. And a temperature-style construct is exactly what MECH-440's predecessor, MECH-313, already was — and what V3-EXQ-687 already falsified as **non-propagating** (the post-softmax temperature was invisible to the committed argmax). So one must not over-read Aston-Jones & Cohen as endorsing MECH-440's *specific* implementation. They endorse the *function* (a utility-tracking tonic exploration drive); they do not adjudicate between the temperature route (falsified) and the per-parameter-weight-noise route (MECH-440's choice). If anything, the gap between "global gain" and "propagates into the committed action" is the very gap MECH-440 was designed to cross — which is why the NoisyNet entry, not this one, carries the implementation weight.

A second, subtler mismatch: the tonic mode is described as *disengagement from the current task and search for alternatives*. That reads more like goal-disengagement or task-switching than like increasing the *diversity of committed actions within* a task. The F-dominance ceiling (MECH-439) is a within-task committed-action-diversity problem, so mapping "tonic NE → abandon the task" onto "tonic NE → more diverse commitments inside the task" is a possible mis-step to guard against when interpreting any PASS.

Net: solid conceptual support for the *existence and utility-contingency* of a tonic exploration floor, discounted because it is a theory review rather than causal primary data (Tervo carries the causal load), and because its "global gain" framing is closer to the route MECH-440 explicitly rejected than to the one it adopted. Mapping fidelity 0.72, transfer risk 0.45, aggregate 0.76.
