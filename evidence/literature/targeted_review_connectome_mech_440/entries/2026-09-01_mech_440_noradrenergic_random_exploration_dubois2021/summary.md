# Human complex exploration strategies are enriched by noradrenaline-modulated heuristics (Dubois, Habicht, Michely, Moran, Dolan & Hauser, 2021)

Of everything in this pull, this is the study with the strongest claim on our attention methodologically. It is double-blind and placebo-controlled with two active arms -- amisulpride, a dopaminergic antagonist, and propranolol, a noradrenergic antagonist -- so it is not inferring separability from a model fit or a correlation but intervening on two neuromodulator systems and observing which component of exploration moves. The finding is that value-free random exploration is attenuated under propranolol but not under amisulpride, from which the authors conclude that value-free random exploration is under noradrenergic control, sitting alongside other, more value-sensitive exploration strategies in the same subjects.

That result lands squarely on MECH-440's own biological foundations, which is why it is worth the entry. The claim is grounded in claims.yaml on the LC-NE adaptive-gain literature -- Aston-Jones & Cohen 2005 at supports 0.78, Tervo et al. 2014 at 0.82 -- and both of those are cited to establish that the tonic exploration floor is state-conditioned and annealed by controllability. This paper agrees that noradrenaline controls a stochastic exploration floor. What it adds is that the thing under noradrenergic control is a *component*: value-free random exploration, separable by drug from the directed, value-sensitive component. The LC-NE grounding survives; the bundling does not. MECH-440 is entitled to say that a tonic stochastic floor is noradrenergically gated. It is not entitled to say that the same mechanism thereby also carries state-conditioning and self-annealing, because the pharmacology dissociates the components rather than unifying them.

Read alongside Dhawale et al., the picture is consistent across levels of analysis: a behavioural decomposition in rats, a pharmacological dissociation in humans, and a lesion dissociation in songbirds all separate the injection of variability from the control of its magnitude. Three independent methods converging on the same architecture is a stronger basis for a narrowing recommendation than any one of them, and it is the reason I have been willing to state the recommendation firmly in the synthesis rather than hedging it.

The caveats are the standard ones for human psychopharmacology and they are not small. These are single acute doses in healthy volunteers. Both drugs are systemic and act far beyond the intended target -- propranolol in particular has peripheral cardiovascular effects that can alter arousal and task engagement in ways that a choice model may absorb into a noise parameter. "Value-free random exploration" is itself a fitted model component rather than a directly observed behaviour, so what is dissociated is a modelled quantity, and a sceptic could reasonably ask how much of the dissociation is carried by the model's parameterisation. Finally this is the entry furthest from MECH-440's actual observable: committed-action entropy in a trajectory selector is not a bandit choice, and I am transferring an argument about architecture, not a measurement. Confidence 0.82 reflects a strong design read against a real mapping gap.
