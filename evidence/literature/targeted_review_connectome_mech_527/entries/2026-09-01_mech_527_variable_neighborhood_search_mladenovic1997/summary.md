# Variable neighborhood search (Mladenovic & Hansen, 1997)

MECH-527 asserts that perturbation should escalate in breadth as stuckness continues and collapse once the impasse resolves. Of the claim's several commitments this is the one with no biological support anywhere in this pull -- Karlsson et al. give the decay but say nothing about escalation -- so it is worth recording that the dynamic is not merely plausible but already formalised, and has been for nearly thirty years.

Variable Neighborhood Search explores a nested sequence of neighbourhoods of increasing size around an incumbent solution. Its control structure is explicit rather than emergent: after shaking and local search within neighbourhood k, an improvement causes the search to recentre on the new incumbent and reset to the narrowest neighbourhood, while a failure to improve escalates to the next, broader one. Systematic change of neighbourhood is what lets the search leave local optima that a fixed-neighbourhood local search cannot. Escalate on stagnation, collapse on success -- which is MECH-527's escalating breadth with post-resolution decay, stated as an algorithm.

Two things follow, and the second is the more consequential for governance. First, the trigger in VNS is failure to improve after searching at the current level, not any measure of ambiguity among candidate solutions. That is an independent formal precedent for keying perturbation to non-resolution rather than to near-ties -- matching MECH-527 and pointedly not matching MECH-440. Second, and this is the reason I recommend a rename rather than merely citing the paper: because a well-known metaheuristic already has this shape and this name, presenting escalate-then-collapse as a newly identified mechanism over-claims novelty. MECH-527 is better positioned as the hypothesis that a VNS-shaped control structure operates *above the action level in a cognitive architecture*, with Karlsson-style network resets as its biological signature. That framing is more defensible and more citable, and it has the virtue of making the claim's genuinely novel content explicit: not the control structure, which is borrowed, but the assertion that a brain-like architecture implements one.

This is the weakest evidence in the pull and I want it weighted that way, which is why confidence sits at 0.70 with transfer risk deliberately unflattering at 0.70. Combinatorial optimization over an explicit solution space with a well-defined objective is not the situation an embodied agent faces. "Failure to improve" is decidable in VNS; for MECH-527 it is exactly the quantity that must be estimated noisily from experience, which is the hard part of the claim and the part the algorithm gives no help with. Nothing here is evidence that any biological system implements VNS. Under this project's biology-before-formal-definitions invariant a formal analogue must not be allowed to stand in for biological grounding, and I would resist any reading of this entry that treats the structural match as raising confidence that the mechanism is real. It is included because the escalation half would otherwise be entirely unsupported, and because a rename recommendation needs a concrete alternative to point at.
