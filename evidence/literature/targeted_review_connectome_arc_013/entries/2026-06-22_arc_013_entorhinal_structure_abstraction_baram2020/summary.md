# Entorhinal and vmPFC abstract and generalize the structure of RL problems (Baram, Muller, Nili, Garvert & Behrens, 2020)

According to PubMed, published in *Neuron*, [DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.11.024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.11.024).

**What the paper did.** Using human fMRI and a task-remapping paradigm, the authors had subjects solve several reinforcement-learning problems that differed either in their *relational structure* or only in their *sensory surface*. They then asked, with representational similarity analysis, whether the co-activation geometry in entorhinal cortex and ventromedial prefrontal cortex was preserved across problems. The headline result: entorhinal representations keep their geometry across different problems *only when the task structure is preserved* — exactly as grid-cell maps keep their structure across different physical environments. The same structure-dependence appeared in vmPFC, and prediction-error representations in vmPFC and ventral striatum also tracked task structure.

**Why it bears on ARC-013.** ARC-013 says hippocampal-formation paths form a cognitive map and that the carried information is persistent latent-space curvature. The spatial cognitive-map literature already supports the spatial case; what this paper adds is that the *same map machinery* abstracts and reuses relational structure for non-spatial, abstract problems, and that the reuse is visible as a *preserved representational geometry*. That is precisely the shape of REE's claim: the map is a structural scaffold whose content lives in the geometry, and that geometry is stable enough to transfer across superficially different problems. It is the cleanest human evidence in this pull that "the cognitive map is geometry, and the geometry is reusable."

**The mapping, honestly bounded.** Two caveats keep this from being decisive for the full ARC-013 claim. First, the measure is representational-similarity geometry in BOLD — a coarse, indirect window compared with REE's posited latent residue field; it tells us a structural map exists and transfers, not that the map decomposes into a residue component separable from salience or uncertainty. Second, the persistence demonstrated is *across problems within a session*, which is structure-generalisation, not the long-horizon persistent curvature REE means by residue. So this entry strengthens the cognitive-map / latent-geometry face of ARC-013 but says nothing about the separability prong that V3-EXQ-697 is queued to probe.

**Confidence.** I set this at 0.72 — the highest in the ARC-013 trio. Source quality is high (Neuron, a well-controlled structure-versus-sensory dissociation from a lab that defined this question), and the mapping to "the map is persistent, transferable geometry" is good. The discount is for the measure mismatch and the species/modality transfer to a latent REE substrate. Read together with Kim et al. (2026), the two give converging support — one at single-neuron geometry in mouse, one at structural-geometry transfer in human — for the geometry of the map, while both leave residue-as-irreducible open.
