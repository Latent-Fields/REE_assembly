# Causal evidence that a rhythm parameter sets capacity (Wolinski et al. 2018)

**Source.** Wolinski, N., Cooper, N.R., Sauseng, P. & Romei, V., *The speed of parietal theta frequency drives visuospatial working memory capacity*, PLOS Biology 16(3):e2005348, 2018. [DOI:10.1371/journal.pbio.2005348](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.2005348), PMC5868840.

**What the paper does.** Thirty-two participants performed a lateralized visuospatial delayed match-to-sample task (memory loads of 4-6 colored squares) while receiving transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS) over parietal cortex at 4 Hz (slow theta), 7 Hz (fast theta), or sham, in a within-subject design. tACS entrains the phase of ongoing cortical oscillations at the stimulation frequency. The authors measure Cowan's K, a standard estimate of the number of items successfully held in working memory.

**Key finding.** 4-Hz tACS significantly increased K-values (and accuracy) relative to sham; 7-Hz tACS significantly decreased them (Cohen's d 0.44-0.57), and the effect was lateralized -- present only for memory items in the visual hemifield contralateral to the stimulated parietal hemisphere. The authors interpret this through theta-gamma phase-amplitude coupling (Lisman & Idiart's original 1995 model, refined by Lisman & Jensen 2013, already on file in `targeted_review_object_files_feature_binding` for MECH-089/ARC-080): a slower theta cycle has room for more nested gamma subcycles, and if each gamma subcycle carries one item, a slower theta mechanically permits a higher item count.

**Why it matters for MECH-521/MECH-522.** MECH-522's central and most distinctive claim is that a field/coupling parameter SETS slot occupancy causally, rather than occupancy being a separately-determined quantity that a field property merely correlates with or is read off from. Correlational neuroimaging cannot establish that direction of causality; this paper can, because it is a stimulation study. Externally imposing a different theta frequency changes realized capacity in the direction the coupling-constant framing predicts (slower rhythm -> more nested capacity, faster rhythm -> less), which is the cleanest causal-manipulation evidence found in this pull for "a rhythm parameter can set count," as opposed to "count is measured and a field property happens to correlate with it." This also strengthens MECH-521's parent claim (capacity is a real, independently-set regulator, distinct from the emergent occupancy of any given trial) by supplying a concrete physiological knob that manipulates the capacity side specifically.

**Where it falls short of MECH-522's fuller claim.** Two honest gaps. First, and most important: this is a single GLOBAL parietal theta rhythm producing ONE scalar capacity number that applies to the whole task -- not a field with a per-population coherence length/frequency giving natively HETEROGENEOUS domain counts across simultaneously-processed streams. That heterogeneity is exactly what MECH-522 claims goes beyond a plain theta-gamma multiplexing account (and is precisely the property Badre & Nee's 2018 review -- see the companion entry in this directory -- reports the evidence does not support a single ordered abstraction axis, consistent with MECH-522's heterogeneity claim, though Badre & Nee themselves are about prefrontal hierarchy, not perceptual grain). Second, tACS entrains synaptic/oscillatory dynamics via the currents that also drive spiking -- it is not a demonstration of a specifically EPHAPTIC (non-synaptic, extracellular-field-mediated) mechanism, which is MECH-522's more particular bet. This paper establishes the causal-direction logic MECH-522 needs; it does not establish that ephaptic coupling specifically is the physical implementation.

**Confidence.** 0.72. Source quality is good: peer-reviewed, a genuine causal manipulation (not correlational), with a clean double dissociation (opposite-sign effects at the two frequencies, both against sham, spatially lateralized as predicted) and a plausible, well-specified mechanistic account. Mapping fidelity is capped by the single-global-rhythm-vs-heterogeneous-field gap described above. Transfer risk is moderate: human parietal tACS to REE's engineered heartbeat/ephaptic-analog substrate is a real domain jump, though MECH-089 was explicitly designed as this analog already, so the transfer path is not novel to this entry.

**Lit-only verdict, not governance.** This is the strongest available causal-direction evidence in this pull for MECH-522's core mechanism (a rhythm/field parameter setting occupancy rather than measuring it), and by extension supports MECH-521's two-regulator structure. It does not evidence MECH-522's heterogeneity claim or its specifically-ephaptic (as opposed to any rhythmic/neuromodulatory) implementation -- those remain open, and V3-EXQ-725a's mixed result (documented in MECH-522's own registration notes) is the closest REE has to a direct test of the latter.
