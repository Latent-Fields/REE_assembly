# Du et al. 2026 -- Interlat: heterogeneous agents coordinating in latent space

Verified on the ACL Anthology: ACL 2026 long paper, pages 27106-27129, DOI 10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.1248; the arXiv version is 2511.09149. Interlat sends an agent's last-layer hidden states as its message and injects them into the receiver's input-embedding sequence through a trainable light-weight self-attention and projection adapter. Both sender and actor are fully fine-tuned (Qwen2.5-7B/0.5B-Base, LLaMA3.1-8B-Base) with a curriculum that stochastically replaces token embeddings with latent states, and a separate latent-space reasoner produces compressed messages for a reported 24x latency reduction.

The result that bears on ARC-139 is the heterogeneous one: a Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct sender feeding a LLaMA3.1-8B-Base actor reached 71.39% on unseen tasks against a 57.46% baseline. Two independently pretrained representational spaces, coordinated through a small interface, with no attempt to make them alike. In the ML domain that is what "integration without representational sameness" looks like, and it is feasibility evidence for the claim.

The reservations are structural. Both endpoints are fine-tuned alongside the adapter, so nothing here isolates the bridge from co-adaptation -- this is not the frozen-endpoint, minimum-bridge measurement MECH-538 specifies, and a frozen replication could look quite different. The paper reports ablations with cross-task, covariance-matched Gaussian and randomly rotated messages that degrade performance, which speaks to content specificity, but the CrossTask numbers as extracted (61.19% versus 61.43%) do not read as a drop and I have not relied on them; the example-specificity question is better carried by the two causal audits banked alongside this entry. And the setting is two LLM agents on ALFWorld and MATH, which the authors themselves flag as narrow, with closed-model inaccessibility and interpretability loss as further limitations.

Nothing in the paper tests ARC-139's non-redundant prediction that specialisation and legibility rise together. Supports, at 0.5, as an existence proof and no more.
