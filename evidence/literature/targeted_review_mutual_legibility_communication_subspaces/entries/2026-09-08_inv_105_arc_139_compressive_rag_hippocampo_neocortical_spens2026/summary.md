# Spens & Burgess 2026 -- compression judged by the generator that reads it

Nature Communications 17:7971, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-74357-6, PMID 42350398, published 25 June 2026 -- the citation is exact. Spens and Burgess model the hippocampus as a modern Hopfield network storing states and one-step transitions, and the neocortex as a transformer language model trained by next-item prediction. The hippocampal trace is a compressed gist embedding plus the high-perplexity details the generator could not have guessed; consolidation replays those traces to fine-tune the neocortex by LoRA, after which the neocortex regenerates episodes increasingly on its own and increasingly distorted toward its priors. The simulations cover Bartlett-style distortion, the gist-plus-details efficiency advantage, structure learning on grids and family trees, and retrieval-augmented inference.

What this offers INV-105 is a worked example of the ladder's central distinction. The trace is not judged by whether it literally contains the episode; it is judged by whether the specific generator that will read it can reconstruct the episode. A trace can be complete and useless, or sparse and sufficient, depending entirely on the consumer. That is "encoded is not usable", built into an architecture rather than stated as a principle. It is modest support because it is a model, not a measurement, and because it evidences the principle rather than any REE mechanism.

For ARC-139 I have scored it mixed, and this is worth saying clearly since the thought files it under resonance. The two systems in this model share one representational format: the hippocampus "must use neocortical representations for input and output", and what crosses the interface is a neocortical text embedding. That is ARC-139's architecture (2), a shared low-dimensional interface format, which sits nearer to ARC-121's shared-object framing than to context-specific translation. A working shared-format model is not evidence for federation over convergence; ARC-139 lists both as live and this paper is an instance of the other one.

The authors' limitations are the right ones: the mapping to cell types is unexplored, backpropagation is implausible, events are pre-segmented, and forgetting is steeper than human. The paper measures no subspace and no bridge. Its natural REE home is MECH-532's compression-needs-a-paired-readout framing, which the thought itself notes. Confidence 0.45.
