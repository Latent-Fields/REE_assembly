# Cheng, Das & Ramnath 2026 -- the mismatched-cache audit, and the 14.7 versus 0.4 cell

arXiv 2608.04893, v1 5 August and v2 27 August 2026, cs.CR, a preprint; I read the full v2 PDF. The paper asks whether multi-agent LLM systems that relay KV caches instead of text are right to credit their gains to transmitted "latent thoughts". Its point is that this credit is a causal claim about WHICH example's cache is relayed, not merely that one is, and that benchmark deltas cannot establish it. So it intervenes on the cache: the correct example's cache (true), a well-formed cache from a different example (deranged), a geometry-preserving zeroed cache, a moment-matched random cache, and an intact but answer-irrelevant relay -- under two regimes, one where the receiver needs the sender's private facts and one where it could solve alone.

The two numbers INV-105 rests on are verified verbatim. On MedQA with Qwen3-8B in the natural regime: "true versus zero is +14.7 points, against tau_TD = +0.4 with 90% CI [-1.34, +2.14]". Zeroing the relay costs the receiver 14.7 points; substituting the wrong example's cache costs 0.4, with an interval spanning zero. The channel is strongly load-bearing and its example-specific content is barely load-bearing. That is the sentence in INV-105's notes, and it is in the paper.

The rest of the design is what makes it trustworthy. In the calibrated regime the same instrument reads ceiling (about 100% against 23-25% for answer-irrelevant relays on Qwen3-8B, replicated across Qwen3-4B/8B/14B, Mistral-Nemo-12B and phi-4), so it is not a blind instrument. In the natural regime it reads equivalence within 2.8 points under Holm-corrected TOST with five seeds on GSM8K, ARC-Challenge and MedQA, with one small bounded advantage on Qwen3-4B GSM8K. Random caches collapse accuracy, confirming the intervention reaches the receiver. And porting the audit to three released systems dissociates them: LatentMAS's native relay reaches ceiling, KVComm's layer subset transfers partially, and C2C's released projector shows no detected example-specific transfer. Receiver need is decisive for the native relay and not sufficient in general.

For INV-105 this is the mandatory-controls clause independently arrived at and executed with pre-registered conventions. What transfers is the evidential logic -- "causally used" and "pairing-specific" are different rungs and only the mismatched-pair test separates them -- not any number, since KV-cache relay in LLMs is nothing like an E1/E2 interface. The 2.8-point equivalence margin is a convention anchored to the audited system's own claim and REE would need its own. Supports at 0.78; preprint status is the only thing keeping it under 0.8.
