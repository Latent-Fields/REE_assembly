# Okyere et al. 2026 -- timing of engagement, not adequacy of interface, decides the consequence

MECH-556's notes carry this as `EXTERNAL MOTIVATION, NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED IN THIS PASS`. It is verified
here and the claim note turns out to be accurate in every particular. The DOI 10.64898/2026.09.02.748628
resolves to *Hippocampal stimulation timed to memory reactivation shapes human sleep oscillatory dynamics and
consolidation*, posted to bioRxiv on 2026-09-07 by Okyere, Li, Raufeisen, Alania, Jones, Steiner, Neufeld,
Kuster, Grossman, Dijk, Cohen Kadosh, Bartsch, Jaramillo and Violante. One warning for a future session: a
PubMed author search for "Okyere" and "hippocampal" returns PMID 39986374, a 2025 *Brain Stimulation* paper
on sleep spindles and corticospinal excitability with P. Okyere as a middle author. That is a different paper
by a different first author. This preprint is not in PubMed at all; it was verified through Crossref.

The study is, as far as the authors claim, the first non-invasive stimulation of the human hippocampus during
sleep. Using temporal-interference stimulation of the left hippocampal head, they delivered stimulation
either concurrently with induced memory reactivation or prior to it. Concurrent stimulation reduced
associative memory forgetting relative to the mistimed arm and enhanced fast spindle amplitude, preserving
the association between slow-oscillation/spindle coupling and memory; the behavioural benefit was mediated by
a distributed, left-lateralised set of spindle and coupling features.

The design is what makes this a good anchor rather than a merely interesting one. Both arms receive the same
stimulation to the same target; only the timing relative to reactivation differs. That is a timing
manipulation at matched engagement, which is unusually close to what MECH-556 asserts -- that an
inter-system mapping can be representationally adequate in every respect and still produce no downstream
effect because it is engaged outside the window in which the receiver is receptive. The mistimed arm is the
mistimed-interface false negative, realised causally in a human brain.

What it does not show, and MECH-556's own note is already careful about this in a way I want to preserve
rather than quietly relax. It is not evidence that sleep performs latent translation between representational
formats. It is not evidence that a hippocampo-cortical bridge of the kind REE posits exists. And the
concurrent-versus-prior contrast establishes *that* timing matters without isolating *which* receiver
property the timing gates -- plasticity availability, replay content, or evaluation of a candidate are all
still live and the study does not separate them. MECH-556's own text is explicit that what is new in that
claim is the evidential rule, not the mechanism: REE already implements temporal gating at three grains.

Confidence 0.45, and the discount is entirely evidential status rather than fit. This is a single unreviewed
preprint of a first-of-its-kind method; the spatial specificity of non-invasive deep temporal-interference
targeting is precisely the kind of claim that independent replication tends to complicate. Per
`feedback_lit_exp_decoupled` it raises no claim's confidence in any case. Gate B2 in
