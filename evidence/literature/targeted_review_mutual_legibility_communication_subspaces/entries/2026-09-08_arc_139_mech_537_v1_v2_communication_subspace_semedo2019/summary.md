# Semedo et al. 2019 -- the origin of the communication subspace

This is the paper the whole mutual-legibility package leans on, and it is what the package says it is. Semedo, Zandvakili, Machens, Yu and Kohn recorded V1 and V2 populations simultaneously in macaque and asked how trial-to-trial fluctuations in one related to the other. Using reduced-rank regression they found that V2 activity is predicted by a low-dimensional subset of V1 population patterns -- a "communication subspace" -- and, the load-bearing part, that this subset is distinct from the directions that dominate V1's own fluctuations. Within-V1 subpopulation interactions were far less selective. Verified through PubMed (PMID 30770252, Neuron 102(1):249-259.e4, PMC6449210).

For ARC-139 this is the biological warrant that integration between areas does not require one area to read the whole of the other. For MECH-537 it is more specific: the consumer-facing subspace is a real, estimable object, and it is not simply "the top principal components of the sender". That is exactly the geometric situation in which a variable could be strongly encoded in the sender and yet weakly present in what the consumer sees.

What the paper does not do is show that situation producing a behavioural failure. It is about shared variability under visual stimulation, not about a task variable being routed or withheld, and nothing in it speaks to an agent whose downstream policy fails because a distinction never entered the interface. That step -- from "the read is low-rank and non-dominant" to "encoded but not exposed, with behavioural cost" -- is REE's inference. I have scored the mapping accordingly: the construct transfers cleanly, the phenotype does not.

Two further cautions. The estimator matters: the subspace here is fitted on trial-to-trial variability, and a REE harness that fitted RRR on stimulus-conditioned means would be measuring something else. And the population is macaque early visual cortex under passive viewing, which is about as far from an E1/E2 interface in an embodied forward-model agent as a mapping can be while still being the same mathematical object. Confidence 0.72: strong source, clean construct, honest gap on the phenotype.
