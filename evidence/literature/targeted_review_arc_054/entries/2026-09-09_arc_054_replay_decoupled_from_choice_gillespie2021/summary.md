# Replay reflects the past, not the plan: counter-evidence to ARC-054's hippocampal framing

**Claim tested:** ARC-054 (D_V trajectory selection over planning horizon H) · **Direction:** weakens · **Confidence:** 0.72

## What the paper did

Gillespie and colleagues in the Frank lab built a task specifically designed to break a confound that had haunted the replay-as-planning literature. In most spatial tasks, where the animal has been and where it is about to go are correlated, so a replay event representing a trajectory is ambiguous between retrospective and prospective interpretations. Their dynamic task, with shifting reward contingencies, dissociates the two. They then decoded replay content with state-space clusterless methods and asked, event by event, whether the content predicted the choice the animal actually made next.

It did not. Replay content was decoupled from subsequent choice, and instead preferentially represented previously rewarded locations. Their conclusion is that awake replay reflects specific past experiences rather than constituting a plan for the upcoming decision.

## Why I pulled this deliberately

I pulled the Shin 2019 paper in this same directory as support for ARC-054's rollout substrate. It would be dishonest to stop there. This is the best-designed direct test of the very inference that entry rests on, and it comes out the other way. A literature pull that only surfaced the supporting half of a contested question would be worse than no pull at all -- it would give governance a confidence number built on a selected sample.

The force of the objection is specific. ARC-054's notes describe its mechanism as "hippocampal rollout evaluation": candidate trajectories generated by hippocampal forward sequences, then scored. Gillespie et al. give good reason to think hippocampal replay is not doing the candidate-generation-for-selection job at all -- that it is a consolidation and credit-assignment process looking backwards. If that is right, then the biological grounding ARC-054 invokes is the wrong grounding, even if the selection rule itself turns out to be correct. The claim would need to find its rollout somewhere else.

## What it does not do, and the distinction matters for governance

It does not falsify ARC-054.

ARC-054's V3 form, per its own `v3_form_note`, has already substantially decoupled from the biology. The V3 implementation reads V_hat from MECH-269 per-region V_s, walks it over the rollout with the MECH-288 BoundaryEvent segmenter, computes D_V with a configurable EMA (`arc054_dv_lambda`, default 0.85), and adds J(pi) as an additive bias on the existing E3 trajectory score behind a default-OFF master flag. Whether that engineering commitment improves selection is a question for V3-EXQ-491, and it is not settled by whether rat CA1 replay predicts rat choice. So what this paper removes is a *support the claim was leaning on*, not the claim's legs.

There is also a methodological limit I should state rather than let the null do more work than it can. A null coupling between replay content and subsequent choice is compatible with rollout evaluation happening in a structure this recording does not cover, or at a timescale the analysis does not resolve. It constrains the substrate attribution considerably more strongly than it refutes rollout-based selection as such.

## Confidence reasoning

Source quality at 0.88 is the highest in this pull, and deliberately so: the task was purpose-built for the dissociation, the decoding method is state-space clusterless rather than the template-matching that inflated earlier effects, and the analysis operates at the individual-event level where the competing hypotheses actually make different predictions. Mapping fidelity 0.70 because it targets ARC-054's named substrate directly while the claim's V3 form has partly moved off that substrate. Transfer risk 0.35.

I am recording this as **weakens** at 0.72, and the practical recommendation for governance is narrower than the direction token suggests: ARC-054's `notes` should stop presenting hippocampal rollout evaluation as settled biological grounding, and the V3 validation (V3-EXQ-491) should carry the weight instead.
