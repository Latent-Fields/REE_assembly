# Ayers et al. (2023) — Physician vs AI chatbot responses to patient questions

Ayers, J. W., Poliak, A., Dredze, M., et al. (2023). *Comparing Physician and Artificial Intelligence Chatbot Responses to Patient Questions Posted to a Public Social Media Forum.* JAMA Internal Medicine. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2023.1838)

## What the study did

The authors drew 195 exchanges from October 2022 on Reddit's r/AskDocs in which a verified physician had publicly answered a patient's question. They generated ChatGPT answers to the same questions, then presented the original question together with the anonymised, randomly ordered physician and chatbot responses to a panel of licensed healthcare professionals. Raters, blinded to source and evaluating in triplicate, chose which response was "better" and separately scored the "quality of information provided" and the "empathy or bedside manner provided." The panel preferred the chatbot's responses in roughly 79% of evaluations and rated them significantly higher on both quality and empathy; the physician responses were substantially less likely to be judged empathetic.

## Why it matters for Q-091

Where Blair (2005) supplies the biological existence proof that concern-relevant competence and affective empathy dissociate, this study supplies the *artificial-system* existence proof — and it is close to a worked demonstration of Q-091's central fear. ChatGPT has, by construction, no other-model slot and no affective-relevance architecture; whatever it is doing when it produces a caring-sounding paragraph, it is not doing it because it cares. Yet an expert, blinded, clinically-trained panel — precisely the kind of evaluator one would trust — rated its output as *more* empathetic than that of humans who do possess the architecture. The language-only evaluation did not merely fail to detect the missing component; it inverted the ordering, scoring the empathy-less system above the empathic one.

For REE this is decisive about one half of Q-091: an evaluation that infers caring from judged linguistic concern is not a weak proxy that occasionally errs — it is systematically capturable, and by surface features. The authors themselves flag a concrete mechanism: ChatGPT tended to write longer responses, and raters may associate length with empathy. That is a form confound with no relationship to any internal caring state. The implication for the REE evaluation Q-091 asks for is that it must rest on something a fluent imitator cannot fake — costly other-directed action under live alternatives, or residue after exploiting a cooperating other (per MECH-407) — not on how caring the output reads.

## Limits, caveats, and the mixed reading

This study measures *perceived* empathy of text; it says nothing about the presence or absence of an internal empathic state in either the model or the physicians, and it does not test any non-linguistic discriminating probe. So it establishes Q-091's premise (the language-only judge is foolable) but not Q-091's proposed answer (a validated non-linguistic probe). There is also a genuinely mixed reading worth recording honestly: if concern-shaped language is itself clinically beneficial to patients regardless of whether it is "genuine," one could argue the masking distinction matters less for *outcomes* than Q-091 assumes. That reading does not dissolve the alignment concern — a caring-commitment substrate is being evaluated for what it *is*, not only for the affect it induces in a reader — but it is a real tension. Finally, transfer risk is substantial: clinical Q&A text is far from an REE V4 caring-commitment setting.

## Confidence reasoning

Confidence 0.7, `supports`. High source quality (JAMA Internal Medicine, blinded triplicate rating, pre-specified design) and strong mapping onto the premise that language-only evaluation is foolable — demonstrated in an actual artificial system, which Blair could not provide. Held below 0.8 because it evidences the problem, not the fix, and because "rated empathy of text" is a perception measure one step removed from the architecture Q-091 cares about.
