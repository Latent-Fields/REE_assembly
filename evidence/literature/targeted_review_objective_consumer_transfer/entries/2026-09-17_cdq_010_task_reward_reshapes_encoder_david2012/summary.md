# Task reward structure reshapes the encoder (David, Fritz & Shamma, 2012)

## The finding

Auditory cortical receptive fields change rapidly during behaviour, and the change depends on the **task's
reward structure** -- not just on which stimulus is attended. Task-relevant representations sharpen; stimuli
signalling aversive outcomes are amplified.

## Why it matters for CDQ-010

Biology does **not** make a sensory code usable by maximising a task-agnostic preservation objective. It routes
the task's own reward structure into the encoder. That is MECH-567 with a biological existence proof, and it is
why MECH-567's proposed lever is an auxiliary **consumer-target head on the same latent during P0a**, not more
preservation budget.

It also orders the two external sources biologically: DreamerV3 (task heads *inside* the representation
objective) is the closer analogue; MuZero (consumer-only training, reconstruction explicitly refused) is the
extreme limit.

## The gap in the evidence

The paper measures **encoder change**, not **consumer competence**. The link from one to the other is exactly
what MECH-567's falsifier has to measure in REE, and it is not imported from here.
