# Communication subspace (Semedo et al., 2019)

## Why this entry exists

The V3-EXQ-1023a autopsy scored the biological-reference layer **ABSENT** for SD-106. CDQ-010 asks what makes
an encoder objective's gains reach a downstream action consumer. This paper is the sharpest biological
statement that **the reaching is a separate fact from the having**.

## The finding

V2's trial-to-trial population fluctuations are predicted by only a low-dimensional subset of V1's population
activity patterns. Critically, that subset is **distinct from the largest fluctuations shared among neurons
within V1**. Activity is selectively routed; the downstream area does not see the upstream population's
dominant variance.

## Translation to the SD-106 gap

SD-106 maximises preserved input variance through a freely-trained linear decoder. Its acceptance instrument
(held-out preservation R^2) rose +0.1184 while the consumer moved +0.0081. In this paper's terms, the objective
was improving the sender's content without any constraint tying that content to the channel the consumer reads.

## What it does NOT license

It does **not** license 'dominant-variance directions are useless'. REE's own PCA-32 anchor keeps exactly the
top-32 principal directions and clears the bar 3/3 at 0.8771/0.8578/0.8702. So the honest reading is the weaker
and more useful one: *preserved variance is not automatically transferred variance*, and what separates two
codes at matched preserved variance is their geometry -- which is MECH-566's question, not this paper's.
