# Information present vs information used (Rumyantsev et al., 2020)

## The finding

Mouse V1 populations carry **far more** orientation information than the animal's behavioural discrimination
threshold reflects. Population information is asymptotically bounded by information-limiting correlations, and
behaviour falls short even of that bound.

## Why it matters for CDQ-010

It establishes, with a direct measurement, that **information present in a representation and information used
by a consumer are different quantities that routinely differ by a large factor**. The V3-EXQ-1023a signature --
objective +0.1184 R^2, consumer +0.0081 -- is an ordinary shape for a sender-consumer pair, not evidence that
the objective failed. This is the biological warrant for the autopsy's correction from *the lever is exhausted*
to *the lever is not connected*.

## What it does NOT settle

The paper's own leading account is a **sender-side** ceiling (information-limiting correlations), whereas
MECH-566/567 are consumer-side and objective-side. This entry sizes the gap; it does not choose among the
registered mechanisms.
