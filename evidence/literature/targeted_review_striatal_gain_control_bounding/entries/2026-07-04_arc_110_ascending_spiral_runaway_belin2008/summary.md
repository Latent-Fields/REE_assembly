# The ascending spiral — and what it looks like when it runs away

Belin, Jonkman, Dickinson, Robbins & Everitt (2008), *Behavioural Brain Research*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbr.2008.09.027)

This is the paper that names the symbol we imported. The review lays out the basal ganglia as segregated parallel cortico-striato-pallido-thalamo-cortical loops threaded by a *serial, dopamine-dependent striato-nigro-striatal ascending spiral* that carries information ventral→dorsal — from the nucleus accumbens (limbic) up through associative to motor striatum. This is the Haber 2000 lineage, and it is exactly the ascending `M_cross` coupling ARC-110 built and the ASG (ascending-spiral gain) mechanism the V3-EXQ-711 substrate implements. So far this is the same ground the arc_110 sibling entry (Haber 2003) already covers.

What makes it worth its own entry is the *failure mode*. The review's whole thesis is a story about the spiral running away. Protracted exposure to addictive drugs, they argue, progressively recruits and amplifies this ascending spiral, dragging control from the flexible action-outcome system into a rigid, compulsive stimulus-response habit — the dorsal-motor loop gets captured, and behaviour collapses onto a single over-driven channel. That is, biologically, what an *unbounded* ascending spiral produces. And it is the same shape as the 711 autopsy finding: a limbic drive that climbs the spiral with no homeostatic ceiling and ends up monopolising downstream selection (`w_eff[limbic]` 10–2274× motor). The compulsive habit *is* the limbic-monopoly runaway, drawn in a different notation.

For the divergence ledger this is the key move. ASG-3 was written as an "honest simplification" — the parity setpoint is a configured scalar, not an emergent tonic-DA-homeostasis loop. Belin et al. let us say something stronger and more honest: the biological spiral is *normally bounded and graded* (ventral biases dorsal by a serial, constrained increment), and its pathological *unbounding* is a recognised disease process. Our raw-scalar gain, pre-repair, was the substrate sitting in the addiction-like regime by construction. The bounded parity controller is not decorative engineering; it is the piece that keeps the spiral out of the runaway regime this review is entirely about — which also earns ARC-106's required psychiatric-failure-mode column for free (unbounded ascending drive → compulsion).

I have marked the direction *mixed* rather than *supports*, deliberately. This is a conceptual synthesis: it documents the spiral and its drug-driven runaway anatomically and behaviourally, but it does not hand us the control law that bounds the healthy spiral — that is the pillar-1 autoreceptor literature (Chen 2020, Gowrishankar 2018). And I should not oversell the analogy: addiction is a neuroadaptive pathology unfolding over weeks, and the 711 blow-up is a numerical instability over training steps. They rhyme in dynamics (positive drive, no brake, single-channel capture); they are not the same thing. Confidence 0.66 — high source quality, strong mapping to both the symbol and its failure mode, discounted for the missing quantitative bound and the pathology-to-numerics interpretive step.
