# MECH-442 biology synthesis -- does committed-action diversity survive selection via a behavioral-repertoire archive? (2026-06-18)

**Claim under test (MECH-442, Quality-Diversity / MAP-Elites convergence intake, CDQ-003):** committed-action diversity should be preserved through the selection/commit step by an explicit behavioral-descriptor-indexed archive of per-niche elites (MAP-Elites analog), generalizing the validated V3-EXQ-569i top-k shortlist, rather than collapsing to F's single global optimum at the E3 argmin (MECH-439 F-dominance).

**Sources (5, PubMed):** Kao & Brainard 2006 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.01138.2005)); Dhawale, Smith & Olveczky 2017 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-neuro-072116-031548)); Markowitz et al. 2018 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.04.019)); Tesileanu, Olveczky & Balasubramanian 2017 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.20944)); Ponzi 2007 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2007.12.040)).

## Verdict: SUPPORTED, with a framing refinement (not a refutation)

The biology robustly supports the **necessity** behind MECH-442 and the **existence** of a behavioral-repertoire archive, but it **relocates the mechanism**: diversity is preserved *upstream of / in parallel with* a winner-take-all commit gate, plus active variability injection -- not by a per-niche structure operating *at* the value argmax.

**Three convergent supports:**
- **A behavioral-repertoire archive is a real biological object.** Dorsolateral striatum encodes the identity and ordering of a discrete repertoire of sub-second behavioral modules and is required to assemble them (Markowitz 2018) -- the closest analog to a behavioral-descriptor partition of the candidate set.
- **Behavioral diversity is an actively-maintained, regulated FEATURE, not noise** (Dhawale 2017) -- grounding REE's `behavioral_diversity_isolation:GAP-B` necessity that diversity must be deliberately kept, not allowed to wash out at selection.
- **Diversity is generated by a dedicated circuit SEPARATE from the motor selector.** A basal-ganglia circuit (LMAN/AFP) generates and context-regulates variability in parallel with the premotor output; lesioning it removes the variability while the behavior keeps working (Kao & Brainard 2006), and the two-stage tutor(variability)/student(motor) architecture is the efficient design (Tesileanu 2017).

**The load-bearing weakens (Ponzi 2007):** the basal-ganglia selection gate is a winner-take-all that resolves to ONE action and collapses from exploratory-random to stable-directed (always the single value-maximizing action) as the reward gradient strengthens. So **F-dominance at the commit (MECH-439) is the biologically FAITHFUL behavior of the gate**, not a pathology to be fixed *at* the gate.

## Implication for MECH-442's registration framing

The biology favors a specific, narrower mechanism than the naive "per-niche archive at the argmax":

1. **The archive / repertoire lives UPSTREAM of (or restricts the eligible set BEFORE) the winner-take-all commit** -- it does not keep multiple niches simultaneously selectable AT the argmax (the gate is single-winner). This is exactly what the validated V3-EXQ-569i top-k shortlist already does (shortlist an eligible set, then run within-set selection). MAP-Elites generalizes it to a *behavioral-descriptor-indexed* eligible-set restriction (vs the descriptor-free F-rank one), an upstream move.
2. **Diversity is coupled to an active variability generator**, so MECH-442's `depends_on` should ADD **MECH-313** (the LC-NE-analog noise floor / variability mechanism) alongside the generation pathway **ARC-065** -- the brain's diversity is generator-plus-repertoire, not repertoire alone.
3. **epistemic_category substrate_conditional and generation v3 hold** (the eligible-set-restriction archive beyond top-k is plannable V3 substrate but not yet built), with a `mapping_caveat` that the committed argmax itself (F-dominance) is biologically correct and the archive operates as an upstream eligible-set structure.

**Net for governance:** registration is appropriately grounded (the necessity + the repertoire object are biologically supported), and the refined framing (upstream eligible-set archive + variability generator, not at-argmax per-niche) is the biology-anchored mechanism -- avoiding the SD-003 / SD-010/011 "philosophy-right, mechanism-wrong" failure by placing the archive where the biology places it.
