# Swift et al. (2018) -- LC silence during sleep is required for consolidation

**What they did.** Swift and colleagues (Poe lab, with Sara and Aston-Jones) started from a well-known but under-exploited fact: the locus coeruleus, which bathes the forebrain in noradrenaline throughout waking, falls silent across REM sleep and just before each NREM spindle. They asked whether that silence is doing work. In rats they optogenetically drove noradrenergic LC neurons at 2 Hz during sleep -- low enough not to wake the animal or change sleep amounts -- after a hippocampus-dependent food-location task.

**What they found.** Holding the LC 'on' reduced spindle occurrence, NREM delta power and REM theta power, weakened ripple-spindle coupling, impaired consolidation of newly learned locations and reconsolidation of old ones, and disrupted next-day place-cell stability. Spontaneous LC activity inside spindles also predicted lower spindle power. Each electrophysiological signature of the disruption correlated with the behavioural deficit.

**How it maps to MECH-016.** MECH-016 says precision gains drift during online action and that sleep is where they get rebalanced, with a 'noradrenaline-like reset' named explicitly. The strongest thing this paper gives us is that the offline regime is not merely waking-minus-input: it requires the active withdrawal of the gain neuromodulator, and forcing that gain to stay on spoils what the offline period achieves. For REE that argues the sleep phase should clamp the arousal/precision channel rather than let it idle -- a design-level constraint, not just a metaphor.

**Where I would be careful.** Nothing here measures precision. The outcome variables are memory and representational stability. The same result is what you would expect if LC silence were simply a permission signal for plasticity -- which belongs to consolidation claims (MECH-120/121) rather than to recalibration of alpha_k. Is 'the gain must be off for reorganisation' the same thing as 'the gain is being recalibrated'? Not obviously. A test that would bite on MECH-016 specifically would look at gain-dependent behaviour after sleep with and without LC silence -- exploration versus commitment, or sensitivity to prediction error -- and that experiment is not in this paper.

**Confidence 0.62.** High source quality and a genuine causal manipulation, discounted for the gap between consolidation and calibration and for rodent-to-architecture transfer.
