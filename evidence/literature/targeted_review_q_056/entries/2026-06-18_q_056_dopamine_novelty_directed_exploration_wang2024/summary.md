# The brain gates exploration on a computed uncertainty signal (Wang, Lak, Manohar & Bogacz 2024)

According to PubMed, this PLoS Computational Biology paper from the Bogacz group ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011516)) supplies the biological precedent behind MECH-343. The model proposes that the direct and indirect striatal pathways jointly estimate not just the mean but the *variance* of reward distributions, and that transient mesolimbic dopaminergic novelty signals supply an uncertainty representation that drives *directed* exploration -- exploration aimed at the options the agent is most uncertain about. The authors validate the basal-ganglia component against electrophysiological recordings, fit the resulting exploration strategies to behavioural data, and show in simulation that the directed strategy outperforms undirected exploration and classic upper-confidence-bound variants.

For Q-056 the relevant lesson is structural rather than quantitative: in the brain, exploration is *gated by a computed state signal*, not applied as a uniform constant. That is exactly the design stance MECH-343 takes -- proposal entropy should be conditional, not fixed. The finding that directed (uncertainty-conditioned) exploration beats undirected exploration is the neuroscience echo of the Bakopoulos result that adaptive beats constant, and it gives MECH-343 a biological-plausibility argument: difficulty-gated proposal entropy is a member of the same family as the striatal-dopaminergic uncertainty-gating the brain appears to use.

The mapping caveat is that the gating *variable* differs. Here it is reward-uncertainty and stimulus novelty; in MECH-343 it is difficulty / stuck-state. A stuck agent stalled at a blocked goal is not necessarily in a high-novelty or high-value-variance state -- it may be in a low-novelty, high-frustration state. So this paper supports "exploration should be gated on something" and "directed beats undirected," but it does not directly validate gating specifically on difficulty. The paradigm is also value-based (bandit-like) action selection rather than the sequential blocked-path environment Q-056 specifies, and there is no harm/safety channel.

evidence_direction `supports`, confidence 0.58. Strong computational-neuroscience work, discounted because the gating signal is novelty/uncertainty rather than REE's difficulty trigger, and because bandit-style choice transfers only partially to REE's sequential arbitration.
