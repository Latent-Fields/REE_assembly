# Camille et al. 2004 -- the counterfactual pathway is separable, which costs the same-scale horn its parsimony

**Claim tested:** Q-090 -- is the retained-alternative admission criterion the interrupt's scale at a lower cut, or an independent relevance criterion?

**Direction:** weakens the same-cut-scale horn. **Confidence: 0.55.**

## What the paper does

Participants play a simple gambling task in which, after choosing, they are shown not only what their chosen gamble paid but what the *unchosen* one would have paid. That second piece of information is what turns disappointment into regret: the comparison is against a road not taken rather than against an expectation.

Healthy controls behave as counterfactual thinkers. They report emotions consistent with comparing obtained against foregone, they shift their choices so as to minimise anticipated future regret, and they learn from the emotional experience. Patients with orbitofrontal cortical lesions do not. They report no regret, and they do not anticipate the negative consequences of their choices -- while continuing to respond to what they actually got.

## Why it bears on Q-090

The same-cut-scale horn has one strong argument going for it, and it is not empirical. It is parsimony. If you already have a predicted-harm magnitude, and you already cut it high to trigger the interrupt, then getting retention for free by cutting the same scale lower is an elegant piece of engineering. No new mechanism, no new criterion, one signal doing three jobs.

Camille et al. take that argument away. What they show is that the nervous system did not, in fact, economise this way. The handling of alternatives depends on identifiable substrate that can be lost while factual outcome processing carries on. If leg 3 were merely the low tail of the distribution leg 1 reads at its top, a lesion should not be able to remove the counterfactual capacity while sparing the factual one -- they would be the same machinery differing only in a number. The dissociation says they are not.

There is a second reason this source belongs here, less about the disjunction and more about whether MECH-485 leg 3 is describing a real thing at all. Leg 3 says the retained alternative is kept "for later counterfactual evaluation and responsibility attribution". Regret is exactly that: an agent holding itself to account against an option it did not take. This paper is the canonical demonstration that the capacity is real, dissociable, and consequential for subsequent choice -- controls used regret to choose better next time. That is a useful independent grounding for the leg, quite apart from what it says about the criterion.

## Caveats and where the mapping strains

The central limit is severe and I want it recorded plainly rather than hedged.

In this paradigm the unchosen outcome is *displayed*. The participant does not have to have retained anything. The alternative is handed over on every trial, so what is being measured is what the system does with an alternative once it is present -- comparison, affective evaluation, learning -- and never which alternatives get admitted in the first place. Q-090 is an admission question. This study does not test admission at all.

So the disconfirming force runs through an extra inference: that a separable evaluation pathway implies a separable gate feeding it. I think that is plausible -- a dedicated downstream consumer usually implies a dedicated upstream selector, and it would be odd to evolve distinct counterfactual evaluation machinery fed by an undifferentiated low-threshold spillover. But plausible is not shown, and someone could coherently hold that admission is a low cut on the shared magnitude while evaluation of what passes the cut is orbitofrontal and separable. That position survives this paper intact.

Lesser limits: lesion samples are small and heterogeneous in extent and aetiology, and chronic patients have reorganised, so absence of a reported capacity is weaker evidence than the same result obtained by manipulation in intact brains. And regret here is self-reported affect. A patient who retained the alternative perfectly well but could not compare it, or could not feel or report the comparison, would look identical in these data.

## Confidence reasoning

Source quality 0.85: Science, a causal lesion design -- the strongest form of evidence for a separability claim -- with converging self-report, autonomic and choice-behaviour measures, and a finding that has anchored two decades of work since. Below 0.9 only for the small heterogeneous sample that lesion work cannot avoid. Mapping fidelity 0.45 is the binding constraint, recording that the paradigm supplies the alternative instead of requiring its retention. Transfer risk 0.4, the lowest in this pull, because the construct transferred -- counterfactual evaluation of a not-taken option for responsibility attribution -- is close to what MECH-485 leg 3 describes in its own words. Weighting mapping fidelity heavily gives 0.55: a strong source making a real but indirect dent in the same-scale horn.
