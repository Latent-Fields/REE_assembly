# Briley & Tucker-Drob (2014) -- genetic and environmental continuity in personality

**What they did.** Personality gets more stable with age; the argument has always been over *why* -- intrinsic maturation or accumulating social influence. Briley and Tucker-Drob pooled 24 longitudinal behavioural-genetic studies, over 21,000 sibling pairs from infancy to old age, and decomposed stability into genetic and environmental parts. Both parts become more stable with age. But the genetic contribution to overall stability stays moderate and fairly flat, while the environmental contribution climbs from almost nothing in early childhood to moderate in adulthood. Their conclusion: the lifespan rise in stability is mostly environmental.

**What it does for ARC-076.** It is the nearest thing to a direct test of the two-layer idea. There *is* an early-present, steady, genetically-indexed layer, and there *is* an environmentally written layer that becomes stable -- so the split McCrae forced on ARC-076 is not an artefact of one framing. That much I'd count as support.

**What it does against ARC-076.** The timing is wrong for a juvenile/adolescent lock. Environmental stability keeps building through adulthood. And the headline -- stability is predominantly environmental -- sits awkwardly with a design whose locked, stable core is the endogenous L1. If I take this seriously, L2 is not crystallized once at window close; it consolidates progressively, and some of what looks like consolidation may be people settling into stable lives rather than anything locking inside them.

**Caveats.** Variance components aren't mechanisms. "Environmental" includes measurement error and non-shared noise; "genetic" isn't the same as "maturational timer"; and none of this touches commitment-loop gains, which is ARC-076's own bridge. The niche-stability explanation also has no counterpart in a single REE agent -- which is itself a design question: does REE's L2 stay stable because it locked, or would it need a stable environment to stay put?

**Confidence.** 0.64, direction mixed: the architecture of the claim is supported, the lock timing is weakened.
