# Dopamine writes the map as well as the value -- the counterweight entry

If Glascher et al. is the paper MECH-034 would cite in its own defence, this is the paper that should
worry it. Sharpe and colleagues used sensory preconditioning in rats: an animal learns that one
neutral cue predicts another neutral cue, with no reward anywhere in sight. That is model learning in
its purest laboratory form -- there is nothing to value, only structure to acquire.

They then showed two things. First, sufficiency: when the stimulus-stimulus learning was blocked
behaviourally, optogenetically activating VTA dopamine neurons at the transition reinstated it.
Second, necessity: suppressing those same neurons across the transition prevented normal
stimulus-stimulus learning from occurring at all. The signal that thirty years of canon assigned to
scalar reward value turns out to be causally required for building the associative model of a world
that contains no reward.

## Why this cuts against MECH-034

MECH-034's falsifier names its own refutation condition rather precisely: the surfaces co-vary, or
the downstream consequence is the same for both, and the claim's "different information" is false.
Sharpe et al. supplies the mechanism by which that could happen in a real nervous system. If the
value-coded broadcast is also the channel through which model structure gets written, then a
harm-only manipulation does not stay on its diagonal -- it raises the mismatch surface as a side
effect, and the off-diagonal bound of 0.2 x the on-diagonal effect fails.

It also sharpens an inferential point I think matters more than the individual result. Glascher et al.
established a dissociation by *read-out*: two regressors, two regions. Sharpe et al. established a
*shared causal driver*. These are not contradictory findings. A system can carry two separable
read-outs that are nonetheless written by one upstream channel, and such a system would pass an
fMRI-style dissociation test while failing MECH-034's manipulation test. So the honest conclusion is
that the existing literature does not settle MECH-034 in either direction, and the V3 experiment --
the harm-without-mismatch versus mismatch-without-harm double manipulation -- is doing real work
rather than confirming something already known.

## Caveats I do not want to paper over

The analogy from dopamine to REE residue is mine, not the paper's, and it is loose. Residue in REE is
defined by harm and violation outcomes; the dopamine transients in this study are carrying associative
error over entirely neutral stimuli. What the paper actually shows is that a *value-coded* substrate
does model work, not that a *harm-coded* one does. That is a weaker statement than the one MECH-034's
falsifier is about, and I have priced it into a mapping fidelity of 0.62.

The paper carries two erratum notices (2017 and 2018). I checked: neither retracts the central
optogenetic result. I record it here because a governance reader should not have to discover that
independently.

## Confidence

0.71, direction `weakens`. High source quality on a genuinely causal design; the discount is all in
the mapping. This entry exists to stop MECH-034 from being read as already-supported, which after
Glascher alone it might be.
