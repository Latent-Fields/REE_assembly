# Integrated neural dynamics of sensorimotor decisions and actions (Thura, Cabana, Feghaly & Cisek 2022)

According to PubMed, Thura, Cabana, Feghaly & Cisek (2022), *Integrated neural dynamics of sensorimotor decisions and actions*, PLoS Biology ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001861)), recorded simultaneously from five regions -- dorsal premotor (PMd), primary motor (M1), dorsolateral prefrontal (dlPFC) cortex and the external and internal segments of the globus pallidus (GPe, GPi) -- while monkeys performed a dynamic decision task in which the evidence changes over the course of a trial. Dimensionality reduction recovered four interpretable components of population activity: a **state transition between deliberation and commitment**, the transformation of sensory evidence into a choice, and the **baseline and slope of a rising urgency** signal. During deliberation, cortical activity lived on a two-dimensional "decision manifold" spanned by evidence and urgency; **at the moment of commitment the state fell off that manifold into a choice-dependent trajectory leading to movement**. Pallidal activity during deliberation was defined primarily by urgency. The authors read all of this as evidence that deciding and acting are not separate mechanisms but two modes of one integrated dynamical system.

This paper earns its place on the commit/de-commit latch shelf for two reasons, one supporting and one cautionary, which is why I have marked it **mixed** rather than a clean support. The supporting half: it gives a strong, recent, multi-region picture of **commitment as a discrete state transition** -- exactly the event MECH-090 models as the gate flipping from "E3 updates internally" to "E3 output routes to action." The "fall off the decision manifold at the moment of commitment" is the biological signature of a latch closing. That the transition is legible as its own component, separable from the evidence-to-choice transformation, is good evidence that a *commitment* state is a real, distinct thing and not just the tail of evidence accumulation.

The cautionary half is the one that makes this paper load-bearing for the **divergence ledger**, and it is the heart of why the BG-3 node was opened. The variable that sets **when** commitment happens here is a **graded, dynamically rising urgency signal**, carried prominently by the **pallidum** -- the basal-ganglia output that REE's gate abstracts away. Commitment timing is not set by a fixed clock; it is set by where the rising urgency intersects the available evidence. REE, by contrast, sets the *duration* of the committed state with a tuned, committed-run-scaled refractory parameter. That is the precise content of "beta-gate refractory dynamics are tuned, not bio-sourced": the biology times commitment (and, by symmetry, the maintenance window before release) with a graded urgency signal that the BG computes online, whereas REE times it with a hand-set refractory. A REE latch cannot, as built, reproduce urgency-dependent shortening or lengthening of the committed epoch -- it has no urgency variable to be sensitive to. MECH-342 (commit-maintenance-release on degraded readiness) is the closest REE has to an online, state-dependent release signal, and this paper is the cleanest biological argument for why that pathway should be graded rather than a fixed timeout.

There is a second, subtler divergence to log. For Cisek's group, deciding and executing are two modes of a *single* integrated system, and commitment is a continuous fall-off from a shared manifold. REE keeps an explicit architectural seam: an E3 selection event upstream and a commitment-gated routing variable downstream, with the commit modelled as a discrete gate flip. This is a defensible engineering choice -- the seam is what makes the latch inspectable and what lets closure (SD-034) act on it as a named object -- but it means REE's commitment is a step function where the biology shows a smooth trajectory off a manifold. For the grounding ledger that is "structural simplification, justified by inspectability," not a silent departure.

I rate this **mixed** at confidence 0.60. Source quality is high (PLoS Biology, the Cisek lab, five regions recorded and analysed together, 2022). Mapping fidelity is moderate-low (0.58): commitment-as-state-transition maps well and the urgency-timing result is the single best anchor for the duration divergence, but the discrete-gate-vs-continuous-manifold difference and the absence of any REE urgency variable cap the fidelity. Transfer risk is moderate (0.45): macaque reaching decisions onto an ANN latch. The right way to use this entry is as the **divergence anchor**: biology times commitment with a graded, BG-mediated urgency signal, which is exactly what REE's fixed refractory is *not* -- and naming that gap honestly is the whole point of the node.
