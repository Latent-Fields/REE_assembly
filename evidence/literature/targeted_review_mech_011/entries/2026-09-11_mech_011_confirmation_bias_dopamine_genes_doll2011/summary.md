# Dopaminergic genes and susceptibility to confirmation bias (Doll, Hutchison & Frank, 2011)

If Doll et al. (2009) established that verbal instruction corrupts reinforcement learning, this companion study asks the question an architect would ask next: is the corrupting pathway the same machinery as the learning it corrupts, or a separate system acting upon it? The answer matters to REE more than the phenomenon itself does, because MECH-011's concluding framing — language as "a multiplier of ethical learning, not a substitute for harm sensing and residue continuity" — is precisely a claim about separability. A multiplier is, by definition, a distinct term.

The method is a genetic dissociation. Participants performed the same instructed probabilistic-learning paradigm while genotyped for three dopaminergic polymorphisms. The prefrontal marker (COMT rs4680) predicted how stubbornly participants persisted with the prior instruction in the face of contradictory evidence. The striatal markers (DARPP-32, DRD2) predicted something different: how much they learned from positive versus negative outcomes, and the degree to which outcomes were inflated or neglected depending on whether they agreed with what participants had been told. Two genetic signatures, two functional roles — holding the verbal prior, and distorting the reinforcement statistic in its favour.

The translation to REE is reasonably clean. A symbol channel feeding a REE agent would need at minimum two parameters, not one: a persistence term governing how tenaciously a linguistic prior is held against disconfirmation, and a gain term governing how strongly that prior reweights incoming residue. MECH-011 as currently written implies one undifferentiated influence of language on learning. This study suggests that is under-specified, and under-specified in a way that matters — because the two parameters fail differently. High persistence with low reweighting gives you a stubborn agent whose harm signal nonetheless stays honest; low persistence with high reweighting gives you an agent that abandons instructions easily but systematically miscounts evidence while it holds them. Only the second is a rationalisation loop. Collapsing them into a single "language influence" knob makes the dangerous configuration invisible.

There is also a finding here that cuts against any attempt to pick a sensible default. Susceptibility varied substantially between individuals as a function of genotype. If the biological system has real trait variance on this parameter, then a REE implementation that hard-codes a single global weight is not modelling the mechanism, it is modelling one draw from it — and there is no particular reason the population mean is the right draw for an agent whose ethical learning we care about.

I want to be careful about how much weight this carries. Candidate-gene association studies from around 2011 have a poor replication record as a class, and I would not stake anything on the specific polymorphism-to-function assignments. What survives that discount is the architectural shape: prefrontal and striatal contributions came apart. That is the part MECH-011 needs, and it is the part least dependent on any individual SNP result holding up. Even so, inferring architecture from genetic dissociation is an inference and not a demonstration, and the task remains instrumental reward learning rather than ethical learning. My honest position is that this makes the two-pathway reading of MECH-011 *more plausible than its alternatives*, not established — which is roughly the epistemic status the claim itself currently occupies at `candidate`.
