# 'Starting point, not culmination' -- the bootstrapping reading, stated outright

If ARC-094 is a developmental claim, this is the paper that says so most plainly, and it is the reason the adult dissociation literature stops looking like a falsifier.

Meltzoff's framework runs in three steps. Infants represent their own acts and others' observed acts in commensurate terms. They recognise the cross-modal equivalence between what they feel themselves do and what they see another do. That recognition then generates the attribution of similar psychological states -- perceptions, intentions, emotions. The framework's own summary sentence is the load-bearing one: self-other equivalence is "the starting point for social cognition, not its culmination."

Set that beside Krishnan and colleagues' finding that adult somatic and vicarious pain patterns cross-predict at chance. Under a runtime reading of ARC-094 those two are in flat contradiction. Under Meltzoff's, they are the same story at two ages: a shared origin that differentiates into specialised routes. Differentiation from a common start is what a scaffold produces. The mature dissociation is the expected endpoint, not a refutation.

So the disagreement I reported earlier today between ARC-094's prohibition and its mechanism largely dissolves once the claim is read as temporal scaffolding rather than concurrent composition. The claim's problem is wording, not substance -- "binding existing motivational-affective streams" reads as things happening together, when what is meant is one capacity seeding another.

But the entry is not unambiguously good news for the claim, and this is the part worth carrying to governance. **Meltzoff's route has no object in it.** It runs from felt own-movement to seen other-movement by bodily correspondence, directly. If that route is sufficient to launch social cognition -- and the framework argues it is -- then the object stage is not on the critical path. This paper supports ARC-094's *ordering* while quietly undercutting the *middle term*. It sits in tension with the Umilta entry in this same directory, where the object turns out to be exactly what makes the mapping fire. Both cannot be the whole story, and which one governs is a real open question rather than a presentational wrinkle.

Two further limits. It is a synthesis, not a test: no result reported here could have come out the other way, so it cannot discriminate bootstrapping from an account where cognitive empathy is acquired on its own. And it concerns mental-state attribution in general, not fast affective empathy specifically, so it leaves underdetermined whether the fast route is the thing doing the bootstrapping that ARC-094 says it is.

Confidence 0.70. High mapping fidelity -- the source's own words carry the distinction -- discounted for being framework rather than evidence.
