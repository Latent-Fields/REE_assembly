# Dehaene et al. (2022) -- the upper level is real, but it may not be downstream of language

ARC-102 makes two assertions bundled into one claim. First, that there are two abstraction levels
and they must not be conflated: a sub-symbolic substrate level and a symbolic level of recombination
over named, grounded primitives. Second, that the symbolic level is the consequent reasoning
capacity of the language layer. This paper gives the first assertion its strongest available support
and argues against the second. That is why the direction is mixed, and the mixture is not a hedge --
it is the finding.

The hypothesis is that humans run multiple internal languages of thought, one per domain -- geometry,
number, music, sequences -- each of which discretises its domain with a small symbol set and composes
those symbols recursively into mental programs encoding nested repetition with variation. The
evidence that makes this more than a framework is comparative. In shape and sequence perception
tasks, minimum description length in the proposed symbolic languages predicts human behaviour and
brain activity; the same tasks in baboons and macaques are captured by simpler non-symbolic models.
Humans show a geometric regularity effect that the primates do not.

That comparison is the cleanest thing I have found for ARC-102's level distinction. The claim that
symbolic abstraction is a LEVEL rather than a point on a gradient is hard to test, because a gradient
account can usually absorb a quantitative gap. What it cannot easily absorb is a change in the class
of model that fits: two species, one task, and the winning model family is symbolic for one and
non-symbolic for the other. If that holds up, the two levels are not a convenient description, they
are a real joint.

The second assertion fares badly. ARC-102 places the symbolic level downstream of the V6 language
layer -- it is "the consequent reasoning capacity" of that layer. Dehaene and colleagues go out of
their way not to say this. Their languages of thought are multiple and domain-specific, and they are
posited beside natural language rather than derived from it; the geometry language is not the number
language, and neither is natural language. On their account, removing the language faculty would not
obviously remove symbolic recombination in geometry.

Two consequences for REE, both of which I would route to governance rather than resolve here. The
dependency direction is the first: if the symbolic level is not consequent on the language layer,
V6's ordering is wrong and the symbolic level should be buildable independently -- which changes
what ARC-102 gates. The second is subtler and may matter more. If the multiplicity is real, then
"the symbolic level" is a category error: there would be several, each grounded in its own domain's
primitives, and ARC-102 would need re-scoping per domain rather than stating one upper level.

Confidence 0.68. Opinion-format synthesis, but resting on a specific and replicated comparative
dissociation. The honest summary is that this paper hands ARC-102 its level distinction and takes
away its causal story.
