# Fedorenko & Varley (2016) -- the upper level survives losing the language layer

This is the entry that argues against ARC-102, and it argues against the half of the claim that is
load-bearing for the V6 plan. ARC-102 does not merely distinguish two abstraction levels; it says
the symbolic one is "the consequent reasoning capacity of the language layer". That is a dependency
assertion, and dependency assertions have a clean test: remove the putative cause and see whether
the effect survives.

In humans the removal is done for us. Fedorenko and Varley synthesise what happens in global
aphasia -- near-total loss of language comprehension and production following left-hemisphere
damage. These patients add and subtract, solve logic problems, reason about what other people are
thinking, hold material in working memory, suppress prepotent responses, listen to music with
apparent appreciation, and find their way around. Arithmetic and formal logic are recombination over
named, grounded primitives in anyone's description. They are ARC-102's symbolic level. And they
survive the loss of language.

The imaging converges from the other direction. In neurotypical adults, the language network
responds robustly to sentences and does not respond to arithmetic, working-memory encoding,
cognitive control, or music. A language-consequent symbolic level makes an anatomical prediction --
that symbolic reasoning should recruit the machinery it is supposedly downstream of -- and in the
one system where that prediction can be measured, it fails. Two independent methods, lesion and
imaging, pointing the same way, which is why I have put the confidence at 0.76 rather than lower
despite this being a review rather than primary data.

There are two ways REE could absorb this, and I want both on the record so that neither happens
quietly. The first is developmental: global aphasics acquired their symbolic competence while
language was intact, so these data constrain ongoing runtime dependence and say nothing about
origin. ARC-102 might be re-read as a claim about bootstrapping ORDER -- the language layer is how
the symbolic level gets built, after which it runs independently. That is a defensible position and
it is compatible with everything here. It is also a real narrowing of what ARC-102 currently
asserts, and it should be registered as an amendment rather than adopted as the reading that was
always intended.

The second escape route is definitional and I think more dangerous. REE's "language layer" is an
architectural component with named grounded primitives; the human left-lateralised language network
is a specific piece of neuroanatomy. If REE's layer is defined broadly enough to include arithmetic
notation, then aphasia evidence does not touch it -- but at that point the claim has been made
difficult to falsify, because any symbolic system the agent possesses counts as part of the layer it
is supposed to depend on. If that is the reading, the claim needs re-scoping, not defending.

Taken with the Dehaene et al. entry in this same directory, the pattern is consistent: the
literature supports ARC-102's two-level distinction and does not support its causal attribution of
the upper level to language. My reading is that the claim should be split -- the level distinction
promoted on its own merits, the dependency clause demoted to a separate candidate with this as its
standing negative evidence. That is a governance call rather than mine to make here, but the
evidence is unambiguous enough that leaving the two bundled will misprice both.
