# Christensen & Grünbaum (2018) — the objection SD-031 has to answer

According to PubMed, this paper ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2018.07.002)) makes a distinction that REE's per-stream comparator topology has not explicitly made, and once made it lands awkwardly close to SD-031's foundations.

The distinction is between **narrow** sense of agency — agency associated with one's movements — and **broad** sense of agency, associated with environmental events. The authors' argument runs: the comparator model was developed from, and draws its evidential support from, studies of sensory consequences *narrowly* tied to movements; narrow agency is explained just as well by a simpler motor-signal model, so the comparator's extra machinery is not licensed by parsimony; and standard experimental paradigms actually study only *broad* agency. They add two experiments that failed to induce illusory narrow agency in healthy participants, where the comparator model predicts it should be inducible.

The awkwardness for SD-031 is direct. z_world is by definition the *broad* stream — non-harm, non-self environmental consequences, "the door opened because of me." SD-031 proposes to instantiate MECH-256's comparator precisely there. That is exactly the extension Christensen and Grünbaum argue is unwarranted by the human data. This is not a peripheral objection to be noted and moved past; it is an objection to the central architectural move, and it is why I have logged this as `weakens` rather than `mixed`.

But there is a limit that materially caps how much it weakens, and it needs stating rather than buried. These authors are arguing about the comparator as a model of the conscious *experience* of agency. SD-031 specifies a functional attribution *mechanism* in an artificial agent, with no phenomenal commitment at all. A model can be a poor account of what agency feels like while remaining a perfectly good way to compute what an agent caused — the arguments are about different things, and the disanalogy is why I set transfer_risk at 0.50, the highest in this pull. Two smaller limits point the same way: the empirical contribution is two *null* illusion-induction results, and absence of an induced illusion is weak evidence against an underlying mechanism; and their positive alternative, the motor-signal model, is offered for *narrow* agency, so they leave the broad/world-state case — the case SD-031 actually occupies — without a worked competitor.

So the honest reading is narrower than "the comparator is wrong for z_world." It is: **SD-031's biological-warrant argument is weaker than its functional_restatement implies.** That restatement invokes parietal outcome-prediction circuits and Sommer & Wurtz corollary-discharge loops as though the comparator architecture straightforwardly extends from movement-tied reafference to world-state consequences. This paper says that extension is the contested step, not the settled one. The engineering case for SD-031 — that an explicit residual is a clean, inspectable way to compute agent-caused versus externally-caused world change — stands on its own and is untouched here.

Two things follow for how SD-031 should proceed. The claim should stop leaning on biological analogy as though it were load-bearing support, and lean instead on the functional argument plus its own validation run; the analogy is a source of design intuition, not evidence. And the pending validation gains a sharper bar: because a simpler non-comparator account may suffice, the run should show the comparator earns its keep — that the residual discriminates agent-caused from externally-caused events *better than a simpler signal available without the forward model*, such as raw action magnitude or an unpredicted-change detector. Read alongside Ohata et al. in this same pull, which warns that attribution and sensorimotor information are easily confounded without a preference analysis, the two entries converge on the same recommendation: the validation needs a discriminative comparison against a simpler baseline, not a bare residual-magnitude gap. The 2026-06-06 activation smoke (self-caused ~2.0 vs externally-caused ~22.6) is exactly the kind of magnitude contrast that a motor-signal-only account could also produce.
