# Murray, Bussey & Saksida (2007) — perirhinal feature-conjunction representations as the biological grounding for SD-009

**Source.** Murray EA, Bussey TJ, Saksida LM. *Visual perception and memory: a new view of medial temporal lobe function in primates and rodents.* Annual Review of Neuroscience 30:99–122 (2007). DOI [10.1146/annurev.neuro.29.051605.113046](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.neuro.29.051605.113046) · PMID [17417938](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17417938/). Citation verified against PubMed on 2026-08-09.

**Why this entry exists at all is worth stating first**, because it is not the usual reason. SD-009's prose in `claims.yaml` — and MECH-100's, which carries the same sentence — has been grounding its central biological argument on "Murray et al. 2004, TICS 8:56–61". That article does not exist. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* volume 8, issue 2 (February 2004) contains no paper by any Murray, and pages 56–61 do not correspond to a single article in that issue: page 57 is a letter by Toates, and pages 60–65 are Stevens & Hauser on the evolution of cooperation. Issue 3 begins at page 95. A tracked literature record for this citation *did* exist, but only against MECH-100 (`targeted_review_connectome_mech_100/entries/2026-03-29_mech_100_ventral_stream_categorical_murray2004`), and its own metadata was wrong in three places: the title it gave is not a Murray/Bussey/Saksida paper, its DOI differs from the real one in the final digits, and the PMID its URL points at (17645520) is a 2007 liver-transplantation paper in *Hepatology Research*. The underlying paper the entry was reaching for is unambiguous from its authors, year, venue and content — it is the 2007 Annual Review above — so nothing here overturns the substance of the earlier pull. It corrects the bibliography and, for the first time, registers the source against SD-009, which until now had no biological or neuroscientific literature entry at all: its `literature_confidence` of 0.724 rested entirely on two ML papers (ADAT/Kim 2022, CURL/Laskin 2020).

**What the paper actually shows.** Murray, Bussey and Saksida marshal lesion, electrophysiological and modelling evidence against the then-prevailing view that the medial temporal lobe subserves memory but not perception, and that its anatomically distinct parts work as one declarative-memory system. The strand that matters here is the perirhinal one. Perirhinal cortex, the terminus of the ventral visual stream for object knowledge, represents *conjunctions* of object features rather than features alone. The signature is performance on discriminations with high **feature ambiguity** — tasks in which each individual feature appears in both the rewarded and the unrewarded stimulus, so that no single feature separates the classes and only a conjunctive representation can. Perirhinal lesions in monkeys and rats impair exactly these discriminations while sparing feature-unambiguous ones; hippocampal lesions do not impair them at all. The representational structure is not a passive readout of input statistics — it is shaped by the demand to discriminate.

**How this translates to SD-009, and where the translation stops.** z_world is REE's ventral-stream analog: a static, single-frame encoding whose downstream consumer, E3 harm evaluation, needs it to separate world states that share nearly all of their surface features and differ only in harm-relevance. That is the same shape of problem as feature ambiguity. And SD-009's original argument follows the paper's logic closely: reconstruction and E1-prediction losses reward accurate decoding of every observation channel equally, so they supply no gradient that would pull hazard-present and hazard-absent states apart; some discriminative pressure has to be applied to the encoder for the representation to carry the distinction. On that antecedent the paper is genuinely supportive.

The translation stops short of what SD-009 actually asserts. SD-009 does not claim that *some* discriminative pressure is required; it claims that a **dedicated event-type cross-entropy head** is. The review supports the general necessity and is silent on the specific choice, and since 2026-07-18 that gap has become the whole question rather than a quibble. SD-070's anti-collapse P0 already trains the world path with class-balanced categorical CE over static scene-structure targets — hazard and resource presence, bucketed nearest-Chebyshev distance — plus a VICReg variance/covariance penalty. The counterfactual this paper argues against, an encoder trained under no categorical pressure at all, is therefore no longer the counterfactual SD-009 is being tested against. V3-EXQ-897 (2026-08-08) tested what remains: whether the dedicated event-CE head adds anything on top of SD-070's grounding heads. It measured the held-out frozen-probe decodability margin above floor in *both* arms, with OFF ≥ ON on all three seeds (deltas −0.025 / −0.010 / −0.005) — sign-consistent but statistically imprecise, and read by the autopsy as `direction=unknown` rather than a weakens.

**Limitations, and the confidence.** Three gaps between source and claim, in descending order of how much they cost. First and largest: the paper grounds the necessity of a discriminative signal, not the identity of the head that supplies it, and SD-070 now supplies one by a different route — this is why `mapping_fidelity` is set at 0.45, lower than the 0.58 the same source carries against MECH-100, which is stated at the more general granularity. Second, the biological mechanism is experience-dependent plasticity across development and extended task training in intact animals; SD-009 is an auxiliary CE head trained inside a short P0 phase, so the alignment is conceptual rather than mechanistic. Third, perirhinal cortex sits at the ventral-stream/MTL boundary and carries mnemonic as well as perceptual function; if z_world's right analog is earlier ventral stream — V4, posterior IT — the mapping is mislocated, a caveat the MECH-100 entry already records and which this one inherits.

Aggregate confidence **0.50**, weighted toward mapping fidelity as the skill prescribes for a design-decision claim. That is deliberately below the level a reader might expect from an Annual Review with a canonical lesion literature behind it. The source is strong; the reach is short. Registering it higher would let a well-supported general principle stand in for a specific design commitment that a controlled ablation has just failed to detect any marginal benefit from, which is precisely the substitution the 2026-08-08 governance cycle flagged and the reason this entry was commissioned.
