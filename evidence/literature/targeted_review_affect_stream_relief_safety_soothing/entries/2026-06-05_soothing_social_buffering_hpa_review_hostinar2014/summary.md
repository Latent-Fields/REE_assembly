# Social buffering of the HPA axis (Hostinar, Sullivan & Gunnar 2014)

**Source:** Hostinar CE, Sullivan RM, Gunnar MR. *Psychobiological mechanisms underlying the social buffering of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenocortical axis: a review of animal models and human studies across development.* Psychological Bulletin 2014;140(1):256-282. [doi:10.1037/a0032671](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0032671) (PMID 23607429).

## What the paper did

This is the canonical integrative review of *social buffering*: the dampening of the HPA-axis stress response to threat or challenge that occurs in the presence or with the assistance of a conspecific. The authors integrate rodent models and human studies from infancy to adulthood, and propose a working model in which oxytocinergic systems and prefrontal neural networks are two putative biological mediators, with early-life attachment experience shaping the later capacity to be buffered. They are explicit that the underlying neurobiology is only partially understood and flag major gaps.

## Why it bears on the differentiation (proposed SOOTHING stream; no registered claim)

This entry establishes the **third pole** of the relief/safety/soothing differentiation, and it is the one REE has *not* registered. Social buffering is mechanistically distinct from the other two on every axis the verdict cares about:

- **Circuit:** oxytocinergic + parasympathetic/HPA + prefrontal regulation -- a neuroendocrine/autonomic regulatory layer -- not the NAc/VTA value-coding reward circuit of relief (MECH-302) and not the cortico-amygdalar/thalamic conditioned-inhibition circuit of safety (MECH-303/304).
- **Time reference:** it acts on the *ongoing* stress state (present-tense down-regulation of an active response), whereas relief is event-locked to an aversive *offset* (past) and safety is a *prospective* predictor (future).
- **Computational role:** a state-gain / recovery modulator (it reduces the magnitude and recovery time of the active stress trajectory), not a reinforcement teaching signal and not a learned predictor.
- **Trigger:** the canonical trigger is a *conspecific* -- another agent -- which is why it leans V4-social.

In REE terms the proposed soothing stream would act on the suffering accumulator (MECH-219) decay/gain and the homeostatic drive layer (SD-012; SD-032e pACC autonomic coupling), analogous to how relief and safety *read* the SD-011 harm streams without *being* them. Folding soothing into relief or safety would be the SD-011/SD-003 philosophy-right/mechanism-wrong failure mode the project's `feedback_biology_before_formal_definitions` rule warns against.

## Limitations and caveats

`claim_ids_tested` is intentionally empty: REE has no registered soothing/social-buffering mechanism, so this is forward-looking grounding for the extension-register decision, not evidence for an existing claim. The canonical mechanism requires a conspecific's presence -- something REE V3 cannot represent (no other-model), which is the basis for the V4-social scope recommendation. A non-social autonomic-recovery core (parasympathetic down-regulation of ongoing arousal) is conceivable as a minimal V3 hook, but that extrapolates beyond what a social-buffering review tests. The review is also explicit about its own neurobiological gaps and the developmental-attachment dependence, a state REE has no representation of.
