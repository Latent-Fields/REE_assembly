# Relief learning requires coincident NAc D1 + NMDA activation (Bergado Acosta et al. 2017)

**Source:** Bergado Acosta JR, Kahl E, Kogias G, Uzuneser TC, Fendt M. *Relief learning requires a coincident activation of dopamine D1 and NMDA receptors within the nucleus accumbens.* Neuropharmacology 2017;114:58-66. [doi:10.1016/j.neuropharm.2016.11.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropharm.2016.11.022) (PMID 27894877).

## What the paper did

Relief learning is the association of a stimulus with the *offset* of an aversive event; the conditioned stimulus later evokes appetitive-like behaviour. Building on their prior demonstration that NAc NMDA receptors are needed for relief learning, the authors asked whether relief recruits the same accumbal dopamine-glutamate interaction known to mediate *reward* learning. Rats received intra-NAc injections of the D1 antagonist SCH23390 and the NMDA antagonist AP5, separately or together, at different phases of a relief-conditioning procedure. SCH23390 dose-dependently blocked both acquisition and expression of conditioned relief. Critically, co-injection of SCH23390 and AP5 at individually sub-threshold doses blocked acquisition (but not consolidation or expression), demonstrating a coincidence requirement. Locomotor responses to the aversive stimulus were unaffected, ruling out a perceptual confound.

## Why it bears on the claim (MECH-302)

This is a load-bearing cellular anchor for MECH-302, which asserts that the relief-completion event reuses goal-achievement / reward-circuit completion machinery rather than a distinct circuit. The NAc D1+NMDA coincidence is the canonical signature of value-coding reward learning; finding the *same* coincidence is required for relief acquisition pins relief to the reward substrate at the receptor level. It complements the structural and cellular anchors already in `targeted_review_relief_completion_mechanism` (Andreatta 2012 ventral-striatum/amygdala double dissociation; Navratilova 2012 VTA-DA / NAc-DA at pain offset). Together they make the relief-as-reward-machinery half of the three-way differentiation the most strongly grounded.

For the affect-stream differentiation specifically, this entry establishes the **first pole**: relief is an event-locked, phasic, value-coding reinforcement signal living on NAc/VTA reward substrate. That is mechanistically distinct from the prospective cortico-amygdalar *prediction* of safety (Corches 2019, Silva 2021) and from the autonomic/neuroendocrine *down-regulation* of ongoing stress (Hostinar 2014, Heinrichs 2003) catalogued in the sibling entries.

## Limitations and caveats

The paradigm is rat cue-offset relief learning, not a suffering-stream comparator, so the mapping is at the level of shared reinforcement substrate, not REE's specific implementation. The paper does not test MECH-302's stated downstream pipeline (MECH-057a commitment-release, MECH-091 phase reset, MECH-094 categorical write gate); it supports "relief reuses the reward/value substrate", which should not be over-read as "relief is identical to REE's commitment-release pipeline". The coincidence requirement is acquisition-specific, a useful constraint for any REE relief-learning rule.
