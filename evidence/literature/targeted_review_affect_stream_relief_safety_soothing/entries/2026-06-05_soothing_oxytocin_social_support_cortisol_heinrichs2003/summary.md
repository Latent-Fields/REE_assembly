# Oxytocin x social support suppress cortisol and anxiety (Heinrichs et al. 2003)

**Source:** Heinrichs M, Baumgartner T, Kirschbaum C, Ehlert U. *Social support and oxytocin interact to suppress cortisol and subjective responses to psychosocial stress.* Biological Psychiatry 2003;54(12):1389-1398. [doi:10.1016/S0006-3223(03)00465-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3223(03)00465-7) (PMID 14675803).

## What the paper did

Thirty-seven healthy men received intranasal oxytocin (24 IU) or placebo 50 minutes before the Trier Social Stress Test, with or without social support from their best friend during preparation -- a 2x2 design. Salivary free cortisol was suppressed by social support; oxytocin produced an anxiolytic effect on subjective anxiety; and the combination of oxytocin plus social support produced the lowest cortisol concentrations together with the greatest calmness and least anxiety. The authors conclude that oxytocin enhances the buffering effect of social support on stress reactivity -- a causal human mechanistic complement to the social-buffering review (Hostinar 2014, sibling entry).

## Why it bears on the differentiation (proposed SOOTHING stream; no registered claim)

This is the human, causal anchor for the soothing pole. It demonstrates that the *ongoing* neuroendocrine and subjective stress response to an active stressor can be down-regulated by an oxytocin-mediated, socially-gated mechanism. That is a different computation from the other two streams:

- It acts on the **present** stress trajectory (reducing cortisol and anxiety *during* the stressor), not on an aversive **offset** (relief, MECH-302) and not as a **prospective** threat-absence predictor (safety, MECH-303/304).
- Its substrate is **oxytocinergic / HPA / autonomic**, not the NAc/VTA reward circuit of relief or the cortico-amygdalar/thalamic circuit of safety.
- The effect is strongest at the **oxytocin x social-support interaction** -- it is socially gated, which is the empirical basis for scoping soothing as V4-social.

In REE terms the proposed soothing stream maps to a state-gain/recovery modulator on the suffering accumulator (MECH-219 decay/gain) and the homeostatic drive layer (SD-012; SD-032e pACC autonomic coupling): it would reduce the amplitude and persistence of the active aversive trajectory. Like relief and safety, it *reads/modulates* the harm-and-drive substrate without *being* it; unlike them, no REE claim currently represents it.

## Limitations and caveats

`claim_ids_tested` is intentionally empty -- no soothing mechanism is registered, so this entry grounds the differentiation and the proposed extension-register row, not an existing claim. The outcomes are human cortisol/HPA and self-report; REE has neither an HPA axis nor an other-model, so the mapping to MECH-219/SD-012 dynamics is analogical and the social trigger is V4-bound. A single healthy-male sample with intranasal oxytocin (whose central penetrance is debated) caps the source-quality weight. None of this undermines the differentiation point -- it sharpens it: soothing is mechanistically distinct and largely V4.
