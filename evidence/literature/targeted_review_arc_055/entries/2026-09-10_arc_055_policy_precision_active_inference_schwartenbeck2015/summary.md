# Precision over policies, which is alpha_A written as an equation

Schwartenbeck and colleagues designed a limited-offer game for fMRI: subjects wait for a high offer, can accept a low one at any point, and lose everything if they wait too long. Behaviour was compared by Bayesian model comparison between active-inference schemes based on surprise minimisation and classical utility-maximisation schemes, and active inference won decisively. The model's precision term -- the confidence that the chosen policy will lead to desired outcomes -- was then computed trial by trial and regressed against BOLD, and midbrain activity encompassing dopamine projection neurons was accurately predicted by it. The authors' summary is that subjects infer both what they should do and how confident they are in that inference.

The reason this belongs in an ARC-055 directory is narrower and more specific than the general resonance between active inference and REE. ARC-055's notes name a control plane -- alpha_S for sensory precision, alpha_A for policy precision which "commits to high-D_V trajectories", tau_E2 for rollout temperature, H for horizon, kappa_commit for the commitment threshold. Of those, alpha_A is the one that sits squarely on the E3 selection leg, and this paper is the closest the literature comes to treating exactly that parameter formally and empirically at once. The distinction worth holding onto is between precision over sensory evidence and precision over policies. Meyniel & Dehaene, Masset et al., Lak et al., and arguably Zajkowski's information bonus as well, are all about uncertainty concerning what is the case or which option pays better. Policy precision is uncertainty about which course of action to pursue, and in active inference it does not merely inform selection -- it *is* the inverse temperature of the softmax over expected free energy that performs the selection. A low-precision agent is by construction one whose selection fails to commit. That is ARC-055's E3 leg restated as a piece of mathematics rather than as an architectural assertion, and it is the first entry in this directory to supply that.

The task helps more than one might expect. A recurring problem across this directory -- the one that motivated the whole selection-leg pull -- is that the causal studies use two-alternative perceptual decisions whose trajectory space is degenerate, so a depth-sensitive or commitment-sensitive term has nothing to do even if the architecture mandates it. The limited-offer game is not like that. The decision is how long to hold out, which is a commitment sustained over a horizon and revised as the horizon shrinks, and the model's precision term evolves across that period. It is not deep in the sense ARC-055 means by D_V, but it has genuine temporal extent, and it is the second entry here (with Zajkowski) whose task is not degenerate in this respect.

Where the entry is weak is where a model-based imaging study is always weak, and I want to state it plainly rather than let the strong mapping carry the reader past it. The precision signal is a posit of the fitted model. The analysis assumes an architecture in which precision over policies is explicitly represented, and then finds that the assumed quantity correlates with midbrain BOLD. ARC-055's load-bearing commitment is that V(t) and D_V are explicit signals and not emergent epiphenomena -- and a design of this shape cannot adjudicate that, because the explicitness is an input to the analysis rather than an output of it. A behaviourally equivalent agent with no represented precision term would, if fitted with this model, yield a fitted precision trace all the same. Relatedly, correlation is not consumption: even granting that the midbrain tracks policy precision, nothing here shows the selection computation reads it. A signal broadcast and read by nobody produces these data. The causal weight on the selection leg is being carried by Zajkowski and Tervo, not by this entry.

Two smaller limits. Active inference's precision is a formal analogue of REE's alpha_A rather than the same object, because the two frameworks differ in what their generative models range over, so the mapping is structural analogy. And midbrain fMRI is spatially coarse; "encompassing dopamine projection neurons" is anatomical inference, not cell-type measurement, so if the responding population is not dopaminergic the biological anchor weakens while the formal mapping survives intact. I have set confidence at 0.68 with mapping fidelity 0.82 -- the highest in the directory -- and source quality 0.70. That inversion is deliberate and is the honest summary of the entry: it is the claim's formal anchor, the place where alpha_A stops being a name in a notes field and becomes a quantity someone has estimated, and it is not the claim's empirical support.
