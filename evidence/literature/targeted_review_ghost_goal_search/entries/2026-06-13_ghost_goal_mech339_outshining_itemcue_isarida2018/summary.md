# Outshining: item-cue strength gates the contextual channel (Isarida et al. 2018)

**Claim under test:** MECH-339 — the ghost-bank retrieval cue is *composite* (a direct `z_goal` match plus a context channel composed from the already-preserved SD-039 payload fields: `arousal_tag`, `last_vs`, `cause`), and the two are combined by an **outshining gate** so that a strong direct `z_goal` match suppresses the context channel. The claim's distinctive content is not "context helps retrieval" (uncontroversial) but the *conditionality*: context-cue retrieval is a fallback regime, not an always-on additive term.

## What the paper did

Isarida and colleagues studied global environmental context-dependent *recognition* — the question of when reinstating the study environment improves recognition of studied items. The long-standing puzzle in this literature is that environmental context effects are robust for free recall but fragile, often null, for recognition. Their account is the **outshining hypothesis** (Smith, 1988; Smith & Vela, 1986): the item cue and the ambient-context cue compete, and the stronger cue dominates retrieval. Because an item cue is associated with a single item whereas a context cue is associated with many, the item cue is usually the stronger of the two and "outshines" the context — so context-dependent recognition appears only where item-based cues are weak. Across their experiments they manipulate item-cue strength and recover the predicted moderation, decomposing the effect into recollection ("remembering") versus familiarity and showing the context effect is carried by recollection and produces a context-based mirror effect (positive on hits/d′, reversed on false alarms).

## The finding that maps to MECH-339

The load-bearing result is the **inverse relationship between item-cue strength and the size of the context-dependent effect**: the effect shrinks as the item cue strengthens. That is the gate function stated as data. MECH-339 asserts exactly this shape — `context_term = context_weight · gate · context_salience`, where `gate = clip((outshine_pivot − goal_match)/outshine_pivot)` falls to zero as the direct match (`goal_match`) approaches the pivot. A strong direct match closes the gate; the context channel only contributes when the direct cue is weak. Isarida et al. is, to my reading, the cleanest behavioural demonstration that this is how cue competition actually behaves, rather than the simpler hypothesis that context adds a fixed increment regardless of item-cue strength. Their additional observation that the surviving context effect is *recollection-based* matches MECH-339's design intent that the context channel feeds a match-conditional retrieval probe (MECH-292/293), not a graded familiarity signal.

## How it translates — and where the translation strains

The structural correspondence is strong: strength-gated competition between a specific direct cue and a diffuse contextual cue, with the contextual cue as conditional fallback. That is the same machine in both systems. What does not transfer is the *identity* of the channels. In the human task the "item cue" is a studied word and the "context" is a room or photograph; in REE the direct cue is the `z_goal` cosine match and the context channel is a composition over `arousal_tag` (and, by design, the other preserved-but-match-unused SD-039 fields). The paper gives no evidence about that particular composition — it validates the *gating rule*, not the *channel contents*. So this entry supports the outshining-gate architecture (the feature that distinguishes MECH-339 from a fixed-weight additive cue) while leaving the arousal-tag composition untested. I have also assessed it from the abstract and the meta-analytic descriptions rather than the full results tables, which is the second reason confidence sits at 0.66 rather than higher.

## Confidence

`supports`, confidence **0.66**. Source quality is high (a theory-targeted behavioural programme in *Journal of Memory and Language*). Mapping fidelity is high for the one thing that matters most — the conditional, strength-gated shape — and lower for the channel identities. Transfer risk is moderate: human episodic recognition to an agent's latent goal-bank is an analogy, but the polarity (strong direct cue suppresses context) and the conditionality (fallback, not additive) carry across cleanly. This is parallel literature signal only; it does not move `exp_conf`, which remains 0 for MECH-339 (candidate / v3_pending). Its job is to confirm that the substrate step landed 2026-05-19 implements a rule the memory literature actually supports, and to fix the falsifiable prediction: if the implemented context term ever behaves as an always-on additive increment, that would contradict both this paper and the claim.
