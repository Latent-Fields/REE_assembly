# An integrative framework for perceptual disturbances in psychosis

Horga & Abi-Dargham (2019), *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 20(12):763-778. DOI [10.1038/s41583-019-0234-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-019-0234-1) · PMID 31712782

**Claim tested:** MECH-043 — *Dopamine-like modulation of precision-weighting for unsigned prediction errors.*
**Direction:** supports (confidence 0.66)

## What the paper did

A narrative synthesis rather than a new measurement. Horga and Abi-Dargham assemble three strands that had largely been developing separately: the physiology of auditory verbal hallucinations (increased baseline activity in associative auditory cortex, increased dopamine transmission in associative striatum), the psychophysics (perceptual biases indicating increased reliance on prior expectations), and the computational theory of perceptual inference. They then lay out the anatomy of associative cortico-striatal loops as the circuit through which auditory perceptual inference might actually be implemented, and propose a working framework bridging the neurobiology to the phenomenology.

## How this translates to REE

This entry supplies MECH-043's architectural context rather than a test of it, and I want to be clear that this is the whole of its contribution.

MECH-043 posits a neuromodulatory gain term acting on an inference balance, whose misallocation produces hallucination-like output. This review assembles the strongest available case that this is, in fact, how hallucinations arise in humans, and that dopamine is the neuromodulator involved. For REE the concrete contribution is anatomical: the associative cortico-striatal loop is the circuit REE would have to nominate as the homologue of its E3 commitment gate if the dopamine framing in MECH-043 is to be more than a useful analogy. This review is where that circuit is set out in the detail such a nomination would require.

That matters for a reason beyond MECH-043 itself. The neighbouring candidate claims ARC-108 (unified dopamine substrate with learned cortico-striatal gating weights) and ARC-109 (D1/D2 population split) are explicitly about building that loop structure in REE. This review is the shared anatomical reference all three would draw on, and reading it alongside MECH-043 makes clear how much of the dopamine story REE currently compresses into one scalar.

## Limitations and caveats

Two limits, one about the source type and one about content, and the first is the more important for how this entry should be counted.

As a review it contributes no new measurement, and its evidence base overlaps substantially with the Cassidy et al. (2018) entry already in this pull — Horga and Abi-Dargham are co-authors on that paper. These two entries should therefore *not* be read as two independent lines of support. They are closer to one line of evidence reported twice, once primary and once synthesised. I have set confidence at 0.66 partly to keep the aggregate honest about that.

On content, the review frames the pathology as over-weighted priors throughout, and never separates unsigned-PE precision from signed value coding. It consequently offers no leverage at all on MECH-043's falsifiers (i) — that signed or valence information enters the computation — or (iv) — non-dissociability from MECH-054's signed channels. Those are precisely the clauses that distinguish MECH-043 from its neighbours, so the review supports the claim's plausibility while contributing nothing to its discriminating content.

Finally, the anatomy on offer is loop-structured and modality-specific, whereas REE's precision term is a single scalar in the E3 selector with no loop structure and no sensory-cortex analogue. The anatomical mapping here describes a target REE has not built, not one it currently satisfies.
