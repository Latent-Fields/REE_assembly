# Cowan (2001) — the discrete ~4-chunk focus of attention

**Claim tested:** Q-077 — is SD-046's multi-slot GoalState a set of *discrete* fixed-capacity slots (Cowan ~4-chunk focus of attention) or a single *flexible* precision resource shared across goals (Bays & Husain)?

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0140525x01003922)), Cowan's target article is the canonical modern defence of a small, fixed short-term storage capacity. Where Miller's "seven plus or minus two" was, in Cowan's reading, a rhetorical estimate that conflated several sources, Cowan assembles evidence across many paradigms that when you carefully control for chunking, rehearsal, and long-term-memory support, the *pure* capacity limit converges on about four chunks. He interprets this as the capacity of a central focus of attention — a discrete, structurally-bounded store.

This is the natural evidential anchor for the *discrete-slot* horn of Q-077, and hence for SD-046's current framing. SD-046 holds N≥2 simultaneously-active goal slots with per-slot z_goal, drive coupling, age, and persistence; the implicit design decision Q-077 surfaces is whether that "multi-slot" structure is genuinely a fixed number of discrete slots or a relabelling of a shared, dilutable resource. Cowan's ~4 gives the slot reading its teeth: it predicts a *step function* — fidelity holds roughly flat as goals are added up to a fixed capacity, then the marginal goal drops to a floor. That is exactly the discriminating signature Q-077's `what_would_answer` assigns to the slot model.

Two honest caveats keep confidence at 0.58 rather than higher, despite the paper's landmark status. First, Cowan's capacity is a *chunk* limit for stored items in the focus of attention, not a count of simultaneously-active goals with their own drives and persistence dynamics. Whether "how many chunks can be held" transfers to "how many goal workstreams can be maintained and acted toward" is a real and unargued leap. A goal is not a passive stored chunk; it is an active controller, and the capacity constraints on active control may differ from those on passive storage. Second — and this is the subtle point that makes even the slot horn less rigid than it looks — Cowan is explicit that the ~4 limit *only appears under boundary conditions that block recoding*. When chunking or rehearsal is available, effective capacity inflates. So a fixed-N slot count in SD-046 could be partly an artefact of the arena's chunking conditions rather than a true structural cap; a clean step-function result would have to rule out the agent chunking goals together.

For governance, this entry supplies the slot-side prior and its predicted step-function signature, while flagging that the slot account itself is softer than "N fixed slots" suggests. It pairs with the Bays & Husain (2008) resource entry and the Johnson, Simmering & Buss (2014) "beyond slots and resources" entry in this directory; together they frame Q-077 as a genuine, still-unresolved fork in the source literature, which is precisely why it was registered as a substrate-conditional V4 open question rather than settled by fiat.
