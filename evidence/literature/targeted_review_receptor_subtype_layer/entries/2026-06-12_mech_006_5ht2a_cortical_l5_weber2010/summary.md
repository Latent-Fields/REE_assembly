# 5-HT2A sits on cortical layer-V pyramidal cells (Weber & Andrade 2010, Front Neurosci)

According to PubMed, Weber and Andrade ([10.3389/fnins.2010.00036](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2010.00036)) addressed a question that is surprisingly under-resolved given how much psychiatry rests on it: *which* cortical cells actually express the 5-HT2A receptor. Using an Htr2a-EGFP BAC transgenic mouse to read out gene expression, a 5-HT2A-knockout-validated antibody to localise the protein, and slice electrophysiology to check function, they identified three cortical Htr2a populations. The largest is layer V pyramidal cells of the anterior cortex; the receptor protein forms a strong laminar band overlying layer Va with a pronounced anteroposterior gradient that tracks those L5 pyramidal cells. The other two populations are middle-layer GABAergic interneurons (mostly parvalbumin fast-spiking cells, which serotonin depolarises via 5-HT2A) and non-pyramidal subplate/layer VIb cells.

For strand 1 this is the postsynaptic anchor of the cortical half of the level-split. The receptor layer assigns MECH-006's account — narrative-/cognitive-level representational exclusivity and top-level precision — to 5-HT2A on dorsal-raphe-driven cortical L5 pyramidal cells, contrasting it with 5-HT1A on hippocampal cells carrying MECH-085's terrain geometry. Weber & Andrade establish the load-bearing fact that the cortical 5-HT2A receptor is genuinely concentrated on the layer-V pyramidal *output* neurons — the cells whose excitability and precision you would expect to govern high-level representational dynamics, and precisely the population that pimavanserin's 5-HT2A inverse agonism (PHARM-015) acts on. The anteroposterior gradient is a nice bonus: it is consistent with a graded, hierarchy-respecting deployment rather than a uniform cortical wash.

The honest caveat is that localisation is not computation. The paper shows where 5-HT2A is, not that it sets "narrative exclusivity" or "top-level precision" — that bridge is REE's interpretation, and it is a real leap. Worse for any tidy story, a substantial fraction of cortical 5-HT2A sits on fast-spiking interneurons that serotonin *excites*, so the net cortical 5-HT2A effect is a pyramidal-plus-interneuron circuit effect (disinhibition and direct pyramidal modulation both in play), not a clean pyramidal-gain knob. A REE model that treats 5-HT2A as a single L5-pyramidal precision dial is therefore simplifying a two-armed circuit.
