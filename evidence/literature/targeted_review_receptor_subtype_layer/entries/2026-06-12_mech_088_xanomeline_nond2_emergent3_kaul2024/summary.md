# A non-D2 antipsychotic works in humans (Kaul et al. 2024, JAMA Psychiatry, EMERGENT-3)

According to PubMed, Kaul and colleagues ([10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2024.0785](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2024.0785)) report EMERGENT-3, a phase 3, multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of xanomeline-trospium (KarXT, now Cobenfy) in 256 adults with schizophrenia experiencing acute psychosis. Xanomeline is a dual M1/M4-preferring muscarinic agonist with no direct D2 dopamine-receptor blocking activity; trospium is a peripherally-restricted muscarinic antagonist added to blunt peripheral side effects. At five weeks the drug reduced PANSS total score significantly more than placebo (least-squares mean difference −8.4; Cohen d 0.60), with extrapyramidal-symptom, weight, and somnolence measures similar to placebo — and these results replicate EMERGENT-1 and EMERGENT-2. The authors frame it as support for the first putative new antipsychotic class without D2 blockade.

For the receptor layer this is the clinical capstone of strand 2. Section 3B and PHARM-017 treat xanomeline as the live demonstration that psychosis — the MECH-088 failure mode — can be corrected from the *muscarinic* side without D2 antagonism, i.e. that the dopamine-selection arm of psychosis is reachable by a non-dopaminergic lever. Foster et al. (2016) supplied the preclinical mechanism (M4 → reduced striatal dopamine release → antipsychotic-like behaviour); EMERGENT-3 supplies the human efficacy endpoint. The benign extrapyramidal and metabolic profile is itself corroborating: those liabilities are signatures of D2 blockade, and their absence is consistent with the drug working through a different receptor system entirely. This is also why the layer holds xanomeline up as evidence that "the field is moving toward receptor-resolved mechanisms" — a real, approved, replicated example.

The caveat that keeps confidence at 0.72 is the one the trial design cannot escape: xanomeline is a *dual* M1/M4 agonist, and PANSS is an aggregate clinical endpoint, so the study confirms the *non-D2 route* works but cannot dissociate the M4 (dopamine-selection) contribution from the M1 (cortical-plasticity/cognition) contribution. The layer's sharper claim — that M4 carries the selection arm and M1 carries the plasticity arm, the two-plane interaction effect — is not adjudicated here. That dissociation rests on the preclinical M4 work (Foster 2016) and the M1/M4 mechanism reviews (McKinzie & Bymaster 2012), and remains an explicit "needed" anchor (M1-selective vs M4-selective dissection) in PHARM-017 itself.

I record this as *supports* for MECH-088 — psychosis correctable via the non-D2 muscarinic route — at confidence 0.72 with transfer risk low (this is the human indication) and mapping_fidelity the limiter (it grounds the route, not the within-molecule plane split).
