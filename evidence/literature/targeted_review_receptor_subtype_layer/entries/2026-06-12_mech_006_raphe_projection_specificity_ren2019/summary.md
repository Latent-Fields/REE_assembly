# Raphe projection specificity grounds the level-split premise (Ren et al. 2019, eLife)

According to PubMed, Ren and colleagues ([10.7554/eLife.49424](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.49424)) used plate-based single-cell RNA-sequencing to resolve eleven transcriptomically distinct serotonin-neuron clusters across the mouse dorsal and median raphe, mapped those clusters to principal-DR, caudal-DR, or MR by in-situ hybridisation, built intersectional viral-genetic tools to access subpopulations, and then performed whole-brain axonal projection mapping plus single-neuron reconstruction. The headline anatomical result for our purposes: DR serotonin neurons co-expressing vesicular glutamate transporter-3 preferentially innervate the cortex, whereas a separate subtype (co-expressing thyrotropin-releasing hormone) innervates subcortical regions, the hypothalamus in particular. Individual reconstructed neurons showed diverse, segregated projection patterns even within DR.

This matters to the receptor-subtype layer for one precise reason. The layer's Section 3A claims to dissolve the long-standing MECH-006 vs MECH-085 serotonin conflict by saying the two claims describe the *same* serotonergic motif applied at two hierarchical levels by two receptor populations on two raphe-to-target channels — 5-HT2A on dorsal-raphe-driven cortical L5 pyramidal cells (narrative-level exclusivity) and 5-HT1A on median-raphe-driven hippocampal cells (terrain basin depth). That reconciliation only escapes being an *ad hoc* save if the underlying anatomy actually supports two molecularly and topographically distinct serotonergic channels with different forebrain targets. Ren et al. supply exactly that precondition: the raphe is not a single homogeneous broadcast source but a set of distinct populations with segregated projection fields, including a cortically-biased DR subset.

What the paper does **not** do is equally important to state plainly, because receptor pharmacology is where REE has historically been "philosophy-right / mechanism-wrong." This is a study of the *presynaptic source* — which raphe neurons go where — not of the *postsynaptic receptor* (5-HT2A vs 5-HT1A) that the layer's reconciliation actually turns on. It says nothing about representational exclusivity or aversive-basin geometry. And the projection picture is graded, not dichotomous: the DR-cortex preference holds for the VGLUT3+ subset specifically, and both raphe nuclei innervate the hippocampus/septum to some degree. So the layer's clean "DR → cortical, MR → hippocampal" mapping is an idealisation of a messier real projection field.

I have therefore set evidence_direction to *supports* but capped confidence at 0.63, with mapping_fidelity (0.55) as the explicit limiter: the paper grounds the architectural *premise* that licenses a two-level serotonergic split, not the receptor assignment or the REE computation itself. It is the anatomical backbone of strand 1, and the entries on 5-HT2A-cortical (Weber & Andrade 2010) and 5-HT1A-limbic (Akimova 2009) carry the receptor-resolution half of the argument. Tagged to MECH-006 and MECH-085 jointly because its function here is precisely to support their *coexistence* at different levels rather than either one alone. The pharmacological predictions that depend on this (PHARM-015/016) remain out_of_domain for the V3 substrate; this literature grounds the design, it does not promote anything.
