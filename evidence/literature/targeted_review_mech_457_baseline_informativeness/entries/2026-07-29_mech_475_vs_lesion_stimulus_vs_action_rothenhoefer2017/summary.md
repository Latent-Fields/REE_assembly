# Rothenhoefer et al. 2017 — VS lesions dissociate stimulus- from action-value learning

According to PubMed. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0631-17.2017) · J Neurosci 37(29):6902–6914.

**What the paper did.** Six rhesus macaques, half with bilateral excitotoxic ventral striatum (VS) lesions, ran a two-arm bandit whose blocks randomly alternated between *stimulus-based* and *action-based* reinforcement learning. The authors fit a reinforcement-learning model (learning rate, choice consistency) and a Bayesian strategy-parser to separate what was learned from how consistently it was expressed. The design is unusually clean for our purposes because it holds the animal, the reward schedule, and the motor demands fixed while flipping *only* whether reward is assigned by the chosen image or the chosen action — which is precisely the axis MECH-475's grounding needed adjudicated.

**Key findings, relevant to MECH-475.** VS-lesioned monkeys had a deficit learning to select rewarding *images* but not rewarding *actions*. Within stimulus-based RL, the lesioned animals were *more influenced by negative feedback* and had *lower choice consistency* than controls, and the Bayesian parser showed they *defaulted to an action-based strategy*. The authors' conclusion is that the VS specifically maintains an estimate of *stimulus* value, not a general state value covering actions.

**How this maps to REE — and why it cuts both ways.** The claim is that an uninformative value critic makes optimisation iatrogenic: with the advantage carrying variance rather than signal, more gradient means more drift, so competence lands *below its own control* rather than plateauing. Rothenhoefer supplies the biological *shape* of a compromised value channel and it is the right shape — degraded choice consistency and an over-weighting of negative feedback, i.e. a noisy, poorly-baselined value estimate driving erratic choice, not a smooth approach to a ceiling. That is genuine support for "degradation, not plateau." But the same paper delivers a divergence the claim's own notes flagged and asked this lit-pull to resolve: the VS deficit was *specific to stimulus value and spared action-value learning*. REE's foraging DV is an action-selection problem over movements in a field. If one naively took "silence the critic and behaviour degrades" from a VS lesion, one would be citing the wrong locus — VS is the *stimulus*-value critic, and its removal does not touch action-value learning at all.

**Resolution direction (carried into the next two entries).** The honest reading is that this paper *redirects* rather than confirms: it says REE's DV must be treated as action-like, and the biological analog of REE's flat critic is therefore the *dorsal*-striatal action-value/RPE channel, not the VS. Samejima et al. 2005 and Stalnaker et al. 2012 (this dossier) supply that channel — action-specific value and action-specific RPE-like signals in dorsal/dorsomedial striatum — closing the gap this entry opens.

**Confidence.** 0.62. Source quality is high (primate excitotoxic lesion with model-based analysis). Mapping fidelity is deliberately held at 0.55 because the load-bearing result for REE is a *divergence* on locus that this paper cannot itself resolve; transfer risk is moderate (macaque interleaved bandit vs a continuous foraging environment). I have recorded it as `mixed` rather than `supports` on purpose — smoothing the stimulus/action distinction over would misrepresent what is genuinely the sharpest caveat in the whole dossier.
