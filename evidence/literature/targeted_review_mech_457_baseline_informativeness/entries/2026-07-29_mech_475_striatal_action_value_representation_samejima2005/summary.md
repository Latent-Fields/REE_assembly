# Samejima et al. 2005 — action-specific reward values are explicitly coded in the striatum

According to PubMed. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1115270) · Science 310(5752):1337–1340.

**What the paper did.** Monkeys chose between a left and a right handle turn whose reward probabilities were manipulated block-wise, so the animal had to track the *value of each action* separately. The authors recorded striatal projection neurons and regressed delay-period (pre-choice) activity onto action values estimated from a reinforcement-learning model, distinguishing neurons tuned to the value of a *specific* action from those tuned to *relative* value or to the *choice* itself.

**Key finding.** More than a third of striatal projection neurons were selective to the value of *one* of the two actions during the delay period; comparatively few coded relative value or the choice. The striatum, in other words, explicitly represents action-specific reward value — the very quantity an actor-critic system's critic must estimate to shape action selection.

**Why this is the pivot for MECH-475.** The claim's notes left one question open and asked this lit-pull to close it: is REE's foraging decision variable *stimulus-like* (resource-directed) or *action-like*? Rothenhoefer et al. 2017 (this dossier) showed a ventral-striatum lesion knocks out *stimulus*-value learning while leaving *action*-value learning intact — which, read alone, threatens to make "silence the critic and competence degrades" a locus error for REE, whose foraging DV is an action-selection problem. Samejima supplies the missing half: action-value learning survives the VS lesion *because it is carried by a different, dorsal/caudate-putamen striatal population that explicitly codes action value*. So the two papers together do not conflict — they partition the labour. REE's DV is action-like, and the biological analog of REE's flat, uninformative critic is the dorsal-striatal action-value channel, not the VS. That is exactly the redirection the claim needed, and it is now grounded rather than asserted.

**What it does not do.** This is correlational single-unit evidence: it establishes that an informative action-value critic *exists* and *where* it lives, not that flattening it *actively degrades* behaviour. The destructiveness half of MECH-475 — competence landing below its own control — is imported from the lesion result (compromised value channel → erratic, negative-feedback-driven choice, not a plateau) and from the computational entries (Fujimoto 2018: critic error propagates into a worse actor; Tucker 2018: the state-value baseline is the load-bearing variance reducer). Samejima is the anatomical anchor that makes those transfers legitimate.
