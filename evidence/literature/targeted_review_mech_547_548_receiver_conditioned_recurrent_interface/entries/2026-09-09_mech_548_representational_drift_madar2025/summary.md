# Madar et al. 2025 — endpoint representations continue to drift

Nature Neuroscience 28:848–860, DOI `10.1038/s41593-025-01894-6`, PMID `40113934`.

The study compares candidate synaptic-plasticity rules with measured hippocampal place-field dynamics and finds behavioural-timescale synaptic plasticity best explains ongoing trial-by-trial field shifting. Rare plasticity events continue to drive population-level representational drift during exploration, including familiarisation.

For MECH-548 the contribution is narrow but useful: an interface between adaptive systems cannot assume its endpoints occupy stationary coordinates. Stability must be measured relative to the receiver's **native changing dynamics**, not simply against yesterday's latent vectors.

The source does not show bridge-induced recursive contamination. Therefore a future REE assay must separate native drift from extra drift introduced by cumulative translated-state reuse; only the latter bears directly on MECH-548's off-manifold compounding mechanism.
