# Gonzalez et al. 2026 — interface plasticity versus stability

Nature 655:192–201, DOI `10.1038/s41586-026-10481-z`, PMID `42129569`.

The paper identifies low-dimensional communication subspaces across the hippocampal–retrosplenial axis and shows that those subspaces change with experience and brain state. Post-experience sleep reveals different plasticity/stability profiles for intrahippocampal and hippocampal-cortical interfaces.

That is good biological motivation for MECH-548's insistence that **static one-step usefulness is not enough**: the interface has to remain coherent as the coupled system evolves.

It is not direct evidence for MECH-548's registered mechanisms. Gonzalez et al. do not recursively feed a translated receiver state back into the bridge, do not test off-manifold compounding, and do not compare cumulative with clean-base/overlay application. The literature support is therefore intentionally scored mixed rather than promoted into mechanism confirmation.
