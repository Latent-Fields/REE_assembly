# Song et al. 2026 — causally relevant reinstatement and situational updating

Nature Communications 17:7362, DOI `10.1038/s41467-026-73914-3`, PMID `42270596`.

In a temporally scrambled narrative, representations of causally related past events were reinstated before participants reported new insight, and this reinstatement preceded abrupt updating of the current cortical situational representation.

For MECH-547 the useful feature is **relational selection**. The past state that matters is selected because of its causal relation to the current problem rather than because it is simply recent or globally similar. This fits the idea that a sender can expose different information depending on current context.

The study does not establish a receiver-conditioned latent bridge, does not isolate hippocampus as the translation locus, and does not distinguish `T(A,B)` from a sufficiently good sender-side retrieval operation. It therefore supports the weaker relational/context-sensitive access principle while leaving the registered discriminator intact.
