# Internally-generated search, a self-referenced frame, and a map that moves with the goal

**Vigano, Bayramova, Doeller & Bottini (2023), _Nature Communications_ 14:8132.**
Claim assessed: **ARC-031** -- HippocampalModule navigates z_self trajectory space in addition to
z_world action-object space O.

## What the paper did

Participants performed goal-directed *mental search* in a learned conceptual space -- looking for a
target concept internally, rather than moving through anything presented to them. Two results.
Parietal cortex evoked egocentric vector representations: where the goal concept lies relative to
the searcher, the conceptual analogue of "the goal is to my left." And the grid-like signal in
entorhinal, prefrontal and parietal cortex was modulated by goal proximity in conceptual space --
the map's expression changing with what was currently being sought.

## Why this is the closest approach in the pull to ARC-031's actual claim

The other three entries each supply one piece. Constantinescu gives the abstract space.
Tavares gives the self having a position in one. Schuck & Niv gives sequences through one. All
three, though, study spaces the agent moves through under external drive or reconstructs from
experience.

ARC-031's actual novelty is that the agent *generates and plans its own traversal* -- deliberation
as navigation, undertaken from the inside. Vigano et al. is the nearest thing to that: the act is
internally generated, the frame is self-referenced, and the map is not a static chart but something
that reconfigures according to the current cognitive goal. If HippocampalModule is going to serve
deliberation-planning rather than only world-planning, those are three properties it would need,
and this is evidence a biological navigator has them.

## The word that will cause the trouble

"Egocentric" is not "self."

Vigano et al. use the searcher's position as the *origin of a coordinate system* over semantic
concepts. ARC-031 requires the searcher's own epistemic condition -- effective dimensionality,
hypothesis-tag integrity, self-coherence -- to be the *content being navigated*. A self-referenced
frame on an external space and a space made of self-states are different objects that share a
morpheme, and I think this is the most seductive available error in the whole pull. Anyone reading
"egocentric representations during mental search" and hearing confirmation of z_self navigation has
made it.

## The thinner problem

Mental search for a target concept is a single-goal retrieval act. ARC-031's deliberation trajectory
is multi-step and structured -- "first resolve uncertainty in domain X, then attend to Y, then
commit" -- and specifies E3 evaluating the whole sequence by self-coherence, hypothesis-tag
integrity and self-maintenance cost. There is no sequence of goals here, and no cost function over
one. The gap between "find the concept" and "plan the deliberation" is most of what ARC-031 asserts.

## Standing

0.48, with source quality discounted to 0.72 because this is a single 2023 study in a fast-moving
area with no independent replication I could find, and because the grid-restructuring conclusion
rests on a correlational goal-proximity modulation rather than a demonstrated mechanism. And the
caveat that governs the whole pull: ARC-031 is phase-locked to v4, zero distinguishing substrate
exists in ree-v3, and HippocampalModule today takes z_self as a conditioning input rather than a
navigated space. Nothing here changes that; it only makes the v4 build look like a reasonable bet.
