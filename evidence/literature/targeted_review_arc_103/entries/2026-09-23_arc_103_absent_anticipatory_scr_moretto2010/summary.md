# Moretto et al. 2010 -- the restraining signal is bodily, and it arrives before the decision

This is the paper that turns the Koenigs finding from a result into a mechanism, which is why it earns its own entry rather than a citation inside one. An independent cohort of ventromedial prefrontal patients judged personal moral dilemmas while skin conductance was recorded. Two things came out. The patients endorsed personal moral violations more often than controls -- replicating the behavioural direction in a separate sample. And the patients failed to produce the anticipatory skin-conductance response that controls produced *before* endorsing such a violation. In healthy participants, the size of that anticipatory response ran negatively against how often they gave the utilitarian answer.

For ARC-103 the useful content is in the word *anticipatory*. ARC-103 commits to symbolic and analogical inference being subordinate to embodied harm sensing. A lazy reading of that commitment would implement it as an output filter: let the inference engine produce its conclusion, then check the conclusion against a harm classifier and block it if it fails. Moretto et al. describes something structurally different. The somatic signal in controls is generated in the run-up to the judgement, not applied to a finished one, and its magnitude predicts whether the utilitarian answer gets given at all. The harm channel is in the decision path, upstream of commitment, rather than bolted to its exit.

The second finding I want on the record is the one in `failure_signatures`: in the patients the harmful endorsement was made with *no anticipatory marker at all*. This is not a case of an objection being raised and overridden. There was nothing to override. That is an uncomfortable design lesson for any architecture that hopes to detect harm-authority failure by watching for suppressed objections -- the failure mode here is silent, and from inside the system the harmful conclusion presumably felt exactly like a well-reasoned one.

The caveats are real and I do not want them buried. Skin conductance is a blunt instrument: it indexes autonomic arousal, not harm specifically, and the control-group correlation is correlational even though the patient-control contrast is lesion-driven. REE has no autonomic nervous system, so what transfers is the functional shape of the signal -- anticipatory, non-inferential, positioned before commitment -- and not the physiology that implements it in humans. And as with Koenigs, nothing in this study isolates *symbolic* inference; the patients' retained capacity was general deliberation. ARC-103's specific worry about recombination and structure-mapping as rationalisation machinery is not tested here; it is tested, from the other end, by the Zeng et al. entry filed beside this one.

Confidence 0.8. Independent replication plus a mechanistic measure that speaks to exactly the two properties -- embodied, anticipatory -- that ARC-103's wording turns on. Held out of the top band by the non-specificity of SCR and by the fact that the physiological substrate does not itself transfer.
