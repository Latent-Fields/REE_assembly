# Chen, Wang & Li (2026) -- Counterfactual Quotient Models

**Verification note:** unreviewed arXiv preprint (2608.22092, submitted 22 Aug 2026, cs.LG). Verified against the arXiv abs page on 2026-09-03: title, three-author list, and abstract all matched the citation as given. No conference/workshop venue is listed on the abs page.

## What the paper did

Chen, Wang, and Li start from an observation any REE reader will find familiar: an action-conditioned forward model that is trained to predict the *entire* next state wastes representational capacity reconstructing whatever evolves independently of the agent's own choice, and in the worst case that shared, action-independent component can dominate the loss so completely that the action-dependent signal is fit to nothing at all. Their fix is a "Counterfactual Quotient Model" -- a canonical centered representation that algebraically cancels the common component shared across actions, leaving only the pairwise action-comparison content that actually matters for choosing between actions. Critically, they learn this representation directly from *synchronized* counterfactual rollouts -- multiple action branches from a shared starting state, with shared stochastic dynamics -- so the common-mode cancellation happens before function approximation rather than being left to a network to discover on its own. They prove several formal properties (decision sufficiency, identifiability, common-mode invariance) and demonstrate the practical payoff in physics-based toy environments: less capacity spent on action-independent variation, support for previously unseen reward queries, and better action ranking than a model trained to predict absolute futures.

## Findings relevant to SD-056 and ARC-002

This is, structurally, the SD-056 problem in someone else's coordinate system. SD-056 was registered precisely because REE's own E2 world-forward predictor exhibited this failure concretely: with world_dim=32 and action_dim=4, action contributes roughly 11% of predicted dimensionality, and reconstruction-shaped training collapsed to a state-dominated local minimum where the action's effect was fit to zero (cand_world_pairwise_dist = 0.0000 across eight candidate actions). SD-056's chosen remedy is an auxiliary InfoNCE-style contrastive loss over sibling CEM candidates that share a starting z_world_0 but differ in first action -- a different mechanism than this paper's quotient/centering construction, but the same underlying strategy: supervise on the *contrast* between action-conditioned outcomes rather than on the absolute predicted outcome, so the action-independent component cannot swamp the loss. Reading this paper alongside SD-056 is reassuring in the sense that matters for literature evidence -- an independent group, working in a different formalism and a different application domain, converged on the same diagnosis and the same general remedy class. For ARC-002 (E2 as forward predictor "of affordances," specifically its own weaker confirming criterion citing SD-056's cand_world_pairwise_dist / world_forward_contrastive_loss), the paper's action-ranking improvement is the same kind of evidence one step further removed: it shows that direct-effect learning can recover action-relevant discrimination that absolute-future prediction destroys.

## REE mapping and limitations

The mapping is at the level of strategy, not mechanism, and that distinction matters for how much weight to put on it. The paper's positive results lean on synchronized counterfactual rollouts -- paired branches sharing environment noise from a common start state -- which is a stronger, more privileged form of supervision than REE's production E2 training loop typically has access to outside of planning-time CEM candidate generation, where sibling candidates do share z_world_0 but are not guaranteed matched stochastic noise the way this paper's construction assumes. The evaluation domain is also narrow: controlled physics-based toy environments, not an embodied agent with REE's action space or reward structure. So this entry should be read as convergent theoretical support for the general strategy SD-056 committed to (contrastive/direct-effect learning over absolute-future reconstruction), not as evidence that REE's specific InfoNCE-style implementation will behave the way this paper's canonical-centered representation does.

## Confidence reasoning

Source quality is capped in the mid-0.5s: this is an unreviewed preprint, and while the formal treatment (decision sufficiency, identifiability, regret properties) is rigorous, the empirical validation is confined to physics-toy benchmarks. Mapping fidelity is comparatively high (0.6) because the diagnosed failure mode is essentially identical to SD-056's own root-cause finding, independently derived. Transfer risk is elevated (0.55) by the synchronized-rollout supervision assumption and the domain gap. The aggregate, 0.54, reflects genuine but indirect and preprint-grade support: useful as a converging signal that REE's SD-056 design direction is sound in principle, not as a substitute for REE's own experimental confirmation of the contrastive lever under V3-EXQ-NEW-1.
