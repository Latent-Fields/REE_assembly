# Radha & Goktas (2026) -- UWM-JEPA: Predictive World Models That Imagine in Belief Space

**Verification note:** unreviewed arXiv preprint (2605.25313, submitted 25 May 2026, cs.LG). Verified against the arXiv abs page on 2026-09-03: title, two-author list, and abstract matched. Comments line notes 14 pages, 6 figures, 7 tables, with code released; no conference/journal venue given.

## What the paper did

Radha and Goktas are working on a specific failure mode in Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) used as world models for partially observed environments: a vector-valued latent has no internal structure for carrying a *belief* over multiple compatible hidden continuations through a blind rollout, so as the rollout proceeds the model's representation of "what might still be true" can quietly collapse. Their proposed fix, UWM-JEPA, gives the latent a density-matrix structure over a joint system-environment space and predicts forward with a learned *unitary* operator, which by construction preserves the joint-state spectrum exactly -- the predictor itself cannot dissipate represented uncertainty, whatever else it gets wrong. The empirical test is a hidden-velocity indicator task requiring five-step forward simulation with the target masked: UWM-JEPA reaches 0.77 accuracy with graceful, monotonic degradation as actions are perturbed, while a parameter-matched LSTM-JEPA trained under an identical counterfactual-target objective collapses to majority-class performance regardless of the action taken. The cleanest part of the result, methodologically, is that both architectures tie on a held-out context probe -- so the difference is not that UWM-JEPA encodes the scene better, it is that its predictor's dynamics behave differently under rollout. The authors also note, almost in passing, that action sensitivity at all requires training against counterfactual rather than teacher-forced targets -- ordinary teacher forcing lets the action-conditioned signal collapse even in their own architecture.

## Findings relevant to MECH-510 and ARC-002

MECH-510 proposes that REE's control plane should carry a *generative* precision axis -- how strongly a predictive attractor constrains the space of interpretations and proposals it generates -- as a quantity separable from prediction-error precision (how much a discrepancy is trusted to drive correction, MECH-043) and from the signed harm/benefit valence split (MECH-054). The claim's own registration is explicit that REE already treats precision as heterogeneous, and that the genuinely new piece is a third, independently-perturbable axis. This paper's central ablation -- hold the encoder fixed, vary only the predictor's rollout dynamics, and watch belief-preservation diverge sharply -- is a demonstration, in a different vocabulary, that "how tightly a generative rollout constrains its own future possibilities" is dissociable from representational/encoding quality. That is functionally adjacent to what MECH-510 calls generative precision, even though the paper never uses that language and has no notion of an error-trust channel to contrast it against. For ARC-002, the finding that teacher-forced targets let action sensitivity collapse is the same failure family SD-056 diagnosed in REE's own E2, one more independent data point that the training-objective choice (counterfactual vs. absolute/teacher-forced) is what determines whether action-conditioned structure survives training.

## REE mapping and limitations

The honest caveat here is that MECH-510 has no built substrate to check this against: it is registered substrate_conditional, implementation_phase v4, with an explicit instruction not to build or queue a V3 experiment -- no generative-precision channel distinct from MECH-043's current_precision exists in ree_core today. So this entry is banked evidence for a mechanism that does not yet exist, not a test of one that does. The density-matrix/unitary-predictor construction itself carries no privileged status for REE -- nothing about REE's architecture calls for a quantum-inspired latent, and the transferable content is only the abstract finding (predictor dynamics, not encoder capacity, is what determines belief preservation), not the specific mechanism that produced it. The evaluation is also confined to one narrow synthetic hidden-velocity task with no analogue of REE's reward structure, action space, or embodiment.
