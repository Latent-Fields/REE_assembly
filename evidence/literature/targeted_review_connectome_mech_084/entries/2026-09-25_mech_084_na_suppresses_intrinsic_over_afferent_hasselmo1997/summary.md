# Hasselmo, Linster, Patil, Ma & Cekic (1997) -- Noradrenergic suppression of intrinsic over afferent synapses

**What they did.** In slices of rat piriform (olfactory) cortex they perfused norepinephrine and recorded synaptic potentials in two layers: layer Ia, where afferent fibres from the olfactory bulb arrive, and layer Ib, where pyramidal cells contact one another through intrinsic associative fibres. They then put the measured effect into a network model of piriform cortex to see what it would do to representations.

**What they found.** Norepinephrine suppressed intrinsic (Ib) synaptic transmission in a dose-dependent way and had a much weaker effect on afferent (Ia) transmission. In the model, this selective suppression lowered activity that depends on excitation spreading through the recurrent network, relative to activity in neurons that receive direct afferent input. The authors note that "noise" is the wrong word for that recurrent activity. It is better called interpretation or retrieval. On their reading, a pulse of LC-driven norepinephrine in an arousing context lets extrinsic input dominate the cortical representation.

**Mapping to MECH-084.** This is the most direct cellular analogue I found for the claim's central move: high NA reduces E2's constraint on E1. If the recurrent, associative contribution to a representation is what the system *expects*, and the afferent drive is what is *arriving*, then NA shifts the balance toward arrival. That matches the "attentional snap to unexpected input" in the claim notes, and it matches "too little NA -> confirmation bias" as the mirror case.

**What it does not show.** Three things limit it. First, carbachol does the same thing, with the same laminar selectivity and dose-response, and the two agonists do not add. So the mechanism does not separate NA from ACh, and MECH-084 leans on that separation (NA orients, ACh writes the trace). Second, bath perfusion is a tonic manipulation. MECH-084's falsifier is specifically about a *phasic* pulse against matched mean NA, and nothing here addresses temporal profile. Third, recurrent fibres inside olfactory cortex are a local circuit. They are not a feedback channel from one module to another, which is what the E2-to-E1 route is.

**Confidence.** Moderate (0.52). It supports the plausibility of the rebalancing mechanism, and it is weak on the NA-specific and phasic-specific parts of the claim.
