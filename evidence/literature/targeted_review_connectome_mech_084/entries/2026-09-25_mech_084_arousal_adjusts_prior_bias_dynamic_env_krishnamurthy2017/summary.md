# Krishnamurthy, Nassar, Sarode & Gold (2017) -- Arousal adjusts prior-driven perceptual bias in dynamic environments

**What they did.** Participants localised sounds in an environment where the source location was sometimes predictable and sometimes jumped to a new place. When the environment is stable, a prior about where the sound will come from is useful. After a change it is misleading. The authors measured how strongly each judgement was pulled toward the prior, fitted normative and idiosyncratic models of how subjects updated the prior, and recorded pupil diameter.

**What they found.** Subjects scaled the size of their prior-driven bias to how relevant and reliable the prior was at that moment. These adjustments showed up in the pupil: a larger stimulus-evoked pupil response went with a smaller bias. The authors conclude that the arousal system helps set how strongly perceptual priors weigh in, given what the subject infers about how the environment is changing.

**Mapping to MECH-084.** This is the paper whose framing sits closest to the claim, just in Bayesian language. MECH-084 says the NA signal sets the ratio between top-down constraint (E2) and bottom-up sampling (E1). Here the arousal response to a stimulus sets the ratio between prior and likelihood in what gets perceived. It also adds something the claim notes imply but do not state: the modulation is adaptive. Loosening the prior when predictability falls improves perception. Clinically that is useful, because it casts the "too little NA -> confirmation bias" and "too much -> snap to noise" failure modes as mis-set gain on an otherwise sensible control.

**What it does not show.** There was no pharmacological or causal NA manipulation. Pupil is a multi-transmitter readout. The DV is a report bias, not where or how the system samples, and there is no reorienting movement. The authors are candid that subjects also used idiosyncratic update rules, so the pupil-bias link is not a clean readout of an optimal NA gate.

**Confidence.** 0.55. The form is faithful and the mechanism behind it is indirect.
