# Maps are not only for places -- Park et al. 2020

MECH-079 has an unstated premise, and it is worth surfacing because without it
the claim is not false but confused. The claim says that phenomenological
continuous selfhood is an artefact of stable hippocampal map geometry. A self is
not a place. So the claim quietly needs it to be true that hippocampal map
machinery applies to abstract, self-referred content at all -- otherwise the
inference from topology to identity is a category error rather than a hypothesis.
This entry is here to check that premise, and it checks out.

Park, Miller, Nili, Ranganath and Boorman had participants learn two
two-dimensional social hierarchies, with each dimension taught separately, and
then asked for inferences about pairs of individuals who had never been directly
compared. Representations in hippocampus, entorhinal cortex and vmPFC/mOFC came
to reflect Euclidean distance within a reconstructed two-dimensional social space
-- a space that combined the separately-learned dimensions and that no
participant had ever directly experienced as a whole. The map supported the novel
inferences. So the map format generalises to abstract relational structure, and
it does so compositionally: dimensions learned apart are assembled into a joint
geometry over which inference runs.

For REE this licenses something the claim's chain needs. If anchor and residue
geometry are to be the analogue of a cognitive map, then the chain from topology
to rollouts to commitments has to be able to run over abstract dimensions rather
than spatial ones, and the biology says it can. That is a genuine contribution:
MECH-079 becomes a coherent hypothesis rather than a confusion.

But coherence is not truth, and I want to mark three limits sharply. The abstract
space here is social status, learned and inferred within an experimental session;
what the claim needs is a map of the *self*, persisting across a lifespan. The
temporal dimension -- which is the entire content of a continuity claim -- is
absent from the design. Second, the evidence is representational similarity in
BOLD signal. It is correlational, with no causal perturbation of the geometry, so
it cannot tell us whether the mapped structure supports the inference or merely
accompanies it; and MECH-079's own falsifier asks for exactly the causal
geometric perturbation this study does not perform.

The third limit is the one I find most instructive, because it is a cost rather
than a caveat. The more domains the hippocampus is shown to map -- space, status,
kinship, task structure, value -- the weaker the prediction 'and the self is
mapped too' becomes. A claim satisfied by almost any relational content is not
doing much work. So the useful question this entry raises for MECH-079 is not
whether the self is mapped but what would distinguish a self-map from any other
abstract map in the same substrate: what is specifically self-like about the
geometry, and what would we expect to see if the self were mapped *badly*.
The claim does not currently answer that, and until it does, evidence of this
kind will keep supporting it without ever being able to discriminate it.

Confidence 0.6: a strong source supplying a premise the claim needs, held down by
mapping fidelity because the premise concerns domain generality rather than
continuity.
