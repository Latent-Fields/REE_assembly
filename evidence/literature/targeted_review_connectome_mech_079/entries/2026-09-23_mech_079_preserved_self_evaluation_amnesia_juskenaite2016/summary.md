# Self-stability outlasting the hippocampus -- Juskenaite et al. 2016

If phenomenal self-continuity were an artefact of stable hippocampal map
geometry, then patients whose hippocampal retrieval machinery is destroyed ought
to present with a disturbance of self. The clinical picture is famously not that.
Juskenaite and colleagues assemble the observation that amnesic patients continue
to make accurate judgements about their own personality traits, and argue that
memory impairment alone does not prevent accurate trait self-knowledge. Their
positive proposal is that access to the self runs partly through an
'introspective computation' route rather than only through declarative retrieval.

The dissociation is in the same direction as the Klein and Nichols case in this
directory, but at a different level of description, and the pairing is what makes
it worth recording. There, the content was present and the ownership absent;
here, the map-dependent retrieval route is absent and the self-model is stable
anyway. Two different cuts, both landing on the conclusion that the stable self
is not simply what falls out of stable retrieval geometry.

For REE the practical consequence is about falsifier design rather than about the
claim's truth. MECH-079's own substrate note asks for a perturbation of
hippocampal topology or attractor geometry while preserving self-state inputs and
stored payloads, with persistence of context-conditioned choices as the dependent
variable. This literature says that a trait-level self-model can survive exactly
that kind of substrate loss. So a V3 experiment of the prescribed shape could
perfectly well find a stable self-model after geometric perturbation and have
established nothing at all about the claim -- because the stable thing it measured
was never the thing the geometry was supposed to be carrying. That is a
pre-registration hazard worth naming before anyone builds the probe.

I have set confidence at 0.5, well below the other two weakening entries, and I
want to be explicit that this is the weakest entry in the directory. Three things
pull it down and they all pull the same way. It is a review, drawing substantially
on its own group's patient series, so it contributes convergence and framing
rather than an independent measurement -- it should not be counted as a fourth
data point when this claim's literature confidence is aggregated. The preserved
measure is semantic trait self-knowledge, which is not phenomenal continuity, and
which is arguably the very operational proxy the R7 disposition accuses the claim
of conflating with phenomenal continuity; on the strict reading of MECH-079 this
entry is close to orthogonal. And 'introspective computation' is a hypothesis the
review advances, not a result it establishes, so the positive half of the proposal
cannot be borrowed as evidence for any particular REE mechanism.

What survives all that is modest and worth having: a second, independent clinical
route to the conclusion that self-stability and hippocampal map integrity come
apart, and a concrete warning about how the claim's prescribed falsifier could
return a null that means nothing.
