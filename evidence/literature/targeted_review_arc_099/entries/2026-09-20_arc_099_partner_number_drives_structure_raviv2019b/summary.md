# Compositional structure can emerge without generational transmission (Raviv, Meyer & Lev-Ari, Cognition, 2019)

## Why this is the most important entry in the directory

The chip that commissioned this pull asked three questions. This paper answers two of them more directly than anything else found, and it was not cited in the Galke et al. 2022 workshop paper that raised them.

## What the paper did

Two studies. Study 1 put 24 adults into six closed micro-societies of four people each and had them invent a language over a communication game -- alternating dyads, typed nonsense labels, visual scenes varying in shape and motion, and a meaning space that grew as the game went on. No new learners, no generations: the same four people for the whole experiment.

Study 2 is where the argument is made. The two candidate pressures in Study 1 -- talking to multiple people, and referring to an expanding set of things -- are confounded in any single experiment. So the authors pooled 24 groups (the six from Study 1 plus eighteen more run on an extended version of the same paradigm, six of four members and twelve of eight) and modelled structure score against both predictors at once.

## What it found

Study 1: structure increased significantly and linearly over rounds (β = 1.19, SE = 0.1, t = 4.4, p < 0.01), with some groups reaching meaning/string correlations as high as 0.6. Compositional languages emerged inside one generation. That contradicts Kirby, Tamariz, Cornish and Smith (2015), who had argued compositional structure emerges only when languages are transmitted across multiple generations.

Study 2 is the result to remember. **Number of partners had a strong effect on structure (β = 0.04, SE = 0.006, t = 6.37, p < 0.001). Number of scenes did not (β = 0.0001, SE = 0.002, t = 0.04, p = 0.96).** Model comparison agrees: adding the meaning space to a partners-only model bought nothing (ΔAIC = 2, p = 0.96), while adding partners to a scenes-only model bought a great deal (ΔAIC = 26, p < 0.0001). In the authors' words, interacting with multiple people "was the main driver for the emergence of compositionality in our design."

## What this does to ARC-099

**It promotes partner variation from plausible to best-evidenced.** ARC-099 lists fourteen conditions asserted to be jointly necessary, and until now the evidence for any individual item was indirect. In the 2019 Proc B study partner variation was a paradigm constant, held the same across conditions, so that study could not license it. Here it is the predictor, it is competed against the obvious rival, and it wins decisively. If the V6-entry gate has to be defended item by item, this is the item with a number attached to it.

**It also tells the gate which nearby lever is inert.** The expanding meaning space is exactly what a designer reaching for "coordination pressure" would build: raise the referential load until holistic naming becomes untenable. This experiment put that lever in the same model as partner number and found it added nothing unique. That does not mean referential load is irrelevant -- it may be necessary background rather than a driver, and Study 1 had it switched on throughout -- but it does mean a V6 ecology that grows its referent set while keeping a fixed interlocutor is pulling the wrong lever.

**And it partly settles the bounded-memory question.** The Galke et al. 2022 entry in this directory reported that bounded memory is load-bearing in humans and absent from ARC-099's inventory, and noted that it *inverts* the polarity of the existing 'episodic memory' item rather than duplicating it. Reading the primary literature makes the picture more usable than that. Raviv et al. do locate the mechanism in memory -- "compressibility pressures emerge due to participants' limited memory capacity: it is simply too hard to memorize many unique and unrelated labels in a relatively short time" -- but they immediately add that "such memory limitations can stem from different sources": a learner-side bottleneck in transmission, or the communicative need to converge with many partners. And the experiment shows the second source suffices without the first.

That is a better shape for a machine-checkable contract than a raw 'bounded memory' flag. What has to be true of a V6 ecology is that an agent cannot individuate its way out of the problem -- that the number of distinct label variants it must handle exceeds what it can simply store. That is a property of the ecology-and-agent pair, it is measurable, and it can be delivered by at least two different configurations. An inventory item reading 'bounded memory' would be under-determined in precisely the way this pull found 'partner variation' to be under-determined: satisfiable on paper by a configuration that produces no pressure at all.

## Reading this alongside the 2019 Proc B entry

The two results are complementary rather than redundant, and they should be read together. Proc B held partner variation constant and varied how many partners there were, finding that input variability -- not shared history -- mediated the outcome. This paper varied partner number directly across 24 groups and found it beats the meaning space. Together: the causal chain runs number of distinct partners -> input variability -> pressure to generalise -> compositional structure. Each link has a measurement behind it. That chain, not the fourteen-item list, is what a V6-entry gate should be testing for.

## Limitations

Study 2 is a meta-analysis, not a designed manipulation. The 24 groups were run under two protocol lengths and at two group sizes, so number of partners is entangled with group size and with duration; the authors present it as a model comparison and do not claim an experimental dissociation, and neither should ARC-099. The partner counts involved are small integers -- three or seven partners per participant -- which says nothing about ecology scale. And the usual boundary applies: these are adults with intact language faculties, so the study is evidence about which ecology conditions elicit structure, and silent on whether ARC-099's substrate inventory is complete.
