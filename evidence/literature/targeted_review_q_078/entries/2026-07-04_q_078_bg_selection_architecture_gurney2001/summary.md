# Gurney, Prescott & Redgrave 2001 — BG action selection as a dedicated architecture

According to PubMed. Source: Gurney K, Prescott TJ, Redgrave P. "A computational model of action selection in the basal ganglia. I. A new functional anatomy." *Biological Cybernetics* 84(6):401-10, 2001. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00007984) · PMID 11417052.

## What the paper did

This is the foundational statement of the "basal ganglia as a central selection device" programme. The authors start from the computational premise that action selection is the primary role of the BG, and make a move that is exactly the one Q-078 is circling: they recast action selection as **signal selection** — each candidate action is summarised by a scalar *salience*, and the problem becomes "let the highest-salience channel through while suppressing the rest." They then ask what neural network *architecture* performs this computation cleanly, and derive a **feedforward off-centre on-surround** selection circuit. Crucially, they decompose the BG into two functionally distinct sub-systems: a **selection pathway** that performs selection per se, and a separate **control pathway** whose job is to regulate the selection pathway so that it keeps working (keeping the off-surround properly scaled, preventing runaway or no-selection regimes), synergistically with dopaminergic modulation. This selection/control decomposition is offered explicitly as an *alternative* to the then-prevailing direct/indirect-pathway story.

## Why it speaks to Q-078

Q-078 asks whether REE's committed-action conversion ceiling is **constitutional** — the rules by which candidates become *eligible to win* (eligibility mask, Go/No-Go pressures, conflict-sensitive hold, threshold/widening, recurrent competition, commit-permission, post-commit latch) are under-specified — rather than **parametric** (upstream signals merely too weak). Gurney 2001 is the reference case for the constitutional pole. Its whole point is that clean selection does not fall out of raw salience magnitude; it requires a **purpose-built architecture** — competitive off-centre on-surround dynamics *plus* a regulatory control pathway. A strong salience presented to a system lacking that architecture does not win; it is merely present. That is precisely the phenomenology Q-078 documents: across five mechanistically distinct channels (CRF, OFC, SD-037, dACC, modulatory), a *formed and measurable* signal reaches E3 authority yet committed action does not change reliably — "signal strength can exist without lawful access to committed action." The paper says, in effect, that lawful access *is* the selection+control architecture, and it is dissociable from signal strength.

## The mapping and its limits

The honest translation is architecture-level analogy, not a measured correspondence. Gurney 2001 models rate-coded motor/behavioural selection in a vertebrate BG; it says nothing about REE's E3 pathway, F-variance-share, or the shortlist levers of MECH-439. What it *does* establish is that the thing Q-078 hypothesises — a dedicated selection *constitution* that is necessary beyond upstream signal strength — is biologically real and functionally load-bearing in the canonical reference system. So it raises the prior on the constitutional reading without confirming it for REE. The decisive test is still the paired experiment Q-078's `next_action` calls for: hold a channel's signal strength fixed and vary whether a BG-like selection/control layer is present, and see whether conversion moves. This paper predicts it should.
