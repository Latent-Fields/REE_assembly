# Jeay-Bizot et al. 2026 -- No evidence that respiratory phase modulates the readiness potential

**Claim tested:** MECH-109. Direction: **mixed**. Confidence 0.50.

This is a critical re-examination of Park et al. (2020). Using Park's original data, a new independent EEG dataset and simulated data built with no coupling at all, Jeay-Bizot and colleagues show that the reported link between respiratory phase and readiness-potential amplitude is produced by the analysis. Most presses fall in expiration, and when EEG is sorted by concurrent respiratory phase within epochs time-locked to movement, the expiration bins are the samples closest to the button press, where the RP is at its peak. Their simulations reproduce the "effect" with no coupling present. Once trials are grouped by the respiratory phase at the moment of movement, the effect disappears, and Bayesian analyses favour its absence.

The behavioural finding survives. They replicate that people press more often during expiration (13 of 17 participants had a mean press phase in expiration). So the observation that breath frames *when* an endogenous action is released holds up; what fails is the neural story of how breathing reaches into motor preparation.

For MECH-109 this cuts two ways, which is why I have marked it mixed and not weakens. The claim never rested on the RP specifically, and the timing phenomenon it needs is intact. But the authors offer an alternative that should be taken seriously: preparing to act may itself reshape breathing (a longer I:E ratio, or a respiratory reset at trial onset). If so, the action is clocking the breath, which is the opposite of MECH-109's picture of breath as a handle that drives the planning clock. Only an intervention can separate the two readings: impose the breathing pattern and see whether action or plan timing follows.

Honest bottom line: this paper doesn't falsify MECH-109, but it removes the most-cited piece of mechanistic support from the literature neighbourhood and names a plausible reverse-causal account. It is a good paper to keep beside Park 2020 so that the latter is never read on its own.
