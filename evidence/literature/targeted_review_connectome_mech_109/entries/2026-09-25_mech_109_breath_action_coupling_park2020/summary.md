# Park et al. 2020 -- Breathing is coupled with voluntary action and the readiness potential

**Claim tested:** MECH-109 (voluntary respiratory modulation as the top-down handle on the E3 plan-sweep clock). Direction: **supports** (partial, low fidelity). Confidence 0.42.

Park and colleagues recorded EEG and respiration while people pressed a button whenever they felt like it (Libet-style), compared with pressing on an external cue. Self-initiated presses were not spread evenly across the breath: they clustered in expiration. The clustering vanished when the press was externally triggered. They also reported that the readiness potential's amplitude depended on respiratory phase. A follow-up (Park et al. 2022, NeuroImage, PMID 36252914) found the same onset coupling for purely *imagined* motor and visual actions, so the coupling reaches covert, plan-like mental acts and not just muscles.

Why does this matter for MECH-109? The claim needs the respiratory cycle to act as a clock on *endogenous* planning transitions, and this is the cleanest human evidence that breathing frames the timing of agent-initiated decisions while leaving stimulus-driven ones alone. That dissociation between endogenous and exogenous timing is what the MECH-108/109 architecture predicts, and it is not trivially explained by arousal.

But look at what is *not* here. Nobody in this study controlled their breathing; the finding is that spontaneous breath phase biases action timing. MECH-109's distinctive assertion is the reverse handle, that the agent can deliberately sigh, slow or hold the breath and thereby move the planning clock, and this paper cannot speak to that. The causal arrow is also open: an agent preparing to act may lengthen or time its expiration, so the action could be clocking the breath rather than the other way round. Jeay-Bizot et al. (2026) raise exactly this possibility, and they showed that the RP-by-phase modulation was a confound of epoching. The event measured is commitment onset, not abandonment of a plan.

So I read this as the empirical floor under MECH-108 (breath as a clock for endogenous action timing), with only indirect weight on MECH-109. Confidence is held at 0.42. The source is strong and replicated, but the mapping to the voluntary-handle claim is thin. What would move it: the same Libet task run with instructed slow breathing, breath-hold or a single instructed sigh, looking at whether onset distributions and the rate of abandoned or withheld actions shift. That is precisely the CONFIRMING design in the claim's `what_would_answer`.
