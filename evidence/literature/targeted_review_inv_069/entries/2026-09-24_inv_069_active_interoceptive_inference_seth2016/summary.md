# Active interoceptive inference and the emotional brain -- Seth & Friston (2016)

This is the review that made "interoceptive inference" a standard piece of vocabulary in predictive-processing neuroscience. Seth and Friston's central move is to re-describe interoception -- the sensing of internal bodily states -- not as a passive readout channel but as the target of the same hierarchical, precision-weighted inference that predictive-processing accounts already give to exteroceptive perception. Autonomic reflexes, on this account, are "enslaved" by descending predictions from deep generative models of the body's internal milieu; classical homeostasis (react after the fact) is subsumed into allostasis (regulate predictively, ahead of need). The paper then traces the anatomical implications (agranular limbic cortex as the likely seat of these predictions), the role of precision and neuromodulation in translating interoceptive uncertainty into affect, and the explanatory reach of the framework across autism, depression and consciousness science.

The relevance to INV-069 is at the level of structure rather than mechanism, but it is a clean structural match. INV-069's claim is that the self is a maintained process, not a stored object; Seth and Friston's account of embodied selfhood is exactly this, specialised to the interoceptive/affective domain -- the sense of an embodied self is constituted by an ongoing predictive-regulatory loop (allostasis), not by a static bodily self-image. That is the same "process, not thing" shape that REE's hippocampal module and E3 selector are meant to instantiate for z_self more broadly. More specifically useful for this session's brief: the paper's account of precision and neuromodulation is the clearest available statement of why a self-relevant prediction only does affective/motivational work once something downstream treats its precision as a value signal. That bears directly on the context flagged for this review -- z_self currently has zero reach into E3 selection in the V3 agent (GFLAG-0481). On Seth and Friston's own logic, a self-model computed but never consumed by a valuation process is not yet playing the role their account assigns to interoceptive selfhood; it is present as a representation without being functionally "embodied" in the sense their title claims.

The caveats are the same ones that recur across this pull's active-inference entries. This is a review of a biological research programme -- autonomic reflexes, agranular cortex, neuromodulatory precision -- not a proposal or test of any specific artificial self-latent, and it offers no operational analogue of per_stream_vs['z_self'], no perturbation-recovery design, and no discussion of what should happen when the maintaining machinery is architecturally present but untrained.
