# Consciousness in active inference: Deep self-models, other minds, and the challenge of psychedelic-induced ego-dissolution -- Deane (2021)

Deane's project is to show that predictive-processing accounts of the brain can support a genuine theory of consciousness, not just a computational theory of perception. His route in is "subjective valuation": a deep inference, computed by a hierarchy of self-models, about how precise or predictable the self-evidencing (fitness-promoting) consequences of one's own action are. On this account, phenomenal experience just is this valuation signal, computed over a "deep self-model" that attenuates incoming sensory precision so that self-generated predictions can dominate. The paper's second half takes on an objection that has become a live debate in consciousness science: if selfhood is constitutive of consciousness, what do we make of psychedelic-induced "ego-dissolution," a state some subjects describe as totally selfless? Deane's answer, again from within active inference, is that these states are best explained as a collapse of precision-weighting on the deep self-model (plausibly via 5-HT2A receptor action), not as a genuine absence of self-evidencing -- and, crucially for present purposes, as a state from which ordinary bounded selfhood is recoverable once precision is restored.

Two pieces of this map onto INV-069 with unusual directness. First, the "subjective valuation" mechanism is exactly the missing half of INV-069's behavioural leg: a self-model only does the work INV-069 asks of it if something downstream treats its output as a valuation input, not merely a passive coherence label. This is worth flagging against the specific gap named in this session's brief: z_self currently has zero reach into E3 selection in the V3 agent (GFLAG-0481). Deane's framework predicts, in its own vocabulary, that a self-model without a route into valuation of self-evidencing outcomes is not doing the job his account (and REE's WWA) assigns it -- it would be present as a representation but functionally inert, which is close to a philosophical restatement of why DR-10's current inertness (no internal self_viability producer) and GatedPolicy's rank-invariant z_self bias leave INV-069's behavioural leg untestable in V3 as it stands. Second, ego-dissolution-and-recovery is the clearest literature analogue available for INV-069's arm (a): a transient collapse of the self-model's operation followed by a return to baseline selfhood, which is structurally the same shape as "perturb the recurrent hidden state for one tick, then watch coherence and candidate-ranking re-converge."

The mapping should not be pushed further than that shape-level correspondence. Deane writes about precision-weighting across a whole predictive hierarchy in a biological brain; he offers no analogue of per_stream_vs['z_self'], no quantitative recovery criterion, and therefore nothing that would tell REE whether a given observed recovery is genuine (a trained maintaining process reasserting itself) or an architectural artefact (the exact ambiguity the 2026-09-24 autopsy resolved against V3-EXQ-1078 by showing the recurrence was untrained and any sufficiently contractive dynamical system would have "recovered" regardless). Nor does the paper distinguish a live-process-with-value-frozen condition from a precision-collapsed condition -- his ego-dissolution case sits squarely in arm (a) territory and gives no traction on arm (b) (the frozen-maintenance DV problem the autopsy left open).

Confidence is set at 0.66. The paper earns credit for reaching, independently and from a different literature, both of the two structural features INV-069's WWA amendment now insists on -- a valuation-consuming self-model and a recoverable-after-perturbation process -- which is a nontrivial degree of convergent support. It is capped short of "strong" because it is a theoretical synthesis rather than a study with a method REE could borrow directly, its evidence base (human psychedelic phenomenology) transfers to an artificial recurrent cell only at the level of concept rather than mechanism, and it has nothing to say about the generic-contraction null or the frozen-maintenance DV that the current V3 evidence actually turns on.
