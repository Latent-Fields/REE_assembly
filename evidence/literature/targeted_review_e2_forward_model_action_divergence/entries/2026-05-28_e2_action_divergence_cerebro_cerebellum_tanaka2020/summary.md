# Cerebro-Cerebellum as Forward Model (Tanaka et al. 2020)

According to PubMed, [Tanaka, Ishikawa, Lee & Kakei (2020), *The Cerebro-Cerebellum as a Locus of Forward Model: A Review*, Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2020.00019).

**What the paper does.** This is a synthesis review arguing that the cerebro-cerebellum -- the phylogenetically newer expansion of the cerebellum -- functions as an internal forward model. Three lines of evidence are reviewed in detail. First, physiological: dentate-nucleus output activities are predictive for the cerebellar inputs (mossy-fibre activity), consistent with the dentate carrying the model's forward prediction. Second, behavioural: in healthy subjects, kinematic components of movement are predictive for target motion, while in patients with cerebellar ataxia the same components lag behind the target -- the predictive function has been knocked out. Third, morphological: the cerebellar cortex and the dentate nucleus receive separate mossy-fibre projections, which is the connectivity prerequisite for optimal-estimation-style forward modelling. The authors propose the predictive computation as the unifying algorithmic principle across motor and cognitive cerebellar function.

**Why it matters for REE.** REE's architectural spec already identifies E2 with a cerebellar-style fast forward model (see [ree-v3-spec.md](https://github.com/Latent-Fields/ree-v3/blob/main/docs/ree-v3-spec.md) and the SD-001 / SD-002 / SD-004 design docs). The biological reference is built around action-distinguishability: different motor commands produce different predicted sensory consequences, and that's the operating regime that motor control and counterfactual rollout depend on. The cerebellar ataxia case -- predictive function lost, kinematics revert from leading to lagging -- is precisely the failure mode V3-EXQ-571 documents in REE-v3: when E2 collapses K candidates to identical predicted z_world, planning loses its predictive content and the downstream substrate is forced into reactive control.

The biological prior here is unambiguous on the architectural commitment: a forward model that does not preserve per-action divergence at one-step horizons is not a forward model in the cerebellar sense. The V3-EXQ-571 collapse is a deviation from the reference architecture, not an acceptable feature. This is the strongest single piece of evidence for option (ii) -- the deeper fix that repairs E2 itself rather than working around it -- because option (i)'s one-hot bypass route, while functional, is exactly the kind of architectural workaround that the cerebellar reference does not need.

**Mapping caveat.** The review operates at the algorithmic-principle level. It is firm on "forward models preserve action identity" but agnostic on *how* the cerebellum mechanically achieves this at the synaptic level -- the granule-cell layer's combinatorial expansion, the deep-cerebellar-nucleus collateral pathway, and the Purkinje-cell inhibitory loop all play roles whose individual contributions to per-action divergence preservation are not isolated. So the biological prior tells us *that* per-action divergence has to be in the architecture without prescribing the exact synaptic-or-loss-function equivalent on the REE side. It supports option (ii) as the architecturally faithful target without picking between PLSM, contrastive RSSM, SWIRL-style MI maximisation, or a structural change to E2's action fan-in.

**Confidence.** 0.74. Source quality is high (Frontiers Systems Neuroscience, mainstream review). Mapping fidelity is strong because REE explicitly names the cerebellar correspondence. The residual transfer-risk concern is that the biological constraint is qualitative ("action identity is preserved") rather than quantitative ("by how much, at what horizon"), so it gives us a *direction* without giving us a *target*.

**Lit-only verdict, not governance.** Strongly supports option (ii) on architectural-faithfulness grounds. If REE is going to be a cerebellar-style substrate, then the E2 forward model has to actually preserve action identity in its predictions; a permanent first-action one-hot bypass on every downstream consumer (option i) would correctly compute the right answer but would commit REE to an architecture that the biological reference does not need.
