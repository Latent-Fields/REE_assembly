# Olafsdottir et al. 2015 -- Reward-related preplay of unexplored space

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.06063), eLife 2015), Olafsdottir, Barry, Saleem, Hassabis and Spiers recorded hippocampal place cells while rats *watched* food being delivered to a part of an environment they had never physically entered. During subsequent offline rest, place-cell sequences corresponding to that unvisited, rewarded region were spontaneously pre-activated ("preplay"). A matched region that was equally novel but **unrewarded** produced no such preplay. The motivational relevance of the space, not mere exposure, determined which sequences the hippocampus constructed offline.

This speaks to MECH-443 from a different angle than Mattar & Daw. Where the normative theory derives that replay *should* be prioritized, this is direct electrophysiological evidence that it *is* -- and that the prioritizing variable is motivational relevance. The rewarded/unrewarded contrast is the experimental embodiment of "the replay write channel is not opened uniformly": only the relevant content is selected. That is precisely the graded, content-selective behaviour MECH-443 wants to add on top of MECH-319's binary mode-gate.

The reason I tagged MECH-444 here as well is the part of the result that is easy to overlook: the constructed sequences covered space the animal had **never traversed**. Replay was not a re-emission of a stored trajectory; it was *generative* -- the hippocampus assembled a novel sequence consistent with the topology and the reward. This is the biological shadow of the reanalyze idea behind MECH-444: the system does not merely re-write the stale stored target, it constructs a fresh target appropriate to current goals. The mapping is looser here -- reanalyze recomputes a value/policy target with the current *model*, whereas preplay constructs a spatial sequence -- but the shared principle (offline replay produces fresh, goal-current content rather than verbatim playback) is the relevant one.

I set confidence at 0.63 (supports), below Mattar & Daw, for two honest reasons. First, this is **preplay** of unexplored space; it demonstrates motivational gating of replay *content* but does not record a downstream write to any rule or value representation, so the write-update step that MECH-443/444 actually gate is inferred from the content bias rather than measured. Second, reward here is a binary present/absent manipulation, so the paper supports prioritization-by-relevance without constraining the *shape* of the priority function. It is good corroboration of the existence and direction of the effect, not of its functional form at the write locus.
