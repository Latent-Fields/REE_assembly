# Martínez-Rivera et al. 2018 — PL→BLA→VS routing of avoidance (SD-059 substrate scaffold)

*According to PubMed.* Martínez-Rivera FJ, Bravo-Rivera C, Velázquez-Díaz CD, Montesinos-Cartagena M, Quirk GJ. "Prefrontal circuits signaling active avoidance retrieval and extinction." *Psychopharmacology (Berl)* 236(1):399–406, 2018. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00213-018-5012-7).

This paper supplies the anatomy that SD-059's "LA/BA→NAcc relief/safety action-credit" half is gesturing at. The authors infused retrograde tracers into basolateral amygdala (BLA) and ventral striatum (VS), trained rats on platform-mediated active avoidance, and then used c-Fos to ask which prefrontal projection populations are active during avoidance *retrieval* (2 unreinforced tones) versus *extinction* (15 unreinforced tones).

The dissociation is the useful part. Avoidance retrieval activated prelimbic (PL) neurons projecting to BLA, and to a lesser extent to VS, but did *not* activate infralimbic (IL) neurons. Extinction, by contrast, activated IL neurons projecting to both BLA and VS, plus PL→VS. The authors suggest PL drives VS indirectly via BLA during avoidance, and that IL→VS converges to inhibit avoidance during extinction.

For REE this is substrate-plausibility evidence for the credit pathway SD-059 says is currently unwired. The amygdala→ventral-striatal route carrying avoidance signals is the biological correlate of MECH-358's per-action-class credit table: a directed action under threat that earns relief/safety should leave a trace along a BLA→VS-like projection that biases future selection. MECH-358's EMA table is the functional abstraction of that routing.

There are two honest caveats, and the second is sharp. First, c-Fos plus tracers tell us which projections are *active* during retrieval and extinction, not that they carry a quantitative relief/safety credit — so this is plausibility, not mechanism identity, and the REE side is an arithmetic regulator rather than a synaptic credit assignment. Second, the retrieval-versus-extinction dissociation is a direct warning against a tempting mis-mapping: it would be easy to assume IL (the suppression node from Moscarello & LeDoux 2013) is also the avoidance-credit node, but here IL is selectively the *extinction* node and avoidance retrieval runs through PL→BLA. SD-059 should therefore keep its suppression half (MECH-357, ilPFC) and its credit half (MECH-358, PL/BLA→VS) anatomically distinct rather than collapsing them onto one region. Confidence 0.66 — good anatomy, correlational activation, moderate transfer risk.
