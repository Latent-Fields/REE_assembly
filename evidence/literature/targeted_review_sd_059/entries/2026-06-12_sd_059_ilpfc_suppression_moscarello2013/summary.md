# Moscarello & LeDoux 2013 — ilPFC suppression of amygdala defence (SD-059 anchor)

*According to PubMed.* Moscarello JM, LeDoux JE. "Active avoidance learning requires prefrontal suppression of amygdala-mediated defensive reactions." *Journal of Neuroscience* 33(9):3815–23, 2013. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2596-12.2013).

This is the paper SD-059's title leans on, so it is worth being precise about what it does and does not show. Moscarello and LeDoux trained rats on signaled active avoidance — a paradigm that pits an instrumental action (move to safety on the cue) against a Pavlovian reaction (freeze to the cue). Their hypothesis was that the instrumental component recruits infralimbic prefrontal cortex (ilPFC) to inhibit central-amygdala (CeA)-mediated freezing. The manipulations are causal, not merely correlative: pre-training ilPFC lesions raised freezing and lowered avoidance; CeA lesions did the opposite; ilPFC inactivation impaired both acquisition and expression; and protein-synthesis inhibition in ilPFC versus CeA produced opposing effects, implying two competing memory traces. They conclude ilPFC is an inhibitory interface that lets instrumental control attenuate freezing, and that the suppression generalises across contexts.

For REE this maps cleanly onto the half we *already* own. The ilPFC→CeA inhibitory interface is MECH-357/SD-058 — the avoidance-efficacy gate that releases the freeze brake. The whole motivation for SD-059 is that this is only half of active avoidance: a global "avoidance works" scalar lifts the no-op/freeze penalty but, by design, "does not compute the escape direction." Read that way, this paper is support for SD-059's *premise* rather than for its added content. It demonstrates that the suppression interface is real and causally necessary; it does not demonstrate any positively-reinforced, action-specific escape response. That absence is exactly the gap SD-059 fills with an affordance-indexed (per-first-action-class) relief/safety credit table.

So I have marked this `supports` for SD-059, but the honest caveat sits in the mapping: the evidence is for the suppression half (MECH-357), not for the credit table that is the novel part of SD-059/MECH-358. The failure signature that V3-EXQ-603h surfaced — agent un-freezes but acquires no directed escape, worst survival — is the behavioural echo of a system that has Moscarello & LeDoux's suppression interface and nothing downstream of it. One should resist the temptation to cite this paper as evidence *for* the credit mechanism; it is evidence for why suppression alone is not enough.
