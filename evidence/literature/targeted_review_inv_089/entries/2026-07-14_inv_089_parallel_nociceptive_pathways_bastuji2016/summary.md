# Parallel nociceptive pathways reach cortex separately from the exteroceptive stream (Bastuji et al. 2016)

According to PubMed, Bastuji, Frot, Perchet, Magnin & Garcia-Larrea (*Human Brain Mapping*, 2016; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23310)) recorded intracranial EEG from 16 brain regions in epilepsy patients while delivering nociceptive-specific laser pulses, and tracked the first second of cortical processing. Their central anatomical finding is the one INV-089's notes lean on: "Nociceptive inputs reach simultaneously sensory and limbic networks, probably through parallel spino-thalamic and spino-parabrachial pathways." Operculo-insular, mid-cingulate and amygdalar activity appears as an early, pre-conscious wave, ahead of and separable from the antero-insular/prefrontal/posterior-parietal fronto-parietal network that tracks conscious access.

Why this matters for INV-089. The claim's architectural premise, inherited from SD-010, is that harm is not a downstream read-off of the world representation but is routed through its own encoder — `HarmEncoder(harm_obs) -> z_harm` — instantiated *outside* the `z_world` encoder and exempt from reafference correction. This paper is the biological warrant for that separateness: in the human brain the nociceptive channel is anatomically parallel to the exteroceptive one, arriving in a distinct cortical territory (operculo-insular / mid-cingulate) rather than being computed out of the sensory-cortical exteroceptive stream. The `EXQ-027b` over-correction paradox — the reafference predictor subtracting hazard when harm was fused into `z_world` — is exactly the failure a separate stream is meant to prevent, and the biology says the separation is real.

The mapping caveat is the honest limit. This is a study of the *mature adult* cortical dynamics of pain, resolved at the scale of hundreds of milliseconds. It grounds the *separateness* premise cleanly, but INV-089's load-bearing content is not separateness per se — it is the *developmental* claim that `z_harm` differentiates on a trajectory distinct from `z_world`'s, and that harm-evaluator quality is bounded by that differentiation. This paper says nothing about developmental time-course. So it supports the premise INV-089 is built on, without touching the maturation-ordering conclusion; that inferential gap is why I hold the confidence at 0.72 rather than higher, and why the second entry (Verriotis/Fitzgerald, on nociceptive-system development) is the one that actually speaks to the maturation leg.

I have scored this **supports** at **0.72**: high source quality (human iEEG, and the exact citation the claim already names), strong mapping to the separateness premise, but a moderate transfer risk because the claim's real payload is developmental and this evidence is adult.
