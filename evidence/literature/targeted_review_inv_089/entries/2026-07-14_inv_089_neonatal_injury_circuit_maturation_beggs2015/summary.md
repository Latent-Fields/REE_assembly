# Nociceptive circuitry is present at birth but must refine before it can discriminate (Beggs 2015)

According to PubMed, Beggs (*Canadian Journal of Psychiatry*, 2015; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/070674371506000404)) reviews the long-term consequences of neonatal injury, and in doing so lays out the developmental scaffolding INV-089 depends on. The key passage: nociceptive circuitry is functioning at birth, "however, there is still considerable organization and refinement of this circuitry that occurs postnatally, before full discrimination of tactile and noxious input is possible." That refinement involves the "separation of tactile and nociceptive afferent input to the spinal cord's dorsal horn and the maturation of local and descending inhibitory circuitry."

The relevance to INV-089 is the *present-but-undifferentiated-then-refines* shape. INV-089 holds that harm-evaluation quality is bounded by `z_harm` differentiation, so that productive harm-evaluator training cannot precede sufficient differentiation of the harm representation. Beggs describes the biological counterpart at the earliest station of the pathway: the nociceptive channel exists from birth but cannot yet *discriminate* noxious from tactile input until postnatal reorganization has separated the two afferent streams and matured the inhibitory control that sharpens them. A harm channel that is present but not yet discriminative is exactly the substrate INV-089 says you must wait on before harm-valuation training becomes productive.

I score this the lowest of the three INV-089 entries, and deliberately. The separation Beggs describes is at the *spinal dorsal-horn afferent* level, whereas INV-089 concerns a *cortical representational encoder* (`z_harm`) and its differentiation — a real level-of-analysis gap. The evidence base is also predominantly rodent neonatal surgical-incision models, and the review's actual focus is injury-induced plasticity, with the normal-development account serving as its backdrop rather than its measured result. So this is corroborating context for the "differentiate before you can discriminate" premise, not a direct measurement of the REE mechanism.

Scored **supports** at **0.60**: genuine on-direction corroboration of the postnatal-refinement premise, held down by the spinal (not cortical) level of analysis, rodent provenance, and the paper's injury-plasticity rather than normative-development emphasis.
