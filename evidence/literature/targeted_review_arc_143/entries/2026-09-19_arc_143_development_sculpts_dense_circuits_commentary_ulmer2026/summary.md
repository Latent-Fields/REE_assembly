# Ulmer and Donato 2026 -- verified, and verified to be a commentary

ARC-143's notes added this citation in the 2026-09-15 ingestion pass with an unusually precise instruction:
"a COMMENTARY on the Vargas-Barroso result, not an independent measurement; a /lit-pull must verify it before
it is cited as support rather than motivation." This entry discharges that instruction, and the finding is
that the instruction was right.

The citation verifies exactly: Ulmer T and Donato F, *Development sculpts dense neural circuits to enhance
memory capacity*, **Trends in Neurosciences** 49(9):706-708, doi 10.1016/j.tins.2026.07.008, PMID 42608247,
both authors at the Biozentrum, University of Basel. The page range is itself the confirmation -- 706 to 708,
three pages, which is a spotlight/commentary format and not a research article. The abstract reads: "The
hippocampal CA3 network supports associative memory through recurrent connectivity. Vargas-Barroso and
colleagues have recently shown that the circuit develops from a dense network in which synapses exert strong
influence into a sparse and structured architecture that requires convergent input for neuronal activation.
Thus, during development, the hippocampus proceeds through distinct computational regimes that collectively
expand its memory capacity." The second sentence names the primary authors explicitly; there is no new
measurement here.

So the content is right and the evidential status is exactly what ARC-143 suspected. The substantive finding
-- dense-and-strong early, sparse-and-convergent later, with the trajectory itself expanding capacity -- does
support ARC-143's thesis that a developing system's capacity envelope is a design variable distinct from the
mature one. But REE already holds that finding. Vargas-Barroso, Watson, Navas-Olive, Schlogl and Jonas (Nat
Commun 2026, s41467-026-71914-x) is the primary anchor and is already VERIFIED at MECH-362, registered from
the same result in June. What this commentary adds is interpretive framing -- "distinct computational
regimes" is a useful phrase and is close to ARC-143's own language about declaring developmental and mature
envelopes separately -- and nothing evidential.

There is a pleasing irony in the failure mode here, and it is the reason I have set confidence to 0.25 rather
than letting a good journal carry it higher. If this commentary were counted as a second independent source
for the dense-to-sparse trajectory, alongside the primary paper it comments on, REE's evidence record would
be counting one lineage twice. That is precisely the error INV-107 forbids and MECH-553 is proposed to
prevent, committed inside the very pass that verified INV-107's anchor. So: recorded as motivation, scored
low, with the non-independence written into the failure signatures where the indexer and a future reader will
both see it.

Two further limits. The commentary is about hippocampal CA3 recurrent connectivity; ARC-143 generalises the
developmental-envelope discipline across z_world, the E1/E2 interfaces, replay pathways, goal influence and
E3 selection, and this source speaks to none of those. And it describes a trajectory that expands capacity
without establishing that a larger developmental envelope is generally better -- ARC-143's own boundary
statement already disclaims that, naming irreversible early mistakes and pathological attractors as live
rivals, and nothing here disturbs it. ARC-143 remains `substrate_conditional` on a developmental
capacity-schedule substrate that does not exist anywhere in `ree_core/`, and the claim's "DO NOT build in V3,
DO NOT queue an experiment from this entry" stands untouched.
