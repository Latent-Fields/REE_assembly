# Galantucci (2005) -- communication systems invented from scratch under coordination pressure

Galantucci put pairs of adults in separate rooms, gave them a shared video game they could only
solve by communicating, and handed them a graphic channel built specifically to make conventional
symbols unusable. What he found is that the pairs did not fail, and did not wait: communication
systems emerged rapidly, and they emerged as hybrids -- explicit invented signs braided together
with information that was implicitly available to both players and with silent, purely behavioural
coordination routines. The sign forms themselves converged on an economical vocabulary that was
perceptually distinguishable, cheap to produce, and robust to sloppy execution.

The reason this matters for ARC-101 is the shape of the manipulation rather than the size of the
effect. ARC-101 names five enabling conditions -- shared attention, partial observability,
coordination pressure, a low-cost channel, and repair -- and asserts that given those, proto-language
partially bootstraps from grounded proto-communication, with grammar acting as a turbocharger rather
than the engine. Galantucci's design instantiates four of those five almost literally, and removes
grammar from the channel by construction. A system still forms. Whatever is doing the constructive
work here, it is not inherited syntax.

I want to be careful about what this does not show, because the obvious over-reading is tempting.
These are adults with complete natural language. Stripping letters out of the channel does not strip
linguistic competence out of the participants, and a plausible deflationary account of the whole
result is that mature language users are simply very good at re-encoding an existing communicative
capacity into whatever medium they are handed. That account predicts the same data. The paradigm
cannot separate the two readings, and REE should not pretend otherwise: the evidence is that a rich
social ecology plus a grammar-free channel is SUFFICIENT for system formation in agents that already
have language, which is weaker than ARC-101 needs.

The second boundary is what emerged. Signs and coordination procedures are proto-communication in
exactly ARC-101's sense, and that is the half of the claim this paper speaks to. Recursive
structure is not on offer here, so the turbocharger half of ARC-101 -- that grammar, when it
arrives, accelerates rather than originates -- gets no evidence either way from this study.

Confidence 0.72. The mapping is unusually faithful for an architectural claim, which is what carries
the number; the prior-language confound is what keeps it out of the 0.8 band. For REE the useful
consequence is a design constraint rather than a settled fact: if the V5 social ecology is going to
be the engine, the falsifier is an agent population with no linguistic prior placed in this same
ecology. That experiment does not exist yet in this literature, and building its analogue is exactly
what ARC-101 is gated on.
