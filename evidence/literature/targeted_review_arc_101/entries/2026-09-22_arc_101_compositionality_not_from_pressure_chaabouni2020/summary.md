# Chaabouni et al. (2020) -- compositionality is selected by transmission, not produced by task pressure

This is the entry in the ARC-101 set that argues with the claim, and it is the most useful one for
that reason. Chaabouni and colleagues ran deep sender/receiver agents in referential games over
synthetic attribute-value inputs, measured compositionality with metrics borrowed from
disentanglement theory rather than by eye, and reported three things. Given a large enough input
space, emergent languages do come to refer to novel composite concepts. Measured compositionality
is uncorrelated with that generalisation ability. And compositional languages are nonetheless easier
for a fresh learner -- even one with a different architecture -- to acquire. Their conclusion:
compositionality does not emerge from a demand to generalise; it survives and spreads once it
happens to arise, because it transmits better.

For ARC-101's negative half this is support, and support of an unusually direct kind. ARC-101 says
the grammar/LLM scaffold is a turbocharger and never the engine. Here are agents generalising to
novel composite meanings with no compositional structure in their protocol at all. Whatever is
carrying the generalisation, it is not grammar. That is the claim's negative half demonstrated
inside a running system rather than argued from human data.

For ARC-101's positive half it is a problem, and I would rather register it as one now than have
governance discover it later. ARC-101 lists five enabling conditions -- shared attention, partial
observability, coordination pressure, a low-cost channel, repair -- and asserts that given them,
proto-language partially bootstraps. Chaabouni et al. found coordination pressure present and
structure absent. What selected for structure was something ARC-101's list does not contain:
transmission to new learners. If that generalises, the V5 social ecology needs population turnover
as a first-class component, not an incidental one, and the enabling-condition list is incomplete as
written.

The caveat that keeps this from being a clean refutation is the poverty of the simulated ecology.
These are Lewis games over symbolic attribute-value vectors. There is no perceptual grounding, no
partial observability of a shared physical world, and no repair channel. By ARC-101's own reckoning
at most two of five conditions are met, and a null result in a thin ecology is weak evidence about
a rich one. It would be a mistake to read this as showing the V5 ecology would fail; it shows that
the two conditions actually tested are insufficient on their own.

Confidence 0.66, direction mixed, and the mixed label is doing real work -- it should not be
collapsed to supports on the strength of the negative half. The concrete consequences I would draw
for REE are two. First, a falsifier for ARC-101 becomes statable: run the V5 ecology with all five
conditions and no learner turnover, and see whether structure appears. Second, a guard for
downstream work: generalisation to novel combinations must not be used as a proxy measure for
proto-grammar having formed, because this paper shows those two come apart.
