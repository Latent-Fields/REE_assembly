# DMN coherence normalises in ECT responders, persists in non-responders — reversibility is conditional

According to PubMed, Mulders, van Eijndhoven, Beckmann and colleagues (2016, *Journal of Affective Disorders*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2016.06.059)) studied 16 patients with treatment-resistant MDD before and after electroconvulsive therapy, alongside 16 matched controls, using a novel "network coherence" metric — how tightly each DMN voxel tracks the network's overall time course. In depression, coherence was reduced in the precuneus and angular gyrus. The key longitudinal result: with ECT this coherence *normalised in the eight responders but not in the eight non-responders*.

For Q-061 this supplies the conditionality that the Li (2012) partial-reversibility result only implies. Here the regime's reconfiguration is explicitly yoked to whether repair *succeeds*: when even a brute-force somatic intervention drives clinical response, the network regime moves back toward baseline; when it fails to, the regime stays put. So reversibility is not an absolute property of the depressive regime — it is contingent on the repair route landing. That is a useful constraint on how REE should think about MECH-367 repair: the relevant question is less "is this regime reversible?" and more "did this particular repair attempt achieve the reconfiguration?", with persistence as the default when it does not.

The mapping caveats are real and hold confidence to 0.5. ECT has no clean analogue among the REE repair routes Q-061 lists (offline integration, decommitment, social re-access, goal re-coupling) — it is a global perturbation, not a targeted reconfiguration, so the transfer is to "a strong repair intervention" generically rather than to any specific REE mechanism. Responder/non-responder status is defined by symptom change, not by illness duration, so this does nothing to isolate the RA-001 duration variable; the non-responders are simply patients in whom this route failed, not demonstrably patients past a chronicity threshold. And with eight subjects per cell the authors themselves flag the finding as tentative.

I have set `evidence_direction: supports` (conditional reversibility) rather than mixed, because the responder normalisation is a genuine demonstration that the regime *can* be reconfigured — the non-responder persistence is the expected complement, not a contradiction. The recorded failure signature is that the strongest available somatic route still failed to reconfigure the regime in a subset of (already treatment-resistant) patients, which is the empirical face of "repair can fail" that Q-061 must hold alongside "repair can work."
