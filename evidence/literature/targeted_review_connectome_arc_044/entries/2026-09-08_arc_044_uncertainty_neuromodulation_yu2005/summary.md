# Yu & Dayan (2005), *Uncertainty, neuromodulation, and attention* -- ARC-044

## What the paper does

Yu and Dayan ask what job the neuromodulators are actually doing, and answer it normatively rather than descriptively. In a Bayesian agent that must infer the state of a changing world from unreliable observations, there are two distinguishable kinds of not-knowing. There is the noise you have already learned to expect -- the cue is only 80% predictive, and you know that. And there is the noise that arrives when the world itself changes underneath you -- the cue was 80% predictive, and now, unannounced, it is not. The paper's proposal is that acetylcholine carries the first (expected uncertainty) and norepinephrine the second (unexpected uncertainty), and that the two together are what lets an agent behave sensibly in an environment that is both noisy and non-stationary. The evidence is a synthesis: pharmacological lesion and agonist studies, attentional cueing behaviour, and physiological recordings, assembled into a model rather than generated by a new experiment.

## Why it bears on ARC-044

ARC-044 claims that ARC-016, MECH-152, MECH-026 and MECH-093 are not four mechanisms but four faces of one. This paper is the strongest independent statement I have found of the first half of that idea. It says, in effect, the same thing: precision control is a single computational job, and the apparently separate arousal, attention and vigilance phenomena are that job being done under different statistics. That is a real convergence, arrived at from the neuroscience side rather than from REE's architectural side, and it is worth more than a paper that merely reports gain modulation somewhere in cortex.

But the paper is equally clear about the part ARC-044 gets wrong, or at least gets ahead of. It does not unify. It insists on two signals, with two sources, estimated from two different statistics of the same input stream, and it derives an interaction that is -- the paper's own phrase -- part-antagonistic and part-synergistic. That is a pointed result for us, because ARC-044's stated experimental prediction is that if the mechanisms are truly facets of one thing, varying context and temporal state jointly should produce a *multiplicative* interaction, and if they are independent, an *additive* one. Yu and Dayan describe a third possibility that ARC-044's falsifier does not have a slot for: signals that genuinely compose, that are genuinely not independent, and whose composition is nonetheless neither additive nor multiplicative but sign-varying across regimes. If REE runs the hazard-context x volatility experiment ARC-044 proposes and gets a mixed-sign interaction, the current prediction structure will read that as noise.

## How this translates into REE

The honest translation is: keep ARC-044's architectural unification, drop its mechanistic unification, and widen its falsifier. The architectural unification -- that effective precision at any evaluation channel is a single quantity assembled from several contributions, and that ARC-016, MECH-152, MECH-026 and MECH-093 should all write into it rather than each having a private threshold -- is what this paper supports and is the part worth building. The mechanistic unification -- one signal, one source -- is what it contradicts. And ARC-044's falsifier should be amended before it is run, to admit a third outcome between additive and multiplicative.

There is also a gap the paper exposes by having something REE does not. Its acetylcholine term, expected uncertainty within a stable context, has no counterpart in ARC-044's four-mechanism list. ARC-016 is closest, but ARC-016 estimates from prediction-error variance in the *temporal* stream, whereas the cholinergic term is about the known unreliability of a *cue* -- a per-channel reliability prior, not a running variance. That is a distinct quantity, and its absence from the list is more interesting than any of the four that are on it.

## Limitations and confidence

The mismatch that matters is the axis of decomposition. Yu and Dayan split uncertainty statistically (expected vs unexpected); ARC-044 splits gain by source and scope (global LC-NE arousal vs context-specific vmPFC/OFC). These are compatible cuts through the same space but they are not the same cut, so this is indirect evidence -- it constrains ARC-044 without testing it. The paper is also two decades old and normative rather than empirical: it organises data it did not collect, and the specific ACh/NE assignments have been contested since. I have set confidence at 0.72, which reflects a high-quality source doing genuinely relevant theoretical work at one remove from the claim, and I have recorded the direction as mixed because a paper that supports your framing while contradicting your headline is not a supporting paper.
