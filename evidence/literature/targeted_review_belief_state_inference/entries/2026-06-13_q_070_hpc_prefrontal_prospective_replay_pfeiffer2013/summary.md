# Hippocampal place-cell sequences depict future paths to remembered goals (Pfeiffer & Foster, 2013)

**Claims grounded:** Q-070 (inference failure-mode register + biology grounding), ARC-091 (inference / belief-state affordance layer). Strand L1 (hippocampal-prefrontal replay).

Pfeiffer and Foster recorded large dorsal-CA1 ensembles in rats navigating an open arena for reward at remembered goal locations. The headline result is that, in the brief pauses before a goal-directed run, the hippocampus generates short place-cell sequences that sweep from the animal's *current* position toward the *goal* -- not a replay of where the animal has been, but a depiction of where it is about to go. The sequences were predictive of the upcoming trajectory and were biased toward the goal even when the goal was in a novel direction.

For the inference layer this is the cleanest single-cell evidence behind one of INF-7's load-bearing premises: hippocampal output is not only retrieval. When the architecture says (ARC-007/ARC-018) that hippocampal completion and rollout *propose candidate trajectories*, the biology it is leaning on is exactly this -- prospective, goal-anchored sequence generation. The inference-goblin's "given smoke, slope, old reef-smell and the remembered left turn, the safe path is probably *there*" has a neural correlate: the hippocampus drawing a line from here to a remembered goal before the body commits.

The honest caveat is the usual one for this literature. What is measured is literal spatial trajectory generation toward a visible/remembered reward in an open field. REE's inference layer claims something far more general -- state-hypothesis construction under partial observability across non-spatial relational structure. The transfer (spatial prospective sequencing -> general relational state inference) is plausible and is exactly what Whittington 2020 argues the same circuit supports, but this paper does not itself demonstrate it. I have set confidence at 0.78: strong source, tight mapping for the "replay-as-hypothesis-generator" half of L1, with the species/generality transfer the main discount.

What would weaken the framing: if hippocampal sequences were found to depict only past trajectories (pure consolidation), the inference-layer reading of replay would lose its biological footing. The result is the opposite -- future-biased, goal-directed -- which is why this anchors L1 rather than merely decorating it.
