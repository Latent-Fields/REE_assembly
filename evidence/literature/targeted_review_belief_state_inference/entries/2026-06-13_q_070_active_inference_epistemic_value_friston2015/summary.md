# Active inference and epistemic value (Friston et al., 2015)

**Claims grounded:** Q-070, ARC-091, MECH-388 (epistemic action pressure). Strand L5 (active inference / epistemic value).

Friston and colleagues give the formal treatment in which an agent minimises the *expected free energy* of future outcomes, and -- the part that matters for INF-7 -- the value of a policy decomposes into two terms: **pragmatic** value (reaching preferred outcomes; reward and harm avoidance) and **epistemic** value (expected information gain; reduction of uncertainty about hidden states). Because epistemic value is an explicit component, the framework selects information-gathering actions even when they are not immediately rewarding: an agent will act to *resolve uncertainty*, not only to collect reward.

This is the backbone of MECH-388. REE's claim is that when goal success or harm avoidance depends on hidden state, the system should be able to assign action pressure to uncertainty-reducing transitions even when they are not immediately reward- or harm-optimal -- "in a dangerous midline state, the intelligent action may be one that *tests* where safety is." Friston's decomposition is the formal warrant that such epistemic actions are well-posed and selectable. It also reframes the Q-044 curiosity machinery: novelty/uncertainty/learning-progress are not only play and exploration; under partial observability the *same* drive is a survival-relevant viability function. The two register failure modes at this pole fall out of the balance: **anti-epistemic panic** (weight only pragmatic value -> commit under threat without sampling) and **epistemic freezing** (over-weight epistemic value -> gather information forever, never commit).

The caveat is that active inference packs pragmatic and epistemic value into a *single* expected-free-energy scalar, and REE neither adopts free-energy minimisation wholesale nor collapses its streams. The transferable claim is the *existence and selectability* of epistemic value, not the unifying formalism -- and MECH-388 is explicitly gated on Q-044's sub-flavour-independence question being adjudicated before curiosity is generalised. Confidence 0.70: canonical and heavily cited, but theory-with-simulation rather than a decisive empirical test, with the formalism-mismatch as the discount.
