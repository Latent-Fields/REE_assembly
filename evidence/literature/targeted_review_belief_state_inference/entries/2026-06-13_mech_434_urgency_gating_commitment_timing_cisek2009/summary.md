# Decisions in Changing Conditions: The Urgency-Gating Model (Cisek, Puskas & El-Murr, 2009)

**Claims grounded:** MECH-434 (epistemic commitment timing), Q-070 (failure-mode register). The mechanism anchor -- commitment timing as a governed control parameter.

Cisek and colleagues argue that in a changing world the brain does *not* integrate evidence to a fixed bound (the classic drift-diffusion picture). Instead it tracks only the momentary, novel sensory evidence and multiplies it by a growing **urgency signal**; commitment happens when that product crosses a criterion. Because urgency rises over time, the *effective* threshold falls -- the agent becomes progressively more willing to commit on less evidence the longer it waits. The decision is the product of evidence and urgency, not evidence alone.

For MECH-434 this is the load-bearing grounding, because the whole claim rests on commitment timing being a *tunable parameter* rather than an emergent accident. Urgency gating is the biological proof of exactly that knob: a single, adjustable gain that sets how quickly deliberation gives way to action. The two failure poles of MECH-434 fall straight out of mis-setting it. **Epistemic-freezing** is urgency held too low / threshold too high -- the agent keeps waiting for more evidence and never commits. **Anti-epistemic-panic** is urgency forced too high / threshold collapsed -- the agent commits almost immediately, before its belief-state set (MECH-385) has been disambiguated. The healthy regime is the tuned middle, which is precisely what REE would need to govern at the seam between the belief-state set, epistemic action pressure (MECH-388), and the commitment gate (MECH-061 / MECH-090).

The caveat is the scope of the original model: it is fit to rapid two-alternative perceptual-motor decisions, with urgency that is motor-related and largely endogenous. REE's commitment is over inferred trajectories under partial observability, and its urgency is threat- and goal-driven (the 603k midline pressure, not a reaching deadline). So what transfers is the *existence and tunability* of a timing parameter, not the specific multiplicative-urgency implementation. Confidence 0.74 -- strong source and a tight conceptual fit to the claim's backbone, discounted by the perceptual-motor-to-trajectory-commitment transfer.
