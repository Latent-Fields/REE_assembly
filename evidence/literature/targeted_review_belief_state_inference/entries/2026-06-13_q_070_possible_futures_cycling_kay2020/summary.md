# Constant sub-second cycling between representations of possible futures (Kay et al., 2020)

**Claims grounded:** Q-070, ARC-091, INV-078 (inferred trajectories remain provenance-tagged as hypotheses). Strand L3 (pattern completion vs preplay; the hypothesis-not-commitment safety principle).

At a maze choice point with two upcoming paths, Kay et al. found that hippocampal activity does not settle on one representation. It *alternates* -- constantly, at roughly 8 Hz, one possible-future scenario per ~125-ms theta cycle. The animal, in other words, holds two competing hypotheses about the future concurrently and cycles between them sub-second, rather than collapsing to a single committed path. HHMI fairly called this the neural basis for hypothetical thinking in rats.

This is the highest-fidelity mapping in the whole pull, because it is almost a one-to-one biological instantiation of two REE claims at once. First, MECH-385's belief-state hypothesis set: the substrate maintains *multiple* competing latent-state/trajectory hypotheses rather than one collapsed state, which is exactly what E3 is supposed to evaluate trajectories over. Second, and more importantly for INF-7, INV-078's safety principle: a completed/pattern-completed trajectory is a *hypothesis*, not a perception and not a commitment. The cycling is the substrate visibly *refusing to collapse uncertainty prematurely*. That refusal is the healthy midpoint between the two register failure modes it sits between -- **premature-collapse** (commit to one path too early; MECH-126 uncertainty collapse) on one side, **hypothesis-proliferation** (cycle forever, never commit; epistemic freezing) on the other.

The caveat is that two discrete spatial paths at a maze junction is a vastly simpler hypothesis space than REE's general partial-observability inference, and the theta-locked ~8 Hz cycling is a navigation-specific mechanism. The *principle* -- hold competing futures, do not collapse early, keep them provenance-tagged as proposals -- transfers cleanly; the *mechanism* should not be over-read as the implementation REE must adopt. Confidence 0.80.
