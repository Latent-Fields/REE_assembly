# The Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine (Whittington et al., 2020)

**Claims grounded:** Q-070, ARC-091. Strand L2 (cognitive maps / relational inference).

The Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine (TEM) is a generative model that factorises *structure* (how things relate -- the abstract graph of a task or environment) from *content* (the particular sensory observations that fill that structure). Trained this way, TEM spontaneously develops entorhinal-like cell types (grid, band, border, object-vector cells) and hippocampal-like remapping place cells, and -- the point that matters here -- it *generalises* learned relational structure to novel environments and reproduces representations recorded in non-spatial relational-inference tasks. The paper's argument is that the hippocampal-entorhinal system is a general relational-memory engine, with spatial navigation a special case.

This is the biology behind REE's "state as a navigable situation" framing and, more specifically, behind the map-generalisation failure axis in INF-7's register. If the cognitive map is a reusable relational structure, then inference is the act of locating oneself within it -- "where am I in this relational space, and what unseen relation follows?" -- and the two characteristic failures fall straight out of the factorisation: **map over-generalisation** (transferring a route across superficially-similar but structurally-distinct states) and **map under-generalisation / oversplitting** (failing to transfer a safe route across genuinely-equivalent states). Those are MECH-126's overmerge and oversplit, seen from the inference side.

The caveat is that TEM is a normative model fit to data, not a causal demonstration that the brain performs this exact factorisation, and REE does not implement TEM. The mapping is at the level of shared computational principle: factorised relational generalisation is a property REE *wants* its L-space to have, and TEM is the existence-proof that a single mechanism can yield both the cell-type phenomenology and the generalisation behaviour. Confidence 0.76 -- high source quality and a clean conceptual mapping, discounted because the biological claims are inferential and REE inherits the principle, not the model.
