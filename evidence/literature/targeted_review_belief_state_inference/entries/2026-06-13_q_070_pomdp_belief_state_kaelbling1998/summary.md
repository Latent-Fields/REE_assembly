# Planning and acting in partially observable stochastic domains (Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra, 1998)

**Claims grounded:** Q-070, ARC-091, MECH-385 (belief-state hypothesis set). Strand L4 (POMDP belief state).

This is the canonical formal statement of the exact problem the inference layer names. In a partially observable Markov decision process the agent cannot see the true state; it must maintain a *belief state* -- a distribution over possible hidden states -- updated from its action and observation history, and its optimal policy is a function of that belief state (equivalently, of the history), never of the current observation alone. The paper proves, in effect, that under hidden state an observation-to-action reflex is provably insufficient.

For REE this is the why behind MECH-385 and the whole ARC-091 thesis. When 603k's ARM_HARM_ON_MIDLINE asks the creature to "infer a route to safety from partial evidence," it is asking it to act on a belief state rather than on the raw scene -- and the POMDP framework is the formal guarantee that you cannot get this right by reacting to observations. REE's bounded top-k hypothesis set, each hypothesis carrying a confidence weight, is best read as a *tractable, non-Bayesian approximation* of the POMDP belief state. The register's **failure-to-infer-hidden-danger / hidden-safety** modes are exactly the cost the POMDP formalism quantifies for acting on observation instead of belief; **context loss** (MECH-126) is the degenerate memoryless policy the formalism rules out.

The discount is real and worth stating plainly. REE deliberately does *not* adopt the POMDP machinery: it keeps a small set of competing hypotheses with heuristic precision rather than an exact posterior, it separates harm/goal/residue rather than folding everything into one reward, and it never commits to closed-form belief updates. So this grounds the *problem* and the *necessity* of belief-over-observation -- not REE's solution. Importing POMDP optimality guarantees into REE would be a category error. Confidence 0.70: canonical source, but the mapping is principle-level with genuine mechanism divergence (hence the higher transfer-risk component).
