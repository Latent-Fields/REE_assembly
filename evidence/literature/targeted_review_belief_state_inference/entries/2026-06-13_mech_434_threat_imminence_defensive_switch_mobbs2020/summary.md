# Space, Time, and Fear: Survival Computations along Defensive Circuits (Mobbs et al., 2020)

**Claims grounded:** MECH-434, Q-070. The anti-epistemic-panic pole.

Mobbs, Headley, Ding and Dayan synthesise the **threat-imminence** framework: defensive decisions play out at different spatiotemporal scales, and the *computation* the brain uses shifts with how close (in space and time) the threat is. Distal or uncertain threats leave room for extended information processing supported by model-based, deliberative circuits. Proximal or surprising threats *contract* the decision and state space and recruit fast, model-free architectures -- midbrain and periaqueductal-gray reactive defence. The switch is graded and governed by imminence.

This is close to a textbook description of MECH-434's **anti-epistemic-panic** pole. As imminence rises, the system abandons epistemic, deliberative inference -- the sampling that would let it work out *where* safety is -- in favour of reactive commitment on a shrunken hypothesis space. It is adaptive when imminence is genuinely high (you cannot deliberate with a predator on you), and pathological when it fires on uncertain or distal threat (panic that commits before the belief-state is resolved). Critically, it grounds the claim that commitment timing is **threat-modulated**: imminence drives the urgency term of MECH-434 upward, which is exactly the pressure the V3-EXQ-603k ARM_HARM_ON_MIDLINE arm manufactures -- danger-sense without a resolved route, pushing toward premature commitment.

The same framework also gestures at the opposite failure: if imminence is *mis-estimated as low*, the agent over-deliberates -- the epistemic-freezing pole -- so the two poles of MECH-434 are the two mis-calibrations of a single imminence-to-timing mapping. The caveat is anatomical: threat-imminence is grounded in mammalian survival circuits (amygdala, PAG, hypothalamus) that REE does not implement, so the mapping is functional (imminence -> shorter deliberation -> faster commitment), not structural, and whether the model-based/model-free switch is the right primitive for REE's belief-state commitment is itself a live design question. Confidence 0.76.
