# The Predictive Coding Account of Psychosis (Sterzer et al., 2018)

**Claims grounded:** Q-070 (inference failure-mode register; the bridge to the existing MECH-126 taxonomy). Failure-register strand.

This consensus review in *Biological Psychiatry* recasts psychotic symptoms as disturbances of hierarchical Bayesian / predictive-coding *inference*. Perception and belief are formed by combining prior expectations with incoming sensory evidence, weighted by their respective precisions; mismatches are prediction errors that drive learning. In psychosis, the review argues, this weighting goes wrong -- aberrantly precise prediction errors (or under-weighted/over-weighted priors) make noise feel meaningful and make false hypotheses feel certain. Hallucinations and delusions become, respectively, perception dominated by priors and explanatory hypotheses that resist correction.

This is the paper that lets INF-7 do the thing the task actually asks for: not invent a parallel failure taxonomy, but *map the inference-specific failure modes onto the existing state-abstraction taxonomy* (MECH-126). Three of the register's entries land directly. **Overconfident-wrong-hypothesis** -- a false inference held with high confidence because priors are over-weighted -- is MECH-126's overmerge compounded with uncertainty collapse. **Hypothesis-proliferation / apophenia / paranoid route construction** -- aberrant salience assigning meaning and threat to noise -- is valence mis-tagging plus threat-spreading. **Cue-hijack** -- a weak cue displacing stronger state evidence -- is the precision-weighting pathology in miniature. The clinical literature, in other words, says the new inference failure modes are largely *re-descriptions of the same state-abstraction failures under partial observability*, which is precisely the reduction Q-070 poses as its open question.

The honest limits: the predictive-coding account of psychosis is itself a *contested* computational framework, not a settled mechanism, and REE does not implement hierarchical precision-weighting literally. So the mapping is at the level of failure-mode *topology* -- which inference errors occur, and how they cluster onto the existing taxonomy -- not mechanism identity. It grounds that these modes are real, clinically attested, and reducible-in-principle to MECH-126; it does not prove REE's substrate will generate them by the same route. Confidence 0.74.
