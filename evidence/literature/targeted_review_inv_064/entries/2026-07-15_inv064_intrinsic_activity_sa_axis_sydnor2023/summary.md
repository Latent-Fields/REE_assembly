# Sydnor et al. (2023) -- the ordering, now in primary data, with the environment prediction attached

According to PubMed ([DOI 10.1038/s41593-023-01282-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-023-01282-y), PMID 36973514), Sydnor and colleagues take the sensorimotor-to-association axis out of review territory and test it directly in 1,033 youths aged 8 to 23. Using resting-state fMRI, they show that intrinsic cortical activity refines developmentally from synchronized and high-amplitude toward sparse and low-amplitude -- the signature that animal work associates with declining plasticity as cortex matures. The refinement does not happen everywhere at once. It is initiated heterochronously, ordered along the sensorimotor-to-association axis from ages 8 to 18, and it tracks the maturation of intracortical myelin, a known plasticity brake. And in the finding that matters most for INV-064, the associations between a youth's neighborhood environment and their intrinsic activity diverged most across this axis in mid-adolescence.

## What it adds over the review entries

The Sydnor 2021 and Konrad 2013 entries establish the ordering at review level. This one supplies the primary data: a large sample, a plasticity-linked readout, and a clean demonstration that the program really does unfold along the axis in the predicted order. That upgrades INV-064's ordering support from "well-synthesised" to "measured."

More specifically, it instantiates a prediction the other two only gesture at. INV-064 claims that intensive novel experience in childhood has disproportionate long-term returns, and that early disruption has outsized, specific effects. The mechanism it implies is a window: experience should bite hardest where and when plasticity is highest. The environment-times-axis interaction here is exactly that -- environmental input has its most differential developmental leverage along the axis during the sensitive period. That is the "high-entropy early experience matters more" prediction, observed.

## The two caveats I want on the record

First, intrinsic-activity amplitude is a proxy for plasticity, not a measure of competence, and the environment associations are correlational -- neighborhood disadvantage, not manipulated experience. So this supports the window mechanism, not the causal-curriculum necessity that is INV-064's real load. Second, and I want this explicit for the confidence aggregation: this paper shares its senior authorship and lab (Sydnor, Satterthwaite) with the 2021 Neuron review already in this directory. The two should be read as one strong line of evidence -- a review head and an empirical body -- not as two independent confirmations.
