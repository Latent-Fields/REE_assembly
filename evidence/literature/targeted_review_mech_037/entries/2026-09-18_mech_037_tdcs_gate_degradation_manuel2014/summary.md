# Causal degradation of the reality filter by frontal tDCS (Manuel et al. 2014)

## What the paper does

Twenty healthy adults performed a reality-filtering task in three sessions separated by at least a
week, receiving cathodal, anodal, or sham transcranial direct current stimulation over the frontal
pole. Before running it, the authors modelled current flow computationally and established that
this montage should produce polarity-specific current across posterior medial orbitofrontal cortex
-- the region the lesion literature implicates. Cathodal stimulation specifically impaired reality
filtering relative to both anodal and sham. The design is within-subject and sham-controlled, and
the specificity is what carries the result: had the deficit appeared under anodal stimulation too,
it would have been a general task-disruption effect.

## Why it bears on MECH-037

MECH-037's confirming signature is written in two parts, and the second part is causal:
"degrading or disabling the gate (removing the trace/ordering signal's influence on
precision/commitment) produces a measurable increase in high-confidence commitments of ungrounded
content -- i.e. confabulation-like failures track gate integrity in the predicted direction". That
is a demanding thing to ask for, and it is worth noticing that the human literature actually
supplies it. Reality filtering can be pushed down selectively, in healthy brains, without touching
the memories being filtered. The gate is not an epiphenomenon of memory quality; it has its own
integrity parameter, and that parameter can be moved.

For REE this licenses the experimental design the claim needs. A gate-intact versus gate-degraded
arm, with content quality held constant, is not an artificial ablation invented to make a claim
testable -- it is the manipulation that established the function in humans. It also says something
about where to put the REE-side lesion. The handle here is on the gate's own parameters, not on the
trace signal feeding it. An experiment that tested MECH-037 by removing the hippocampal trace
signal would be testing a different thing: whether the gate has inputs, not whether the gate exists.

## The gap I would not paper over

The dependent measure is filtering accuracy. MECH-037 predicts something related but distinct: an
increase in *high-confidence* commitments of ungrounded content. Accuracy and confidence come apart
routinely, and the whole interest of the confabulation phenotype is that it is high-confidence
error, not merely error. If REE writes a falsifier that scores only commitment accuracy under gate
degradation, it will have left the claim's actual signature untested while appearing to test it.
The instrumentation has to carry the precision or confidence attached to each commitment, not just
its correctness. I would treat that as a pre-registration requirement for any experiment built off
this claim.

## Limitations and confidence

tDCS is a blunt instrument. The orbitofrontal targeting rests on a current-flow model rather than a
measurement, and a frontal-pole montage affects a good deal besides posterior medial OFC, so the
anatomical attribution should be held loosely even though the functional effect is clean. Twenty
healthy adults in a single laboratory. And the anatomical point cuts the same way it does across
this whole directory: the causal handle on reality filtering in humans is prefrontal, which makes
this strong evidence that the gate is a degradable faculty and much weaker evidence that the gate
is the Papez-like loop MECH-037 names. Confidence 0.68, direction `supports` -- it supports the
claim's structure and its experimental design, not its anatomical routing.
