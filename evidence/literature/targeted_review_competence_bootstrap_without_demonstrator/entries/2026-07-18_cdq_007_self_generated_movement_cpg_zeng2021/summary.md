# Zeng et al. 2021 -- The bootstrap is demonstrator-free but not experience-free

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.10.005), *Current Biology* 2021), Zeng and colleagues asked a question that has been open since the earliest observations of embryonic movement: are the precocious twitches seen in embryos of many species *instructive* for motor development, or are they merely a by-product of immature circuits firing? They imaged emerging motor activity in Drosophila embryos with and without proprioceptive feedback and found that proprioceptive experience is *essential* for locomotor CPG formation. They then identified the circuit: two pairs of segmental interneurons forming a pioneer premotor network that autonomously generates rhythmic plateau potentials via IP3-mediated calcium release. That autonomous rhythm drives muscle contraction, the contraction produces proprioceptive feedback, and the feedback is required for the gap-junctional coupling that assembles the mature CPG.

I regard this as the most mechanistically informative paper in this pull, because it describes the *architecture* of a demonstrator-free bootstrap rather than merely asserting one exists. Nothing outside the embryo teaches it. But equally, the innate seed circuit alone does not produce competence -- deprived of the feedback its own activity generates, the CPG fails to form. The innate component is an *activity generator*, and the competence is built by a closed loop in which the generator supplies its own curriculum. That is a considerably more specific and more demanding claim than "add an innate drive".

For CDQ-007 this cuts both ways, which is why I have logged it as mixed rather than supports. In favour of the H-approach-primitive leg: it is an existence proof that a competent motor program can be assembled with no conspecific input at all, purely from an innate generator plus self-generated experience. Against a naive version of the same leg: an innate non-extinguishing drive that merely raises action rate, without a channel by which the resulting experience trains the policy, would not reproduce this mechanism. The REE converter's failure is not obviously a low-action-rate failure -- readiness was met on every leg -- so the paper warns that "add drive" is underspecified unless one also names what the drive's output trains.

The caveats are real. This is an invertebrate embryo; the training signal is proprioceptive reafference, not reward or prediction error; the cellular physiology (gap junctions, IP3/Ca2+ pacemaking) has no REE counterpart. What transfers is the *shape* of the solution, not its implementation. Confidence 0.7, direction mixed. It is a strong causal result whose main value here is that it constrains what a credible innate-primitive story would have to look like, rather than adjudicating whether that story is the right one.
