# Kesner, Calva & Ikemoto 2022 -- Seeking as an architecturally separate drive

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pneurobio.2022.102252), *Progress in Neurobiology* 2022), Kesner and colleagues review the substrates of curiosity and exploratory behaviour and make the observation that motivates this whole line of inquiry: reinforcement learning and goal-seeking are usually attributed to midbrain dopamine neurons, yet remarkably little is known about the substrates of behaviour that occurs *in the absence of a clear goal or reward* -- despite behavioural scientists having argued for decades that such behaviour is regulated by an innate drive. They propose a circuit (supramammillary region to hippocampal formation for the cognitive component; supramammillary glutamatergic to medial septal glutamatergic to VTA dopaminergic for vigour) and a currency (environment prediction error). They also reframe phasic dopamine responses as signalling potentially important stimuli rather than rewards.

For CDQ-007 the useful content is architectural. If the review is right, biology keeps the drive-to-explore and the reward-prediction-error critic *anatomically distinct*, with the former modulating the latter's vigour rather than being derived from it. That is precisely the structural possibility the REE converter's failure invites: four axes have been eliminated (capacity, drive-schedule, reward-coupling, credit-horizon) and every treatment arm forages at floor despite readiness being met -- which is at least consistent with a missing channel rather than a mistuned one. This paper is evidence that the missing channel, if it is missing, is a channel biology actually has.

There is a caveat here that I think is the single most important qualification in this entire review, and I want it stated plainly rather than buried. The currency Kesner et al. propose is *environment prediction error*. An error-driven drive quiesces as the world-model improves. That is not the same object as the non-extinguishing drive the H-approach-primitive leg posits. Indeed it is close to its opposite: an agent that has modelled its environment well would, on this account, stop seeking -- whereas the REE cold-start problem needs something that keeps producing goal-directed action precisely when nothing is paying off. So the paper supports the *existence and separability* of an appetitive drive channel while leaving the *non-extinguishing* property, which is the load-bearing part of the hypothesis, unaddressed and arguably in tension.

The other limits are ordinary: it is a review, so it synthesises rather than generates; competing accounts fold information-seeking back into reinforcement learning as an intrinsic-motivation bonus term, and this review does not decisively rule those out. Confidence 0.62, direction supports. It earns its place because it is the clearest statement in the literature of the architectural separability that H-approach-primitive assumes -- but read carefully it also supplies the sharpest available objection to that leg.
