# Veit et al. 2023 -- The demonstrator can be a ghost

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10071-022-01740-3), *Animal Cognition* 2023), Veit and colleagues gave 36 free-ranging domestic pigs a two-step manipulative foraging task -- remove a plug from its recess, then slide a cover aside to expose a food compartment -- under four demonstration conditions: a conspecific demonstrator, a human demonstrator, a "ghost control" in which the objects moved by themselves with no agent, and a ghost control accompanied by an inactive conspecific bystander. Twenty-two further pigs previously tested with no demonstration served as non-observer controls. Observers in every condition interacted longer with the relevant objects and solved the task more often than non-observers -- and there was no difference in success between the four observer types. The authors conclude the pigs learned about the *apparatus*, not about the *actions*: emulation rather than imitation, and social agents are not necessary.

I included this as the deliberate counterweight in the pull, and it is worth being clear about what it counterweights. The BC arm is currently REE's only floor-clearing existence proof, at 32.72, and it is natural to read that as "the converter needs a policy to copy". Veit et al. suggest an alternative reading of what demonstrations actually deliver. If a self-moving plug teaches a pig as much as a skilled conspecific does, then the informational content of the demonstration is *task structure* -- what the objects afford, what moves and in what order -- not a policy. Translated across, that would mean BC's success in REE might be evidence for the value of a structural prior rather than for imitation as such, and that a non-social channel supplying the same structure could substitute.

The result cuts against the strong social version of H-bc-prior but I do not think it cuts against the leg as a whole, and the distinction is important enough that I want it explicit. Observers of *every* type still beat non-observers. External information was required. What was not required was a *conspecific*. So the paper is evidence against the necessity of a demonstrator-as-agent, and no evidence at all against the necessity of an informational prior of some kind -- which is arguably the part of H-bc-prior that matters for REE.

Further limits. The task is affordance-dominated: a fixed two-step apparatus where knowing what the objects do is nearly the whole problem. Temporally extended foraging, where the difficulty is action selection over a horizon, may be exactly the regime in which action-level information carries weight that it does not carry here. And these are adult free-ranging pigs with a fully developed exploratory and manipulative repertoire -- this is skill acquisition on top of existing competence, not a cold start. Confidence 0.72, direction weakens (of the imitative reading specifically, not of the leg). It is the best-designed dissociation I found and it earns inclusion for the ghost control alone.
