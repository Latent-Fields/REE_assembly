# The motif travels, but not in the shape we specified (Swiney & Sousa, 2014)

## What the paper did

The traditional comparator account of auditory verbal hallucination is elegant and has been
influential for twenty-five years: inner speech carries a motor prediction, that prediction
attenuates the sensory consequences, the attenuation is what makes inner speech feel like one's own,
and when the process is disrupted the speech is misattributed to an external source. Swiney and
Sousa take that account apart carefully. They argue the specification of inner speech it depends on
is untenable, and that the electrophysiological evidence usually cited in its favour -- the N100
suppression literature above all -- has been interpreted inconsistently across studies.

Then they rebuild it. Following leading models of motor imagery, they propose that inner speech is
not attenuated by motor prediction but *derived directly from it*. On that account a failure of motor
prediction changes the phenomenology of inner speech itself, and also produces a mismatch between
motor prediction and motor intention -- a comparison, but between different quantities than the
traditional model compares. They argue this fits the emerging evidence that hallucinations are
heterogeneous and phenomenologically distinct from ordinary inner speech, and they close by
suggesting the account may extend across a range of imagistic modalities.

## Why this is recorded as mixed rather than supports or weakens

Because it genuinely is, and the two directions bear on different parts of ARC-061.

The supporting direction is the family thesis. ARC-061's substantive assertion is not that any one
comparator exists -- it is that the *same* motif recurs across representational levels and modalities,
and that this recurrence reflects a general solution to the reafference problem rather than three
unrelated mechanism discoveries. Swiney and Sousa's closing move is that same generalisation,
proposed independently and from a clinical starting point: the account should extend across imagistic
modalities. That is ARC-061's structural claim arrived at by people who have never heard of it.

The weakening direction is the implementation. Their entire contribution is that the attenuate-then-
compare topology is wrong at this level. If inner speech is *derived from* the forward model rather
than *checked against* it, then the level does not instantiate reafference cancellation in the form
ARC-061 specifies -- forward model, comparator, authorship tag, in that order. Content generated by
the forward model cannot be independently compared with the forward model's prediction. A different
comparison is available in their model, between prediction and intention, but that is a different
architecture wearing the same word.

## The mapping is the loosest of the three ARC-061 entries

Level 3 in ARC-061 is MECH-094's hypothesis_tag write gate: simulation-mode content gets marked
non-real so it does not accumulate into the viability field. That is a write-gating function on
internally generated content. The sense of agency for inner speech is an attribution over a
linguistic-motor process, and REE has no language substrate at all -- a point the MECH-011 work
established independently only last week. So the two share a functional description, "mark internally
generated content as self-produced and not real", while sharing no substrate whatsoever. I have set
mapping fidelity at 0.6 to record that, and the entry should not be read as evidence about
MECH-094's implementation.

The clinical framing carries the usual hazard on top of that. The argument is built around a symptom
class, and inferring healthy architecture from psychopathology is the standing risk in this
literature -- the more so given the authors' own conclusion that hallucinations are heterogeneous and
distinct from ordinary inner speech, which is an argument against treating the pathological case as a
clean lesion study of the normal one.

## What it adds to the ARC-061 picture

Read against the other two entries, a pattern emerges that is more useful than any single verdict.
The family thesis keeps being reinvented -- Tajadura-Jiménez and Tsakiris arrive at interoceptive
source assignment, Swiney and Sousa propose extension across imagistic modalities, and both do so
without reference to each other. That convergence is real support for ARC-061's *architectural* level.
But at every specific level the implementation is contested: Christensen and Grünbaum say the motor
comparator is unnecessary, Swiney and Sousa say the inner-speech comparator has the signal flow
backwards. ARC-061 may well be right that the motif recurs, and wrong about what the motif is at each
site. That is a distinction the claim does not currently draw, and governance may want it drawn
before Level 2's SD-048 calibration work commits to a shape.
