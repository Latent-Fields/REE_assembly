# An interoceptive comparator, and what its miscalibration looks like (Tajadura-Jiménez & Tsakiris, 2014)

## What the paper did

The study sets two accounts of self-representation against each other. On the exteroceptive account,
distinguishing self from other is a multisensory integration problem dominated by visual feedback --
the enfacement and rubber-hand family of effects. On the interoceptive account, the sense of being a
particular material self rests on signals from inside the body. Tajadura-Jiménez and Tsakiris asked
what happens when you run both at once: do people who are better at reading their own internal state
resist having their self-representation rewritten by synchronous multisensory stimulation with
somebody else?

They do. Interoceptive sensitivity predicted the malleability of self-representation across three
response modalities -- behavioural, physiological and introspective -- and the effect was categorical
in shape rather than merely graded: only participants with *low* interoceptive sensitivity showed
changed self-other boundaries under the stimulation. The authors read this as interoceptive
predictive-coding models being used to monitor and assign the source of sensory input to self or
other, and point to insular cortex as the convergence zone where interoceptive feeling, multisensory
integration and self-processing meet.

## How this bears on ARC-061

ARC-061's structure is a family claim: the same reafference motif -- forward model, comparator,
authorship tag -- instantiated at motor, interoceptive and propositional levels. Level 2 is the
interoceptive one, and it is the level where REE's own position has been least settled; the claim's
notes record it as V3-tractable but conditional on SD-048, its calibration SD. What this study
supplies is exactly the shape of evidence a comparator architecture predicts at that level, and it
supplies it in the source's own vocabulary rather than through a strained translation. The
theoretical framing the authors chose *is* an interoceptive predictive-coding model performing source
assignment. I did not have to reinterpret them into REE terms; they were already there.

The more interesting detail is the direction of the moderation, because it is the failure signature
rather than the main effect that does the work. ARC-061's shared diagnostic signature across levels
is symmetric over- and under-attribution error under substrate miscalibration. Here, participants
whose interoceptive model was imprecise were the ones whose self-other boundary shifted -- external
sensory input got absorbed into the self-representation when the internal model could not adjudicate.
That is the over-attribution arm of the predicted signature, observed at Level 2, in a paradigm that
was not designed to test REE's claim. Independent arrival at a predicted signature is worth more than
independent arrival at a prediction.

## Limitations, and the reason I stopped at 0.72

The design is correlational. Interoceptive sensitivity is measured as a stable individual-differences
trait, not manipulated within participants, so the causal arrow from forward-model precision to
authorship stability is inferred rather than demonstrated. An alternative account is available and
this design does not exclude it: low interoceptive sensitivity might index a general susceptibility
to multisensory illusions -- suggestibility, attentional style, something else entirely -- with no
specific comparator deficit involved. Distinguishing those would need precision manipulated
experimentally, which is what a Level-2 analogue of the SD-047 noise sweep would actually be for.

There is also a constructs gap I want on the record rather than smoothed over. The outcome here is
identity and self-other boundary change under multisensory stimulation. REE's Level 2 is ARC-058's
HarmForwardTrunk tagging somatic and affective state change as self- or other-generated. These are
adjacent but not the same: the paper supports the existence of an interoceptive source-assignment
process, and does not validate REE's particular affective-prediction-error implementation of one.
Support for the architecture, then, not for the mechanism as built.
