# The comparator model, challenged where ARC-061 anchors it (Christensen & Grünbaum, 2018)

## What the paper did

This is the paper in today's pull that should make us least comfortable, which is why it is here.
Christensen and Grünbaum argue that the comparator model is not a satisfactory account of the sense
of agency, and they argue it on four fronts plus two experiments.

The first front is a distinction most of the literature does not draw: *narrow* sense of agency,
over one's own movements, versus *broad* sense of agency, over environmental events downstream of
those movements. Once that distinction is made, two things follow. The comparator model emerged from
studies of sensory consequences narrowly tied to movement -- so its evidential base is the narrow
case. But the standard experimental paradigms that are routinely cited in its support, they argue,
measure only the broad case. The model is being credited with evidence about a different thing than
it was built to explain.

The third front is parsimony. Narrow sense of agency *can* be explained by a comparator, but a motor
signal model -- authorship indicated by the presence of an efference signal, without any forward
prediction or comparison step -- explains it equally well and has less machinery. The fourth is
empirical: they report two experiments that attempted to induce illusory narrow sense of agency in
healthy participants and failed. Their inference is that these paradigms should have produced the
illusion if narrow agency really were comparator-generated.

## How this bears on ARC-061

ARC-061 is a family claim, and a family claim is only as secure as its anchor. The architecture
asserts one motif -- forward model, comparator, authorship tag -- recurring at motor, interoceptive
and propositional levels, with MECH-095's counterfactual gap as the Level 1 instance and the levels
above treated as the same solution applied at different timescales. This paper attacks the motif at
Level 1. If the motor comparator is under-evidenced or unnecessary, the generalisation across levels
loses the case it was generalising *from*, and what remains is three mechanisms that happen to share
a description rather than an architectural pattern that recurs for a reason.

I do not think the critique lands cleanly, and the reason is worth stating carefully rather than
used as a way of dismissing it. Christensen and Grünbaum's target is the *phenomenology* of agency --
what the subject reports feeling authorship over, which is why illusion-induction is their empirical
method. REE's Level 1 is a functional attribution mechanism: E2_harm_s produces a counterfactual gap,
a threshold licenses a self-attribution tag, and nothing in that requires the tag to be introspectively
accessible or to feel like anything. A critique built on failures to induce a *feeling* does not
straightforwardly reach a mechanism that makes no claim about feelings.

## What does transfer, and why I recorded this as weakens

The parsimony argument transfers, because it is about mechanism rather than phenomenology. If the
presence of an efference signal alone suffices to tag authorship, then a forward model plus a
comparison step is extra structure that has to earn its place. REE's version is not identical to the
one being criticised -- our Level 1 operates on counterfactual state-change gaps rather than on
sensory attenuation, which is a real difference -- but the question survives the translation: what
does the comparator buy that efference-presence does not? ARC-061 should be able to answer that, and
at present its answer is architectural symmetry, which is precisely the kind of answer this paper is
challenging.

The narrow/broad distinction transfers too, as a methodological warning. If human work has been
conflating agency-over-movement with agency-over-outcomes for twenty-five years, a REE diagnostic
reading authorship off environmental state change could inherit the same conflation without anyone
noticing.

I have recorded `weakens` at 0.68. The discount from higher is that two failed inductions are a null,
and the inference from that null rests on the authors' own auxiliary assumption that their paradigms
ought to have worked. But a well-argued parsimony challenge to the anchor level of a family claim is
substantive, and ARC-061's notes already record that Level 1 (MECH-095) sits at `substrate_ceiling`
with its ceiling re-pointed to multi-agent ecology in V5. A claim whose anchor level is both
substrate-ceilinged and facing a live parsimony objection in the literature is not close to
promotion, and this entry should be read as evidence for that reading rather than against it.
