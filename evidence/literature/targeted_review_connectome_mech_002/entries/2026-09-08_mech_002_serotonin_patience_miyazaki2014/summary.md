# Miyazaki et al. (2014) -- Optogenetic activation of dorsal raphe serotonin neurons enhances patience for future rewards

**Claim tested:** MECH-002 (Precision control analogues shape cognitive regimes)
**Direction:** supports | **Confidence:** 0.60

## What the paper did

Transgenic mice, channelrhodopsin in dorsal raphe serotonin neurons, a delayed-reward waiting task,
and stimulation delivered specifically *during* the wait rather than at outcome. Activating those
neurons made the mice wait longer -- it reduced premature cessation of waiting for conditioned cues
and food rewards. The authors ran the two controls that this inference actually needs: the effect was
not generalised behavioural inhibition (the animals were not simply frozen), and it was not the
stimulation being reinforcing in its own right (they were not waiting because waiting had been
rewarded). It stands as the first causal demonstration that dorsal raphe serotonin promotes patience
for delayed reward.

Causally, this is clean work. The question for us is what it can and cannot license.

## Findings relevant to MECH-002

MECH-002's serotonin-like regime is the anti-collapse one: it "reduces premature commitment by
limiting precision escalation" and "stabilises exploratory hippocampal rollouts across longer
horizons", with a phenomenology of patience and tolerance of ambiguity. Premature cessation of
waiting is about the cleanest behavioural instance of premature commitment available in an animal
model -- the mouse collapses onto the alternative trajectory (leave, go elsewhere) before the current
one has resolved. Suppressing that collapse by driving serotonin is the directional prediction the
REE knob makes, demonstrated causally rather than by correlation.

The controls are what make this worth including at all. Without the behavioural-inhibition control,
the deflationary reading -- serotonin suppresses movement, movement includes leaving, therefore the
animal stays -- would be entirely available and would have nothing to do with decision horizons.
Ruling it out is what converts a motor result into a candidate decision result.

## How this translates to REE, and where it strains

This is the weakest mapping in the directory, and I would rather say so directly than let the
optogenetic rigour of the source paper carry more inferential weight than it can.

The study measures behaviour. The claim is about a precision parameter. Wait duration is degenerate
with respect to internal mechanism: "limits precision escalation so the current trajectory does not
collapse early" predicts a longer wait, but so does "raises the subjective cost of abandoning", so
does "slows an internal decision timer", and so does "lowers the temporal discount rate". Those are
genuinely different architectures and this experiment does not separate them. What we have is
evidence that the *direction* of MECH-002's serotonin regime is right, not evidence for its stated
mechanism.

There is also a specific gap on the rollout clause. MECH-002 says serotonin stabilises *exploratory
rollouts* across longer horizons. A mouse waiting out an externally imposed delay is not performing
rollouts -- the horizon is imposed by the apparatus, not generated by the animal's planning depth.
So the part of the serotonin regime that is architecturally most load-bearing for REE (what happens
to imagined trajectories) is untouched here.

One further caveat that bears on implementation rather than on the direction of the claim. Later
work from the same group (Miyazaki et al., *Nature Communications*, 2018) found that the patience
effect is conditional on reward probability and timing uncertainty. That means the
serotonin-to-patience relation is not a fixed gain setting but a context-modulated one. An REE
implementation exposing the serotonin-like analogue as a context-independent scalar would be
simpler than the biology it is named after -- the same shape of concern raised in the Yu and Dayan
entry about treating the ACh and NE channels as independently settable.

## Confidence reasoning

Source quality 0.85 -- causal, cell-type specific, well-controlled, in a good journal. Mapping
fidelity 0.60, and this is the binding constraint: a degenerate behavioural readout standing in for
a precision parameter, in a task with no rollout analogue. Transfer risk 0.50, the highest in this
directory, because rodent behaviour is being carried all the way to an architectural parameter with
no measured intermediate quantity anywhere in between.

Aggregate 0.60. Held down by mapping fidelity rather than by source quality, per the skill's
instruction to weight mapping fidelity heavily for architectural claims. A strong paper doing modest
work for this particular claim.
