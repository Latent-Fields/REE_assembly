# Drive at the cue gates goal learning -- Berrios et al. 2021 (Nature)

According to PubMed. Source: Berrios J, Li C, Madara JC, Garfield AS, Steger JS, Krashes MJ, Lowell BB. "Food cue regulation of AGRP hunger neurons guides learning." *Nature* 595(7869):695-700, 2021. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03729-3)

**What the paper did.** Berrios et al. confirmed that AgRP activity returns toward baseline on three timescales -- rapid and transient at food cues, slower with gut nutrients, slowest with restored energy balance -- and then asked what the *rapid* cue-driven component is for. They identified the circuit that mediates it (a lateral-hypothalamic glutamatergic -> dorsomedial-hypothalamic GABAergic -> AgRP pathway) and selectively perturbed it. Interfering with the circuit impaired the cue-driven inhibition and, strikingly, greatly impaired *learning* of a sensory-cue-initiated food-acquisition task -- while leaving an identical water-acquisition task intact. They propose that the cue-driven reduction in aversive AgRP activity increases the incentive salience of food cues, and that this is what facilitates food-specific learning.

**Why it matters for MECH-306.** This is the most direct bridge in the cluster from the hunger-drive circuit to the *seeding-of-learning* function that MECH-306 is ultimately about. MECH-306 is not merely a claim about drive dynamics; it is a claim about whether the drive state present at the moment of benefit contact determines whether a benefit gets seeded into the downstream goal pipeline. Berrios et al. give causal evidence that the hunger-drive circuit's regulation at food cues is *necessary* for food-specific goal learning -- and is food-specific, not a general learning deficit. That is the biological analogue of "drive-state-at-contact gates whether a benefit acquires goal value," which is the premise effective_benefit seeding rests on.

**Where the mapping strains.** The causal variable here is the *fast* cue-driven component, whereas MECH-306 prescribes a *sustained* trace. The two are reconcilable -- the fast signal could initiate seeding and the slow trace could sustain it -- but this paper does not adjudicate which carries the seeding role, and indeed it hints that the fast signal, far from being merely a collapse to engineer around, may itself be the teaching signal. That is a genuine tension with MECH-306's framing that the instantaneous signal is the problem and the sustained trace is the solution; both may be load-bearing. The incentive-salience interpretation is also proposed rather than directly measured (the data show a learning deficit; salience is the inferred mechanism).

**Confidence.** 0.78. Source quality is very high (Nature, definitive circuit-level causal manipulation, clean food-vs-water dissociation). Mapping fidelity moderate-high for the drive-gates-goal-learning premise, reduced by the fast-versus-sustained-component tension and the interpretive incentive-salience step. Read alongside Chen 2016 and Su 2017, this paper extends the cluster from "a sustained signal exists" to "the drive signal at the cue is causally required for goal learning."
