# Sustained hunger signal -- Chen et al. 2016 (eLife)

According to PubMed. Source: Chen Y, Lin Y-C, Zimmerman CA, Essner RA, Knight ZA. "Hunger neurons drive feeding through a sustained, positive reinforcement signal." *eLife* 5:e18640, 2016. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.18640)

**What the paper did.** The authors confronted a genuine paradox in the hunger circuit. AgRP neurons in the arcuate nucleus are activated by energy deficit and, when stimulated, produce voracious feeding -- so they look like the substrate of the hunger drive. Yet in vivo recordings had shown that these same neurons are *inhibited within seconds* by the mere sight or smell of food, before a single calorie is consumed. If the drive signal collapses the instant food is detected, how can it sustain a meal? Chen et al. resolved this with optogenetics: a brief burst of AgRP activity delivered *before* food was available produced intense appetitive and consummatory behaviour that persisted for tens of minutes with no further neuron activation, and they showed this persistence is carried by a long-lasting potentiation of the rewarding properties of food. AgRP activity, they further demonstrated, is itself positively reinforcing.

**Why it matters for MECH-306.** This is the closest biological mirror I have found of the exact failure mode MECH-306 was registered to handle. The claim states that z_goal seeding cannot rely on *instantaneous* homeostatic drive because, in a well-fed agent, drive_level collapses at consumption and anti-correlates with benefit_exposure, so effective_benefit (drive x benefit_exposure) never crosses the seeding threshold -- the EXQ-536a observation. Chen et al. give us the biology of precisely that collapse-at-contact, and crucially they show the brain's own solution is to carry the signal *forward in time* rather than read it instantaneously. That is the empirical licence for REE's design choice to floor or slow-trace the drive at the SD-012 drive-multiplier site.

**How it translates, and where the mapping strains.** The honest caveat is that the biology places the sustained signal on the *reward* side -- a potentiation of how rewarding food is -- rather than on a sustained drive scalar. REE's slow-trace of drive_level (an EMA or a floor) is functionally analogous but architecturally distinct: it puts the memory in the drive term, whereas the mouse puts it in the benefit term. The paper therefore supports the *necessity* of a sustained signal at contact without dictating *where* REE should hold the trace. It also does not test a seeding-threshold concept; that remains REE's own construct.

**Confidence.** I set this at 0.85 -- the highest in the MECH-306 cluster. Source quality is very high (causal optogenetics, clean behavioural quantification, strong venue). Mapping fidelity is high because the collapse-then-sustain dynamic is the precise phenomenon the claim addresses. The discount is for the rodent-circuit-to-REE-scalar transfer and the trace-site mismatch (reward-side vs drive-side). This paper is best read as the anchor that converts MECH-306 from a plausible engineering fix into a biologically-motivated one.
