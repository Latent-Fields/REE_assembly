# Algorithmic Primitives and Compositional Geometry of Reasoning in Language Models (Lippl, McGee, Lopez, Pan, Zhang, Ziadi, Eberle & Momennejad, 2025, arXiv preprint)

This one is included for a specific, narrow purpose: the task brief for this pull explicitly asked whether "reusable computational motifs" is a convergent finding across independent lines of work, or something closer to a single paper's framing that the source thought over-generalized from citing two corroborating results. This paper is a genuinely independent test of that question, because it comes from a domain with no shared lineage to the other four entries in this pull -- not rodent cortex, not primate cortex, not even a biologically-inspired vanilla RNN, but the internal activations of large transformer-based language models solving abstract symbolic reasoning problems (Traveling Salesperson, 3SAT, AIME competition math, graph navigation).

The finding, at the level that matters for SD-101's general thesis, rhymes closely with the other four entries despite the completely different substrate: the authors extract "algorithmic primitive" vectors from clustered internal activations, using function-vector methods, and show these primitives are reusable and composable -- they combine via addition, subtraction, and scalar operations in activation space, and they transfer across both different reasoning tasks and different models (Phi-4, Phi-4-Reasoning, Llama-3-8B). The causal test is the strongest part of the design: injecting a primitive vector associated with Phi-4-Reasoning's more systematic verification and path-generation behavior into the base Phi-4 model induces behavioral hallmarks of the fine-tuned reasoning model. That is a genuine causal demonstration that the extracted primitives are doing real computational work, not just a post-hoc redescription of activity patterns.

What this paper does NOT do is speak to anything specific about MECH-503's proposed mechanism. There is no coalition, no topology instantiation, no analogue of MECH-481's typed-demand sequence in a transformer's forward pass -- "reuse" here means linear composability of activation vectors across abstract reasoning steps, which is a different sense of the word than the dynamic recruitment-and-binding MECH-503 is describing for REE's embodied control plane. The transfer from this paper to REE is therefore about as loose as transfers get in this pull: different substrate, different task class (abstract symbolic problems vs. embodied multi-modal cognition), and a different operationalization of "primitive." It is also, like the Shan/Minni/Duncker entry, an unreviewed preprint with modest citation traction so far.

The reason to include it anyway, at appropriately low confidence, is that its VALUE in this review is not as an individual strong anchor -- it is as an independence check. If "reusable computational structure separable from content" only ever showed up in closely related experimental lineages (say, everyone working from the same handful of task-trained-RNN traditions), that would be much weaker support for SD-101's claim that this is a general architectural principle worth representing explicitly, rather than a pattern specific to one class of system. Finding qualitatively the same phenomenon -- composable, reusable, causally-verified internal structure -- in a completely unrelated substrate (transformer latent reasoning) is exactly the kind of independent convergence the source thought's framing implicitly claims exists, and this pull's broader search bears that out, at least provisionally and at low individual confidence per source.

Confidence: 0.45, the lowest in this pull. Weighted down heavily for domain mismatch and preprint status, but retained specifically because its role here is corroborating the BREADTH of the convergent pattern the source thought asserts, not evidencing any specific REE mechanism.
