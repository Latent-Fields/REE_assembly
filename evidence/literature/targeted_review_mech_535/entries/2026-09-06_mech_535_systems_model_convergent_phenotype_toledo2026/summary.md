# Toledo et al. (2026) -- toward a systems model of catatonia

**Questions served:** Q1 (the GABA-A, NMDA and D2 routes, and where each puts the deficit) and Q2 (a review-level null on the representational locus).

## What the paper is

A 2026 narrative review from an interventional psychiatry group that asks whether catatonia is better understood as distributed psychomotor-network dysfunction than as a schizophrenia-linked syndrome. Its answer is yes: catatonia is transdiagnostic, "a final common phenotype of disturbed psychomotor regulation rather than the expression of a single disease process," internally heterogeneous along hypokinetic, hyperkinetic and aberrant-volitional dimensions that overlap within episodes. The best-supported circuit model implicates cortico-striatal-thalamic, cortico-cerebellar, orbitofrontal, cingulate and motor-premotor networks; neurochemically, interacting GABAergic, glutamatergic/NMDA and dopaminergic disturbances rather than one transmitter; immunologically, a real but subgroup-limited role (autoimmune encephalitis, inflammatory CNS disease); genetically, vulnerability without a syndrome-specific architecture. "No single biomarker or unified mechanism fully accounts for catatonia across contexts."

## What it says about the locus, route by route

The GABA-A route (benzodiazepine responsiveness, reduced GABA-A binding in OFC and parietal cortex) is read as loss of inhibitory control over psychomotor networks. The NMDA route (anti-NMDAR encephalitis presenting as malignant catatonia; amantadine and memantine as second-line agents) is read as glutamatergic excitation imbalance in the same networks. The dopaminergic route (D2 blockade precipitating catatonia and its overlap with neuroleptic malignant syndrome; clozapine and aripiprazole as partial treatments) is read as neuromodulatory gain on the selection loop. Every one of these is a statement about circuit excitability or gating; none is a statement about what the goal representation carries. The field's own dimensional taxonomy has a motor-output axis and a volitional axis and no representational axis. That is the Q2 null, stated at the level of a current review rather than of any single study.

## How this bears on the claims

For ARC-086 -- one degraded mechanism, several failure signatures; index the taxonomy on generative axes -- this is direct support at the level of the stance: the clinic has arrived at many-routes-one-phenotype from the other side. For MECH-535 it is *unknown*: the review is exactly the kind of structure a third route would slot into, and it documents that the slot is empty, but a review that says "many upstream liabilities converge" cannot be evidence for any particular new liability. The `evidence_direction_per_claim` field carries the split (ARC-086 supports, MECH-535 unknown).

## Caveats

Narrative, not systematic; from a treatment-oriented group; scored on the abstract because the full text was not accessible at pull time (published July 2026). The absence of a representational axis could be read either as an omission MECH-535 fills or as the field having found no need for one. Confidence 0.4 overall.
