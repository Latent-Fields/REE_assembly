# Bush, Fink, Petrides, Dowling and Francis (1996) -- the Bush-Francis Catatonia Rating Scale

**Question served:** Q1 (operational definition of ambitendency).

## What the paper is

The scale paper that gave catatonia its modern measurement: 23 items with operationalised definitions drawn from Kahlbaum (1874) onward, a 14-item screen, and reliability data (inter-rater 0.93 for total score, 88% mean item agreement across 44 simultaneous ratings of 28 cases). Seven per cent of consecutive admissions to an academic inpatient unit met criteria, which is the origin of the "moderately prevalent, distinct syndrome" framing. The companion paper (Bush et al. 1996, part II) defines the lorazepam test; that one is treated separately in this pull only through later cohort work, because the pull's interest in lorazepam is analogical.

## The item that matters here

Ambitendency is operationalised as the patient appearing motorically "stuck" in indecisive, hesitant movement, and is elicited by offering a handshake while instructing the patient not to shake hands: the patient repeatedly extends and withdraws the hand. Stupor (extreme hypoactivity, immobile, minimally responsive) and posturing/catalepsy (spontaneous maintenance of posture, including mundane postures) are separate items. This matters for MECH-535 because the claim asserts that stupor and ambitendency are two attractors of one map; the clinic scores them as independent items that nevertheless co-vary within a patient and within an episode, which is exactly the structure the claim predicts and exactly what an item-level scale can show.

## How this bears on MECH-535

The 978 log's dominant pattern -- one step toward the resource cell, one step away, repeated until the contamination rule kills the agent -- is a goal movement started, retracted and restarted. Because `resource_field_max` alternates high/low across the two cells, it is approach/withdraw relative to a goal rather than undirected dithering. That is the operational sign, so the phenotype-match half of the claim holds. The entry is scored *supports* for that half only, and carries `support_tag:phenotype_match_only` so that governance does not read it as mechanistic support. Nothing in a reliability study can speak to whether the sign arises from a gate, a harm lock or a direction-blind representation.

## Caveats

The scale defines the sign in a *conflict* context: the examiner's contradictory instruction supplies the two competing tendencies. In 978 there is no instruction, no examiner and no explicit conflict source; the competition is between what one proximity value tells a reflex to do on two adjacent cells. The resemblance is real at the level of the trajectory and absent at the level of what generates the two tendencies. Confidence 0.4: a trustworthy instrument, deliberately low mapping fidelity.
