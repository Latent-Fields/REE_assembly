# Rounis and Humphreys (2015) -- limb apraxia and the affordance competition hypothesis

**Question served:** Q2 (ideomotor apraxia: intention without correct movement -- representational or gating?).

## What the paper is

A conceptual review that takes limb apraxia -- Liepmann's disorder of higher-order motor control, classically split into ideational apraxia (a deficit in the *concept* of a movement, ventral stream) and ideomotor apraxia (a deficit in *implementation*, dorsal stream) -- and reads it through Cisek's (2007) affordance competition hypothesis. Because responses to objects are shaped by the actions they afford, the authors propose that "at least some aspects of apraxia may reflect an abnormal sensitivity to competition when multiple affordances are present and/or a poor ability to exert cognitive control over this competition."

## What it says about the locus

The deficit is placed in the competition among candidate actions and in the top-down control of that competition. It is a selection and control account. The ideomotor/ideational split is mapped onto implementation vs concept, a much coarser division than proximity vs direction within one representation, and a degraded goal or direction representation is not considered as a cause. Nothing about oscillation, hesitation or alternation appears.

## How this bears on MECH-535

The chip asked whether ideomotor apraxia, the neuropsychological syndrome most often glossed as "intention without the right movement," has been read as a representational deficit. The answer from this review is that the field reaches for competition and control -- MECH-202's territory in REE -- before it reaches for representation. That is the same explanatory habit the catatonia literature shows (Northoff's top-down modulation), now visible in an adjacent syndrome. Q2's null is therefore not peculiar to catatonia; it runs across the intention-without-direction family. The entry is recorded with `evidence_direction: unknown`: it neither supports nor weakens MECH-535, it documents where the neighbours have been filed.

## Caveats

A framing paper, not a test; it proposes a reading and reviews compatible evidence. Apraxia concerns gesture and object use after left-hemisphere stroke, far from a foraging gridworld. Confidence 0.3.
