# Padoa-Schioppa & Assad 2006 — OFC neurons encode economic value (option-specific)

**Claim grounded:** MECH-359 (candidate-differentiated affect vector)
**Direction:** supports · **Confidence:** 0.72
**Source:** Padoa-Schioppa C, Assad JA. *Nature* 441:223–6 (2006). According to PubMed; [DOI 10.1038/nature04676](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04676).

## What the paper did

Single-unit recordings in macaque orbitofrontal cortex during an economic-choice task (e.g. one raisin vs one piece of apple at varying ratios). The decisive result: OFC neurons encode the *value of the offered goods* — and they do so independently of which side the good appears on and independently of the motor response used to obtain it. A neuron tuned to "value of apple" fires for that value whether apple is on the left or the right, and whether the choice is made by a leftward or rightward saccade. The authors conclude that economic choice is, at the neural level, *choice between goods* rather than *choice between actions*, and that OFC is the natural candidate network for value assignment.

## Why it grounds MECH-359

MECH-359's load-bearing assertion is that *a scalar added equally to all K candidates cannot change an argmax — only a per-candidate-differentiated contribution can carve behaviour.* That is an architectural claim about representation format: value (or affect) must be keyed to the option, not pooled across options. Padoa-Schioppa & Assad is the canonical biological existence-proof that the primate brain does exactly this — it carries value in an *option-specific, response-abstracted* code. This is the neuroscience anchor the AE-9 readiness gate flagged MECH-359 as currently lacking (L1: "option-specific / per-candidate value coding"). The companion entry (Rich & Wallis 2016) supplies the *dynamic, ensemble-level* version of the same idea — OFC alternating between option-specific value states during deliberation.

## Limitations and the mapping caveat

Two honest gaps. First, OFC offer-value is a *single economic-value scalar per good* — it is not the multi-channel affect vector (curiosity / safety / harm-sensory / harm-affective / effort / relief / blocked-agency) that MECH-359 posits. This paper evidences the *per-candidate differentiation* axis but is silent on the *multi-channel decomposition* axis and on affect-vs-reward content. Second, the paper's headline — *value independent of action* — is the opposite emphasis to MECH-360's expression-as-action-geometry; OFC offer-value is deliberately action-abstracted, whereas REE binds its affect vector to E3 candidate *trajectories*. So the transfer is real but partial: it licenses "affect must be option-keyed," not "affect is multi-channel" and not "affect is action-bound."
