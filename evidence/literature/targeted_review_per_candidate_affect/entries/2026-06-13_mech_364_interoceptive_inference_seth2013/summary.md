# Seth 2013 — Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self

**Claim grounded:** MECH-364 (laughter discharges E3 conflict/constraint load; affect-as-control-precision lens)
**Direction:** supports (frame-level) · **Confidence:** 0.55
**Source:** Seth AK. *Trends Cogn Sci* 17(11):565–73 (2013). According to PubMed; [DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.007).

## What the paper did

Seth extends the predictive-coding / Bayesian-brain programme — until then applied mostly to exteroception and action — *inward*. His "interoceptive inference" proposal casts subjective feeling states (emotions) as arising from actively-inferred generative models of the *causes of interoceptive afferents*. On this view emotion is not a separate evaluative module bolted onto cognition; it is precision-weighted prediction-error minimisation over internal bodily signals. The framework generalises classical appraisal theory and is used to explain body ownership and conscious selfhood (and their breakdown in neuropsychiatric illness).

## Why it grounds MECH-364

The AE-9 readiness gate names L3 — "affect-as-control-precision (Seth 2013; Critchley & Garfinkel interoceptive inference) for MECH-364 conflict/constraint-load." MECH-364 holds that laughter discharges *accumulated E3 conflict/constraint load* and thereby marks a safe transition between control-plane regimes (ARC-016). The deep question that framing presupposes is: *what kind of variable is "load," biologically?* Seth's answer-shaped frame is the right one for REE: affective load is naturally represented as **precision / uncertainty over an internal generative model**, and a *drop* in that load — e.g. after iterated safe-confirmation — is a precision/regime update rather than a reward delivery. That is exactly the reading MECH-364 needs to stay faithful to the biology-before-formal-definitions rule: it lets "constraint-load discharge" be cashed out as a precision dynamic, not a hand-waved energy metaphor.

## Limitations and the mapping caveat

This is *frame-level, indirect* support and I have rated it conservatively. Seth addresses emotion-as-interoceptive-precision and selfhood; he says nothing about laughter, respiration, regime transitions, or load discharge specifically. The mapping is by analogy (precision dynamics), not direct evidence. And MECH-364 is itself explicitly flagged *compass-level* — its prompting source is a popular-science synthesis, not a primary study. Grounding it in a respected precision-inference review raises the respectability of the *frame* but cannot manufacture the missing primary evidence for the laughter-load-release mechanism. There is also a real over-claiming risk: affect-as-precision is broad enough to fit many REE affect claims, so it must not be read as *specific* evidence for MECH-364.
