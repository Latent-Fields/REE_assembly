# Critchley & Garfinkel 2017 — Interoception and emotion

**Claim grounded:** MECH-364 (laughter discharges E3 conflict/constraint load; affect-as-control-precision lens)

## What the paper did

This review consolidates the case that emotional feeling states arise from afferent visceral signalling — interoception — and its central representation. It surveys recent progress in conceptualising interoception and its neural underpinnings, and reports empirical findings that flesh out the predictive-coding picture: short-term, phasic interoceptive effects on neural and mental processing (including fear-specific cardiac effects, where the phase of the cardiac cycle modulates threat processing), the recognition of *dissociable psychological dimensions* of interoception, and interoceptive predictive-coding models that explain emotion and selfhood.

## Why it grounds MECH-364

This is the empirical-review counterpart to Seth 2013, supplying the second half of the AE-9 L3 anchor ("Critchley & Garfinkel interoceptive inference"). It reinforces the same lens — affect realised as precision-weighted inference over internal bodily state — but draws closer to data, with concrete demonstrations that internal-state signals causally modulate cognitive/emotional processing on short timescales. For MECH-364 this matters because the claim's "conflict/constraint *load*" must be a real, dynamically-varying internal variable; the interoceptive-precision literature shows such internal variables exist and shape processing, which is the substrate the load-discharge story rides on.

A secondary, lighter benefit: the paper's emphasis on *dissociable* psychological dimensions of interoception is mild independent support for ARC-088's distinctness principle — affect is realised across separable channels, not collapsed to a single scalar.

## Limitations and the mapping caveat

The same honest gap as Seth, and I have scored it marginally lower for partial redundancy. The empirical examples are weighted toward fear/anxiety and the cardiac cycle; there is *no* treatment of laughter, respiratory load discharge, or control-plane regime transitions — so the MECH-364-specific mechanism remains unevidenced by direct data. And because it shares Seth's predictive-coding frame, the two L3 entries are partially-redundant lenses rather than two independent confirmations; I have not treated them as additive. The affect-as-precision-generality caution applies: this grounds the *lens*, not the specificity of the laughter claim.
