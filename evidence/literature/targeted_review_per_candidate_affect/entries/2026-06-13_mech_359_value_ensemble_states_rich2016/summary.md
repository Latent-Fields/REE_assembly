# Rich & Wallis 2016 — Decoding subjective decisions from OFC (per-option value states)

**Claim grounded:** MECH-359 (candidate-differentiated affect vector)
**Direction:** supports · **Confidence:** 0.73
**Source:** Rich EL, Wallis JD. *Nat Neurosci* 19:973–80 (2016). According to PubMed; [DOI 10.1038/nn.4320](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4320).

## What the paper did

Rich & Wallis recorded OFC ensembles in macaques making subjective value-based choices and used state-space decoding to recover temporally precise neural states during *single* decisions. The key finding: within one choice, OFC does not hold a static value map — it *alternates* between distinct neural states, each associated with the value of one of the available options. Individual value-encoding neurons shift their activity as the network evaluates each alternative in turn. Critically, the *dynamics* of this alternation are behaviourally diagnostic: they predict whether the animal will decide quickly or vacillate between the two options.

## Why it grounds MECH-359 (and seeds MECH-360)

Where Padoa-Schioppa & Assad establishes that value is option-specific, Rich & Wallis shows the *dynamic, co-considered* form of it — the brain holds multiple option-keyed value representations for the same world-state and arbitrates among them over time. That is almost a verbatim biological instantiation of MECH-359's framing: "the same world-state can support different candidate rankings under different internal pressures." For REE this is the strongest available L1 anchor because it grounds *both* halves of the candidate-differentiated story at once:

1. **Differentiation** — distinct, co-active, option-keyed value states (not a pooled scalar).
2. **Deliberation dynamics** — the alternation/vacillation between candidate states is exactly the substrate MECH-360 later reads out as *expression* (oscillation between candidates, hesitation, decision latency). The affect vector that selects a trajectory leaves a visible deliberative signature, and Rich & Wallis shows that signature is real and decodable.

## Limitations and the mapping caveat

The decoded states still carry a *single subjective value per option*, not the multi-channel affect vector (curiosity/safety/harm/effort/relief/blocked-agency) that MECH-359 posits — the channel decomposition remains the unproven, genuinely-novel part of the claim. And the task is binary; REE evaluates many candidate trajectories, so the K-candidate generalisation is an extrapolation beyond two-option evidence. So this paper licenses "per-candidate, co-active, deliberative value states exist," not "affect is multi-channel."
