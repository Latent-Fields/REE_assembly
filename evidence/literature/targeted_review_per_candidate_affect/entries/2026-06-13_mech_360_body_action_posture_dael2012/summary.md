# Dael, Mortillaro & Scherer 2012 — Emotion expression in body action and posture

**Claim grounded:** MECH-360 (expression as emergent action geometry)
**Direction:** supports · **Confidence:** 0.60
**Source:** Dael N, Mortillaro M, Scherer KR. *Emotion* 12(5):1085–101 (2012). According to PubMed; [DOI 10.1037/a0025737](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0025737).

## What the paper did

Emotion-communication research had concentrated on the face and voice, leaving the body understudied and saddled with the old assumption that body movement only signals emotional *intensity*. Dael et al pushed back: using the Body Action and Posture (BAP) coding system, they had 10 professional actors portray 12 emotions and coded the resulting body movement and posture in fine detail. They then tested the patterns against basic-emotion theory, bidimensional theory, and componential appraisal theory. Result: body movement and posture carry *emotion-specific* information (not just intensity); several movement patterns systematically recur for specific emotions; and — the load-bearing finding for REE — most patterns are best explained as reflecting *functional components of emotion*, namely modes of **appraisal** and **action readiness**, rather than as arbitrary expressive codes.

## Why it grounds MECH-360

MECH-360 says expression *begins as emergent action geometry* — the visible style (hesitation, latency, approach angle, retreat, oscillation, avoidance margin, decommitment, repair-seeking) is the geometric residue of which trajectory the affect vector selected, not a separately-engineered output channel. Dael et al is the empirical demonstration that human emotional expression is in fact organised this way: as configured *action* tied to *action readiness*. Where Niv 2007 grounds the intensity axis from the motor-control side, Dael et al grounds the *shape/style* axis from the behavioural-measurement side, and explicitly ties it to action-readiness — which is precisely MECH-360's framing. Together the two L2 entries cover both axes the AE-9 gate flagged.

## Limitations and the mapping caveat

Three honest caveats. (1) The portrayals are *acted*, not spontaneous — posed display may overstate how lawful the geometry→emotion mapping is in naturally-emergent behaviour. (2) The mapping is *many-to-one*: most emotions were expressed by multiple body patterns, so reading state back from geometry is genuinely ambiguous — a useful caution for the V5 social-inference consumer that AE-3 explicitly defers (an observer inferring internal state from *how* an action occurred will face real ambiguity, not a clean code). (3) REE has no body; human full-body posture maps only metaphorically onto an agent's trajectory geometry. So this grounds the *principle* (expression = action-readiness geometry), not a specific REE read-out.
