# Counterfactual choice and learning in the frontopolar network (Boorman et al. 2011)

According to PubMed, Boorman, Behrens and Rushworth ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001093), *PLoS Biology* 9(6):e1001093, 2011) extended the 2009 result to a three-alternative task with hypothetical feedback. Using fMRI and a Bayesian RL model, they showed that a network centred on lateral frontopolar cortex -- with dorsomedial frontal cortex and posteromedial cortex -- does two things about options *not* taken. First, it encodes the reward evidence favouring the *best* counterfactual option at future decisions. Second, it carries a *counterfactual prediction error*: a learning signal for updating from hypothetical feedback about what the unchosen option would have yielded. Individual variation in these signals predicted both the propensity to switch to more profitable choices and the ability to learn from hypothetical feedback. Notably, the authors show these counterfactual prediction errors cannot be reduced to regret theory.

This paper carries the *licitness* half of the chip's scope. The question posed to MECH-486 is not only "can rejected candidates be retained?" but "is using them legitimate, or is it just confabulation with extra steps?" Boorman 2011 is direct evidence that retaining and *learning from* unchosen options is a normal, dedicated, adaptive function of a specific cortical network -- the brain routinely improves future behaviour from options it did not execute. That is the biological warrant for admitting MECH-486's retained candidates into later counterfactual evaluation, and it lines up with the earlier memory's LICIT category #4 (counterfactual exploration surfaces priors for future waking testing). The crucial qualifier, and the reason this does not collapse into a MECH-094 violation, is that legitimacy is conditional: the retained content must feed learning as a *prior for future testing*, bounded and provisional (the MECH-322 / Janner-MBPO template), not as a self-validating write into the world model.

It also speaks to Q-090 (the still-open admission-criterion question). The network encodes the *best* counterfactual option, not an undifferentiated bag of every rejected alternative. Retention in biology is selective. That favours a "same-scale-with-selectivity" reading of MECH-486 leg 3 over a hoard-everything design -- which matters because indiscriminate retention would enlarge exactly the confabulation surface the guard has to police.

Limitations and confidence. The signal is prospective and next-decision -- forward value-learning -- and the authors explicitly separate it from regret and retrospective evaluation. So it supports licitness-of-retain-and-learn and the selectivity of admission, but it does *not* directly evidence the retrospective self-attribution use MECH-486 leg 3 (and INV-012 leg 3) ultimately need; treating it as such would be an unlicensed transfer, which I flag rather than assume. Confidence 0.7.
