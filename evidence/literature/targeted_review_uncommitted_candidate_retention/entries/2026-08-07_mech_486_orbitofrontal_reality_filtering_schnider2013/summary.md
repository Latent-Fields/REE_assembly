# Orbitofrontal reality filtering (Schnider 2013)

According to PubMed, Armin Schnider's review "Orbitofrontal reality filtering" ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbeh.2013.00067), *Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience* 7:67, 2013) synthesises two decades of lesion and evoked-potential work into a single claim: beyond forming memories and using them to build a mental past and future, the brain runs a distinct, preconscious control process that decides whether an evoked memory *pertains to ongoing reality and can be acted upon*. Schnider locates this "reality filter" in the orbitofrontal cortex acting with reward-system structures, and argues it recruits a phylogenetically old faculty -- extinction -- to keep thought and behaviour in phase with the present. When it fails, patients confabulate and become disoriented: they act on memories that do not refer to now.

The reason this paper anchors the present lit-pull is that it names, in biological terms, exactly the danger the MECH-485 / INV-012 Leg-3 notes flag as load-bearing. The chip's scope is the *licitness and mechanism-shape* of retaining rejected/uncommitted candidate-trajectory content. Schnider's result says: retention alone is not the hard part; the hard part is keeping retained content *typed* as not-present, not-actionable. A store of considered-but-rejected E3 alternatives with no equivalent of the reality filter is, mechanistically, a confabulation generator -- the retained "what I could have done" leaks into the channels that represent "what is real," which is precisely the "hard-learned delusion" positive-feedback loop the user flagged as the central risk.

How this translates to REE. REE already has the enforcement primitive: MECH-094's simulation/imagination write-gate and its committed_vs_imagined tag. Schnider's work is the warrant for insisting that MECH-486's retained-alternative content ride *on top of* that tag -- retained candidates must be marked considered-but-not-real and denied the residue/valence/hippocampal write paths that committed action gets -- and that the check be intrinsic and early (Schnider's signature is a 200-300 ms frontal positivity), not a late readout filter after the content has already entered valuation. This is why Leg 3 is stated as a metacognitive requirement tied to INV-033 (second-order access to the status of one's own mental content), not a memory-engineering one.

Limitations and confidence. This is a review of human clinical and ERP data about a filter over *evoked memories*; it is not evidence about a buffer that stores *which* unchosen trajectory existed, nor about persistence past a decision tick. It tells us a provenance filter is necessary and roughly where and how fast biology runs it; it does not tell us the retention store's representational form. I therefore hold confidence at 0.7 -- high for "a dedicated provenance/reality tag is architecturally required," lower as a model of the retention mechanism proper, with a moderate clinical-to-substrate transfer discount. Note a distinct earlier Schnider (2003) entry already exists under `targeted_review_papez_circuit_write_gating` mapped to MECH-094; this 2013 review is used here for the different purpose of grounding MECH-486's guard requirement, not duplicated.
