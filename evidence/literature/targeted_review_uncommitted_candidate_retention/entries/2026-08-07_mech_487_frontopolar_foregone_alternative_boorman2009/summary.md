# Frontopolar cortex and the foregone alternative (Boorman et al. 2009)

According to PubMed, Boorman, Behrens, Woolrich and Rushworth ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2009.05.014), *Neuron* 62(5):733-43, 2009) used fMRI and a Bayesian reinforcement-learning model in a voluntary two-armed choice task where payoff magnitudes varied each trial and win-probabilities drifted slowly. Frontopolar cortex (FPC) tracked the *relative advantage in favour of switching to the foregone alternative* -- literally, how green the grass looked on the other side -- while ventromedial prefrontal cortex encoded the value of the currently chosen option. FPC functional connectivity changed at the moment subjects committed to switching, and individual differences in the FPC signal predicted how well individuals adapted their behaviour.

This is the single most direct neuroscience anchor for the retained-alternative question, and it plays a deliberately double-edged role here. On the supporting side, it establishes that representing the value of a rejected/unchosen course of action is a real, dedicated, adaptive computation -- and it is specifically the biological warrant for REE's own SD-033e / MECH-264 frontopolar counterfactual-value mechanism (`cfv_now`). The brain does keep a live handle on "the option I did not take."

On the gap-confirming side, the FPC signal is an *online relative-advantage magnitude*, computed at the point of comparison and switching. That is exactly the form MECH-264 already takes in REE: a transient scalar overwritten every tick. What Boorman 2009 does *not* show -- and this is the crux the MECH-485 and INV-012 Leg-3 notes identify -- is any persistence of *which* alternative existed, carried past the decision into a store usable for later retrospective counterfactual evaluation and responsibility attribution. So the paper simultaneously supports "a foregone-value substrate exists" and sharpens what is genuinely novel and unbuilt in MECH-487: identity-plus-persistence of the rejected candidate, not just its momentary value. I record the evidence_direction as **mixed** for exactly this reason.

Limitations and confidence. This is BOLD fMRI of a value/advantage magnitude in a simple two-alternative task; it addresses representing the foregone option's value at decision time, not storing its identity or trajectory. Reading persistence into it would over-transfer. Confidence 0.68 -- high source quality and a strong, replicated result that anchors MECH-264, but the mixed direction reflects that its main service to MECH-487 is to delimit what the existing substrate already does (transient scalar) versus what must still be built (retained identity past the tick).
