# Reality filtering dissociates from source monitoring (Bouzerda-Wahlen et al. 2015)

According to PubMed, Bouzerda-Wahlen, Nahum, Liverani, Guggisberg and Schnider ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/jocn_a_00686), *Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience* 27(1):164-74, 2015) juxtaposed two memory-control processes inside a single continuous-recognition task with high-resolution evoked potentials in healthy adults. *Context source monitoring* is remembering in what precise past context a piece of information was acquired. *Orbitofrontal reality filtering* is sensing whether an upcoming thought -- assembled from fragments of memory -- refers to present reality and can be acted upon. Both have been blamed for false memories and confabulation, and a prior literature had questioned whether they are really distinct. The study found they dissociate both behaviourally and electrophysiologically: reality filtering produced a frontal positivity and posterior-medial orbitofrontal activity at 200-300 ms, while source monitoring had no signature in that early window and instead induced a slower (from 400 ms), less accurate, prolonged frontal positivity.

Why it matters for this lit-pull. The MECH-487 gap is not only "retain the rejected candidate" but "retain it *without confusing it with reality*." This paper is the cleanest available evidence that the brain treats those as two different jobs: knowing *which* remembered content this is and where it came from (source/content monitoring) is a separate, slower, more error-prone mechanism from knowing *whether it is real and actionable now* (reality filtering). That directly informs the shape of a retention record. MECH-487 should hold two separable fields -- an actionability/reality tag (the MECH-094 committed_vs_imagined bit) and the which-alternative content -- rather than trying to infer actionability from the content. The dissociation is the design permission to keep "which alternative was considered" richly represented while independently and firmly stamping it "not executed, do not act."

The failure-mode reading is equally useful. Because reality filtering is fast and source monitoring slow and less accurate, a retention architecture that decides actionability by *reasoning over the retained content* (the source-monitoring route) inherits the higher error rate -- exactly the regime where confabulation appears when the fast filter is damaged. This supports building MECH-487's guard as a fast standalone gate, consistent with the MECH-322 template's fail-closed conditions, not a late semantic check.

Limitations and confidence. This is a scalp-ERP dissociation of two memory-control processes in healthy subjects, not a demonstration of a stored-alternative buffer, and the mapping from "two memory-control processes" to "two fields of a retained planning record" is architectural analogy rather than measured equivalence. I set confidence at 0.62 -- solid for the claim that actionability-tagging and content/source-tagging are separable and should be separately engineered, discounted for the indirect, healthy-human-to-substrate transfer.
