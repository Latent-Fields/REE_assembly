# When another agent stops being modelled as one (Harris & Fiske, 2006)

**Claim tested:** ARC-056 -- ethics-as-coherence: ethical trajectory selection as optimisation of shared temporal-depth coherence across self and represented others.
**Direction:** supports (violence-condition branch only) | **Confidence:** 0.42

## What the paper did

The stereotype content model sorts social groups along two dimensions, warmth and competence, producing four quadrants, and predicts that only the extreme low-warmth low-competence quadrant -- in the authors' materials, addicts and homeless people -- is dehumanized rather than merely disliked. Ten participants viewed 48 photographs of social groups spanning the four quadrants; twelve viewed objects. The prediction was made in advance of the scan, not fitted to it.

Medial prefrontal cortex, previously established as engaged by social cognition, responded to every social category except the extreme out-group, which instead especially engaged insula and amygdala in a pattern consistent with disgust. Objects, though rated with comparable emotions, did not engage mPFC either.

## Why this entry exists

ARC-056's violence condition has two branches, and the first is `beta_j` collapse: the other is no longer inferred to be self-like, its `D_{V,j}` term drops out of `J_ethical`, and trajectories that sacrifice it stop being penalised. Nothing else in this directory touches that branch at all. This paper is the closest empirical shadow of it I could find -- circumstances in which a being that *is* an agent stops being processed as one, as a property of the perceiver's model rather than of the target.

The object control is what makes the entry worth including rather than merely suggestive. Without it, the missing mPFC response could be low arousal or low interest. With it -- objects rated with comparable emotions, also failing to engage mPFC -- "processed less like an agent" becomes the natural description rather than one of several.

## Why the confidence is low, and what that low number is doing

I have set this at 0.42 and I want to be explicit that this entry marks a gap rather than filling one.

The central inference runs through a null. Absence of mPFC recruitment is read as absence of social-agent modelling, which is reverse inference on an absence, at n=10, with 2006-era acquisition and analysis standards. That would not be adequate evidence for a positive architectural claim standing alone, and it is not adequate here either.

The design contains no choice, no trajectory and no valuation -- only passive viewing. ARC-056's violence condition is a statement about what happens to *trajectory selection* when `beta_j` collapses. This study evidences a precondition of that condition, not the condition. A demonstration that the perceptual model changes is a long way from a demonstration that the objective function loses a term.

And the mapping from category to coefficient is loose in a way that matters for the claim specifically. This is a category effect: one quadrant differs from three. ARC-056 needs a weight that takes intermediate values, and it is the intermediate values that carry its non-degeneracy -- the Lockwood entry in this same directory is precisely about whether the effect is graded rather than switch-like. A study whose result is a categorical difference is, if anything, in mild tension with the graded picture the other entry supports, though the two are measuring different things and I would not press that.

Finally, the branch this speaks to is one half of a disjunction that ARC-056 asserts is *jointly exhaustive* -- `beta_j` collapse or self-preservation forcing a `D_{V,j}` sacrifice. Exhaustiveness is LEG 1's actual falsifier, and nothing here bears on it.

## Confidence reasoning

Source quality 0.55: theoretically motivated with an a-priori category prediction and a useful object control, but small samples, dated methods, and a central claim resting on a null activation. Mapping fidelity 0.50: the right target -- a branch with otherwise zero coverage -- but a category effect standing in for a continuous coefficient and perception standing in for selection. Transfer risk 0.55, the highest in this pull. The aggregate 0.42 is deliberately low, and governance should read this entry as evidence that the violence-condition branch is under-evidenced rather than as evidence that it holds.
