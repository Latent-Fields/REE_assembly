# Chan et al. (2013) -- The neural substrates of incongruity detection and resolution

**Claim tested:** MECH-110

## What the paper did

Chan and colleagues used an event-related fMRI design with three kinds of verbal material: unfunny items with no incongruity, nonsensical items with an incongruity that cannot be resolved, and funny items with an incongruity that can. Contrasting the conditions separates two stages of humour comprehension. Detecting an incongruity recruited the right middle temporal gyrus and right medial frontal gyrus; resolving it recruited the left superior frontal gyrus and left inferior parietal lobule. The authors propose a three-stage circuit: detection, resolution, then elaboration into amusement. Rating-based analyses converged on the same picture.

## Relevance to MECH-110

MECH-110 predicts that humour needs both an activation and a resolution. The nonsensical condition is close to what the claim's `what_would_answer` asks for as a control ("threat without resolution"): the incongruity is detected but never resolved, and humour does not follow. So there is neural evidence that the two stages dissociate and that the second is necessary. A follow-up ERP/sLORETA study on the same materials (Shibata et al. 2017, *Brain Research*) adds timing: detection shows up at P2 and resolution at P600, one ordered pass of a few hundred milliseconds.

## Where the mapping strains

The incongruity in a verbal joke is semantic, not threatening. Reading 'incongruity detection' as 'writing a harm-evaluation tag' is REE's step, not the paper's. The unresolved condition produced unfunniness, not distress, which is a weaker result than MECH-110's prediction that unresolved activation yields distress. Most importantly for the distinctive part of the claim, the timing data describe *one* detection-resolution pass per joke, taking roughly half a second. Laughter's expiratory pulses run at about 4-5 Hz (see Filippelli et al. 2001 in this directory), faster than the resolution component itself. That makes it hard to read each pulse as a fresh detection-resolution cycle at this cognitive level.

## Confidence

0.4. Solid neural evidence for the structural two-stage prediction; indirect on threat; no bearing, or a mildly adverse one, on per-exhalation cycling.
