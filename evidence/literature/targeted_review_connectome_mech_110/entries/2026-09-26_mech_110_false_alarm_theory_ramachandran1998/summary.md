# Ramachandran (1998) -- The false alarm theory of humour, laughter and smiling

**Claim tested:** MECH-110

## What the paper argues

Ramachandran's short hypothesis paper proposes that laughter follows a build-up of expectation (a model) and then a sudden twist that forces a change of model -- but only when the new model is non-threatening, so that expectation deflates. The explosive vocalisation, he suggests, tells conspecifics that there has been a false alarm, one they need not orient to. The same logic is offered for tickling (a menacing approach followed by a light, harmless touch) and for the smile (a threat display aborted halfway on recognising kin). The most striking datum is clinical: patients with insular damage and pain asymbolia sometimes giggle in response to pain. They can still detect the danger, but it no longer hurts, so both conditions for laughter are met.

## Relevance to MECH-110

This is the closest intellectual ancestor of the claim. Threat activation followed by safe resolution, with laughter as its expression, is exactly MECH-110's core structure, and the social 'false alarm' broadcast anticipates the V5-scoped extension in which group laughter serves as a joint 'nothing to fear' signal. The pain-asymbolia giggle is an intriguing natural experiment: the threat signal is present and the aversive consequence is absent, which is what the claim would call a written tag that the sweep reliably fails to confirm.

## Where the mapping strains

Ramachandran's unit of analysis is the *event*: one false alarm, one laugh. MECH-110 makes a much stronger and stranger claim, that each forced exhalation within a laugh is its own tag-write/tag-clear cycle, and that the pleasure lies in the kinetic repetition. Nothing in this paper supports that per-pulse reading. The clinical observation is anecdotal, and a disinhibition account would explain it at least as well.

## Confidence

High mapping fidelity for the structural logic, low source quality, so 0.35 overall. This entry documents that MECH-110's threat/safe-resolution structure is an established theoretical position, not an REE invention, and that the per-exhalation cycling is where REE departs from it.
