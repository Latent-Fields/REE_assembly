# A limbic circuitry involved in emotional stress-induced grooming (Mu et al. 2020)

**Claim(s):** SD-060 (non-terminal drive register)
**Direction:** supports · **Confidence:** 0.62
**Source:** Mu, Geng, Rong, Peng, Wang, Geng, Qian, Yung & Ke (2020), *Nature Communications* 11:2261. According to PubMed, [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-020-16203-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16203-x).

## What the paper did

The entry's job is to test the project rule behind DRV-3: that non-feeding drives -- grooming, play, exploration -- are genuine homeostatically-regulated systems rather than mere deficit-reduction, and so deserve first-class status in a drive register (SD-060). Mu and colleagues give that premise a clean circuit-level instance. They studied self-grooming, a repetitive displacement behaviour that ethologists have long believed contributes to post-stress de-arousal with adaptive value, and they asked what drives it. Using optogenetics and fibre photometry in rats, they traced a di-synaptic limbic circuit -- ventral subiculum to ventral lateral septum (LSv) to the tuberal nucleus of the lateral hypothalamus -- and showed that activating it triggers delayed but robust grooming whose patterns closely resemble those evoked by genuine emotional stress. LSv activity peaks *before* grooming onset, and inhibiting the circuit suppresses stress-triggered grooming.

## Key findings relevant to the claim

Three features matter for SD-060. First, grooming here is explicitly framed as serving **behavioural homeostasis**: it returns the animal toward an arousal/affective set-point after a perturbation, which is the regulatory logic SD-060 wants to attribute to non-feeding needs. Second, the behaviour carries **positive affective valence** -- it is not just a reflexive twitch but a rewarding, sought de-arousal, which is what makes it drive-like rather than purely stimulus-driven. Third, it has a **dedicated circuit** with anticipatory activity (LSv peaking before onset), exactly the kind of own-integrator architecture SD-060 posits for each non-terminal axis rather than collapsing everything into the SD-012 energy scalar. In short: here is a non-feeding behaviour that is regulated, valenced, and circuit-specific -- a worked example of the homeostatic-style integrator SD-060 proposes.

## How it translates to REE

SD-060 asks REE to carry needs beyond hunger and thirst -- exploration, play, social, learning, grooming, rest -- as first-class drive axes, each with its own homeostatic-style integrator. Mu et al. ground the *kind* of object SD-060 wants by giving a fully-mechanised instance of one axis. The regulated variable is not a consumed resource but an affective/arousal state, which is precisely the point the L-non-terminal-needs rule insists on: these drives are homeostatic in the control-theoretic sense (regulate a variable to a set-point) without being deficit-reduction in the metabolic sense. This is the biology-before-formal-definitions grounding the DRV-3 node was opened to obtain -- it tells REE that when it builds a non-energy drive axis, "homeostatic integrator" is a biologically honest framing, not a metaphor stretched past breaking.

## Limitations and caveats

The load-bearing caveat is the inductive gap. This paper evidences *grooming* as homeostatically regulated; it says nothing directly about whether exploration, play, social contact, or learning are each independently integrated, nor whether they share grooming's architecture. Generalising from one axis to the whole SD-060 register is a leap this single circuit does not license -- which is why the confidence sits at 0.62 and the mapping fidelity at 0.60. There is also a category subtlety with teeth: grooming here is triggered by an *aversive* emotional stressor and functions as de-arousal, so it is evidence for a comfort/de-arousal need. SD-060 also enumerates *appetitive* non-terminal drives -- curiosity, play -- whose regulation may follow a different logic (drive *toward* stimulation rather than *away* from arousal). The de-arousal homeostasis demonstrated here should not be assumed to transfer wholesale to the appetitive axes; those likely need their own grounding (curiosity is already partly covered under MECH-111, and play under ARC-073).
