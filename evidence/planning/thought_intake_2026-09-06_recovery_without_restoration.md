## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> the capacity to preserve or recover a useful function by reorganizing causal responsibility across
> internal processes rather than by returning the system to one canonical implementation.

And the reframing it licenses:

> When a pathway is weakened, the first question should no longer be only *how do we fix the failed
> component?* It should also be: **What did the organism try to become in order to keep functioning?**

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "**pathology may arise not only because the original mechanism is damaged, but because an adaptation to that damage becomes over-stabilized.**"
- "A global parameter increase is weak evidence. Selective amplification of a process specifically when it contributes to the recovered behaviour is much stronger evidence of adaptive control."
- "Similar surface behaviour alone cannot establish multiple realizability."
- "**Perturb -> observe spontaneous compensation -> identify the substitute representation or route -> selectively amplify it -> test causal dependence and generalization -> over-amplify to locate the adaptive-to-maladaptive boundary.**"
- The source's own limit, restated: "The REE mapping below is therefore a derived hypothesis, not a claim about what the paper itself demonstrates."

Both `candidate` / `substrate_conditional` / `registered_utc: 2026-09-08`.

**Why two claims and not one:** they are falsified by opposite results. ARC-140 is weakened if
compensation never happens; MECH-541 is weakened if compensation happens but never over-stabilises. The
amplification sweep testing MECH-541 presupposes ARC-140 already holds. A merged claim could not be
cleanly falsified in either direction.
