## Trigger

The user proposed that brain-wide coordination involving travelling waves, phase/cross-frequency structure, thalamocortical systems, and possibly ephaptic coupling may be important for REE, particularly for escaping monostrategy and for selecting among competing perceptions, attention states, memories, and processing routes.

A literature/repository synthesis suggests the strongest version of the idea is computational rather than substrate-specific:

> REE may need an endogenous mechanism that changes the effective causal topology among already-present representations and consumers.

Anaesthesia is relevant because multiple anaesthetic classes converge on altered population dynamics, reduced flexible coordination, impaired information transfer, and reduced metastable repertoire without simply deleting all local representations.

## Mutual-legibility / consensus bridge

Javadzadeh et al. (2026) provide the missing bridge between the mutual-legibility programme and the monostrategy/dynamic-coordination programme. Reciprocal V1-LM coupling generated slower congruent than incongruent joint modes, suggesting that once systems can exchange appropriately matched information, reciprocal dynamics can make some joint states more persistent than others.

- Javadzadeh M et al. 2026, *Nature Neuroscience*, DOI 10.1038/s41593-026-02437-3 — reciprocal connections dynamically build consensus between neocortical areas.

REE already owns this result under `docs/thoughts/2026-09-19_dynamic_consensus_post_translation_integration.md` and `docs/architecture/dynamic_consensus_integration.md`. The new intake synthesis is that this mechanism may sit **between mutual legibility and strategy expression**.

Candidate chain:

```
mutual legibility
-> dynamic routing / causal access
-> selective reciprocal coupling
-> defeasible consensus
-> behavioural authority / strategy expression
```

This adds a third monostrategy class:

- **Type A repertoire absence** — alternative never learned/represented;
- **Type B repertoire-access collapse** — alternative exists but cannot reach the relevant consumer;
- **Type C consensus collapse** — alternative reaches the system but recurrent distributed dynamics pull the organism back to the same joint attractor.

The coordination target should therefore be decomposed into **routing, reconciliation, and escape**.

## Candidate experimental gate

Do **not** run a dynamic-coordination/phase/ephaptic rescue assay until known Type-A monostrategy causes are sufficiently repaired:

- proposer diversity non-degenerate;
- at least two useful alternatives present/decodable;
- E3 scoring non-flat;
- reversal ecology requires strategy switching;
- stable baseline competence exists.

Then compare:

1. static routing;
2. matched stochastic routing;
3. open-loop periodic/phase routing;
4. endogenous state-dependent dynamic routing;
5. closed-loop activity↔coordination feedback only if arm 4 is insufficient.

Primary questions:

> Does endogenous dynamic coordination allow already-present alternative competence to gain causal authority when context changes?

> Once mutually legible alternatives have access, can the coupled system form a new evidence-supported consensus and escape an obsolete one rather than returning automatically to the historical attractor?

Promotion trigger for phase/dynamic-coordination substrate should require:

- latent alternatives present;
- static routing failure;
- no rescue from random/periodic matched controls;
- rescue from endogenous state-dependent routing;
- causal loss of rescue under routing shuffle/intervention;
- replication across seeds/ecology;
- no new privileged content entering through the coordinator;
- for the consensus branch, an interpretable agreement/disagreement timescale or perturbation-recovery signature that depends on selective reciprocal coupling and remains defeasible under strong counterevidence.

### C. Key formulations (verbatim)

> REE may require ... an endogenous mechanism that continually reconstitutes its own effective causal
> topology: which representations can influence which consumers, at what moment, with what strength,
> and for how long.

> the physical/architectural graph can remain largely intact while the set of dynamically accessible
> functional configurations narrows.

> decodable -> legible -> routed -> reciprocally supportable -> causally authoritative

> The target is therefore not maximum consensus. It is defeasible, context-sensitive consensus.

> A monostrategy organism may lack strategies, lack access to strategies, or possess alternatives that
> cannot become a stable distributed consensus.

> the organism can hear the alternative, but cannot reorganise itself around it.

> the same represented candidate becomes behaviourally effective or ineffective depending on the
> endogenous coordination state, with no change in the candidate's semantic content.

> Dynamic coordination is justified only when useful alternatives already exist and simpler
> diversity/translation fixes cannot make them acquire and retain context-appropriate distributed
> authority.
