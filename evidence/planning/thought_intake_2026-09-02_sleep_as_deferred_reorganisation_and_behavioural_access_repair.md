## Verbatim prompt (core proposal)

> REE may need a protected offline mode in which four different kinds of work can occur:
> 1. **retention and interference repair** ... 2. **representational reorganisation** ...
> 3. **recalibration and attribution repair** ... and 4. **behavioural-access repair** --
> restore the route by which retained predictive or procedural knowledge can again influence
> proposal, selection, and action.
>
> These operations share a reason to be offline ... They do **not** share the same write target,
> provenance rule, or success measure.
>
> The most important refinement is therefore:
> **Sleep may repair access to behavioural authority, but sleep must not confer authority directly.**

The thought is explicitly hypothesis-generating and self-limiting: "This does not presently justify a
new sleep module. It more likely justifies an intake that refines experiment design, reconciles
existing sleep claims, and asks whether behavioural-access repair is already owned by the
ARC-130/131 causal-reach framework plus MECH-094." This intake takes that instruction literally --
most of the content is already owned, and the yield is deliberately small.

Six external papers are load-bearing for the thought's argument (Solomon 2026, Bazhenov 2026,
Nijjer 2026, Chen 2026, Calcagno 2026, Oyarzun 2017) and eight more are supporting. **None was

## Key formulations (verbatim, load-bearing)

> Sleep may repair access to behavioural authority, but sleep must not confer authority directly.

> Offline processing may repair access to competition; waking operation determines authority.

> All four may use replay. Replay is a transport and sampling operation, not a sufficient
> description of the transformation.

> A system may retain the correct prediction yet fail at any downstream edge.

> Offline scheduling and offline competence develop together.

> A cold-start agent that fires many empty or degenerate sleep cycles has not demonstrated
> developmental consolidation. Longer observation under `no_grad()` is also not development if no
> relevant learning update occurs.

> rehearsing retrieval is not writing a new episodic fact; rehearsing a ranking is not receiving
> new reward; adjusting an eligibility mapping is not minting a commitment; testing a
> counterfactual is not updating residue from harm that never occurred; and preserving a skill is
> not proof that the skill remains appropriate in the current ecology.

> Sleep is not where REE escapes reality; it is where the organism can temporarily stop acting in
> order to reorganise what action has taught it.
