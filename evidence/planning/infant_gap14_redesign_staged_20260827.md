## 1. Why this cannot be another 591-letter

Per EXQ versioning policy, a new letter is for a bug fix to an unchanged scientific question; a new number is for a changed mechanism or substantially different design. Everything below changes the mechanism under test (the training regime the curriculum runs on, and possibly which scheduler object the "curriculum" arm even is), not just a parameter. **This proposal recommends a new EXQ number when it is eventually authored, not a `591g` letter.**

## 2. What is already resolved — do not re-derive

- **(a) MECH-307 default-value recalibration** — cleared 2026-05-15 (V3-EXQ-540e).
- **(c-2) Phase 0→1 gate over-permissiveness** — resolved end-to-end and split into its own `done` node, `infant_substrate:GAP-14-c2`. The 591d→591e→591f lineage identified a **crossing-count** criterion (sustained-level crossings of a lowered spike bar, `h_pos_frac_of_max=0.20` ≈ raw 0.994, `crossing_count_min=3`) that discriminates genuine explorers (seeds 42/43/44, random-policy `h_pos_mean` 0.562/0.323/0.842) from the seed-45 false-advancer (0.140), and it is **already wired** into the live `InfantCurriculumScheduler` as an opt-in kwarg pair: `phase_0to1_use_crossing_count=True`, `phase_0to1_crossing_count_min=3` (default `False`/legacy single-episode spike gate for backward compatibility). **Any redesign turns this flag ON. Do not re-open the gate-criterion question.**
- **The 667 magnitude-sweep lever is exhausted and re-deriving it is foreclosed by a code trace, not just an experiment.** `governance_2026_07_21b`'s read-only trace of `ree-v3` HEAD establishes that the two knob families V3-EXQ-667 swept (`MECH-313 noise_floor_alpha`, `MECH-314 curiosity_{novelty,uncertainty,learning_progress}_weight`, jointly scaled 1×–8×) cannot move the committed E3 selection point under 667's own config: `noise_floor_alpha` reaches only the *uncommitted* sampling branch (the committed branch is a temperature-invariant `argmin`); the curiosity weights *are* on the committed path but 314b/314c are uniform (argmin-invariant) broadcasts and the elementwise clamp at `curiosity_bias_scale=0.1` saturates the vector to uniform once `curiosity_uncertainty_weight*unc + curiosity_lp_weight*lp >= 0.1`, annihilating the one per-candidate term (314a) that could matter. **A `667a` "same design, lift ON" re-run is not just unqueued — it is proven-dead by this trace.** Any future exploration-magnitude lever must sweep `use_gap_scaled_commit_temperature` (the one committed-path temperature consumer), the 314a weight *alone* with 314b/314c OFF, and `curiosity_bias_scale` jointly (or the clamp eats the sweep) — but see §5 for why I do not recommend chasing this axis at all in the redesign.

## 3. The real gap: prereq (b) "z_goal collapse cleared" is true in the wrong apparatus

This is the load-bearing finding of this scoping pass, and I did not find it stated anywhere in the existing governance notes — it appears to have gone unnoticed, not deliberately deferred.

The GAP-14 `blocked_by` prose treats prereq (b) — "goal-pipeline training regime produces non-trivial z_goal in default config" — as **cleared 2026-06-10 via V3-EXQ-603n PASS**, citing all four load-bearing gates (`G0_stage0_positive_control`, `G1_p1_survival`, `G2_p2_contact`, `G3_p2_zgoal_consumption`) as non-degenerate and passing.

That is accurate, but **V3-EXQ-603n does not run `InfantCurriculumScheduler`** — the object `infant_substrate:GAP-14`/`EXQ-ISEF-005` actually needs to exercise. It runs a *different* staged-training harness, `scaffolded_sd054_onboarding` (`ScaffoldedSD054OnboardingConfig`), with its own stage names (`Stage0 → Stage0b → P0 → Stage-H → P1 → P2`), its own short episode budgets (`P0_BUDGET=100`, `P1_BUDGET=50`, `P2_BUDGET=15` — on the order of 200 total episodes), and — critically — an explicit **harm/survival co-training warmup** (`scaffold_train_harm_pathway=True`, a "Stage-H-survival-competent policy" reaching P2 alive) that `InfantCurriculumScheduler`'s Phase 0 ("babbling," ep 0–99, plain random-policy stepping, no harm/survival training of any kind) does not have and was never designed to have.

Consequence: **a straight re-run of `InfantCurriculumScheduler` with the crossing-count gate turned on — even seeded past Phase 0 by four genuinely-exploring seeds — inherits none of 603n's z_goal-forming scaffold.** Nothing in 603n's PASS licenses the expectation that `InfantCurriculumScheduler`'s Phase 1 exit gate (`z_goal.norm() >= Z_GOAL_THRESHOLD = 0.30`, `infant_curriculum.py`) will now clear. The most likely outcome of a naive "just flip the crossing-count flag and re-run 591" is a **second, still-vacuous** z_goal≈0 null — for a reason that has nothing to do with exploration strength (c-1) and everything to do with a scaffold that was validated on a different harness and never ported.

**This is the actual re-operationalization question**, and it has two live resolutions, both requiring a short design/engineering decision before any experiment is authored — I recommend flagging both to `/governance` rather than pre-deciding:

- **(i) Port the warmup into `InfantCurriculumScheduler`.** Compose a `scaffold_train_harm_pathway`-style P0/Stage-H harm-and-survival co-training step into (or immediately preceding) `InfantCurriculumScheduler`'s Phase 0, so the curriculum-vs-flat contrast still runs on the scheduler under test, now with a working goal-pipeline underneath it. Smaller surgery; preserves the existing phase semantics and the 27+8 contract suite that already pins Phase 0→1 behaviour. Risk: the port itself needs validating (does the ported warmup reproduce 603n's G3 pass *inside* the different phase timing/budget of `InfantCurriculumScheduler`?) — that is a `/implement-substrate`-shaped spike, not a given.
- **(ii) Re-home the comparison on `scaffolded_sd054_onboarding`'s own stages.** Retire `InfantCurriculumScheduler` for this specific comparison and define "curriculum" vs "flat" as phased-vs-constant environmental-feature introduction *within* the already-z_goal-validated scaffold's stage boundaries (Stage0→P0→Stage-H→P1→P2). Larger surgery (a materially different curriculum object than the one GAP-9 built and GAP-14's history has been chasing), but inherits 603n's z_goal result directly with no porting risk.

I lean toward (i) — it keeps EXQ-ISEF-005 testing the artifact the plan has always meant it to test (`InfantCurriculumScheduler`, GAP-9's deliverable) — but this is a genuine architectural fork and I am flagging it rather than deciding it unilaterally.

## 4. Option (b) from the brief — training a "competent" policy first — is a category error, not a fix

The brief asked me to consider running the comparison on a trained/competent policy rather than random-policy, and to say whether the redesign should consume the MECH-457 competence-bootstrap-explorer outcome. Having read the Infant Stage's own architectural spec, I recommend against this coupling, for three independent reasons:

1. **It contradicts the Infant Stage's own design.** `developmental_curriculum.md`'s Infant Stage table is explicit: `commit_threshold` very high / `sweep_amplitude=1.0` ("near-random exploration, BreathOscillator always in sweep"), `E3 planning disabled or near-random`, `novelty_bonus_weight` maximised. The infant stage's entire purpose (ARC-046/INV-043) is to populate substrate — residue geography, z_goal seeds from *accidental* benefit contact, an E1 prediction model — through **undirected** exploration, not to demonstrate task competence. A "trained competent forager" is a childhood/adulthood construct. Testing GAP-14's curriculum-vs-flat question on a competence-optimized policy would be testing a different developmental stage than the one the claim (ARC-046) and the node are about.
2. **The dependency it would create is itself unresolved and far from GAP-14's question.** `mech457_competence_bootstrap_explorer` is `status: blocked_pending_discrimination` as of this session (checked live, not from a stale note) — four axes (capacity, drive-schedule, reward-coupling, credit-horizon) are eliminated, and it is gated on an unstarted `GOV-FANOUT-1` discrimination portfolio (H-bc-prior vs H-approach-primitive) that has not been queued. Even the best currently-landed "trained all-ON" checkpoint (V3-EXQ-728b, the WS-3 capability-yardstick point) sits **at the competence floor** for `foraging` (−0.005 normalized) and `goal_reach` (0.0 normalized) — "survives but does not forage." Coupling GAP-14 to this would tie a tractable re-operationalization to a genuinely open, deeply-blocked frontier for no scientific necessity.
3. **It may work against the criterion, not for it — untested, but worth stating as a risk.** `H_pos` (the C2 criterion, and the Phase 0→1 exit signal) is a Shannon entropy over grid-cell visitation — it rewards *broad, undirected coverage*. A policy trained toward foraging competence is expected to *narrow* its trajectories toward known/likely resource locations, which plausibly **lowers** positional entropy relative to near-random babbling, not raises it. I have not run anything to confirm this, and flag it only as a reason not to assume "more competent" means "clears the gate more easily" — the opposite could be true.

**Recommendation: do not gate the redesign on MECH-457 or any competence-training result.** The z_goal-formation problem (§3) has an already-validated, non-MECH-457 fix path (603n's scaffold); use that instead.

## 5. Seed-46-style exploration collapse (c-1): stop treating it as a magnitude problem, check it against the acceptance criterion's own tolerance first

667's magnitude sweep is exhausted and proven dead by the code trace (§2) — no further same-shape sweep is owed, and I am not proposing one. But before authoring any *different* exploration-strength lever, there is a cheap check nobody has run: **V3-EXQ-591's own PASS criterion already tolerates one seed failing.**

> "PASS criterion: ARM_2 ≥ 6/7 in ≥ 4/5 seeds AND (ARM_0 OR ARM_1) ≤ 4/7 in ≥ 3/5 seeds." (`failure_autopsy_V3-EXQ-591_2026-05-27.md` §1)

Seed 46 has never once reached genuine-explorer status across 591/591b/591c/667's 8× joint sweep — but every one of those runs tested (c-1) in isolation, against gates and training regimes that are since-superseded (the old single-episode spike gate, or no z_goal-forming scaffold at all). Nobody has yet run seed 46 against the **combination** the redesign in §3 would produce: the crossing-count gate (c-2, fixed) **and** a working goal pipeline (whichever of §3(i)/(ii) is chosen). It is entirely possible seed 46 keeps collapsing under that combination too, in which case it may simply *be* the one-of-five the acceptance criterion was written to absorb — not a defect requiring a further substrate lever. Recommend testing this directly, cheaply, as part of the redesigned run's own analysis (does {42,43,44} + at least one of {45,46} clear Phase 0→1 genuinely?) rather than pre-committing to "seed 46 must be fixed before the comparison can run."

## 6. The degenerate advisory criteria need a second look once z_goal is non-trivial

591's autopsy already found C3 (`residue_coverage_pct > 0.15`) trivially saturating to 1.0 in **every** arm regardless of behaviour (uninformative by construction in this substrate), and C5/C6/C7 (`harm_benefit_ratio`, `post_sleep_z_goal_retention`, `traj_pairwise_cosine_mean`) emitting −1.0 "no data" sentinels in every seed of every arm because their preconditions never fired (C6's retention path in particular is gated on `final_z_goal_norm > 0.1`, which requires exactly the fix in §3). If §3 succeeds, C6 becomes newly measurable for the first time in this node's history and should be **re-instrumented and checked for non-degeneracy before being counted**, not silently folded back into the 7-criterion tally on the assumption it now works. This is the same "vacuous readout" pathology `governance_2026_07_18`'s note already flagged for the H_pos anchor (criterion A: re-specify as a per-seed fraction statistic, not a max; criterion B: fix the seed-45 false-advancer's zero-margin threshold) — that note's instruction to the reviving session stands unchanged and should be applied in the same authoring pass as this redesign, not as a separate later pass.

## 7. What I recommend the eventual `/queue-experiment` session build (proposal only — nothing queued)

A single new-numbered EXQ (not a 591-letter), design sketch:

- **Arms:** retain the original 3-arm shape (`ARM_0_ctrl_a` flat-all-on, `ARM_1_ctrl_b` flat-all-off, `ARM_2_curriculum`) so the comparison stays interpretable against the original claim framing (ARC-046), unless the §3(ii) re-homing fork is chosen, in which case the "flat" arms need re-defining against the scaffold's own stage boundaries.
- **Seeds:** keep 42–46 (do not drop seed 46 — see §5, its behaviour under the fixed combination is itself informative).
- **Gate:** `InfantCurriculumScheduler(phase_0to1_use_crossing_count=True, phase_0to1_crossing_count_min=3)` for `ARM_2` (§2 — already wired, just needs the flag flipped).
- **Goal-pipeline fix:** whichever of §3(i) (ported warmup) or §3(ii) (re-homed on the scaffold) `/governance` picks — this is the one piece of new build work the redesign actually needs, and it should land via `/implement-substrate` *before* the comparison is authored, with its own validation that it reproduces a 603n-shaped G3 pass in the new context.
- **Instrumentation, added up front (not discovered after a FAIL, as happened three times already with 591d/591e/the C3-C5-C7 finding):** per-seed crossing-count trace, raw H_pos trajectory, z_goal_norm trajectory, and the live/dead status of C3/C5/C6/C7's preconditions, logged and checked for degeneracy *before* the 7-criterion PASS/FAIL tally is computed.
- **Explicitly NOT gated on:** `mech457_competence_bootstrap_explorer` / `GOV-FANOUT-1` (§4). **Explicitly gated on:** the §3 goal-pipeline port/re-home decision, and folding in the standing `governance_2026_07_18` anchor re-specification (§6) in the same authoring pass.
