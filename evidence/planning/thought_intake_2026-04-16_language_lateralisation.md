# Thought intake: hemispheric separation of affective coupling and symbolic routing

**Date:** 2026-04-16 (raw); intake written 2026-06-05
**Status:** intake / candidate (NOT yet registered)

**Origin:** user observation that the arcuate fasciculus lateralises with the symbolic-

## 1. Core idea

Two separable systems with a binding interface:
- **Affective coupling** -- bilateral, right-biased, ventral (uncinate); fast, approximate,
  continuous, socially tuned; does rapid other-agent state estimation ("fast empathy").
- **Symbolic-sequential language** -- left temporal + Broca/Wernicke + dorsal arcuate;
  discrete, compositional, high-fidelity, tightly-timed routing.
- **Interface/binding layer** -- ventral pathways + anterior temporal cortex bind affective/
  social meaning to symbolic form (affect <-> semantics <-> linguistic output).

General principle proposed: lateralisation reflects **separation of incompatible
computational regimes** (precision/low-ambiguity routing vs flexible/probabilistic
inference), not mere functional localisation. The arcuate is a *routing constraint*, not a
meaning generator.

## 5. Caution (per memory `feedback_biology_before_formal_definitions`)

The "lateralisation = incompatible-regime separation" principle is a clean abstraction but is
the kind of broad neuro-import that has previously gone philosophy-right / mechanism-wrong.
Before registering the regime-separation principle as an INV, commission the dorsal/ventral +
lateralisation lit-pull and keep the claim to the *dissociation* (which has clinical anchors:
conduction aphasia preserves comprehension; RH lesions impair prosody), not to the broad
precision-vs-flexibility generalisation.
