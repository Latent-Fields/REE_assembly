## Verbatim core proposal

"Play is one expression of a developmental information-hunger system that preferentially explores regions of action space in which useful learning remains possible." The self is part of the model: `P(outcome | self-state, world-state, action)` — growth makes yesterday's affordance calibration obsolete without environmental change. The play frontier MIGRATES (bodily → object → compositional/causal → social → symbolic/counterfactual → abstract) as learning progress saturates; human neoteny preserves the developmental exploratory mode. Architectural propositions: curiosity should be self-relative; development should generate its own curriculum (externally imposed stages risk hidden oracles); **play may emerge without a play module** (safety + spare capacity + reachable learnable uncertainty + learning-progress preference); mastery should MOVE curiosity, not extinguish it; counterfactual play internalises exploration; sleep/replay metabolises play. Explicit qualification: do NOT encode `play = information gain`.
