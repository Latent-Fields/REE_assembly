## 3. Biological-reference triage

- **MECH-314 (curiosity bonus)** -- closest mechanism: frontopolar / ventral-striatal uncertainty- and novelty-driven exploration (Daw et al. 2006; Wittmann et al. 2008; Wilson et al. 2014). In real brains the exploration bonus is a **competing valuation channel** (frontopolar cortex can override striatal exploit), not a small additive nudge swamped by primary value. A curiosity bias that contributes 0 to selection resembles the exploration channel being **disconnected from the action-selection arbitrator**. lit_status: **present** (targeted_review_arc_065). is_formal_import: partial (novelty bonus is a computational analog, biologically anchored). -> discovered **prerequisite (selection authority)**, not falsification.

Tonic DA vigor is a **global gain on response rate**, strongly behaviourally expressed. A `v_t=0.05` penalty that does not change action_density resembles a vigor scalar with **no motor-gain coupling to action rate**. lit_status: **present** (ARC-066/068 synthesis). is_formal_import: partial (Niv opportunity-cost is a formal derivation; DA-vigor is biologically solid). -> same prerequisite.
