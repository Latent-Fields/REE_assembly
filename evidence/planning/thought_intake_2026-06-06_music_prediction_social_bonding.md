The raw thought left the source unverified: the `share.google` link was inaccessible and the

- **Article (popular):** PsyPost, "Scientists show how common chord progressions unlock social
  bonding in the brain" (~2026-05-07).
  https://www.psypost.org/scientists-show-how-common-chord-progressions-unlock-social-bonding-in-the-brain/
- **Primary paper (peer-reviewed):** Watts DA, Allsop AZA, Compton S, Zhang X, Noah JA, Hirsch J.
  "Listening to a Consonant Chord Progression during Live Face-to-Face Gaze Enhances Neural
  Activity in Social Systems." *The Journal of Neuroscience* 46(15):e1116252026 (2026). Yale School
  of Medicine. https://www.jneurosci.org/content/46/15/e1116252026
- **Institutional coverage:** Yale News, "Striking a chord: How music primes our minds for
  connection" (2026-03-18); Neuroscience News, "Biological Link Between Music and Bonding Identified."

**Method (verified):** fNIRS (functional near-infrared spectroscopy) on 40 healthy adults arranged
as 20 paired dyads (mean age ~27). Two musical conditions: structured/**predictable** consonant
chord progressions vs **scrambled** versions with identical notes. A glass barrier toggled
transparent/opaque to switch live face-to-face eye contact on and off.

**Findings (verified):**
- During live face gaze + structured (predictable) chord progression, increased activation in the
  **right angular gyrus** (social processing, DMN-adjacent), the **right somatosensory association
  cortex** (physical/body sensation), and **bilateral dorsolateral prefrontal cortex** (planning /
  social behaviour).
- **Cross-brain neural synchrony:** the two partners' brain activity began to mirror each other in
  the face-to-face structured-music condition.
- **Predictability is necessary:** the scrambled (unpredictable) condition with identical notes did
  not produce the effect. The authors propose predictable structure may *upregulate innate social
  mechanisms* underlying bonding.

**Verification verdict:** the study **directly corroborates the raw thought's core intuition.** Two
of its specific predictions land precisely:
1. the "music-and-somatosensory connection" the raw note flagged maps onto the observed
   **somatosensory association cortex** activation; and
2. "shared predicting between agents" maps onto the observed **cross-brain neural synchrony** that
   appears *only* under predictable structure.

This raises the note from speculative-observation to **literature-corroborated compass** (single
study; fNIRS dyads; correlational; not yet an REE evidence target). It does **not** make any REE
claim testable on the V3 substrate — there is no multi-agent / shared-attention substrate to

**Novelty verdict.** REE's social stack is rich but almost entirely **dyadic state-reading**: agent
A reads/predicts agent B's broadcast signals (MECH-041/183/191-193, ARC-010). The genuinely new
content here is **triadic joint attention**: two agents co-predicting and co-resolving the *same
third thing* (a temporal pattern / external structure), where the co-prediction itself produces
coupling — independent of either reading the other directly and independent of instrumental
goal-alignment. The verified study's cross-brain-synchrony-under-shared-predictable-structure result
is exactly this triadic mechanism. REE has no first-class primitive for it.

## 3. Key formulations

1. **Triadic shared-prediction coupling (new).** Bonding/cooperative-readiness can increase when two
   agents entrain to, anticipate, and resolve the *same* external temporal structure. The coupling
   variable is the *coherence of their prediction-error-and-resolution trajectories over the shared
   target*, not the accuracy of A's model of B.

2. **Inversion relationship with MECH-041.** MECH-041 already says affective expression broadcasts
   an agent's control-plane regime *to reduce the other's social prediction load*. The new idea is
   the complement: a **shared external predictand** reduces *both* agents' prediction load
   simultaneously and yokes their error timecourses — a different mechanism with the same currency
   (prediction load), so MECH-041 is the natural neighbour, not the owner.

3. **Three coupling modes (new taxonomy).** Instrumental (goal-alignment) | representational
   (shared attention/prediction of external structure) | affective (synchronised valence/body-state
   trajectories). Music exposes modes 2 and 3 cleanly with no propositional content — bonding
   without a verbal proposition.

4. **Somatosensory / interoceptive coupling channel.** The verified somatosensory-association-cortex
   finding grounds the raw note's "music-somatosensory connection": bodily entrainment is a real
   third channel, adjacent to REE's interoceptive/allostatic line (the Barrett |interoceptive PE|
   arousal channel referenced near MECH-024/INV-014).

5. **Safety fork: affiliative vs coercive coupling.** Shared prediction is value-neutral as a
   mechanism. The same entrainment substrate underlies soothing affiliation *and* coercive
   synchrony (crowd capture, manipulation). A future social architecture must be able to distinguish
   them — this is the genuinely REE-relevant guardrail, not the chord-progression detail.

6. **Predictability is load-bearing (from the study).** Scrambled identical notes failed. The
   coupling requires *structure that can be predicted* — consistent with REE's prediction-error
   machinery being the substrate that entrainment rides on, not melody/affect per se.
