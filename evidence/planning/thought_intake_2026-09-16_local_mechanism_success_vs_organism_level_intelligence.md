## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> **A mechanism earns architectural confidence locally. An organism earns cognitive confidence only when
> those mechanisms remain jointly useful under novelty, perturbation, development and ecological change.**

The thought's own chain, which it insists is not a mandatory ladder but a set of places evidence can sit:

```text
mechanism validity != local causal consequence != closed-loop behavioural benefit
                  != ecological generalisation != adaptive organism-level competence
```

And its strongest forward-looking claim:

> **The strongest eventual demonstration is not that REE performs a known task well, but that it can
> discover that its own working model has become wrong and recover without being told which part failed.**

Section 12 says what the thought wants to produce, explicitly NOT "a bundle of new mechanism claims":
(1) an organism-level validation doctrine as interpretive checklist; (2) a later adaptive-recovery assay
family, planned not queued; (3) progress summaries that separate mechanism, closed-loop and
ecological/adaptive evidence; (4) an explicit `H-other / model misspecification` route in important
hypothesis-space discriminators; (5) periodic simplification/deletion challenges. Item 1 was landed with
the thought (the companion doc). Items 3-5 are rules about how Assembly works; the registry form of such a
rule is a `governance_rule` claim (GOV-CONTRACT-2/3, GOV-MATCHAUX-1, GOV-HELDOUT-1 are the pattern). Item 2
is an open question.

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "A project can become extremely good at diagnosing and repairing local mechanisms while quietly allowing
  **mechanistic progress to stand in for organismal progress**."
- "seven seeds in one world family are still seven samples from essentially the same ecology."
- "the same mature architecture may behave differently depending on the developmental path through which
  it was reached."
- "**If the organism retains competence after machinery is removed, simplification itself is scientific
  progress.**"
- "once several clean alternatives exist, the experiment can begin to feel as though reality must choose
  among them. It need not."
- "The machinery should remain an **umpire**, not quietly become a ruler."
- "The crucial constraint is that the experimenter must not tell the creature which module failed."
- "It is that the solution was **not fully specified in the intervention**."
- Doctrine compact rule: "Local evidence should stay local until closed-loop consequence is shown. Closed-loop
  consequence should stay ecological-specific until transfer is shown. Transfer should stay
  developmental-specific until developmental history is tested."
