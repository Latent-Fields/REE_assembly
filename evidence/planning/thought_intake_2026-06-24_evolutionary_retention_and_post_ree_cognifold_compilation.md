## Genuinely new — four things

### N1. GOV-CEIL-1 has no tail/perturbation escape clause — and that is precisely the failure mode the thought names

This is the sharpest and most immediately actionable item in the whole intake.

GOV-CEIL-1 demotes a claim after N>=3 substrate-ceiling verdicts **without a single positive discrimination on any richer substrate**, and thereafter carries the "the mechanism is inert / doing no work" null co-equally with the hypothesis. That is a sound rule against indefinite parking.

But the thought's central observation is that a **robustness reserve is silent except under the rare condition it protects against.** Such a mechanism *cannot* produce a positive discrimination under tests that never instantiate its trigger. It will therefore accumulate substrate-ceiling verdicts and be demoted — not because it does nothing, but because nothing has ever asked it to.

GOV-CEIL-1 contains no exemption, no requirement to record *which perturbations were never run*, and no distinction between "tested and found inert" and "never tested under its own trigger condition". The thought supplies exactly that distinction:

```
redundant_under_current_tests     !=     functionally_unnecessary
```

This converts an abstract worry into a concrete, small amendment to a rule that is already live and already demoting claims. It is not a future-programme item.

### N2. Five-way mechanism classification is not representable in the registry

Constitutive / developmental scaffold / robustness reserve / historical implementation contingency / true redundancy. `claims.yaml` types claims by *epistemic* kind (invariant, architecture, mechanism, design_decision, open_question, governance_rule) and by status, but has **no axis for a mechanism's functional role in the system's viability** — which is the axis that determines whether removing it is safe.

Note the registry already contains members of at least two of these classes without naming them: ARC-025/MECH-069 are constitutive claims; ARC-090/MECH-381/MECH-362/INV-055 are developmental-scaffold claims. The class exists in practice and is invisible to the machinery.

### N3. Competitive redevelopment is a stronger removal test than mature lesion — and REE currently does only the latter

REE's ablation practice is mature-state arm ablation: every multi-arm experiment runs an OFF arm against a trained agent. That answers *can the mature system operate without M*. It does not answer *could the system have developed without M* — and REE holds several claims (INV-055, MECH-202, MECH-362, ARC-090/MECH-381) asserting that specific machinery is **developmentally** necessary and subsequently quiet.

So the gap is not hypothetical: REE has claims of exactly the type for which mature lesion is the wrong instrument, and no protocol that would test them correctly.

The honest counterweight, which must be registered with the claim: competitive redevelopment is **expensive** — a full development run per arm — and the thought's own risk list flags that testing it for every mechanism is prohibitive. That is why candidate 3 below is framed as *one decisive probe*, not a standing requirement.

### N4. Internal-invariant equivalence, not behavioural equivalence, is the compression criterion

```
behavioural imitation  !=  functional equivalence  !=  ethical-process equivalence
```

A compressed mechanism could reproduce the action distribution of a system that explicitly tracks uncertainty, vulnerability, harm, others and responsibility **without preserving those representations or their causal authority**. INV-077 keeps typed signal classes non-collapsible at the *evidence* layer; the SENT-* block gates welfare at the *release* layer. Nothing states that a substitution must preserve internal ethical-process representations **with their causal authority intact**. That is a safety-relevant claim with a natural home beside the SENT-* block.

## Candidate claims (for registration at digestion)

1. **GOV-CEIL-1 tail-escape amendment: absence of positive discrimination does not license demotion when no test ever instantiated the mechanism's trigger condition.** *Candidate, `governance_rule` — an amendment to a live rule, not a new one.* A claim whose registered function is tail-protective, developmental, compensatory or otherwise conditionally expressed is demotable only if either (a) at least one test instantiated its trigger condition, or (b) the demotion record explicitly states that none did and demotes anyway on stated grounds. *PASS-FAIL shape (compliance audit + retrospective scan):* PASS = every substrate-ceiling demotion of a conditionally-expressed claim carries one of (a) or (b). FAIL = a demotion of such a claim with neither. The **retrospective scan is the immediately useful half**: are any *already*-demoted claims of this type? *Decidability precondition (governance analogue of non-degeneracy):* the amendment requires (i) at least one claim whose registered function is conditionally expressed — supplied by candidate 2's classification — and (ii) trigger conditions being recorded at all. Absent either, the rule is **vacuous rather than satisfied** and must report `no_instances` / `schema_not_ready`, never PASS. *Cross-ref:* GOV-CEIL-1, GOV-DIAG-1, GOV-FANOUT-1, candidate 2, INV-055, MECH-362, ARC-090.

2. **Mechanism functional-role classification, and a removal-justification vocabulary.** *Candidate, `design_decision` on the registry schema.* Five revisable classes — constitutive / developmental scaffold / robustness reserve / historical implementation contingency / true redundancy — plus the paired removal-justification distinction `redundant_under_current_tests` vs `functionally_unnecessary`, the second requiring materially stronger evidence than the first. *PASS-FAIL shape:* PASS = the classification changes at least one demotion or retention decision (i.e. it is load-bearing). FAIL = populated but never decision-relevant, i.e. decorative — the same "load-bearing, not decorative" test ARC-106 applies to biological grounding, applied here to the registry's own schema. *Non-degeneracy precondition:* the classes must be non-degenerately populated — if essentially every mechanism lands in one class the axis carries no information and should be reported as `insufficient_class_spread` rather than shipped. A cheap pre-check: classify a sample of existing claims before building the field. *Cross-ref:* SD-062, ARC-025, ARC-090, MECH-381, MECH-362, INV-055, candidate 1.

3. **Competitive redevelopment dissociates from mature lesion — or it does not, and one probe decides which.** *Candidate, methodological, with a genuinely decisive test.* For a mechanism registered as developmentally necessary, running (a) mature-state lesion and (b) development-without-the-mechanism should give **different** verdicts. *Falsifier / PASS-FAIL shape:* pick one such mechanism — **INV-055's infant stage is the natural candidate**, being both registered as developmentally necessary and already instrumented via the curriculum. PASS = the two protocols **dissociate** (mature lesion says tolerable, redevelopment says not, or the reverse), which establishes that the expensive protocol buys something and justifies a standing requirement for this claim class. FAIL = they agree, which establishes that mature lesion suffices here and the expensive protocol is **not** justified — a genuinely useful negative that should close the question rather than invite a second attempt. *Non-degeneracy precondition:* the mature-lesion arm must produce a **measurable effect at all** — non-zero cross-arm and cross-seed variance on the discriminating metric. A mechanism whose mature lesion is a no-op *and* whose develop-without is a no-op is simply inert; that is a GOV-CEIL-1 matter, not a protocol comparison, and the run self-routes `substrate_not_ready`. *Cross-ref:* INV-055, MECH-202, MECH-362, ARC-090, MECH-381, INV-041, GOV-CEIL-1, candidate 1.

4. **Any mechanism substitution must preserve internal ethical-process representations and their causal authority, not merely the action distribution.** *Candidate, `governance_rule`, binds at V4+.* Behavioural equivalence is insufficient; a substitution that reproduces actions while deleting the representations of uncertainty, vulnerability, harm, others and responsibility — or severing their causal authority over selection — fails the criterion regardless of behavioural match. *PASS-FAIL shape:* PASS = a substitution passes only when both a behavioural-equivalence check **and** an internal-invariant check clear; the internal check requires the named representations to be present *and* to remain causally load-bearing (ablating them still changes behaviour in the substituted system). FAIL = behavioural match accepted alone. *Decidability precondition:* the internal invariants must be **instrumented and logged**; if the ethical-process representations are not observable in the run record the criterion is unmeasurable and must report `instrumentation_not_ready` rather than PASS. This is the binding constraint and should be recorded as the rule's dependency. *Cross-ref:* INV-077, the SENT-* block (natural home), SENT-13 (co-instantiation risk map), ARC-025, MECH-069.

5. **Reference-cognifold record: what must be captured to make a working V3 a manipulable reference artefact.** *Candidate, `design_decision` / `governance_rule`.* Behavioural invariants, internal-state invariants, developmental milestones, ethical constraint traces, failure modes, recovery profiles, computational costs, and memory/latency/sample requirements. *PASS-FAIL shape:* PASS = the record suffices for a later version to be compared against V3 on every listed axis without re-running V3. FAIL = any axis is unrecoverable after the substrate has moved. *Decidability precondition:* requires an operational definition of V3 closure — the same `definition_not_ready` gate the 2026-06-21 intake's candidate 4 exists to resolve. **Register jointly with that intake's candidate 1**, not separately. *Cross-ref:* the 2026-06-21 closure intake candidates 1 and 4, SENT-14, `docs/closure_dashboard.md`.

6. **(Stance, not a claim) Finish an overbuilt V3 rather than pursue premature minimality.** Record as a scoping note. It endorses current practice and needs no falsifier; registering it as a claim would put a proposition in the registry that nothing will ever test.
