## Verbatim prompt (core proposal)

> REE's control plane should not be conceived only as a collection of global scalar settings --
> precision, gain, arousal, mode, veto, or care budget. L-space may need a **continuously evolving
> authority field**: a structured conformation that transiently determines which predictive,
> mnemonic, goal, self/other, and counterfactual representations can influence one another and
> committed action.

> The authority field should provisionally be understood not as an additional subsystem beside
> L-space, but as the **moment-to-moment functional conformation of L-space**. ... Two representations
> can both exist, yet be effectively unable to influence one another; alternatively, previously distant
> content can be brought into a temporary coalition without changing its durable encoding.

Formal sketch: `r_i(t+1) = f_i(r(t), u(t), d(t), g(t))`, `d_ij(t+1) = h_ij(d(t), E1(t), E2(t),
memory(t), goal(t), self_other(t), g(t))` -- "the essential property is not the particular equation.
It is that the causal geometry has its own state, persistence, competition, and dynamics."

The live fork the revision adds:

> **Routing-only field** ... a temporal readout of it adds no task-relevant information beyond the
> content state it gates. **Representational field** ... its evolving trajectory itself encodes globally
> integrated task structure ... A trajectory-aware readout predicts relevant world structure or later
> action better than any equally resourced instantaneous-content readout.

The thought's own brakes, honoured here: "No implementation should follow directly from this intake";

## Key formulations (verbatim, load-bearing)

> The field is not the content of thought, not a second world model, and not a homunculus. It is the
> condition that makes one coalition of content causally effective at a particular time.

> fast reconfiguration of causal geometry without relearning the world.

> Authority is not synonymous with salience, intensity, confidence, moral importance, or conscious
> availability, although each may contribute to it.

> some control-plane variables become spatially and temporally organised relations, rather than only
> global settings.

> Ethical consequence requires not only representation of another, but appropriate causal access by
> that representation to the organism's prediction, valuation, and commitment processes.

> Success is not a prettier activation plot. Success is a reproducible change in ecologically
> consequential behaviour, with the field showing endogenous recruitment, local operation, competitive
> authority, committed throughput, and retention/generalisation.
