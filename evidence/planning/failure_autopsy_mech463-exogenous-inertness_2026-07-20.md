## 6. Learning extracted

1. **A share-based criterion and its denominator can move in opposite directions, and reading only
   the share hides it.** 785's total rose 14.1x while the incumbent share fell -- amplification
   confirmed, concentration contradicted, from one run. Always record and read the denominator.
2. **Exogenous manipulation of a driver that co-varies endogenously is the discriminating design,
   and it inverted the verdict.** The endogenous 14.1x looked like strong support; three exogenous
   manipulations show it is not attributable to the proposed agent. Where a claim rests on an
   observed covariation, the exogenous control is not a refinement -- it is the test.
3. **A modulator on an ADMISSION threshold dilutes rather than concentrates.** Where a claim's
   prediction depends on the sign of a threshold effect, name the parameter and check its direction
   in the code before queuing. This was derivable at design time.
4. **An expressivity positive control is what converts a null into a verdict.** 787's control
   (log10 var span 1.35, per-seed monotone fraction 1.0) is the reason "flat" reads as
   `flat_with_expressive_channel` rather than as an uninterpretable non-result.
5. **A frozen rival set can be exhausted while the phenomenon stays real.** All registered
   hypotheses are eliminated and the 14.1x endogenous profile still needs an explanation. That is
   a `mystery (known data)` node -- the data are in hand and the frame is wrong. More runs on this
   axis will not settle it.
