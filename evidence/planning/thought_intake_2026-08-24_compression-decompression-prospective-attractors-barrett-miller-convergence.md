> "This is describing REE like processes very closely if not exactly how I imagine them" ...

The thought is a structured comparison between REE and Barrett & Miller, "Categorization is
'baked' into the brain," *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 27:435-456 (2026), prompted by a Quanta
Magazine summary. The narrow, structural claim of convergence: **compression into a
low-dimensional multimodal state; prospective generation of possible sensorimotor futures;
internal-state-conditioned interpretation; precision-weighted reconciliation with incoming
evidence; and selective learning from mismatch, in approximately that causal order.** From this
the thought derives eight named candidate mechanisms (compression/decompression as an explicit
bridge, attractors as generative dispositions rather than stored propositions, a pre-rollout
possibility field, dual precision routing, deep-update eligibility, adaptive representational
granularity, non-oracular evidence ingestion, and compression-history dependence), a two-axis
attractor-failure taxonomy (excessive precision vs. excessive compression), a developmental
hypothesis, and a set of critical distinctions the biological paper's language must not be
allowed to collapse into REE's architecture (selection is not commitment, prediction error is
not residue, the limbic-core analogy is not one-to-one with any single REE module, allostasis is
not REE ethics, prospective categorisation is not proof of explicit rollout).

The thought is explicit throughout that it treats the paper as a plausibility/organisation
cross-check, not as evidence: "The significance is not that a neuroscience Perspective has
'validated REE.' It has not." Per `feedback_lit_exp_decoupled`, this intake treats the paper the
same way -- literature corroboration, never substrate evidence, and it strengthens no existing
claim's confidence.

## Key formulations (verbatim, load-bearing)

> An attractor is a compressed, history-conditioned generative disposition defining a family and
> probability distribution of possible sensorimotor trajectories. Its precision controls how
> strongly that family constrains interpretation and future generation; incoming precision-
> weighted discrepancies may select within it, modify it, or -- if sufficiently consequential --
> remodel or displace it.

> No injection may directly set the truth-state of E1. It may supply evidence capable of
> modifying E1 only through ordinary precision-, provenance-, and learning-eligibility machinery.

> deep-update eligibility = f(error, error precision, future relevance, organism/other
> consequence, provenance, current mode)

> Experience is not simply stored. It changes what the organism subsequently compresses together
> as equivalent, which changes what futures it can subsequently imagine.
