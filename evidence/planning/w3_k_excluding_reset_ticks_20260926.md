## 3. Verdict rule (fixed before any registered seed)

Per seed, per knob setting, compare the `t>=8` pool's `k` (and, secondarily, disc4_h1 and the per-horizon err/persistence ratio) against the `all-ticks` pool's `k`:

- **ARTEFACT-CARRIED**: under fix OFF, the `t>=8`-restricted `k` collapses materially below 10 (i.e. the all-ticks `k=10` finding does NOT survive dropping near-reset ticks) -- this would mean the OFF k=10 result itself is carried by near-reset ticks, independent of whether the reset-init fix is even applied (the SAME artefact-diagnosis logic `gate_c_ema_reset_init_probe.py`'s second, fix-free route used: excluding early-tick states alone, no fix needed, closes the gap).
- **GENUINE**: under fix OFF, the `t>=8`-restricted `k` stays at 10 (the OFF k=10 finding is NOT an artefact of near-reset ticks -- it holds on the SAME held-out ticks the reset-init fix's ON-arm k-collapse (`w3_reliability_ema_fix_20260926.md`) large-magnitude, uniform err/persistence shift (+0.269, all 15 seeds) would then need a DIFFERENT explanation, to be named from what this probe measures (e.g. the ON arm's ratio shift persisting even on t>=8-only ticks, or something else the results surface).
- **MIXED**: neither of the above cleanly -- e.g. `t>=8` k drops for some seeds but not others, or drops only partially (e.g. to 7-9 rather than 0), or the ON-arm comparison tells a different story than the OFF-arm one. Reported with the per-seed breakdown, not forced into a binary.

**Also answered per the brief:** which statistic, per this evidence, measures "the head learned action-conditioned dynamics" without the reset-transient artefact -- read directly off whichever of {disc4 (argmin-over-candidate-actions classification), the t>=8-restricted err/persistence ratio, the unfiltered ratio} the results show is NOT confounded by the reset transient's effect on the persistence baseline's own difficulty.
