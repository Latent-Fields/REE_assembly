### Verdict: ARTEFACT-CARRIED (unambiguous, 6/6 seeds)

Per the pre-registered rule (sec 3): under fix OFF, the `t>=8`-restricted `k` collapses materially below 10 in **6 of 6** seeds (values 5, 0, 2, 3, 0, 9 -- none at 10), while the `t<8`-restricted `k` stays at exactly 10 in **6 of 6** seeds. **The OFF `k==10` finding (`w3_reliability_sweep_20260926.md`'s "k=10 in every row") is carried by the held-out set's near-reset ticks (~44% of the sample), not evidence that the trained head beats persistence at every horizon uniformly across the episode.** Away from a reset (t>=8), the OFF head's 1-step advantage over trivial persistence is marginal-to-absent (ratio 0.97-1.03, i.e. essentially tied) in 5 of 6 seeds, with seed 912 the sole partial exception (ratio 0.97, k=9 -- closest to a genuine, non-artefactual pass).

This resolves `w3_reliability_ema_fix_20260926.md` sec 5 U6 in one direction, and reframes it: **the `k==10` conjunct was never a reliable discriminator of "the head learned action-conditioned dynamics" even OFF** -- it was already an artefact of the reset transient before the fix was ever applied, not something the fix broke. The fix's own already-documented ON-arm k-collapse (`w3_reliability_ema_fix_20260926.md`) is therefore not "a regression the reset-init fix introduces" so much as **removing the specific artefact (near-reset trivial-beat-persistence) that was propping the OFF number up**, while leaving the genuine, non-reset-conditioned signal (disc4, and the t>=8 ratio, both of which move in a broadly consistent direction OFF-to-ON: disc4 mean delta across these 6 seeds is +0.6% at t>=8 vs +2.1% at all-ticks per `w3_reliability_ema_fix_20260926.md`'s own 15-seed figure) roughly where it was.

### Which statistic measures "the head learned action-conditioned dynamics" without the reset-transient artefact

**disc4 (the argmin-over-candidate-actions classification), not the raw err/persistence ratio (and hence not `k`, which is a hard streak built on that ratio).** Reasoning, read directly off the tables above:
- **disc4 is comparatively stable across the t<8 vs t>=8 split** (e.g. seed 902: 0.509 vs 0.496; seed 904: 0.513 vs 0.424; seed 912: 0.538 vs 0.426) -- a modest, not qualitative, gap, and if anything disc4 tends to be slightly HIGHER on t>=8 (5 of 6 seeds OFF), the OPPOSITE direction from the `k`/ratio artefact. disc4 is a RELATIVE comparison across the SAME small candidate-action set at a FIXED tick, computed from the SAME (possibly-degenerate) starting representation for every candidate -- so a reset-tick's absolute-scale collapse in z_world affects all candidates equally and mostly cancels out of the argmin.
- **The raw err/persistence ratio (and `k`) is an ABSOLUTE-scale comparison** between the model's rollout error and a naive "persist x0" baseline's error. Right at a reset, the true underlying state is changing rapidly (first moves away from a fixed spawn point) while (pre-fix) z_world's own encode is collapsed toward near-zero -- inflating the persistence baseline's absolute error disproportionately relative to the model's, which trivially "wins" without having learned anything about action-conditioned dynamics specifically. This is exactly the confound the reset-init fix targets, and exactly why `k` collapses under ON even as disc4 improves.
- **This is continuous with (not a new finding relative to) `w3_reliability_ema_fix_20260926.md`'s own sec 3 reading** ("this is not a disc4 regression, a k-collapse in the gate's OWN readout instrument") and `gate_c_ema_reset_init_probe.py`'s independent, fix-free t_pick>=8 route reaching the same qualitative conclusion for a different growth-leg readout -- three independent instruments (gate (a)'s k, gate (c)'s growth-leg, and this probe's own tick-restricted re-pool) now agree that an ABSOLUTE-scale, persistence/growth-relative-to-baseline readout is reset-transient-confounded, while a RELATIVE, same-tick classification readout (disc4) is not.
