# Convergence as a General Computational Signal — REE Archaeology Seed

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Status:** archaeology workset / first-pass genealogy; not a claim registration  
**Parent thought:** `docs/thoughts/2026-09-09_convergence_as_general_computational_signal.md`

## Question

Is the proposed convergence signal genuinely new to REE, or is it a synthesis of mechanisms already distributed across information hunger, closure, basal-ganglia selection, claim rotation, TCRT, uncertainty and developmental curiosity?

The initial answer is:

> **REE already contains many of the ingredients, but no single currently evidenced document makes cross-view agreement itself a general meta-signal spanning action, research allocation and representational insight.**

This archaeology must distinguish direct genealogy from later convergence.

---

## 1. Earliest obvious precursor: plural support plus residual uncertainty

### `docs/thoughts/2026-08-12...` — Persistence Must Earn Continuation

Commit: `72889d1bc7aa03acf11566cfd5c97e2d38692fe5`.

The thought explicitly imagines several partly independent systems supporting an action:

- prediction says a trajectory is viable;
- memory finds no important contradiction;
- ethical constraints are satisfied;
- the goal remains appropriate;
- evidence favours commitment;

while another system still reports uncertainty.

Its central problem is closure: when is further uncertainty reduction no longer worth the cost of delaying action?

### Archaeological relation

**Direct precursor to action convergence, but not yet a convergence-signal theory.**

It already says multiple independent supports can matter to closure. The new thought asks whether the **pattern and independence of that agreement** can itself be represented and used to estimate commitment readiness / value of further deliberation.

## 2. Basal-ganglia selector constitution: plural voting without a scalar monarch

### `evidence/planning/thought_intake_2026-06-20_basal_ganglia_selector_constitution.md`

Commit: `f8a9c6ffafc258351b0873a63092944e3fd62a0b`.

This note explicitly reframes E3 away from:

```text
candidate actions
→ scalar score dominance
→ committed argmax
```

and toward:

```text
candidate actions
→ eligibility set
→ Go / No-Go pressures
→ conflict-sensitive hold
→ threshold modulation
→ recurrent competition
→ context-sensitive arbitration
→ permission to commit
→ latch / release
```

Later intake registered ARC-107 and MECH-447/448/449, followed by ARC-108/109 and MECH-450.

### Archaeological relation

**Strong architectural precursor.**

The selector constitution provides the natural place where differently weighted drives, rules, harms, goals and action pressures can coexist.

But it does not currently say:

> compute an independence-aware measure of agreement among these inputs and treat it as a separate meta-signal.

The new thought should therefore not duplicate the selector. It should ask whether convergence is a useful **observable/control-plane quantity over selector inputs**.

## 3. Information hunger and self-generated curriculum

### `docs/thoughts/2026-08-31_play_as_developmental_information_hunger.md`

Commit: `851476e6f61bdd88194e3b246c87611d0dad1c2d`.

The thought proposes intrinsic exploration of learnable affordances, a moving frontier of play and a developmental curriculum organised by reducible uncertainty / learning progress rather than externally imposed stage rewards.

It emphasises:

```text
generate intervention
→ observe consequence
→ update model
→ seek another tractable uncertainty
```

### Archaeological relation

**Direct precursor to epistemic convergence.**

The missing extension is cross-model leverage:

```text
information value of question q
not only = expected uncertainty reduction
but may also depend on = how many partially independent models q can update.
```

This could explain why convergent unknowns attract disproportionate research attention without treating novelty alone as valuable.

## 4. Existing curiosity architecture

The developmental information-hunger thought records that V3 already contains a hippocampal curiosity drive (`SD-025 / ARC-057`) using representational density / unfamiliarity and anti-perseveration logic.

Other work has considered curiosity-budget splitting and whether basal-ganglia-like eligibility is a better solution than a single arithmetic test.

### Archaeological relation

**Existing substrate family, not evidence for convergence.**

A convergence signal should first be tested diagnostically against the existing curiosity / information-hunger machinery, not introduced as a replacement drive.

Key question:

> does convergence across models predict useful information-seeking above density, unfamiliarity, uncertainty, expected information gain and learning progress?

## 5. Claim rotation and predictive equivalence

The 3 September claim-rotation work introduced the practice of examining one underlying proposition from several representational views rather than proliferating claims.

The subsequent representational-drift / predictive-equivalence thought sharpened this into:

- equivalence only within a declared domain;
- predictive equivalence versus predictive sharpness;
- intervention as a way to reveal hidden disagreement;
- “reality as semantic checksum”.

### Archaeological relation

**Strong epistemic analogue.**

This is almost the scientific-method version of the present proposal:

- multiple views converging is informative;
- agreement may be spurious if their assumptions are shared;
- carefully chosen experiments should target where views diverge;
- reality decides whether the apparent unification survives.

The convergence-signal thought generalises this from governance into a possible cognitive computation.

## 6. TCRT and mutual legibility

Temporary Coordinated Representational Transformations (TCRT) separates information being present from information being in the form required by the current computation.

Mutual-legibility work then emphasises specialised systems retaining different internal geometries while communicating through constrained bridges/read surfaces.

### Archaeological relation

**Important representational precursor.**

A genuine insight may occur when several specialised views become mutually legible through one new abstraction or transformation.

But the convergence thought must not assume that agreement requires common coordinates. Independent systems may converge at the level of **predictions, actions or relations** while retaining different internal representations.

This is an important guard against equating convergence with representational homogenisation.

## 7. Degeneracy already appears as a positive design principle

REE's compensation/ablation work explicitly treats multiple realizability and compensatory routes as important, while the recent compensatory-amplification thought warns that lesioning one mechanism can recruit or reveal others.

### Archaeological relation

**Independent convergence.**

This supplies a reason to preserve several routes rather than optimise them into one. The current thought asks whether the *agreement of surviving distinct routes* can be informative.

---

## 8. E3 commensurability and a warning about naive “votes”

Recent E3 work showed scale monopoly: one channel could dominate aggregate variance despite other channels being alive. A commensurability operator was built, but its first validation criterion was correctly blocked because normalising each channel by its own variance makes equalised variance shares partly tautological.

### Archaeological relation

**Critical methodological warning.**

Any convergence assay must avoid defining agreement by a transformation that forces agreement.

For example:

```text
normalise every evaluator to equal scale
then observe equal contribution
```

is not evidence of useful convergence.

A convergence metric must be evaluated by an **independent consequence**, such as held-out decision robustness, information allocation or predictive transfer.

## 10. What appears genuinely new

The first-pass novel synthesis has four parts:

1. **agreement topology matters:** not just output magnitude but how many effectively independent routes support it;
2. **cross-model information value:** resolving one unknown can be unusually valuable when several independent models depend on it;
3. **convergence and diversity are complements:** the system must preserve heterogeneous routes for their agreement to mean anything;
4. **insight candidate:** a representational change may deserve an Aha-like meta-signal when it suddenly improves/compresses several previously distinct views at once.

No existing source identified in this first pass appears to contain all four.
