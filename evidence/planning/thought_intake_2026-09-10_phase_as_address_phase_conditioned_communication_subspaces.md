## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> **Phase may function as an addressing coordinate for causal access: a representation can remain
> present continuously while its ability to influence a particular receiver depends on when, and in
> which organism-level dynamical state, it is presented.**

Formally, the relevant object may be not merely `S_comm(A -> B)` but:

> `S_comm(A -> B | phi, c)`

where `phi` is an endogenous phase or routing coordinate and `c` the current control / organismal
context. Shortest form: **"Phase may be part of the address."**

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "geometry = where information lives; communication subspace = which portion of that geometry is
  visible to a receiver; phase = when that address is readable; travelling wave = where the read
  window moves next; regulation = what controls the read schedule; causal privilege = what
  information consequently gains jurisdiction over downstream state."
- "A sender dimension could lie inside the receiver-potent subspace at one phase and outside it at
  another without the sender representation itself disappearing."
- "The system can therefore achieve the functional equivalent of orthogonalisation through temporal
  multiplexing."
- "**A travelling wave may instantiate a moving pattern of receiver availability.**"
- "information governance is not equivalent to filtering-out. It may instead consist in preserving a
  rich representation while continuously keeping non-current content outside the causal path
  relevant to the present computation."
- "local and global regulatory state alters the effective geometry and timing of communication
  without necessarily altering what is represented."
- "If no literal oscillatory variable exists, derive candidate cyclical or phase-like coordinates
  from recurrent dynamics rather than inventing one."
- "the **combinatorial richness and selectivity of `(representation, subspace, phase, receiver)`
  configurations** available to the organism."
- "No permanent phase mechanism should be introduced merely because biological cognition is
  oscillatory ... The goal is not biological decoration."
- "**Phase may be part of the address.**"

## 7. Implementation-gap audit -- the findings specific to this file

**See the hub intake's section 7 for the full 13-row audit.** This file's central assay is the one
place where the family's "REE lacks X" assumption is **most wrong**, and that is worth stating
plainly.

**Section 11.2's assay is buildable today. Nothing is missing.**

- **The estimator exists.** `ree-v3 experiments/_lib/interface_probe.py` (1202 lines, commit
  `a83f2fb`, 2026-09-10, on `origin/main`) supplies cross-validated RRR `communication_subspace()`
  and `principal_angles()` -- exactly the two primitives s.11.2 asks for. It has **no
  phase-conditioning argument** (`groups=` is a cross-validation grouping argument, not a
  conditioner), but the assay only requires a caller to stratify rows by phase bin, call
  `communication_subspace()` per bin, and compare the returned bases with `principal_angles()`.
  Held-out receiver prediction (`selected_heldout_r2`), the complexity-matched comparison
  (`bridge_ladder()` with `_capacity_report`-style parameter counts), the receiver-manifold guard
  and `dynamic_compatibility()` are all in the same module.
- **The endogenous `phi` exists -- three of them.** `ree_core/heartbeat/clock.py` carries
  `_e3_phase_step` (the E3 heartbeat phase, with MECH-091's salient-event `phase_reset()`; the
  trigger wiring landed `ree-v3 6293b2395248` and V3-EXQ-944b PASSed on 5 of 6 seeds),
  `_breath_phase_step` (a literal cyclic counter over `[0, breath_period)` with a `sweep_active`
  window, MECH-108), and the E1/E2/E3 multi-rate tick grid (ARC-023 / SD-006 phase 1). All are
  readable via `MultiRateClock.get_state()`. So s.11.1's fallback ("derive candidate cyclical
  coordinates from recurrent dynamics rather than inventing one") is not even needed -- literal ones
  are already logged.
- **Two caveats that must travel with that finding.** (i) `breath_period` defaults to `0`, so the
  BreathOscillator is **OFF** in the default configuration; a run intending to use it must enable it,
  and any historical trajectory recorded under the default has no breath phase at all. (ii)
  `sweep_active` is consumed at `ree_core/agent.py:7316` to reduce the E3 **commit threshold** -- it
  is a phase coordinate, not evidence that any communication is phase-conditioned. Mistaking the
  second for the first would be the easy error here.
- **What is genuinely missing for the CAUSAL half** (s.14's lesion ladder): no per-directed-edge gain
  `g_ij(t)` exists to shuffle, flatten or swap (hub audit row 7), so the lesion ladder needs an
  experiment-layer overlay that does not exist. The **observational** half needs nothing new.
- **Rival that must be run alongside, and is already registered:** **MECH-466** asserts that
  event-relative coordination exceeds clock-relative coordination. If true, an event-relative index
  may outperform any cyclic `phi`, which is falsifier 3 of this file's own list ("random temporal
  labels perform as well as the endogenous phase coordinate") in a sharper form -- not random labels
  but a *better-motivated* alternative index. Q-081's surrogate discipline applies in full, because
  streams ticking at 1, 3 and 10 steps will manufacture apparent phase structure.
