The raw note recorded the `share.google` link as inaccessible and exact-title / broad web

- **News item:** "How early brain activity may shape speech-linked circuits before babies ever speak,"
  Medical Xpress, 2026-05 (`medicalxpress.com/news/2026-05-early-brain-speech-linked-circuits.html`).
  Mirrored on EurekAlert ("Beyond the blueprint…", release 1128445) and News-Medical (2026-05-19).
- **Primary peer-reviewed paper:** Shih-Yun Chen (first author) & Hsiao-Ying Kuo (corresponding),
  *"Activity-dependent development of vocal circuits in the neonatal rodent forebrain,"*
  **EMBO Reports**, published 2026-05-19, **DOI 10.1038/s44319-026-00798-1**.
  Institute of Neuroscience / Institute of Anatomy and Cell Biology, National Yang Ming Chiao
  Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan.
- **Findings (verified against EurekAlert + News-Medical):** the team identified a
  vmPFC -> striatum communication circuit in neonatal mice. **vmPFC neural activity rises
  *before* pups emit ultrasonic calls** (i.e. pre-behavioural / endogenous, not merely
  accompanying vocalization). Experimentally driving the circuit increases vocal output and
  transition diversity, elevates striatal **Foxp2** expression and Vglut1+ corticostriatal
  terminal signals, promotes synaptic development, and **partially restores vocal deficits in
  Foxp2-mutant mice**. Framing: communication circuits are dynamically refined through
  *activity-dependent gene regulation* during early development — "beyond the blueprint,"
  i.e. not static genetic programming. Human relevance: FOXP2 mutations -> childhood apraxia
  of speech.

**Source status: VERIFIED (animal-model primary study + concordant press coverage).** This is
a rodent neonatal vocal-circuit study, not a human infant-speech study. The REE-relevant
*architectural* extraction below is therefore an analogy/compass, not transferable empirical
evidence. The raw note's own caution ("do not infer that speech/language develops without
social input"; "do not over-map human infant speech development to REE version numbers") stands.

The mechanism is also a striking anatomical echo of an existing REE anchor: the circuit under

converts stored affective/normative/residue content into active components of the navigable

## 3. Key formulations

1. **Endogenous-scaffold primitive (the new bit):** a capacity's circuit can be organised by
   self-generated internal activity *before* the capacity is expressible — formation precedes
   both rehearsal and permission. (Biological anchor: vmPFC activity rises *before* the first
   ultrasonic call and *causes* corticostriatal/Foxp2 maturation; retinal-wave-style
   pre-sensory self-organisation is the broader class.)

2. **Capacity-readiness ladder (re-statement of owned gates, not new):**
   `latent_scaffold -> rehearsal -> supervised_expression -> committed_use`. Maps onto
   MECH-261's operating-mode write-gate vector + INV-019 rehearsal/durable-write separation +
   ARC-019 curriculum gates. The thought's `capacity_readiness = f(internal_rehearsal,
   circuit_stability, provenance_control, error_recovery, social_safety, action_authority_gate)`
   is a *readout over existing signals*, not a new substrate.

3. **Readiness-not-from-output governance rule (the new corollary):** "a capacity is safe
   because it can generate output" is a fallacy; and conversely, "a capacity is absent because
   it produces no output" is also a fallacy during the latent-scaffold phase. Readiness should
   be assessed on circuit-internal maturity, not on the presence/absence of overt behaviour.

4. **Anatomical cross-reference (not evidence):** the studied circuit is vmPFC -> striatum;
   REE's ARC-035 already names vmPFC as the store-to-active-state converter, and MECH-038
   already posits an arcuate-like sequence-to-motor channel nudging language. The new study's
   forebrain (vmPFC/striatum/Foxp2) emphasis is consonant with REE placing language scaffolding
   above brainstem vocal centres.
