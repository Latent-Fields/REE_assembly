## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> **Convergence and divergence may be opposite signs of one meta-computational quantity: how several
> differently generated views constrain the same unresolved decision, hypothesis, or representation.**

> The value comes from knowing **who agrees, who disagrees, why, and how independent their routes to
> that judgement were**.

> seek observations or interventions where the credible views disagree most sharply and where the
> disagreement is expected to be reducible.

> two routes that inherit the same underlying evidence should not be treated as two independent reasons
> merely because they arrive through two named modules.

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "credible independent convergence -> this may be safe to trust, close, commit, or deprioritise /
  credible structured divergence -> this may be worth investigating, rotating, or sampling"
- "The same vote count can therefore carry radically different information."
- "This fits the broader REE principle that **content alone is not enough; provenance and access
  structure matter**."
- "But the signal should be **closure pressure**, not truth."
- "The important fact may not be that uncertainty is high globally. It may be that **specific competent
  views make different predictions in a particular region of the problem**."
- "Not all disagreement deserves curiosity."
- "The correct action may be recalibration or down-weighting, not endless investigation."
- "information hunger may become richer partly because **the architecture becomes more degenerate and
  multi-view**."
- "**structured disagreement among several useful views collapses because a new representation exposes
  a relation they were each seeing partially.**"
- "The umpire does not have to manufacture the winning reason."
- "**maturity could increase the value of convergence while preserving the diversity that makes
  convergence meaningful.**"
