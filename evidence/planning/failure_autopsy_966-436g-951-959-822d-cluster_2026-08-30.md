## 1. V3-EXQ-966 -- the two arms are the same run

**C1 (MECH-143).** `c1_repeat_pass` true on 4/4 seeds; `c1_flip_pass` false because seed 61's
`flip_differs` is false. The driver computes `c1_pass = repeat AND flip` and then
`MECH-143 = "supports" if c1_pass else "weakens"`.

But `flip_differs` is the driver's **own declared positive control** -- its docstring says the
flip leg exists so that *"a 'no difference' verdict elsewhere in this driver cannot be waved away
as 'the test can't detect anything anyway'"*. A power-control failure on one seed is evidence the
instrument is not established on that seed. Charging it to the claim as `weakens` inverts what it
means.

**C2 (MECH-144).** Recorded `supports`, `mean_delta = -0.125`, `margin = 0.640`. Three separate
things are wrong with that pass, and the third is decisive.

*(a) The equivalence bound is set by the noise it exists to exclude.*
`margin = max(K_EFFECT * pstdev(delta), ABS_FLOOR)` = `max(2 x 0.32005, 0.10)` = **0.640**, on a
DV bounded in `[0, 1]`. The equivalence bound is 64% of the DV's entire range. A noisier
measurement buys itself a wider bound.

*(b) Two of three verdict branches map to `supports`.* Driver L592-599: only
`mean_delta > +margin` yields `weakens`; both `|mean_delta| <= margin` and `mean_delta < -margin`
yield `supports`.

*(c) The two arms produced bit-identical behaviour.* `block_contacts` are identical between
`ARM_FIXED_VALUE` and `ARM_SHUFFLED_VALUE` on **4/4 seeds across 4/4 blocks**, including blocks 3
and 4 where the arms' amplitude assignment differs:

| seed | both arms, blocks 1-4 (type_1 : type_2 contacts) |
|---|---|
| 42 | `3:2` `2:6` `9:5` `2:6` |
| 47 | `2:1` `4:1` `0:1` `1:2` |
| 53 | `0:4` `1:3` `3:1` `2:2` |
| 61 | `5:9` `6:5` `6:9` `8:4` |

`frac_high` is *(contacts of the block's current high type) / (total)*, and the arms designate
**different** types as high in block 4. So `frac_high_SHUFFLED = 1 - frac_high_FIXED` exactly --
which is why `fixed_post + shuffled_post` sums to precisely 1.000 on every seed. The delta is a
**relabelling**, not a contrast. The "equivalence" the verdict celebrates is the signature of the
manipulation having had zero effect.

**Why it was inert -- UNRESOLVED, and stated as such.** An earlier revision of this section
asserted that the amplitude has *no observable route to the policy*, on the grounds that
`agent_health` is ceiling-clamped (with `NUM_HAZARDS = 0`) and `agent_energy` floor-clamped.
**That mechanism is withdrawn: it is wrong on `agent_energy`.** Energy resets to 1.0 each episode
and decays 0.01/step, so it spends most of a 120-step episode strictly between the clamps, where a
contact's `+contact_benefit*0.5` bump lands **unclamped and amp-scaled** -- `+0.15` (HIGH) vs
`+0.0225` (LOW) -- into `body[3]`, an observed channel. The `min(1.0, ...)` clamp only absorbs
contacts in roughly the first 15 steps after a reset.

What *is* established, by code reading:

- `_consume_resource_at` is genuinely on the live path (`causal_grid_world.py:2436`, the legacy
  branch, since `consummatory_act_enabled` defaults False and this driver does not set it), and
  `resource_type_benefit_amplitudes` is a plain attribute with no property or `__slots__`, so the
  driver's `_set_high_idx` live mutation really does reach the L2133 read.
- The **residue** route is open but **sign-gated**: `harm_signal = contact_benefit` (L2174) goes
  to `agent.update_residue` every tick (`_harness.py:361`), and every consumer of the value there
  is gated on the sign -- `harm_occurred=(harm_signal < 0)` (`agent.py:10354`) and the
  magnitude-reading branches at L10463/10477/10500, with `harm_magnitude = abs(harm_signal)` at
  L10501 reachable only inside `if harm_signal < 0`. A benefit is positive, so 0.30 and 0.045 are
  both "not harm" and are discarded identically. That is a real substrate asymmetry -- the residue
  field has a consumer for harm magnitude and none for benefit magnitude -- but it is **not**
  sufficient to explain the bit-identity, because the energy route remains open.

So the *empirical* finding is decisive and the *mechanism* is not. Two live explanations remain,
with opposite implications:

- **H-inert-in-fact** -- something prevents the amplitude difference from reaching behaviour
  despite the open energy channel. If so the run measured nothing and the fix is environmental.
- **H-insensitive-policy** -- the amplitude is applied, energy genuinely differs, and E3 selection
  is nonetheless unmoved by it. That would be a substantive and more troubling finding about the
  selection pathway's sensitivity to body state.

**Cheap confirmer, and it should run before any substrate work is specified:** one seed, blocks 3
and 4, both arms, logging `env.resource_type_benefit_amplitudes` and `agent_energy` at every
contact plus the per-step action index. That separates the two in minutes.

**One over-claim also corrected:** identical `block_contacts` are identical *counts*, not proven
identical *trajectories*. Thirty-two exact integer matches is overwhelming evidence of no
measurable behavioural difference, and it is what the direction withdrawals rest on -- but "the
two arms are the same run" is stronger than the evidence supports and is withdrawn as a phrase.

**Two corroborating symptoms.** `incentive_bank_base_values` is `{}` in all 8 cells -- the
IncentiveTokenBank EMA, which the design nominates as the re-tracking pathway, recorded nothing.
And P0's ResourceEncoder warmup reads `obs_dict.get("resource_field_view")`, a proxy-field channel
