**Primary source: VERIFIED (raw note had it as "source-check partial").** The saved item's
`share.google` link was never opened, but secondary coverage (Live Science, Washington Post)
correctly identified the study, and the primary paper is now located:

- **Grotzinger A. D. et al., "Genetic overlap across 14 psychiatric disorders," _Nature Genetics_,
  2025.** DOI [10.1038/s41588-025-02494-7](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02494-7).
  (Note: the raw note and the popular coverage say "Nature"; the paper is in **Nature Genetics**.)
- Large-scale genomic SEM across **14 childhood- and adult-onset psychiatric disorders** in
  **>1 million** individuals (broader cohort context cited as >6 million).
- Finds pervasive genetic overlap; the majority of case-vs-control genetic differences are
  captured by **five "genomic factors"** spanning **238 genetic variants**.
- The five factors (per the paper / Harvard Gazette / Mass General Brigham coverage):
  **Compulsive, Schizophrenia–bipolar (psychotic-mood), Neurodevelopmental, Internalizing,
  Substance-use.** (Matches the WaPo five-grouping list in the raw note.)
- Biological hooks reported: factor-specific gene sets, including **oligodendrocyte** genes
  (myelin / wiring-infrastructure cells) enriched in the **Internalizing** factor; signals
  acting early in brain development.

Confidence: HIGH that the paper exists and the headline finding (five shared genomic factors /
238 variants / 14 disorders) is as described. The paper itself was not read in full; the factor
labels and 238-variant count are from the abstract + four independent press summaries.

**Domain caveat (carried into every claim below):** this is a *human genetics cohort* finding.
It is **out-of-domain** for REE — no REE substrate at any level can test it, and it must never be
cited as REE mechanism evidence. Its REE value is as an external *corroboration anchor* for a

## 3. Key formulations

1. **Surface-cluster principle.** Psychiatric categories are *emergent clusters over overlapping
   latent vulnerability axes*, not primitive explanatory units. (Raw §0/§9; the canonical
   extraction.)

2. **Two-axis indexing.** A failure-mode taxonomy should be indexed two ways at once: by
   **named failure mode** (the existing entries — confabulation, belief fixation, psychosis-tag-
   loss, etc., *kept mechanistically distinct*) **and** by a finite set of **transdiagnostic
   vulnerability axes** that cut across the named entries. The named entry says *what breaks*; the
   axis says *along which shared dimension the vulnerability sits*.

3. **Candidate axis set (descriptive, for the future arch note):**
   precision / confidence allocation · commitment threshold / action-release authority ·
   provenance / source tagging · agency attribution · residue / consequence persistence ·
   goal / salience / incentive coupling · self-other coupling · offline integration / replay ·
   social-contagion / cross-agent coupling · global instability (p-factor analogue).

4. **Syndrome-as-vector.** A clinical syndrome ≈ a *weighted combination* of axis failures;
   comorbidity ≈ overlapping axis activation; the "same axis participates elsewhere" property is
   the whole point (pleiotropy analogue = one mechanism influencing multiple capacities).

5. **Etiological factoring (raw §4 last row).** Genetic-risk ≠ inevitability maps to
   *predisposition + environment + developmental timing + plasticity* — i.e. axis *predisposition*
   is distinct from axis *expression*, gated by REE's developmental / plasticity machinery.

6. **Non-conflation guardrail (mandatory).** The axis layer is an *indexing* layer, not a
   *merging* layer. Mechanistically distinct failure modes stay distinct:
   psychosis (MECH-094 hypothesis-tag loss / MECH-201 synthetic→real) ≠ confabulation (MECH-094
   tag-loss in the *recall/completion* sense, a separate failure) ≠ derealization (MECH-200
   real→synthetic) ≠ OCD (SD-034/SD-045/SD-046 action-arbitration, downstream of belief) ≠
   depressive attractor (INV-053/054 goal-pipeline) ≠ catatonia subtypes (SD-036/MECH-279 harm-
   stream lock vs MECH-343 action-release). Two modes sharing an axis does **not** make them the
   same failure. (Per `memory/feedback_psychosis_confabulation_distinction.md`.)
