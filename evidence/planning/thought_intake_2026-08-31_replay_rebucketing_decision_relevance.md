## Verbatim core proposal

E1 needs a way for experience to teach it **which distinctions in its content are worth preserving**. The adequacy test is whether representation preserves distinctions that predict consequentially different futures while compressing those that don't. The underspecified path is the **slow return path**: `decision consequences → evidence about representational sufficiency → later E1 reorganisation`. Decision relevance is often discoverable only AFTER the episode (episode D reveals the feature that splits A/C from B), so **replay provides access to non-contiguous experiences under a common evaluative frame** — retrospective representation learning: split / merge / feature reweighting / relation discovery / boundary revision / abstraction change. Counterfactual replay guards against single-episode over-splitting ("does representing X as distinct improve predictions under relevant alternative actions?"). Sleep's distinct role: **safe maintenance of the representational coordinate system itself** (rebucketing while relying on the buckets is unstable; re-indexing episodic traces must preserve addressability). Hard constraint: **E3 must not become E1's teacher** — typed insufficiency signals only, never category labels (non-oracular). Creates explicit version-placement pressure: minimal non-degenerate forms of ARC-134 grain, MECH-507 equivalence, MECH-512 variable grain, MECH-508 revisable attractors, and replay/sleep re-indexing may be v3 prerequisites inside v4-labelled claims — decided by the 8-question necessity test in the raw file, likely by SPLITTING claims, not wholesale relabelling.
