## 2. Direct answer: at DEFAULT config

**Computed at defaults (unconditional code):**
1. E3 `error_var` / `_running_variance` (rv), with `current_precision = 1/rv`.
2. E3 `_last_instantaneous_pe`.
3. E3 `_volatility_estimate` (var of rv over 100 ticks).
4. The hippocampal visitation counter (per-region count).
5. `hippocampal.last_novelty_score` is *not* computed: it needs the familiarity tracker, and `curiosity_weight=0`.
6. The serotonin module exists, but `tonic_5ht` stays at 0.5 and never moves.
7. The E2 forward rollout (whose world head is untrained unless the driver trains it; GFLAG-0485).

**Of these, only rv changes a controlled variable:** the E3 `committed` flag, via `rv < 0.4 * (sweep/urgency/velocity modulators)` at `e3_selector.py:4246-4251`. Everything that consumes `committed` belongs to the commit/BG group. Items 2, 3, 4 and 6 are telemetry at defaults. No surprise, curiosity, volatility, neuromodulator-named or regulator signal changes any variable at defaults. No acetylcholine-family mechanism exists in code on either tree.
