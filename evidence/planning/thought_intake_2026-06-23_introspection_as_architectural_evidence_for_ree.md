## Genuinely new — three things

### N1. The phenomenology-side counterpart to ARC-106 does not exist

ARC-106 registers biology as a standing construction constraint with a load-bearing requirement. **No claim registers introspection's role at all** — neither licensing it as a hypothesis source nor, more importantly, *bounding* it. The thought's own formalism is the missing rule:

```
INTROSPECTION := HYPOTHESIS_GENERATOR
NOT := SOLE_VALIDATOR
VALIDATION := TRIANGULATE(PHENOMENOLOGY, BIOLOGY, COMPUTATION, EXPERIMENT)
```

This matters more than it looks. REE's architecture *was* substantially generated this way — that is the user's plain statement — and the practice is currently entirely undocumented. An unwritten methodology cannot be audited, cannot be defended externally, and cannot be corrected. Registering it is as much a constraint as a licence.

### N2. Claim-origin provenance is not recorded — a concrete schema gap with an audit attached

`claims.yaml` carries a `source:` field, but it holds **file paths**, not **epistemic origin class**. There is no way to ask which claims were first noticed introspectively versus derived from literature, from computational analogy, or from an experimental anomaly.

The payoff is not bookkeeping. It is the thought's own question — *which hypothesis sources have been most productive, and where are the systematic blind spots?* — which becomes answerable the moment the field exists and is backfilled. Given ~900 claims with a full promotion/demotion history, that audit is unusually cheap for what it would tell us. It is also the enabling precondition for N1: a triangulation rule that cannot be checked is decorative.

Note the honest hazard: backfilling origin classes retrospectively is itself reconstruction, and will be partly confabulated (which is, satisfyingly, the thought's own MECH-094 point turned on the registry). Backfill should therefore be marked as reconstructed rather than recorded.

### N3. Generation, metacognitive readout, and linguistic reconstruction as three non-collapsible stages

The felt sequence — nonverbal settling -> felt direction/confidence -> verbal reconstruction -> external testing — implies three distinct things REE currently treats as two at most:

- the process that **generates** cognition (cognifold dynamics);
- the **metacognitive readout** that signals convergence (INV-033/MECH-059 territory);
- the **language process** that reconstructs and communicates it (ARC-048/MECH-380 territory).

Each stage has claims. The **non-collapse requirement across all three has never been asserted**, and it is the same argument-shape as ARC-025 (engine irreducibility) and MECH-069 (error incommensurability). Its practical consequence is sharp: if a system derives its confidence readout *from* its verbal reconstruction, then degrading the reconstruction should corrupt the confidence — which is a real, testable dissociation and a real safety property.

This is closely related to, but distinct from, the sibling resonance intake's candidate 1 (which separates externally-induced agreement from internally-derived confidence). Same non-collapse family, different cut.

### Also new, and worth carrying as a standing caution

**Designer-overfit.** The user's ADHD, strong nonverbal systems reasoning, spatial/motor intuition and long immersion in REE shape what is available to introspection. The thought's own guard — `high-value hypothesis source, not universal proof` — has no home in the registry, and REE's n=1 phenomenological base is a genuine external criticism with no registered answer.

## Candidate claims (for registration at digestion)

1. **Triangulation rule: introspection generates architectural hypotheses; it never validates them alone.** *Candidate, `governance_rule` — the phenomenology-side counterpart to ARC-106.* A mechanism first noticed introspectively acquires standing only when it also maps onto neuroscience or cognitive literature, is computationally coherent, produces measurable predictions, and survives experiment. *PASS-FAIL shape (compliance audit, not experiment):* PASS = every claim whose recorded origin is introspective carries at least one non-introspective support before promotion above `candidate`. FAIL = any such claim is promoted on phenomenological plausibility alone. *Decidability precondition (governance analogue of non-degeneracy):* the audit is undecidable until claim-origin provenance exists — without candidate 2 the rule cannot be checked and self-routes **`schema_not_ready`**. Do not register 1 as auditable before 2 lands. *Cross-ref:* ARC-106, INV-062, INV-077, MECH-094.

2. **Claim-origin provenance: the registry records the epistemic origin class of each claim.** *Candidate, `design_decision` on the registry schema.* Classes: introspective / literature / computational-analogy / experimental-anomaly / convergent (several). Backfilled entries marked as **reconstructed**, not recorded, per MECH-094's own logic. *PASS-FAIL shape:* the field's testable proposition is the productivity audit — do origin classes differ in promotion rate, demotion rate, and substrate-ceiling rate? PASS = a class-difference beyond the null, giving an actionable read on where hypotheses are worth sourcing. A **null result is a real and reportable finding**, not a failure: it would say origin does not predict productivity, which is itself worth knowing and would justify not maintaining the field. *Non-degeneracy precondition:* two conditions. (a) Each class must be non-degenerately populated — a class with a handful of members supports no rate comparison; require a floor on per-class count before computing anything. (b) The outcome variable must be live — promotion/demotion outcomes must have non-zero spread across the claim set; if nearly all claims sit at `candidate` the rate comparison is floor-pinned and self-routes `insufficient_outcome_variance`. *Cross-ref:* SD-062, INV-077, ARC-106.

3. **Generation, metacognitive readout, and linguistic reconstruction are three non-collapsible stages.** *Candidate, architectural / `substrate_conditional` (V5 — no language stream in V3).* The confidence readout must not be derived from the verbal reconstruction, and neither may substitute for the generating dynamics. *Falsifier / PASS-FAIL shape:* dissociation under degradation. Degrade the reconstruction channel; a three-stage system's confidence readout should be substantially preserved, while a collapsed system's should degrade with it. PASS requires the two architectures to be **distinguishable** on the readout under matched reconstruction degradation, not merely that performance drops. *Non-degeneracy precondition:* the metacognitive readout must be **live** in the intact baseline — non-zero cross-arm and cross-seed variance, not floor-pinned; a readout that never moves cannot be shown to be preserved or degraded, and the run self-routes `substrate_not_ready`. *Cross-ref:* ARC-025 and MECH-069 (same non-collapse argument at other levels), INV-033, MECH-059, ARC-048, MECH-380, MECH-424, and the sibling resonance intake's candidate 1.

4. **Designer-overfit standing check: a mechanism first sourced from one person's phenomenology requires cross-task and cross-agent validation before being treated as general cognition.** *Candidate, `governance_rule`.* *PASS-FAIL shape (audit):* PASS = every introspectively-originated claim treated as general carries validation in at least two structurally different task families. FAIL = generality asserted from a single environment. *Decidability precondition:* requires (a) candidate 2's origin field, and (b) at least two structurally different task families to exist. **V3 currently has effectively one**, so this rule is presently **vacuous rather than satisfied** — and saying so plainly is the point of registering it. Report `insufficient_task_families`, never PASS. *Cross-ref:* candidate 2, ARC-106, and the 2026-07-14 rule-apprehension intake's candidate 2 (domain-generality fork), which hits the same single-domain wall.
