# Literature synthesis: object-bound incentive salience before z_goal (goal-stream repair)

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Intake:** `thought_intake_2026-06-01_goal_wanting_liking_stream_repair.md`
**Purpose:** ground the proposed incentive-object / wanted-token layer (chain links L2-L3-L4-L6-L9)
in primary literature and high-quality reviews. For each source: mechanism extracted, REE
mapping, claim implication (supports / weakens / suggests-missing), implementation
implication, experiment implication.

**Certainty discipline:** these are biological analogies, not isomorphisms. Where the
mapping is loose or the biology contested, it is flagged. No biological result is treated
as proof of a REE claim; lit grounds *plausibility and design*, experiments adjudicate
*support*.

## A. Incentive salience and wanting vs liking

### A1. Berridge & Robinson 1998 -- dopamine and incentive salience
Berridge KC, Robinson TE. "What is the role of dopamine in reward: hedonic impact, reward
learning, or incentive salience?" *Brain Research Reviews* 28(3):309-369, 1998.
- **Mechanism:** dissociates three reward components -- *liking* (hedonic impact),
  *wanting* (incentive salience), and *learning* (predictions). Dopamine is necessary for
  wanting, NOT for liking. Wanting is the attribution of motivational salience to a
  stimulus/representation, making it attractive and "wanted".
- **REE mapping:** REE's `benefit_exposure`/VALENCE_LIKING is the *liking* term; z_goal /
  goal_proximity is meant to be *wanting*. The lit says these MUST be separable signals
  with different generators -- REE currently derives "wanting" (z_goal) *from* the liking
  pulse at the same instant and same location, which collapses the dissociation.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS the *intent* of MECH-117 (wanting/liking dissociable);
  WEAKENS the current *implementation* (wanting is a re-labeled liking write). SUGGESTS-MISSING
  the binding step that gives wanting its own generator (PROP: MECH-INCENT-token).
- **Implementation implication:** wanting amplitude must be a distinct state that can be
  raised by a cue without a concurrent liking pulse.
- **Experiment implication:** the dissociation metric (514k `wanting_liking_dissoc_fraction`)
  is the right probe but only meaningful once wanting has an independent generator.

### A2. Robinson & Berridge 1993 -- incentive-sensitization
Robinson TE, Berridge KC. "The neural basis of drug craving: an incentive-sensitization
theory of addiction." *Brain Research Reviews* 18(3):247-291, 1993.
- **Mechanism:** repeated reward can sensitize *wanting* independent of *liking* -- craving
  for an object/cue can grow while hedonic impact does not. Establishes wanting as a
  persistent, object-directed state.
- **REE mapping:** the *persistence* + *object-directedness* of the wanted-token (L3) -- a
  slowly-decaying per-object amplitude, not a per-step recomputation.
- **Claim implication:** SUGGESTS-MISSING persistent per-object wanting (PROP: MECH-INCENT-token).
- **Experiment implication:** Stage 2 (cue re-presentation raises wanting before benefit).

### A3. Berridge, Robinson & Aldridge 2009 -- dissecting reward components
Berridge KC, Robinson TE, Aldridge JW. "Dissecting components of reward: 'liking', 'wanting',
and learning." *Current Opinion in Pharmacology* 9(1):65-73, 2009.
- **Mechanism:** review consolidating the tripartite model; cue-triggered "wanting" can be
  generated by mesolimbic activation at the moment a reward cue is encountered.
- **REE mapping:** cue-triggered wanting = L6 (MECH-295 bridge fires on cue, before
  consumption).
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS MECH-295 (drive->liking->approach) as the cue-triggered
  arm; SUGGESTS-MISSING the cue-recall path (MECH-CUEWANT) since REE has no mechanism to
  re-present a learned cue and trigger wanting from memory.

### A4. Zhang, Berridge, Tindell, Smith & Aldridge 2009 -- computational incentive salience
Zhang J, Berridge KC, Tindell AJ, Smith KS, Aldridge JW. "A neural computational model of
incentive salience." *PLoS Computational Biology* 5(7):e1000437, 2009.
- **Mechanism:** incentive salience V = r(reward) * kappa(physiological/drive state),
  where kappa multiplicatively re-weights a learned cue value by current drive at the moment
  of cue encounter -- so a cached value is dynamically amplified by present need WITHOUT
  re-learning.
- **REE mapping:** this is almost exactly REE's `(1 + drive_weight * drive_trace)`
  multiplier -- but REE applies it to the *seeding gate* (whether to write z_goal), not to a
  *cached per-object value at cue encounter*. The lit says the multiplier belongs on a
  stored cue value retrieved at encounter time.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS SD-012/MECH-306 (drive modulation) AND relocates it:
  drive should re-weight a *stored object value at cue time*, the missing L3/L6 step.
- **Implementation implication:** the incentive-object token stores a base value; drive
  multiplies it at recall -> wanting. This is the single cleanest computational target.

### A5. Flagel et al. 2011 -- sign-tracking vs goal-tracking, dopamine
Flagel SB, Clark JJ, Robinson TE, et al. "A selective role for dopamine in stimulus-reward
learning." *Nature* 469:53-57, 2011.
- **Mechanism:** some animals attribute incentive salience to the *cue* (sign-trackers,
  approach the lever/CS) vs the *goal location* (goal-trackers, approach the food cup).
  Phasic dopamine tracks incentive salience attribution, dissociable from predictive value.
- **REE mapping:** directly mirrors REE's "object/cue vs location" confusion. REE currently
  is forced into "goal-tracking" (z_goal = where I was fed). An object-bound token allows
  sign-tracking-like attribution to the cue/object.
- **Claim implication:** SUGGESTS-MISSING object-vs-location binding (PROP: MECH-BIND-obj).

## B. Object/cue binding in reward learning

### B1. Cardinal, Parkinson, Hall & Everitt 2002 -- amygdala/ventral striatum/PFC circuit
Cardinal RN, Parkinson JA, Hall J, Everitt BJ. "Emotion and motivation: the role of the
amygdala, ventral striatum, and prefrontal cortex." *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews*
26(3):321-352, 2002.
- **Mechanism:** BLA binds cue->outcome *identity*; ventral striatum (NAcc) converts
  motivational value into action/approach; PFC maintains goal. A *circuit-level* division of
  labour: identity binding, value->action, maintenance.
- **REE mapping:** REE's amygdala module (`bla.py`/`cea.py`) currently does NOT bind
  cue->outcome identity (`cue_features` is an explicit V3 no-op stub); the NAcc->approach
  role is partly MECH-295; PFC maintenance is E1 LSTM (MECH-116). The identity-binding node
  is the structural hole.
- **Claim implication:** SUGGESTS-MISSING object-identity binding (PROP: MECH-BIND-obj),
  located conceptually at the amygdala/BLA analogue.

### B2. Corbit & Balleine 2005; 2011 -- specific vs general PIT
Corbit LH, Balleine BW. "Double dissociation of basolateral and central amygdala lesions on
the general and outcome-specific forms of Pavlovian-instrumental transfer." *Journal of
Neuroscience* 25(4):962-970, 2005. (See also Corbit & Balleine, *J Neurosci* 31(33):11786-94, 2011.)
- **Mechanism:** **specific PIT** (a cue boosts the action earning *that specific outcome*)
  depends on **BLA**; **general PIT** (a cue boosts responding broadly) depends on **CeA**.
  A cue selectively energizes pursuit of the *identity-matched* reward.
- **REE mapping:** specific PIT is the behavioural definition of object-bound wanting:
  cue -> wanting for the matching object -> approach. This is precisely the capability 514k
  failed to show (dissoc fraction 0 = no identity-matched selectivity).
- **Claim implication:** SUGGESTS-MISSING cue->identity-matched wanting (PROP: MECH-CUEWANT),
  and gives the canonical behavioural test (specific-PIT-style: cue for resource X raises
  approach to X, not Y).
- **Experiment implication:** Stage 2/4 acceptance = identity-matched approach selectivity.

### B3. Schoenbaum, Roesch, Stalnaker & Takahashi 2009 -- OFC and value identity
Schoenbaum G, Roesch MR, Stalnaker TA, Takahashi YK. "A new perspective on the role of the
orbitofrontal cortex in adaptive behaviour." *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 10(12):885-892, 2009.
- **Mechanism:** OFC represents the *identity and specific value* of expected outcomes
  (not just scalar value) -- supports outcome-specific expectancies that guide goal-directed
  behaviour and are needed for revaluation.
- **REE mapping:** the "what" of the goal -- z_goal should encode outcome identity, not just
  a location attractor. Supports writing z_goal FROM an object/affordance pointer (L4).
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS PROP: MECH-GOALPTR (z_goal from object pointer) and the
  SD-015 z_resource direction.

### B4. Wilson, Takahashi, Schoenbaum & Niv 2014 -- OFC as cognitive map of task space
Wilson RC, Takahashi YK, Schoenbaum G, Niv Y. "Orbitofrontal cortex as a cognitive map of
task space." *Neuron* 81(2):267-279, 2014.
- **Mechanism:** OFC encodes a *state/task-space map* including unobservable identity
  states; value is read off this map. Identity/state representation is upstream of value.
- **REE mapping:** the object/affordance representation (L2/L4) is a *state-identity* the
  goal pointer indexes -- argues for a learned identity embedding, not just a per-cell tag.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS a learned affordance/identity embedding as the z_goal
  write-source; relevant to whether SD-049 per-cell tags suffice or a learned token is needed
  (open question Q in intake Section 5).

### B5. Balleine & Dickinson 1998 -- goal-directed action and incentive learning
Balleine BW, Dickinson A. "Goal-directed instrumental action: contingency and incentive
learning and their cortical substrates." *Neuropharmacology* 37(4-5):407-419, 1998.
- **Mechanism:** goal-directed action requires (a) action-outcome contingency and (b) the
  outcome's current *incentive value*; incentive value is updated by experience with the
  outcome in a given motivational state. Distinguishes goal-directed from habitual control.
- **REE mapping:** wanting must be revaluation-sensitive -- the wanted-token value should
  track the outcome's incentive value, not be frozen at first contact.
- **Claim implication:** SUGGESTS the wanted-token must be revaluable (a stronger property
  than the current write-once-decay attractor); informs the decay/update rule of MECH-INCENT-token.

## C. Goal representations & prefrontal maintenance

### C1. Miller & Cohen 2001 -- PFC and goal maintenance
Miller EK, Cohen JD. "An integrative theory of prefrontal cortex function." *Annual Review
of Neuroscience* 24:167-202, 2001.
- **Mechanism:** PFC sustains goal representations as persistent activity that biases
  posterior processing toward goal-relevant actions; maintenance is active and decays
  without recurrent support.
- **REE mapping:** E1 LSTM working memory for goal (MECH-116), z_goal as a maintained bias
  on E3 trajectory scoring.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS MECH-116/ARC-032; the maintenance link L5 is biologically
  sound. (Note MECH-116 EXQ-076d showed goal persisted *too long* in both arms -- consistent
  with maintenance present but un-tuned, not absent.)

### C2. Balleine & O'Doherty 2010 -- goal-directed vs habitual; corticostriatal
Balleine BW, O'Doherty JP. "Human and rodent homologies in action control: corticostriatal
determinants of goal-directed and habitual action." *Neuropsychopharmacology* 35(1):48-69, 2010.
- **Mechanism:** dorsomedial striatum / associative cortico-striatal loops support
  goal-directed (outcome-sensitive) control; dorsolateral / sensorimotor loops support
  habit. Pre-consummatory approach is goal-directed early, habitual late.
- **REE mapping:** approach_commit should be outcome/goal-sensitive (goal-directed regime),
  i.e. modulated by z_goal/wanted-token, not a fixed policy. Supports L7/L8 consumer-readout
  being goal-conditioned.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS ARC-030 (approach-avoidance symmetry) being goal-conditioned.

## D. Neuromodulator / developmental-window relevance

### D1. Schultz 1998; Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997 -- phasic dopamine RPE
Schultz W. "Predictive reward signal of dopamine neurons." *Journal of Neurophysiology*
80(1):1-27, 1998. Schultz W, Dayan P, Montague PR. "A neural substrate of prediction and
reward." *Science* 275(5306):1593-1599, 1997.
- **Mechanism:** phasic DA = reward prediction error; with learning, the DA response
  *transfers* from reward to the predictive cue. Cue acquires the phasic signal.
- **REE mapping:** the transfer-to-cue is the substrate for cue-triggered wanting (L6).
  REE's VALENCE_SURPRISE / MECH-307 anticipatory write is the analogue; signed-PE was the
  GAP-1 fix. The transfer step (signal moves to the cue/object) is what lets the cue trigger
  wanting later.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS MECH-307 conjunction architecture; SUGGESTS the cue must
  carry the transferred anticipatory signal (PROP: MECH-CUEWANT recall path).
- **Caveat:** the strict DA=RPE view is contested (see E-notes); incentive-salience (A1)
  and RPE are complementary, not identical. Do not over-map DA to a single REE channel.

### D2. Miyazaki, Miyazaki, Doya -- serotonin and patience/waiting
Miyazaki KW, Miyazaki K, Doya K, et al. (e.g. *Current Biology* 24(17):2033-2040, 2014;
*Nature Communications* 9:2048, 2018). DRN 5-HT activity promotes waiting for delayed reward.
- **Mechanism:** serotonergic signaling supports patience / sustained pursuit toward a
  delayed goal -- maintenance of goal-directed waiting.
- **REE mapping:** already encoded in MECH-186/187/188 (serotonergic wanting floor / seeding
  gain / PFC top-down persistence). Relevant to L5 maintenance, NOT to the L2-L3 binding gap.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS existing MECH-186/187/188 as *maintenance* mechanisms;
  explicitly NOT the missing link. Flagged to avoid mis-attributing the binding gap to a
  maintenance/serotonin fix.

### D3. Acetylcholine / plasticity windows
- **Mechanism (general):** ACh gates cortical plasticity windows; developmental critical
  periods bias what cue-outcome associations are learnable.
- **REE mapping:** maps loosely onto the scaffolded_sd054_onboarding developmental-window
  scheduler (P0/P1/P2). NOT directly useful for the minimal L2-L3 repair.
- **Claim implication:** OUT OF SCOPE for the minimal repair; noted for completeness so it
  is not invoked prematurely. (The "developmental window" framing of 626 was the wrong lens
  given 626 never formed z_goal.)

## E. Computational analogues

### E1. Dayan 1993 (SR); Barreto et al. 2017 (successor features)
Dayan P. "Improving generalization for temporal difference learning: the successor
representation." *Neural Computation* 5(4):613-624, 1993. Barreto A, Dabney W, Munos R, et al.
"Successor features for transfer in reinforcement learning." *NeurIPS* 2017.
- **Mechanism:** factor value into a state/successor representation (the "where you'll go")
  and a reward weight vector (the "what's good"). Successor *features* let value retarget when
  the reward vector changes -- separating dynamics from goal identity.
- **REE mapping:** the goal pointer (L4) should index a *reward/identity weight* (the "what"),
  separable from z_world dynamics (the "where"). REE conflates them by writing z_world into
  z_goal. SF/SR is the formal statement of why object binding helps.
- **Claim implication:** SUGGESTS-MISSING the what/where factorization (PROP: MECH-GOALPTR).

### E2. Schaul et al. 2015 (UVFA); Andrychowicz et al. 2017 (HER)
Schaul T, Horgan D, Gregor K, Silver D. "Universal value function approximators." *ICML* 2015.
Andrychowicz M, et al. "Hindsight experience replay." *NeurIPS* 2017.
- **Mechanism:** condition value/policy on an explicit *goal* input; goals are vectors the
  agent is steered toward. Goal-conditioning is a first-class input, not an emergent attractor.
- **REE mapping:** z_goal-as-conditioning-input (e1_goal_conditioned=True) is the UVFA shape.
  Supports keeping z_goal as an explicit conditioning vector; argues the *source* of that
  vector (object token) is what matters.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS the z_goal-conditioning design; relocates the problem to
  the write-source (L4).

### E3. Object-centric RL -- Diuk et al. 2008; Watters et al. 2019
Diuk C, Cohen A, Littman ML. "An object-oriented representation for efficient reinforcement
learning." *ICML* 2008. Watters N, Matthey L, Bosnjak M, et al. "COBRA: ... object-centric
... exploration." 2019 (arXiv:1905.09275).
- **Mechanism:** represent the world as objects with attributes; value/transition defined
  over object identities -> dramatic sample-efficiency and transfer; supports binding reward
  to object identity.
- **REE mapping:** the incentive-object token (L3) is an object-centric value handle. SD-049
  per-resource type tags are a minimal object-centric substrate already present.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS building L3 on SD-049's existing identity tags before any
  learned-embedding investment.

### E4. Khetarpal et al. 2020 -- affordances in RL
Khetarpal K, Ahmed Z, Comanici G, Precup D. "What can I do here? A theory of affordances in
reinforcement learning." *ICML* 2020.
- **Mechanism:** formalizes affordances as state-dependent action availabilities; restricts
  models to relevant intents, improving planning.
- **REE mapping:** the "affordance representation" option for the z_goal write-source (L4):
  z_goal could point to an affordance ("this object is consumable") rather than a raw latent.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS the affordance-pointer variant of MECH-GOALPTR; lower
  priority than the object-token variant for the minimal repair.

### E5. Dayan & Berridge 2014 -- model-based Pavlovian / revaluation
Dayan P, Berridge KC. "Model-based and model-free Pavlovian reward learning: revaluation,
revision, and revelation." *Cognitive, Affective, & Behavioral Neuroscience* 14(2):473-492, 2014.
- **Mechanism:** Pavlovian incentive values can be model-based (revaluation-sensitive) --
  cue-triggered wanting can reflect current, recomputed outcome value.
- **REE mapping:** ties A1/A4/B5 together: the wanted-token should be drive-revaluable at
  cue time (matches Zhang 2009 kappa). Confirms the multiplier-at-recall design.
- **Claim implication:** SUPPORTS the design where drive re-weights a stored object value at
  cue encounter (L3/L6).

**Net:** the literature strongly and consistently supports the intake's central structural
claim -- an object/cue value representation that (a) is bound to identity, (b) is
drive-revaluable at cue encounter, and (c) generates wanting/approach dissociable from
hedonic liking -- sits *between* the benefit pulse and the goal representation. REE has the
endpoints (benefit pulse, z_goal, approach bridge) but not this middle layer. No source
licenses claiming REE *has* demonstrated wanting/liking dissociation; the biology only
licenses the *design* and the *tests*.
