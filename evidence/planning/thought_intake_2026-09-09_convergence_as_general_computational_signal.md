## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> **Agreement among sufficiently independent, differently generated evaluations may itself be useful
> information.**

> **Cross-view convergence can be represented as a meta-signal that helps allocate commitment,
> attention, compute, exploration or representational revision.**

> The computationally important quantity may be neither `max(A,B,C,D,E)` nor simply `A+B+C+D+E` ...
> Instead the system may benefit from knowing: How many genuinely different evidential routes point in
> the same direction, with what reliability, and how much of that agreement is redundant?

> **Use convergence first as an attention / compute-allocation signal, not as an automatic truth or
> action signal.**

> **Convergence is valuable only while meaningful diversity is preserved.**

And the strongest formulation the author himself settles on, which is already a retreat from the title:

> A cognitive system containing genuinely different evaluative and representational routes can treat
> unexpected agreement among them as information. The value of that agreement depends on reliability,
> relevance and effective independence. ... Its complement -- structured disagreement -- must remain
> preserved because diversity is what makes convergence informative.

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "The computationally important quantity may be neither `max(A,B,C,D,E)` nor simply `A+B+C+D+E`."
- "five routes agreeing independently" versus "one error echoed through five routes."
- "This is closely analogous to scientific evidence: multiple methods are most informative when their
  failure modes are not identical."
- "A convergence signal should not merely reward agreement. It should make both of these events
  salient: unexpected convergence / unexpected divergence."
- "a constitutional selector gives multiple drives, valuations and constraints lawful access to action;
  a convergence signal could be one meta-variable describing the geometry of their current agreement or
  disagreement."
- "Two strongly correlated evaluators should not count as two independent votes merely because both are
  loud."
- "curiosity asks where useful uncertainty exists; convergence asks where resolving one uncertainty may
  simultaneously update several partially independent models."
- "The next work should therefore be evidence-first: archaeology, literature review, formalisation, and
  discriminating assays. No claim should be minted from this thought until those passes are complete."
  -- **all four of those passes have since been completed**, which is the procedural argument for
  registering now.
