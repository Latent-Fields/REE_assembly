## 1. The refusal, in one sentence

**C1' (content alignment vs. shuffled/non-attained controls) is unpassable, and the readiness gate meant to certify the substrate could support it (G3) does not actually certify what it needs to.** Both defects trace to the SAME root cause the red-team found in the environment, not the criterion arithmetic: the representation credited at a subgoal-attainment tick is sensed AFTER the agent's own position marker overwrites the waypoint cell in `local_view`, so the credited z_world snapshot at the exact moment of attainment carries little to no waypoint-specific signature for any encoder, however well-trained, to amplify. This is the mirror-image failure to 884a's: 884a's C1 could not FAIL; 884b's C1' can basically only ever FAIL, for a *different*, environment-level reason that P0-recipe tuning cannot fix.

## 3. Red-team findings (fable, 2026-09-14, condensed -- BLOCKING)

Full text preserved in the session transcript; reproduced here for governance/future-session reference.

**F1 -- BLOCKING -- C1' is unpassable by construction.** `delta_group` is bounded above by `1 - cos(parent_true, parent_non_attained)`, which is the same between-group mean-direction separation G3 measures (ceiling ~0.0007 at the adopted P0 config). `CONTENT_DELTA_ABS_FLOOR=0.05` demands 70-250x that ceiling. Over 200 independent shuffle/non-attained draws on the red-team's own re-walk: 0/200 reached 0.05, and only 55% were even positive (sign is a coin flip). Once G0-G3 pass, the only reachable verdict is `FAIL/weakens`, predetermined.

**F2 -- BLOCKING (with F1) -- the verdict attributes an encoder-resolution limit to MECH-428.** `_replay_parent_from_reprs` is an exact deterministic replay of the substrate's own decay+credit math (independently re-verified against `ree_core/goal.py` and confirmed bit-identical, `max|diff|=0.0`, for the true-order case). C1' therefore reduces to "does z_world's EMA over attained ticks differ from its EMA over transit ticks" -- G3's own question at a stricter threshold -- and carries no information about `credit_subgoal_attainment` (the mechanism MECH-428 actually claims) that G3 did not already test. A weakens (or, hypothetically, a supports) verdict would be a statement about the encoder/observation model, not the bootstrap mechanism.

**F3 -- CONFIRMED, BLOCKING as F1's precondition -- G3 certifies noise, not content-discriminability.** A label-permutation null (200 random 10-vs-70 partitions of the SAME probe representations) gives median dissimilarity 0.000859, p95 0.0027; the observed "trained" G3 value sits at the ~62nd percentile of ITS OWN null, and 96% of random partitions clear the 0.0002 floor. G3 is measuring finite-sample noise of a small-group mean against a large one, scaled by encoder output variance (monotonically increasing with `preservation_weight` for that reason) -- not whether waypoint ticks are represented differently from transit ticks. It cannot do the job asked of it (rule out "the encoder is too degenerate to test this" as a reading of a C1' failure).

**F4 -- CONTESTED, and the actual root cause -- the credited observation carries no signature of the attained waypoint.** `causal_grid_world.py` overwrites the grid cell with the agent's own marker on arrival (confirmed at the relevant `grid[new_x,new_y] = agent` assignment; `local_view` reads `self.grid` directly), and SD-094(b)'s marker restoration fires only on departure. On the credit tick, the waypoint marker is therefore hidden under the agent's own marker in `local_view` on a majority of attained ticks (measured 62% on the red-team's own env-only replay, i.e. a marker is visible on only 38% of attained ticks vs 61-69% of transit ticks -- the signal points the WRONG way). This is an environment/observation-timing property, not an encoder-training deficiency -- no P0 recipe change can recover a signature the observation never carried in the first place. It is upstream of, and explains, both F1 and F3.

**Minor, non-blocking, worth carrying forward:** G1 (the forced-seed positive control) is not actually a structure test -- `FORCED_CREDIT=20` saturates `a=min(1,alpha*credit)` to 1.0, so `parent=z_world` exactly every credited tick; G1 only tests `||z_world|| >= floor`. NO_SUBGOAL enters no scored criterion (readiness-only, per its own DV-symmetry declaration -- expected, not a new finding). The G3-fail `degeneracy_reason` string in the parked script still describes an earlier (superseded) version of the G3 statistic and should be corrected before any reuse.

## 4. What ratifying a fix would need (for whoever picks this up)

The calibration trail (section 2) already rules out "just tune P0 harder" -- three separate G3 statistic redesigns and a 5x P0-strength escalation all converged on the same ceiling. F4 names the actual lever: the CREDITED REPRESENTATION must be sensed at a tick where the waypoint's own signature is actually present in the observation, not after the agent's marker has overwritten it. Two candidate directions, neither evaluated by this session (time-boxed out; see chip note):

1. **Credit the PRE-ARRIVAL representation** -- snapshot z_world one tick before the move that lands on the waypoint (i.e. credit using last tick's `_current_latent`, sensed while the agent was still adjacent, not on top of, the target cell). Cheap, stays within the existing hazard/resource-free env design; needs a full recalibration pass (G3 threshold AND the C1' margin) since the credited representation's statistics change.
2. **Credit from the SD-WAYPOINT-FIELD observable** (`waypoint_proximity_field_view`, `causal_grid_world.py`, already validated by V3-EXQ-1004) instead of raw `z_world` -- this channel is specifically designed to encode directional proximity to the pending waypoint and does not depend on the local-view marker at all. Trades the "generic, not waypoint-specific" P0 design principle (module docstring point 1, itself motivated by V3-EXQ-1030's circularity concern) for a channel that is waypoint-specific by construction -- would need its own circularity argument, since 1030's caution was specifically about training P0 ON the waypoint field before testing decodability of it; crediting the field directly (not training toward it) may or may not inherit that concern and needs its own red-team pass either way.

Either direction needs: an updated F1-style feasibility check (measure the achievable ceiling BEFORE fixing `CONTENT_DELTA_ABS_FLOOR`, not after -- exactly the gap this session's own dv_headroom block should have caught and didn't, since it recorded a bound (`[-1,1]`) rather than an achievable estimate); a G3 statistic that survives F3's permutation-null test by construction; and one more `/queue-experiment` Step 4.5 pass (the causal chain would change, so a re-spawn is warranted, once, per the skill's own rule).
