## Trigger

Discussion of REE learning requirements identified several mechanisms for which adjacent research exists but no clear ready-made learning rule is apparent: loveability internalisation, correction without global self-devaluation, responsibility-generated repair, self-to-other valuation transfer without a moral reward, responsibility-sensitive residue, endogenous internal-interface repair, and language-mediated ethical-model repair.

The key methodological concern is that REE's literature-first discipline could accidentally become a veto on precisely the mechanisms the project may need to discover experimentally.

## Core intake statement

Absence of direct literature must not by itself block implementation or experimental promotion of a mechanism required to make REE's ethical propositions testable.

Where a scoped search finds no direct precedent:

1. record the nearest analogues and the unsolved remainder;
2. classify the candidate as a frontier mechanism rather than literature-supported;
3. implement it as a falsifiable, preferably competing, hypothesis;
4. require stronger causal, ablation, negative-control, replication, and generalisation evidence;
5. permit strong REE experimental evidence to promote the mechanism even if literature confidence remains low.

A literature pull may legitimately terminate with "no direct mechanism found; proceed experimentally."

## Candidate governance fan-out

- Consider a `literature_status: direct | adjacent | frontier` provenance field.
- Preserve independent literature and experimental confidence.
- Consider an explicit exception/termination condition for biology-before-formalisation: adjacent evidence constrains the design, but no direct precedent exists.
- Do not automatically register or promote any mechanism from this intake.
- Any future governance change should be separately reviewed because this doctrine changes evidential routing, not merely one architectural claim.

## Methodological literature now supporting the frontier route

A targeted literature check found direct methodological precedent for discovering computational mechanisms when the investigator can specify the target phenomenon more clearly than the implementation.

Relevant anchors:

- **AutoCog** — Jagadish et al. (2026), *Closing the Loop to Discover Psychological Theories with an Automated Cognitive Scientist*: executable competing theories, discriminating experiments, behavioural data, failure diagnosis, successor synthesis. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26448
- **Automated Adversarial Collaboration** — Chandramouli, Kachergis & Jagadish (2026): LLM theory agents + program synthesis + information-theoretic experimental design; candidate models and discriminating experiments can both be discovered during adjudication. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25521
- **ATLAS** — Éltető et al. (2026): alternating mechanistic-hypothesis generation and active experiment design intended to distinguish the candidates. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12386
- **Symbolic cognitive-model discovery** — Castro et al. (ICML 2025): FunSearch-style program synthesis discovered interpretable cognitive learning/decision rules from behavioural data. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/castro25a.html
- **FunSearch** — Romera-Paredes et al. (Nature 2024): user specifies an evaluator/skeleton; search evolves the difficult program logic and can generate genuinely novel algorithms. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6
- **Optimal experimental design for model discrimination** — Myung & Pitt (2009): select experiments to maximise informativeness for distinguishing rival cognitive models. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016104
- **Robin** — Hinks et al. (Nature 2026): iterative agentic literature/hypothesis/experiment/data-analysis/update loop in experimental biology. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10652-y

### Implication for REE

The frontier route can therefore be grounded as:

1. specify the phenomenon, causal contract, forbidden writes/shortcuts, and falsifiers;
2. generate multiple small executable candidate mechanisms;
3. prove causal reach;
4. actively search for experiments in which the candidates diverge;
5. apply negative controls and ablations;
6. diagnose the failure mode of losers;
7. synthesize constrained successor candidates;
8. escalate from simple fixed rules to program search or learned mappings only when simpler hypothesis classes fail;
9. promote on replicated causal evidence, not on prosocial-looking phenotype.

### Evaluator warning

The evaluator is load-bearing. REE must not turn this into scalar reward engineering.

A frontier candidate should not be scored simply on "kindness", "low harm", or "prosociality". The adjudication battery should include non-compensable gates for the causal properties that make the ethical claim meaningful, including where relevant:

- live unethical alternative remains available;
- correct causal attribution;
- content-specific rather than channel-presence effects;
- self-valence stability versus global self-collapse;
- repair versus appeasement/punishment avoidance;
- other-state information genuinely used;
- ablation removes the effect;
- novel-ecology/developmental generalisation.

This literature strengthens the doctrine from "REE is allowed to proceed without prior literature" to "there are established and emerging methods for systematically discovering the missing mechanism rather than guessing it."

## Falsification principle

If a proposed axiom-derived mechanism repeatedly fails unless the desired ethical outcome is directly inserted as reward, rule, privileged label, or hard constraint, that failure should count against the derivation rather than being hidden by increasingly elaborate scaffolding.

### Key formulations (verbatim, load-bearing)

> Literature is evidence, prior constraint, analogy, warning, and source of candidate
> mechanisms. It is **not permission**.

> no direct literature -> mark the mechanism as frontier/conjectural, identify the closest
> precedents and what they fail to solve, then make the mechanism earn standing experimentally.

> Class F is **not a lower-status scientific category**. It is a different provenance category.

> "no paper found" should not be entered into governance as if it were evidence against a
> mechanism unless there is a defensible reason to believe that the relevant literature
> **should already contain the mechanism if it were viable**.

> The absence of literature should therefore increase the **experimental burden**, not
> prohibit the experiment.

> Frontier status means only: **the answer must come primarily from experiment.**

> If repeated implementations cannot make one of the axiom-derived transitions work without
> inserting the desired conclusion as a reward or rule, that counts against the claimed
> derivation.

> **REE must be allowed to discover mechanisms that the literature does not yet contain, and
> must be allowed to reject mechanisms that its own philosophy predicts.**
