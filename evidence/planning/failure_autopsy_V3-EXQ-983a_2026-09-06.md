## 3. Biological-reference triage

Closest mechanism: hippocampal/striatal eligibility trace + passive-avoidance (one-trial aversive) learning. Its dependencies: a persistent trace written on harm; trace-modulated action selection with a repertoire to select among; an environment the agent survives long enough to revisit, in which revisiting is a choice. Not a formal import; literature present on both sides (targeted_review_ext_002, targeted_review_connectome_arc_013), no lit-pull owed.

The load-bearing divergence is at the measurement level: the biological signature of a persistent error trace is **reduced re-approach** (the animal stops going there), not a reduced harm probability conditional on re-approaching. In a deterministic world the conditional probability is fixed by the world. The 983 lineage's DV measures the conditional quantity, so on a pinned board it cannot move whatever the residue field does.
