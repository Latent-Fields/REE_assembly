## 0. Premises re-measured before probing

- **P6 / GFLAG-0486 (D0, confirmed by direct re-read on this sha, matching the inventory's independent D0 finding):** `REEAgent.update_residue` (`agent.py:12191-12220`) passes `z_world = self._current_latent.z_world` -- the **pre-step** latent set by this tick's own `sense()`, not yet advanced -- into `E3TrajectorySelector.post_action_update(actual_z_world=z_world, ...)` (`e3_selector.py:4998`). Inside it, `predicted_world = ref_trajectory.world_states[1]` and `prediction_error = actual_z_world - predicted_world` (`e3_selector.py:5019-5024`), where `ref_trajectory` is `self._committed_trajectory or self._last_selected_trajectory` -- the trajectory selected at the **most recent E3 tick**, not necessarily this tick's. So: on an E3 tick itself, this compares z_t to E2's prediction of z_{t+1} made *from* z_t -- a **predicted displacement**, not a realised error. On the very next env step (no new E3 tick, `_last_selected_trajectory` unchanged), the same `world_states[1]` is compared against the genuinely-arrived z_{t+1} -- a real one-step error. On every subsequent env step before the next E3 tick, it is compared against later, increasingly stale states -- drift against a stale prediction. **This holds exactly as the inventory's P6 correction stated; nothing here contradicts it.**
- **Commit rule (D0, confirmed):** at defaults (`use_harm_variance_commit`, `use_conditional_precision_gate`, `use_variance_tracking_commit_threshold` all False on both arms tested), `committed = self._running_variance < effective_threshold` with `effective_threshold == config.e3.commitment_threshold == 0.40` (`e3_selector.py:4322-4327`, bar at `config.py:1308`). `E3TrajectorySelector.get_commitment_state()` (`e3_selector.py:5300-5325`) exposes `running_variance` and `committed_now` directly -- the probe reads this every env step (rv itself updates every step per `agent.py:12213-12216`'s comment, not only on E3 ticks) rather than re-deriving it.
- **rv is never reset per episode** (P7, confirmed): `_running_variance` persists across the probe's 2 in-run episode resets at step ~200; no reset call exists on `E3TrajectorySelector`.

## 2. Result: commit occupancy, and whether INT and NATIVE sit in different regimes

**They do not, in this probe.** Every one of the 9 (arm x seed) runs commits **permanently by env-step index 4** (i.e. the 5th tick from a fresh agent) and stays committed for at least 396 of the remaining 396 steps (`commit_frac` 0.99 in all 9; `commit_frac_second_half` 1.00 in all 9). This is arm-INDEPENDENT to within measurement noise:

| arm | rv p50 (per seed 0/1/2) | rv p90 (per seed) | commit_frac | goes-permanent-at (env step) | mean \|\|z_world\|\| (per seed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NATIVE | 0.00309 / 0.00370 / 0.00429 | 0.0641 / 0.0646 / 0.0651 | 0.99 / 0.99 / 0.99 | 4 / 4 / 4 | 0.467 / 0.446 / 0.539 |
| INT-CODEC | 0.00281 / 0.00259 / 0.00711 | 0.0637 / 0.0647 / 0.0652 | 0.99 / 0.99 / 0.99 | 4 / 4 / 4 | 4.143 / 3.503 / 3.979 |
| NATIVE-NOKNOBS | 0.00302 / 0.00371 / 0.00431 | 0.0640 / 0.0646 / 0.0650 | 0.99 / 0.99 / 0.99 | 4 / 4 / 4 | 0.179 / 0.104 / 0.485 |

- **The commit-onset timing (`goes_permanent_at`) is identical (env-step 4) in all 9 runs**, and this is explained mechanically, not coincidentally: `_running_variance` starts at `precision_init = 0.5` (`config.py:1310`) and decays by a **fixed** EMA (`precision_ema_alpha = 0.05`, same constant, same config field, in every arm) toward whatever the arm's steady-state error sits at. Since every arm's steady-state rv (p50 0.0026-0.0071, p90 ~0.065) sits **1-2 orders of magnitude below the 0.40 - 0.5 gap the EMA has to cross**, the number of ticks to first cross 0.40 is dominated by the fixed `(precision_init, alpha, threshold)` triple, not by which arm is running: `0.95^4 x 0.5 ~ 0.407` (still above bar), `0.95^5 x 0.5 ~ 0.387` (below) -- matching the measured crossing at index 4 exactly, arithmetic that does not reference the arm at all.
- **The C.4 worry -- that W6a's z_world scale-up (measured here: INT-CODEC's mean \|\|z_world\|\| is 7-9x NATIVE's and 8-38x NATIVE-NOKNOBS's, in the direction and rough order the `w6a_world_encoder_member_build_20260925.md` "10-14x" figure predicts, though not yet fully converged at 400 steps) would inflate INT's rv by ~100-200x and thereby put it in a different commit regime -- does not materialise.** INT-CODEC's rv (p50 0.0026-0.0071 across seeds) is the **same order of magnitude as NATIVE's** (p50 0.0031-0.0043), not 100-200x higher. This confirms the audit's own alternative reading rather than the naive one: **W3 trains the very E2 head rv reads, on the same (enlarged) z_world scale, so the relative error -- not the raw scale -- is what rv tracks**, and training keeps that relative error comparable to NATIVE's rather than letting the raw scale-up leak through. INT-CODEC seed 2's higher p50 (0.0071 vs seed 0/1's ~0.0027) shows this isn't a free lunch -- there is real seed-to-seed variation in how well the co-scaling holds -- but no seed came close to crossing an order of magnitude, let alone flipping the commit regime.
- **Caveat, stated plainly:** this is a 400-env-step probe (56-325 E3 ticks depending on cadence per arm/seed), far short of A1's 3,000-step arms, and by the time any scale-driven divergence between arms could have grown, the gate is already saturated (>=98% committed from tick ~5 onward) in every arm. So the honest claim is narrower than "INT and NATIVE always agree": it is **"in every arm tested, the gate saturates near-total commitment almost immediately from a fresh agent, and z_world's scale-up does not visibly disturb that outcome at this horizon."** Whether a much longer run could still pull the two apart (e.g. if INT's rv continues drifting upward as seed 2 hints) is not answered here.
- U3 in `dynamic_control_audit_20260926.md` sec D asked whether this needed a change (ARC-029(D) bar in every arm, or other). **Given this result, no change is indicated**: the absolute-bar-driven regime split the audit worried about is not observed at this horizon, so the recommendation is to leave the bar as-is and note this as a bound on the concern's applicability, not a rebuttal of GFLAG-0346 (the absorbing-commitment finding itself is independently reconfirmed here, sec 3).

## 3. GFLAG-0346 (absorbing commitment) reconfirmed here, arm-independently

`commit_frac_second_half` is 1.00 in all 9 runs (i.e. after the first ~half of each 400-step run, the agent is committed on every single subsequent tick). This is the same absorbing-state phenomenon `config.py:1291-1306`'s GFLAG-0346 comment describes ("committed_step_fraction is 1.0000 even at MECH-108 sweep_amplitude 0.999"), now also confirmed **without any waking-trainer learning at all** (the NATIVE and NATIVE-NOKNOBS arms have `waking_trainer_enabled=False`, i.e. no gradient learning per P2 in `dynamic_control_audit_inventory_20260926.md`): the EMA's fixed decay from `precision_init=0.5` toward a small steady-state error is sufficient on its own, because the agent's own committed argmin choice quickly settles into a self-consistent repeated-action loop whose one-step "error" (predicted-displacement, per sec 0) is small essentially by construction, independent of whether E2 was ever trained.

## 4. D1 check: is rv's raw input E2's realised one-step error, or its predicted displacement? (GFLAG-0486)

Per-tick `prediction_error` (the raw quantity that feeds the rv EMA, `e3_metrics["prediction_error"]`, i.e. `error_var` before smoothing) was bucketed by **ticks since the last E3 tick** (k=0: an E3 tick itself, i.e. the self-referential-displacement case; k=1: the immediately following tick, the first genuinely realised one-step error; k>=2: increasing staleness):

| arm | seed | k=0 | k=1 | k=2 | k=3 | k=4 | k=5+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NATIVE | 0 | 0.00308 | 0.00307 | 0.00302 | 0.00301 | 0.00301 | 0.00301 |
| NATIVE | 1 | 0.00368 | 0.00368 | 0.00364 | 0.00364 | 0.00364 | 0.00365 |
| NATIVE | 2 | 0.00424 | 0.00423 | 0.00423 | 0.00423 | 0.00423 | 0.00424 |
| INT-CODEC | 0 | 0.00129 | 0.00171 | 0.00254 | 0.00307 | 0.00374 | 0.00288 |
| INT-CODEC | 1 | 0.00147 | 0.00147 | 0.00184 | 0.00232 | 0.00287 | 0.00361 |
| INT-CODEC | 2 | 0.00471 | 0.00424 | 0.00554 | 0.00707 | 0.00883 | 0.01009 |
| NATIVE-NOKNOBS | 0 | 0.00292 | 0.00293 | 0.00289 | 0.00291 | 0.00295 | 0.00310 |
| NATIVE-NOKNOBS | 1 | 0.00364 | 0.00359 | 0.00372 | 0.00378 | 0.00385 | 0.00401 |
| NATIVE-NOKNOBS | 2 | 0.00414 | 0.00418 | 0.00419 | 0.00420 | 0.00421 | 0.00423 |

- **Confirmed (D1), in the arm where it is actually visible: INT-CODEC.** In all 3 seeds, k=0 (predicted displacement) is smaller than k>=2..4 (growing staleness), and in 2/3 seeds (0, 1) the growth from k=0/1 through k=4 is monotonic, exactly the "small at the tick itself, real error one tick later, then drifting against a stale prediction" pattern the P6 correction predicts. INT-CODEC seed 2's non-monotonicity between k=0 and k=1 (0.00471 -> 0.00424) is a single-seed exception on a small n (58 vs 93 ticks) and does not overturn the pattern -- k=2 through k=5+ still rise monotonically from there.
- **Flat, not contradictory, in NATIVE and NATIVE-NOKNOBS.** Neither the trainer nor the world model changes here, and the agent has already settled into a self-consistent repeated-action loop (sec 3): if the same action is taken every tick, the "one-step-ahead prediction" made at the last E3 tick stays valid for many subsequent ticks (nothing is actually drifting), so k has no real staleness to reveal. This is the expected consequence of the degenerate absorbing loop, not evidence against the P6 mechanism -- the mechanism is about what the *quantity measures*, and it is only observable when the referent (E2's prediction accuracy over time) actually varies, which requires either genuine training (INT-CODEC) or a non-degenerate trajectory.
- **Verdict:** GFLAG-0486 premise correction (rv's input is E2's predicted displacement on an E3 tick, not a realised error, becoming a real one-step error one tick later and then stale drift) is confirmed **at both D0 (code read, sec 0) and D1 (measured, this section)**. No refuting evidence was found in any of the 9 runs.

## 5. Limits and what stays open

- **Domain reached: D1** throughout (the quantities were measured to exist and move as predicted; no intervention was run to show a *native consumer* changes as a result -- that would be D2, e.g. actually degrading E2 and watching rv/committed respond, which this probe did not attempt and was not asked to).
- **Not tested:** INT-ACT (action-space-proposal variant), GROUNDED mode (W5/valuation), the reset-init asymmetry itself as a controlled contrast (both NATIVE and INT-CODEC here have knobs ON; NATIVE-NOKNOBS is the OFF reference but is not compared to an INT-CODEC-NOKNOBS arm, since the brief's three arms did not include one) -- C.4 observation 2 (U2 in the audit) is untouched by this record.
- **400 steps is short.** The seed-2 INT-CODEC rv drift (p50 0.0071, more than double seed 0/1's ~0.0027, still rising at k=5+ in the D1 table) is the one hint in this data that the co-scaling protection (sec 2) may not hold indefinitely; a longer run than this probe's budget allowed would be needed to say whether it stabilizes, keeps rising toward NATIVE's order of magnitude, or beyond.
