## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> **Specialised cognitive systems may preserve distinct high-dimensional internal representations while
> coordinating through low-dimensional, context-sensitive communication subspaces and low-complexity
> transformations. Development can increase both local specialisation and selective inter-system
> legibility. Replay and sleep may selectively stabilise or reconfigure these interfaces rather than
> globally aligning all representations.**

And the operative experimental principle:

> **Before declaring a representation inadequate, determine whether the needed information is absent,
> merely inaccessible to the native consumer, outside the active communication subspace, dynamically
> incompatible, or present only as a generic channel effect.**

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "**decodable != accessible != bridgeable != pairing-specific != causally used != behaviourally beneficial.**"
- "**a representation can improve while behaviour remains unchanged because the consumer cannot express the improvement.**"
- "**try the least expressive transformation first.**" -- "The simpler the bridge that works, the stronger the evidence that the sender already contained an appropriately structured representation."
- "A channel can therefore be strongly load-bearing while its **example-specific informational content is barely load-bearing**."
- "**representation adequacy is relational.**" -- "A sufficiently powerful decoder can become a covert second cognitive system."
- "**local specialisation can rise at the same time as interface legibility rises.**"
- "**Integration does not require representational sameness. It requires selective mutual legibility.**"
- On sleep: "the campaign has not yet found direct biological evidence that sleep reduces a formal bridge-complexity metric between two independently specialised systems. That remains a **REE hypothesis**."
