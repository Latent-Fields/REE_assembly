## 2. Method

- **Training:** `probes/w3/w3_l2r_member_probe.py`'s exact recipe (real arm only -- the SHUF twin tests action-map durability, not rollout boundedness, so it is not needed here), reproduced in `gate_c_probe.py`: `E2WorldMember` via `WakingTrainer`, W2a `StructuredBabbler` babbling over 12 Phase-0 episodes (k=0..11) into the FROZEN retained set, 3000 pre-updates, then 1200 native closed-loop on-policy steps (k=50..55) at 8 member updates/step with 25% retained replay, re-encoded. Seeds 106-110 (the same 5 seeds the W3 record itself used, for direct comparability).
- **Readout:** the trained `e2.world_transition` / `e2.world_action_encoder` state dicts are loaded into `ref`, a full production `REEAgent` built via `rollout_fidelity_probe.build_B` (`REEConfig.from_dims`, `CausalGridWorldV2` 8x8, world_dim 32) -- so `ref.hippocampal` is the **real deployed** `HippocampalModule` (K=32, H=30 per P1, A=5, I=3), not the W1-alt build's ad-hoc `horizon=10` stand-in used pre-W3.
- **Held-out states:** `BB.gen_policy` at env k-index 300+ (disjoint from every k-range training touched: babble 0-11, post-phase 50-55), stepped through `ref.sense()` (the same "native read path" `babble_probe.encode_segs()` uses for its own test set, extended here to also keep `z_self`, which `encode_segs` discards). One `(z_world, z_self)` sample per generated episode segment at a per-episode-random tick. `CausalGridWorldV2` terminates early on a hazard hit inside a nominal 200-step block, so `gen_policy`'s 20 outer episode-blocks split into 166-183 segments per seed (not 20) -- more probe states than planned, not a bug, and reported per seed below.
- **Gate (c) computed exactly as defined**, per mode (ASP-E = `stratified`, ASP-0 = `stratified_uniform`, ASP-R = `refit`), per seed: pool every candidate's rollout ratio (`world_states` norm at t=H / norm at t=0) and every candidate's per-step growth (norm at step i / norm at step i-1) across all held-out states x K=32 candidates; PASS iff the pooled median ratio is in [0.5, 2] **and** the pooled max growth is <= 1.05.
- **Cross-check:** every per-state readout was independently recomputed from `Trajectory.get_world_state_sequence()` and compared against the module's own `action_space_rollout_norm_ratio_median` / `action_space_rollout_max_step_growth_max` diagnostics (same pattern as `test_gate_c_rollout_norm_readout_matches_independent_recomputation`). Every candidate's `metadata["source"]` was also checked, to separate genuine ASP candidates (`"action_space_cem"`) from anything injected by machinery outside the ASP mechanism (support-preserving CEM injection, scaffold, chunk splice, ghost probes) -- all default-OFF on the isolated W1-alt contract harness, but `use_support_preserving_cem` is **default True** on the full production `REEConfig.from_dims()` agent used here (see sec 4 finding F2).

## 4. Is the gate ill-posed on the real head? What I found, stated plainly

The design's (c) text ("no per-step growth > 1.05 ... read with the W3 head") is not ambiguous about the bound, but it under-specifies **which steps and which statistic** count as "per-step growth" once a genuinely trained head is in hand, and the substrate's own adjacent, already-passing readout uses a materially different convention:

- **This readout** (matching the ASP module's own `action_space_rollout_max_step_growth_max` diagnostic, and the module's literal per-step ratio computation) takes the **MAX** growth ratio over **every one of the H=30 simulated steps**, across every candidate, across every held-out state. This is the natural, and I think correct, literal reading of "no per-step growth > 1.05" -- it is what actually reaches E3 as a candidate trajectory, and it is what the module itself reports.
- **The W3 record's own gate (e)** (`w3_e2_world_member_build_20260925.md` sec 3, "rollout t30 bounded, no x1.2/step growth") computed a **MEAN** growth ratio over only the **late** steps of a 30-step rollout (steps 21-30), from **uniform random** one-hot actions (not CEM/ASP-selected ones), and passed comfortably on all 5 seeds (median late growth 1.001-1.012, max 1.011-1.047).
- These are different statistics of different action distributions over different step ranges, and they give opposite verdicts on the same trained heads. I did **not** substitute the W3 record's late/mean convention for the design's literal per-step/max wording to make gate (c) pass -- the number reported as the gate-c verdict above is the literal reading, and it FAILS. But the discrepancy itself is worth naming: **either** (a) the design's (c) threshold was calibrated against the untrained head's obviously-bad ~1.15-1.2/step signature and was never validated against what a genuinely well-trained head produces at the deployed 30-step horizon under CEM-style (not uniform-random) action selection, in which case 1.05-per-any-step-anywhere may simply be the wrong bound for this horizon/selection regime and the corrected form should either shorten the step range it applies to (e.g. only the first k <= 10 steps, matching the contract file's own now-corrected `H=10` intuition) or switch to a late-window mean like gate (e)'s; **or** (b) CEM/ASP selection genuinely finds action sequences that are more rollout-destabilizing than uniform random ones (plausible: CEM optimizes toward the residue-terrain objective, which has no reason to avoid regions where E2's world_forward is poorly conditioned), in which case the growth-leg failure is real evidence that the trained W3 head's simulated rollouts are not safe to plan over at H=30 under an optimizing proposer, independent of which proposer (ASP or codec) does the optimizing. I did not have the budget in this probe-only readout to distinguish (a) from (b) (that needs the same measurement repeated on the native codec CEM pool at matching H, which was out of scope here); I am reporting the finding and the two candidate readings rather than picking one.

## 5. Verdict

**Gate (c): FAIL for ASP-E, ASP-0 and ASP-R, 5/5 seeds, on the literal design text.** The ratio leg ([0.5, 2] median-candidate bound) PASSes cleanly on all 5 seeds for all three modes; the growth leg (no per-step growth > 1.05) FAILs on all 5 seeds for all three modes, driven by a substantial (23-76%, seed-dependent) fraction of held-out states, mode-independently, on genuinely ASP-sourced candidates (not an injection artifact). Domain D1.
