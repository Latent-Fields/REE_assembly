## 1. Verbatim core proposal

> **An Aha-like event may be associated with a representational change that produces a sudden joint gain
> across several previously partly independent models or views.**

> The proposed novelty is not "insight involves restructuring." That is established territory. The
> possible new piece is: **some especially strong insights may be restructuring events with unusually
> broad cross-model explanatory reach.**

> **Insight can unify models without homogenising them.**

> a computational Aha signal must reward **compression under preserved reality contact**, not compression
> alone.

## 3. Key formulations (verbatim)

- "'Oh -- that is what all of these things were.'"
- "The subjective feeling should not be treated as the definition. It is a candidate consequence or
  metacognitive appraisal of the underlying restructuring."
- "So REE should distinguish: computational restructuring from metacognitive Aha signal."
- "If `Z` preserves or improves prediction while reducing the number of independent rules required, then
  several previously separate degrees of freedom collapse at once."
- "**'Same thing' must not mean 'same coordinates'.**"
- "The underlying evidence accumulation may therefore be gradual while the **accepted representation
  changes discontinuously**."
- "one change -> many errors reduced -> several contradictions disappear -> many future computations
  become cheaper"
- "Conspiracy theories, delusional systems, overconfident attractors and simplistic grand theories can
  make many facts 'fit' by discarding inconvenient constraints."
- "generates evidence and then counts it as independent confirmation; becomes less falsifiable as it
  becomes more confident."
- "Aha should probably be treated as an **event detector** over changes in these quantities, not as an
  always-on value signal."
- "preserve provenance so the abstraction does not count its own outputs as new evidence."
- "Only after the computational event is demonstrable should REE receive an Aha-like internal tag."
